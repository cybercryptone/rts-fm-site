Soultape's debut album, *The Last Universe*, is the 12th release on the RTS.FM label. Soultape has been recorded for RTS.FM since at least 2019, and the album ties new material to tracks that go back further. Published biographical detail is sparse, so this page follows the record and the sets the label has documented.

## On the RTS.FM label

*The Last Universe* is catalogued as RTSFM012 and was released on 14 November 2025 as a digital album, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/the-last-universe). The label calls it Soultape's debut album, 14 tracks none of which were previously released, with a style that moves between ambient, house and minimal, electro and breakbeat. Some tracks were originally recorded as far back as 2012, the description says, and are blended with entirely new work.

The tracklist runs "Singularity (intro)", "Lost Paradise", "Merkaba", "Intergalactic", "Dub Souvenir", "In My Eyes", "Destroy The Matrix", "Desert Ghosts", "Memories Of Future", "Hello 303", "Old Good Times", "Choices", "Let Me Know" and "Supernova (outro)". The opening and closing tracks are labelled intro and outro, and the label describes the whole as a hypnotic musical journey. Bandcamp tags it deep house, house, minimal house, ambient, breaks and electronic, with Moscow as the label's location.

## Sound and style

The album description is the best published statement of the sound: hypnotic, long-form and genre-fluid, with track lengths that run from about five to ten minutes. The opener "Singularity (intro)" is the longest at just over ten minutes, per the Bandcamp tracklist, and the closer is the shortest at around five. Titles such as "Hello 303" and "Destroy The Matrix" point to a taste for acid-adjacent synth lines, though we do not claim more than the titles and the label's own description show.

## Live recordings and presentation

RTS.FM's own YouTube archive holds the clearest picture of the live side. A session recorded for RTS.FM St. Petersburg in partnership with Taka Taka is dated 2 May 2019 in its description, and an "Album Presentation" for *The Last Universe* was recorded as a collaboration between RTS.FM and the Moscow venue Substance on 30 October 2025. The presentation's description lists a tracklist that mixes the album with other Soultape tracks, including "From The Heart", "Volatura", "Plastic Pop" and "Retromantic", plus a remix of Dibe's "Mystery Of Suffering".
