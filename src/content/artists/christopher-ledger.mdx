Christopher Ledger is an Italian DJ and producer from the coast near Rome who splits his time between Rome and Berlin and runs the Hidden Folder label. His sets mix garage, breaks, house and minimal techno. His solo Yoyaku instore session, published on 3 July 2024, has more than 108,000 YouTube views.

## Where is Christopher Ledger from and how did he start?

Ledger is Italian. In an interview published by Trommel on 4 October 2018 he said he was born and raised in a small seaside city about 50 km from Rome, without naming it. In a February 2023 interview with Delayed, he named his hometown as Anzio, in the province of Rome. His father played Bach and Mozart at home, according to Trommel, and he later studied classical composition. As a teenager he was into hip-hop, graffiti and basketball.

Delayed says he found vinyl at about 13 or 14, buying a pair of Stanton cartridges and teaching himself to mix on hip-hop records. At about 16 he was drawn into the Roman rave scene. He told Delayed that he moved to Berlin at 20 for a couple of years, and Trommel dates that stay to 2012 to 2014. He started playing clubs regularly around the end of 2016, shortly after his first record, and had a Saturday residency at Goa Club in Rome for the NoZoo crew.

Sources disagree on where he lives. Nightclubber and Beatportal call him Berlin-based, while Yoyaku and his Bandcamp page say Rome. Delayed describes him as "Berlin via Rome", living on the Roman seaside and spending most summers in Berlin. No source we opened gives his birth date, year or legal name.

## What does Christopher Ledger sound like?

Mixgraph tags his catalogue as minimal and deep tech, with UK garage, bassline, breaks and electro also appearing. Nightclubber and Delayed add ambient, deep house, drum and bass and IDM.

Lately he has pushed toward the dancefloor. In a 2024 Nightclubber interview he said he wants his own-name material to focus on peak-time tracks, citing peak-time UK speed garage, breakbeat and raw, deep and minimal cuts as influences. He also said he thinks of himself as a DJ before a producer. Beatportal's May 2025 review of "Can't Get Over" described classic UK garage sensibilities with Rhodes chords, chopped vocals and a warm low end.

His other work is multidisciplinary. Trommel describes CL Series, a platform that grew out of his graffiti and drawing background, and Delayed notes sample packs he made for Sample Market.

## What are the career highlights of Christopher Ledger?

### Early releases and Berlin imprints

Delayed dates "Lost with K" to 2015 on RoundQube Musik, his earliest release in our sources. By April 2019, XLR8R described him as an Italian artist based between Berlin and Rome who had appeared on Dana Ruh's Brouqade Records, on Mayak and on his own CL Series, where he had put out a mini-album with Luigi Ranghino's Trio.

### Meander, Amphia and Adam's Bite

XLR8R announced his *Dark Moon* EP as the 27th release on DeWalta's Meander, with vinyl on 17 May and digital on 14 June, in an article from April 2019. Trommel's premiere of "Swap Function" from the *Anticipation* EP on Adam's Bite, published on 25 November 2022, called it his first release in almost three years. The label's own 2025 announcement of *Inspection Report*, his return to Adam's Bite, listed Cocoon, Amphia, X-Kalay, Craigie Knowes and Sushitech among other labels on his CV.

### Hidden Folder

His own label opened with the *Inizializzazione* EP, HDF001, on 25 October 2023, a 40-copy vinyl run mixed at 043.soundlab in Rome, a studio where he jams on analogue gear. Nightclubber's 2024 interview lays out plans for compilations, a sub-label and showcases, and a dubplate series under another alias. Bandcamp also lists a Taylor Aaron EP, HDF999, from August 2023, and Yoyaku names Taylor Aaron as an alias.

### Club and live work

Trommel's 2018 piece places him at Goa Club for NoZoo, the Brouqade parties in Berlin and a set in Iasi, Romania on 7 July 2018. The same 2024 interview mentions recent gigs in Belgium, Italy and France. We did not find a verified festival list, so we give none.

## Which labels is Christopher Ledger associated with?

- **Hidden Folder.** His own imprint, launched in October 2023, with catalogue numbers HDF001 to HDF006 on his Bandcamp page.
- **Adam's Bite.** Two EPs in our sources, *Anticipation* (ADAM007, 2022 per Trommel) and *Inspection Report* (19 September 2025).
- **Meander.** *Dark Moon* (MEANDER027).
- **Amphia, Brouqade and RoundQube.** Yoyaku lists releases on each, including Amphia's AMP021 and Brouqade's BQD044 *Mpc Sketches*.
