Alec Falconer is an English DJ and producer from Kent who runs the Ba Dum Tish group of labels and plays house, UK garage and techno. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 23 August 2023, runs about 150 minutes and has 147,842 YouTube views, just outside our top 10.

## Where is Alec Falconer from and how did he start?

Falconer grew up in Kent, in south-east England. In a 2019 XLR8R Studio Essentials feature he said he was in bands through school, moved to Newcastle for university and taught himself production in Reason. He credited a former bandmate, Rob Amboule, with early help and Harry Wills with teaching him once he moved to Ableton. 

A later interview with Nightclubber.ro adds detail. He said his sister took him to the Tunbridge Wells Forum when he was about 11, and that seeing a local ska punk band made him want to play drums. Amboule joined his band as a synth player when Falconer was 14 or 15, and began showing him how to make music in Reason and then how to mix. He moved toward electronic music at university.

Before music became his job he worked as an environmental lobbyist in London and then, after moving to Paris, imported English alcohol to sell to bars and restaurants. He told Nightclubber that the move to Paris, where he began making music every day, changed everything. Both are first-person accounts.

## What does Alec Falconer sound like?

Descriptions vary, which suits an artist who works under several names. Trommel's March 2020 review of his *2XGREEN* 12" says his style moves between off-the-wall garage, tech house and playful techno, with punchy drums, mischievous 303 lines and vintage keys. Nightclubber says he is as likely to make UK garage as house and techno. The set79 profile, which indexes his recorded sets, says they mix house, speed garage and minimal cuts.

His live sets are broader than his releases. Trommel's podcast, number 172, was recorded live at the Lion & Lamb in London and drawn from his first all-night set there, in April 2023. It moves from deeper house through jazzy piano tracks to bass-driven cuts.

## What are the career highlights of Alec Falconer?

### Early releases and Phone Traxxx

By 2019 XLR8R described an outing on Oval Distribution's EXT series, an EP called *Flicker Zone* on Rue De Plaisance, and a 12" in the Entity series, catalogue 005, with a Harry Wills remix. It also credits him as a co-runner, with Wills and Amboule, of Phone Traxxx, a series of uptempo UK garage 12" records. Trommel describes the same trio as Phone Traxxx. XLR8R says he first played in the United States in 2019.

### 2XGREEN and the Jisul EP

*2XGREEN* was his debut on his own 2XGREEN label, reviewed by Trommel on 30 March 2020. The review names "Reflector Dub" and "Something For Mtkvarse" and says it had sold out at most major shops. Nightclubber says his last full EP under his own name, released on Jisul at the end of 2021, includes "Down The Lanes" and "Anna Notherthing". The interview does not say what the EP is called.

### Ba Dum Tish

Nightclubber says he founded the distribution company Ba Dum Tish at the start of the pandemic and that it released roughly one record a week. The label's Bandcamp page now calls it a one-time distributor and a selection of labels run by Falconer, with a London location. Its catalogue there includes Harry Wills EPs and the *Punchline* compilation series. In September 2025 the blog Torture The Artist described his "ever-mischievous" SUNNY label, whose third release, SUNNY03, was a two-tracker by Ballan.

### Touring

Nightclubber mentions recent shows ranging from Lebanon to London. The set79 index lists Ba Dum Tish nights on 20 April and 18 May 2024, the second as a back-to-back with Dr Banana.

## Which labels is Alec Falconer associated with?

Ba Dum Tish is the centre of his work. Sources tie him to these labels and series:

- **Ba Dum Tish.** The label and distributor he runs. His *No Turn Unstoned EP* carries the catalogue number TISH01, according to Trommel's 17 June 2024 premiere, which calls the label new.
- **2XGREEN (2X).** His own imprint, which carried his 2020 debut 12".
- **Jisul.** Released his EP at the end of 2021, per Nightclubber.
- **Rue De Plaisance.** Released *Flicker Zone*, per XLR8R.
- **Phone Traxxx.** A UK garage 12" series run with Harry Wills and Rob Amboule, per XLR8R.
- **SUNNY.** A label linked to the Ba Dum Tish brand, per Torture The Artist.

Our [S.A.M. profile](/artists/sam) cites Trommel for a Punchline label of Falconer's. The Ba Dum Tish Bandcamp page lists *PUNCHLINE07* and *PUNCHLINE08* as various-artist releases instead, so we describe Punchline as a series and treat the earlier wording as unconfirmed.
