Genning is a producer and DJ whose records lean toward dub, deep house and minimal house, and who released the EP RTSFM002 on the RTS.FM label in September 2024. He was recorded for RTS.FM St. Petersburg in 2023, and a compilation of the label's resident artists included his track a year later. There is no published biography or interview in the sources we could open, so this page follows the discography, the label releases and the archive.

## On the RTS.FM label

The release is catalogued as RTSFM002 and was released on 13 September 2024 as a digital release, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/genning-rtsfm002). The title there is "Genning - RTSFM002". It has three tracks: "The Green Fruit", "Second Rainbow" and "Ocean's Lamantin". The page credits mastering to Kirill Kandaurov and tags the release deep house, minimal house, house, electronic and dub techno, with Moscow as the label's location.

On 17 October 2025 Genning appeared on [RTSFM011](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm011), the compilation of resident artists from the label, with "Taken Other Way". The other contributors were [Victor Polo](/artists/victor-polo), [Nemec](/artists/nemec) and [Nicolas Barnes](/artists/nicolas-barnes).

## Discography

[Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/6407550) lists a Genning described as a DJ, with the given name Maksim, and a long run of releases from 2018. The SoundCloud handle RTS.FM published for him, maxim-genning, matches that first name, but the SoundCloud link on the Discogs profile is a different one, so we treat the match as likely rather than confirmed.

On that profile, the main credits begin with *Arround* on Sezonaz in 2018 and *Winter's Exhale* on MixCult in 2019. Later entries include *At The Balcon / Mountains Road* on Maison Profonde Recordings (2020), several MixCult EPs in 2021 and 2022, *Moon's Rain* as a limited clear 10-inch lathe cut on CRYSTAL MUD (2023), *Evening Space* on Greyscale (2023), *Sunlight EP* on TAMIZDAT (2024), *Discovery* on MixCult (2024), *Wind Blows Above Flowers* on Vuo Records (2024) and *Dub Echoes* on Refraction Records (2024).

He has also remixed for others. Discogs lists his remix of Sapurra's *Never Been To Detroit* on MixCult in 2022, and the same MixCult EP by Natural Piece of Aloe that [Ilya Schulz](/artists/ilya-schulz) remixed in 2021. [Sapurra](/artists/sapunov), the house alias of Sapunov and the artist behind RTSFM017, is another artist from the RTS.FM label family.

## Sound and style

The label's own tags for RTSFM002 include dub techno alongside deep and minimal house, and a Discogs title such as *Dub Echoes* points the same way. The tags are the strongest evidence we have, since no interview describes his approach. His appearances on MixCult's Marbled Dubs compilation volumes, which Discogs lists from 2018 to 2020, carry the same dub reference in their title.

## FAQ

### Who is Genning?

Genning is a producer and DJ with EPs on MixCult, Greyscale and TAMIZDAT, and the RTSFM002 EP on the RTS.FM label in September 2024.

### How old is Genning?

Genning has not published a birth date or year in any source we opened, so no age can be given.

### Where is Genning from?

No birthplace is published. RTS.FM recorded him in St. Petersburg in March 2023, and the label is based in Moscow.

### What genre does Genning play?

Dub-minded house. The label tags RTSFM002 as deep house, minimal house and dub techno.

### What is Genning's real name?

No distinct real name is public. A Discogs profile gives only the first name Maksim, and he records as Genning.

### What is on RTSFM002?

Three tracks: "The Green Fruit", "Second Rainbow" and "Ocean's Lamantin", mastered by Kirill Kandaurov.

### Is Genning on RTSFM011?

Yes. He contributes "Taken Other Way" to the label's resident compilation, released on 17 October 2025.
