Cristi Cons is a Romanian producer and DJ who arrived at minimal house through the cello. Trained at a Bucharest music university, he co-founded the label Amphia Records with his longtime collaborator [Vlad Caia](/artists/vlad-caia), records with Caia as [SIT](/artists/sit). [Bandcamp Daily's 2026 scene report](https://daily.bandcamp.com/scene-report/romanian-minimal-techno-scene-report) on Romanian minimal techno puts him among its current artists, and the story fits into our overview of [rominimal](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno).

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Bandcamp Daily describes him as a trained cellist with "a knack for nuanced darkness." [XLR8R's 2020 Romania issue](https://xlr8r.com/features/a-deep-dive-into-the-present-and-history-of-romanian-house-and-techno/) says he studied at the National University of Music Bucharest, where he met the pianist Mischa Blanos. [Fabric's introduction to SIT](https://fabriclondon.com/posts/introducing-sit-cristi-cons-and-vlad-caiai) calls him an artist formally educated in the Romanian capital and counts him among the premier talents to emerge from the country. In that interview, undated on the page, he says he met Vlad Caia in 2006 and that the two slowly began working together as producers before taking the name SIT, short for Sideways Invisibility Theory.

## Sound and Style

The sound is patient and a little dark. Bandcamp Daily calls his solo work nuanced and says *Out of Cycle* is bewitching in its repetition. Amphia's release notes describe the album as a mix of hypnotic trip hop, downtempo and groovy house. On stage, Fabric reported that SIT played live with two notebooks, a drum machine, an effects box and two MIDI controllers, mixing improvisation with unreleased material.

## The Label Story: Amphia Records

[Amphia Records](https://amphiarecords.bandcamp.com), based in Bucharest, was started by Cons and Caia, according to the label's [Discogs profile](https://www.discogs.com/label/289398-Amphia) and Bandcamp Daily, which names Cons as co-founder. Its first catalogue number, AMP001, is Cons's own *Traversa* EP, listed by Discogs as 2011. Releases since include Caia's *Division I* and *Division II*, Cezar Lazar's *Archetypes*, Amorf's EPs and, in 2021, *Out of Cycle*. In the Fabric interview, Cons says the label began as a way to present the release, with a focus on connecting image and sound: the designer Claudiu Stefan does the physical artwork, and Caia makes the videos.

*Out of Cycle LP* came out on 26 August 2021 as Amphia 023.1, a double 12-inch with six tracks, including "Western Habits" and "Politics of Entertainment." The label's page calls it the first part of his debut album. Discogs lists a second part, catalogued AMP023.2, with a 2026 year.

## Career Highlights

Cons performs in Petre Inspirescu's π Ensemble and in Amorf, alongside Caia and the pianist Mischa Blanos, Bandcamp Daily says. XLR8R traces Amorf's origin to a studio session in January 2016 and says the public first heard Amorf in May 2016, when Rhadoo played one of their piano tracks at Sunwaves. It describes SIT as one of the few Bucharest projects to break out and achieve sustained international success.

A Raresh link runs through Amphia too: [an Xceed artist bio](https://xceed.me/ca/artist/cristi-cons) describes the *Numiris* EP as a collaboration between Caia, [Raresh](/artists/raresh) and Cons under the name Verico, and Discogs lists it as Amphia's fifth release, from 2013. For bookings, Bandcamp Daily says Sunrise represents him, and [Electronic Groove](https://electronicgroove.com/sunwaves-35-a-farewell-to-mamaia-the-final-romanian-chapter/) listed SIT (Cristi Cons and Vlad Caia) on the bill of SW35, the final Romanian Sunwaves in May 2025. Our [Sunwaves festival page](/festivals/sunwaves) has the history of the festival.

## FAQ

### How old is Cristi Cons?

Cristi Cons has not published his date of birth in any source we found, so we do not give an age. Fabric says he met Vlad Caia in 2006.

### Where is Cristi Cons from?

Cristi Cons is Romanian and was educated in Bucharest, where Amphia Records is also based. Fabric calls him formally educated in the capital, and XLR8R says he studied at the National University of Music Bucharest.

### What genre does Cristi Cons play?

Cristi Cons plays minimal and deep house with downtempo and trip hop influences. Amphia describes his album *Out of Cycle* as hypnotic trip hop, downtempo and groovy house.

### What is Cristi Cons's real name?

Cristi Cons is the name he records under. The writing credit on the Amphia page for *Out of Cycle* gives Cristian Munteanu as writer and producer, but we did not find a source that states his legal name as such, so we do not list one.

### Who is Cristi Cons's collaborator in SIT?

SIT, short for Sideways Invisibility Theory, is his duo with Vlad Caia. Bandcamp Daily lists their 2019 album *Invisibility*, and XLR8R calls the duo one of the few Bucharest projects to achieve sustained international success.

### What is Out of Cycle?

*Out of Cycle* is Cristi Cons's solo album on Amphia Records, released on 26 August 2021 as a double 12-inch with six tracks. Bandcamp Daily lists it among the key releases of the scene.

### Did Cristi Cons study music?

Yes. He is a trained cellist, according to Bandcamp Daily, and XLR8R says he studied at the National University of Music Bucharest.
