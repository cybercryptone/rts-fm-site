Camion Bazar is a Paris-based duo of the Italian Benedetta and the French Romain Play, working as DJs, producers, live performers and owners of the Enemy Dance label. Known for raw, dubby techno and house, they have two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, published in February 2025 and May 2026.

## Where is Camion Bazar from and how did they start?

Camion Bazar describes itself on Bandcamp as a Paris-based duo of an Italian, Benedetta, and a French artist, Romain, who also go by "CB". The same bio lists their roles as DJs, producers, party curators, label managers, live performers and Rinse residents, touring across Europe. Release credits and Yoyaku's label pages name them as Benedetta Bertella and Romain Hemono.

In an interview with the blog Bolting Bits, the duo said they met about 15 years earlier at an after-party in France, where members of the Heretik crew were also present. They began going out together and later started throwing their own events. The interview is undated but points to early 2024.

The name comes from a van. They said the project began as a van fitted with a DJ booth, turntables, a sound system, a lot of vinyl and stage decor, which ran its own dance floor at parties and festivals. From there it grew into production, live shows, a label and residencies.

Romain also has a solo profile as Romain Play. His Bandcamp bio calls him a vinyl DJ based in France and lists Ritter Butzke, Rex, Sisyphos, Fusion and Concrete among places he has played, a claim from his own page. For the Berlin venues, see our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide).

## What does Camion Bazar sound like?

The duo's own phrase for Enemy Dance is raw music born from jam sessions, close to techno with touches of electro and rave house. Yoyaku's description of the Sangue EP covers electro, rave house, progressive trance and techno, with a nod to 1970s Italian horror cinema and Dario Argento. Yoyaku describes the later Shining Valley EP as four hard-hitting originals running from minimal house to deep techno with a strong dub influence.

Their earlier material was more mixed. Bolting Bits previewed the Alteration EP as a "tripped-out twist" on house, techno and electronica, with vocals by Benedetta, while their Bandcamp bio says the Alteration project blends rock, house, techno and punk.

One listing site, Festapp, instead describes them as playing deep house, downtempo and Balearic beats. That does not match their releases or their Yoyaku session, so we treat it as unreliable.

## What are the career highlights of Camion Bazar?

### The Alteration live show

Bolting Bits said Alteration came out alongside the duo's debut live show at La Machine du Moulin Rouge in Paris. In the later interview the duo give the premiere as February 2023 and say the tour ended in March 2024. They named the Paris premiere, a set at Fusion Festival in Germany, a 360-degree sound show at the Sound Biennale in Le Mans and a mountain festival show among its highlights.

### Voltage and Rinse France

The duo also run a residency series called Voltage. In the interview they called one February edition sold out and said it would return in the autumn. Bandcamp lists them as Rinse residents, but we could not open a Rinse France page to confirm the format or dates of their show.

### Festivals

Festapp lists [Nuits Sonores](/festivals/nuits-sonores) 2018 in Lyon as their only festival entry, which we could not confirm on the festival's own site. The French ticketing site JDS lists a 2026 date at Grand Parc Miribel Jonage in Vaulx-en-Velin on 27 and 28 June 2026, and marks it as no longer available. Both are listings, so we do not count them as played.

## Which labels is Camion Bazar associated with?

Enemy Dance is their own label. Resident Advisor places it in France, dates it to about 2023 and lists Underscope as distributor. It names Benedetta and Camion Bazar as artists and "No Flag" as the debut single. In the interview the duo said the plan was a single every one to two months plus remixes, and that the name is a joke about music so good that even enemies will dance. Yoyaku sells the physical releases as ENEMY007 and ENEMY009.

Jaki Records is the other name. Bolting Bits called it the duo's own label when it previewed Alteration, and Yoyaku lists their Honeymoon 20 record as JAKI005. Some search snippets call Enemy Dance a sub-label of Jaki, but no page we opened says so, so we leave that out.

## FAQ

### How old is Camion Bazar?

We could not find a birth year for either member in any source we opened, so we do not state an age. The duo said they met about 15 years before their Bolting Bits interview, which does not fix a birth year.

### Where is Camion Bazar from?

The duo is based in Paris. Benedetta is Italian and Romain is French, according to their Bandcamp bio and Yoyaku's description of the Shining Valley EP.

### Who is Camion Bazar?

Camion Bazar is a Paris-based duo of Benedetta and Romain Play. They DJ, produce, perform live and run the Enemy Dance label.

### What is Camion Bazar's Yoyaku instore session?

There are two on YouTube. One was published on 5 February 2025 and has 55,830 views, the other on 28 May 2026 and has 20,693 views, both as of 24 November 2026. Neither is in our top 10.

### Which label is Camion Bazar on?

They run Enemy Dance, which has released their Sangue and Shining Valley 12" records. Earlier releases such as Alteration and Honeymoon 20 are linked to Jaki Records.

### What does Camion Bazar's name mean?

The duo said the project began as a van with a DJ booth, turntables, sound system and stage decor, which ran its own dance floor at events.

### What instruments does Camion Bazar use live?

According to the interview, Romain plays drums, bass and guitar alongside hardware synths, drum machines and Ableton.

### Why is the label called Enemy Dance?

The duo said it is a joke that the music is so good even enemies will dance, and that the word also reflects anger they feel inside.
