Hugobeat is a DJ and producer whose recorded catalogue stretches from a run of digital singles and EPs in the mid-2010s to RTSFM014, his 2026 EP on the RTS.FM label. The sources we could open say little about the person: no birth date, birthplace or interview. What they do show is a working discography, a label release and a long RTS.FM session recorded in Thailand, and this page sticks to those.

## On the RTS.FM label

RTSFM014 was released on 20 February 2026 as a digital EP, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm014). The page describes it as RTSFM014 by Hugobeat with a remix by Kandaurov, mastered by Kandaurov. The four tracks are "Sanuksai", "Interception", "Unity" and the Kandaurov remix of "Unity". Bandcamp tags it deep house, minimal house, house and electronic, with Moscow as the label's location.

Kandaurov also remixed [Nemec's RTSFM008](/artists/nemec) and [Type B.'s RTSFM013](/artists/type-b) on the same label, according to the Bandcamp pages for those releases.

## Earlier releases

[Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/4391700) describes Hugobeat simply as a DJ and producer and lists 19 credits between 2015 and 2020, and the same profile appears in [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/artist/a97e83c0-d3be-4917-9c85-7ac0e6f76294). The artist's SoundCloud link on Discogs matches the one RTS.FM published with its Thailand set, which is how we tie the discography to the label artist.

Main credits include *Fire Cracker* on Prospection Records and the *Real Man EP* on Digiment Records, both 2015, and *Make Choice Part 1* with Max Lyazgin on Disco Cat the same year. In 2017 Discogs lists *Get Money* with Teddy-A on G-Mafia Records, and "Do Me A Favor" on Pepper Cat, credited to Leeroy Thornhill, Max Lyazgin and Hugobeat featuring SevenEver. Remixes include Dmitrii G on Crumpled Music (2015), Rafael Lambert featuring Martin Blix on Dbeatzion Records (2016), Kvant and Wanroux on KudoZ Records (2017) and Evgeny Svalov on Soviett Records (2019). He also appears on compilations such as *The Sound Of Indie Dance 09* and *Underground Nu Disco Sessions 11* from LW Recordings, which suggests the nu disco and indie dance end of house in his earlier work.

## Sound and style

The label's tags are deep house and minimal house. The earlier credits, on labels that sit closer to nu disco and indie dance, point to a broader palette than the 2026 EP alone shows, but that is our reading of the labels and compilation titles, not a statement from the artist. The RTS.FM set he played in 2025 is billed as own productions only, so it is the best single hour of evidence for how he builds a set.

## RTS.FM Connection

RTS.FM's archive holds a session titled "HUGOBEAT Own Productions Only | RTS.FM Koh Phangan, Thailand 1.08.2025", uploaded on 1 August 2025 and running just over an hour. The video description links his SoundCloud and Instagram pages next to the label's own channels. It is the only archive video with Hugobeat in the title, so the label release and this set make up the full connection. The archive is covered more widely in our look at [the RTS.FM archive](/blog/rtsfm-archive-data-2009-golden-era).
