Maayan Nidam is an Israeli-born DJ, producer and live performer based in Berlin, known for Perlon records, her Hellium label and night, and her alias The Waves. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 6 December 2019 and built around the shop's techno section, has 92,079 YouTube views.

## Where is Maayan Nidam from and how did she start?

XLR8R's 2018 interview says she was born in Israel, near Tel Aviv, where her parents still live. A MUTEK artist profile says she grew up in Tel Aviv and travelled widely as a young person before finding Berlin's underground club scene. The Amsterdam Dance Event biography says she began DJing and producing in 1999.

XLR8R's 2016 podcast feature says she spent short periods in Amsterdam and New York and arrived in Berlin in 2004. A 2024 GROOVE podcast feature puts the move just after New Year's Eve 2003, so the sources differ by about a year. Her early records came under the name Miss Fitz, on labels including Archipel, Sushitech and Eklo, per the ADE biography, and she has also used the alias Laverne Radix. No source we opened gives her birth date or full legal name.

## What does Maayan Nidam sound like?

ADE calls her best known for machine grooves and moody funk. MUTEK lists trippy techno, mystical funk grooves and woolly bass. GoOut's profile describes deep, stripped-back sets from a large vinyl collection, and a solo live set built on hardware, with live dubbing and improvisation.

She is not only a techno artist. XLR8R's 2016 feature says her work spans bands and side projects, and describes her turning back to techno after a stretch of reduced touring. GROOVE says she layers vocals, beat poetry and short mantras over long, hypnotic records. Her 2020 album as The Waves, *māyā*, is six tracks of pop, indie and new wave, with her singing on five.

## What are the career highlights of Maayan Nidam?

### Early albums and the Powershovel debut

XLR8R says her first full-length under her own name, *Nightlong*, came out in 2009 on Powershovel Audio, and she describes it as the first time she felt comfortable without an alias. ADE says it draws on recordings of Cuban instrumentalists. The second album, *New Moon*, followed in 2012. XLR8R also lists Cadenza, [Luciano](/artists/luciano)'s label, and Wolf + Lamb among her labels, and says she appeared on [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz)'s trip imprint.

### Perlon and Sea of Thee

XLR8R reports that she released the *Deep Under Sobriety Regime* 10-inch on the label in 2016, then the album *Sea of Thee* in 2018, her first in six years and her first on Perlon. XLR8R's pre-release piece gave the date as 23 November, and the video for "Dust and Dirt" was shot in Berlin and included footage of her live set at Paris's Concrete club. XLR8R's interview says Thomas Franzmann, known as Zip, helped select and sequence the tracks.

### Hellium and Tresor

Resident Advisor reports that she launched Hellium in 2019 to release "obscure techno and ambient". XLR8R's December 2019 news item says the label's first record, by Hubble, came out in May, that her own *HELL02* EP followed in February 2020, and that Hellium is also her residency at Tresor. The 2018 interview says Hellium began as a bi-monthly night at Renate, with a move to Tresor planned, while GROOVE describes her as a Tresor resident since 2018, so the exact start of the residency is not settled.

### The Waves and Cool Mom Records

She revived The Waves in 2020. The project had appeared once before, in 2014, on a collaborative Wolf + Lamb EP, per XLR8R. Resident Advisor says the album *māyā* launched her new label Cool Mom Records on 11 December 2020.

### Studio, teaching and festivals

XLR8R says her studio was in East Berlin above Renate, and Trommel announced her as guest tutor for a 2020 analogue production course at Apollo Studio in Berlin. XLR8R's 2016 feature said she planned a solo live debut at Get Perlonized, followed by [MUTEK](/festivals/mutek) Montreal in June, and our [Waking Life](/festivals/waking-life) page lists her among acts announced for its August 2018 edition.

## Which labels is Maayan Nidam associated with?

- **Perlon.** The Berlin label behind *Deep Under Sobriety Regime* (2016) and *Sea of Thee* (2018), and the one GROOVE ties most closely to her.
- **Hellium.** Her own label and club series, launched in 2019 for obscure techno and ambient, per Resident Advisor.
- **Cool Mom Records.** Her label for The Waves, launched with *māyā* in December 2020.
- **Powershovel Audio.** The Japanese label that issued *Nightlong* in 2009.
- **Cadenza, Wolf + Lamb and trip.** Labels XLR8R lists among her releases, alongside Raum Music and Oslo.
- **De Stijl.** The label of her 2025 collaboration with Artefakt.

## FAQ

### How old is Maayan Nidam?

We could not verify her age. None of the sources we opened gives a birth year or date. She says she began DJing and producing in 1999, per the ADE biography.

### Where is Maayan Nidam from?

XLR8R says she was born in Israel, near Tel Aviv, and MUTEK says she grew up in Tel Aviv. She has been based in Berlin since 2003 or 2004, depending on the source.

### Who is Maayan Nidam?

She is a DJ, producer and live performer in Berlin whose releases include the Perlon album *Sea of Thee*. She runs the Hellium label and club series and releases pop-leaning material as The Waves.

### Did Maayan Nidam play a Yoyaku instore session?

Yes. It was published on 6 December 2019, runs about 134 minutes and has 92,079 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 25th of 178 sessions on the channel.

### What is Hellium?

Hellium is Maayan Nidam's label and club series, launched in 2019 for obscure techno and ambient, per Resident Advisor. The series is also a Tresor residency, per XLR8R.

### What is The Waves?

The Waves is her band-style alias for pop, indie and new wave. It appeared on a 2014 Wolf + Lamb EP and returned with the 2020 album *māyā*.

### What other names does Maayan Nidam use?

Miss Fitz, Laverne Radix and The Waves, plus Mara Trax with Vera Heindel and Uli Künkel with Lee Curtiss and Shaun Reeves, per ADE, XLR8R and MUTEK.
