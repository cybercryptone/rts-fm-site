D'Julz is Julien Veniel, a French DJ and producer based in Paris, who has run the Bass Culture night at Rex Club since 1997 and founded the Bass Culture label in 2009. He has two instore sessions on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel, from 2018 and 2025, with about 137,000 views between them.

## Where is D'Julz from and how did he start?

Several outlets give his real name as Julien Veniel, including Resident Advisor, XLR8R, Trommel and his Bandcamp page. Yoyaku calls him a French DJ and producer based in Paris. Mixmag's 2016 profile says he began at underground parties in the city and built his skills on turntables, and Amsterdam Dance Event dates his career to 1992. Resident Advisor's 2011 podcast notes say lesser-known French DJs introduced him to dance music and that trips to New York and London shaped him, naming the Sound Factory club.

Trommel reported in November 2021 that he was about to mark 30 years in the business and his 50th birthday in 2022. That points to a 1972 birth, which would make him 53 or 54 on 24 November 2026 depending on his birthday. It is a single source and gives no birthplace.

## What does D'Julz sound like?

Mixmag's 2016 profile described his music as seductive, classy and smooth with a French flair. Trommel says he mixed Chicago house, UK breakbeat and Detroit techno in the 1990s. His records sit mostly in house and tech house, with breakbeat and gospel touches. Yoyaku describes the 2022 Est. 1992 EP as drawing on the sounds of the early nineties in contemporary packaging.

In a February 2026 interview with Magnetic Magazine he described his DJing as back to two records and a mixer. He said his growth has been more psychological and energetic than technical, built around reading a room and managing energy.

## What are the career highlights of D'Julz?

### Bass Culture at Rex Club

Mixmag reported in January 2017 that he started Bass Culture in 1997, motivated by frustration with the spread of commercial French touch in Paris. The night marked 20 years in 2017 with six parties, with guests including [Derrick May](/artists/derrick-may), Lil Louis and [Seth Troxler](/artists/seth-troxler). Mixmag says it helped break several DJs at the club, including Luciano, [Cassy](/artists/cassy), [Loco Dice](/artists/loco-dice) and [Raresh](/artists/raresh). Trommel calls it the longest continuous residency at Rex Club, and in 2018 said he was resident DJ and art director there.

### Circoloco, fabric and abroad

Mixmag's 2016 profile says he played the Circo Loco opening on the Terrace at DC10 in summer 2015 and would return as a resident in 2016. Trommel's 2018 piece lists him regularly at Circoloco. Fabric's account says he has played the London club almost every year since 2000 and planned a Bass Culture takeover in Farringdon, and that he first played Panorama Bar around 2008, where he met Cassy.

### The Bass Culture label

Trommel dates the label to 2009. Mixmag lists Mr G, Cassy, John Dimas and Mr Tophat and Art Alfie among its artists, and Trommel adds Leo Pol and Steve Rachmad. After a pause, Yoyaku says it was relaunched at the start of 2022 with the Est. 1992 EP, and Trommel reported that Yoyaku had become its distribution partner.

## Which labels is D'Julz associated with?

- **Bass Culture.** His own label, founded in 2009 per Trommel, relaunched in 2022 with Yoyaku as distributor.
- **JV Recordings.** His vinyl-focused label for his own productions. Yoyaku's JV01 page, dated 31 October 2018, announces the launch. Yoyaku's later text and his Bandcamp page say it launched in 2019, and Nightclubber says 2018, so the start year is disputed.
- **Circus Company, Ovum, Rekids, Robsoul, 20:20 Vision.** Labels he has released on, according to XLR8R, Mixmag and his Bandcamp bio.
- **Chiwax.** Yoyaku lists his Raw Toolz 1 and 2 on the label.
- **Remix work.** Yoyaku lists his remixes for Jorge Savoretti and Mateo Dufour.
