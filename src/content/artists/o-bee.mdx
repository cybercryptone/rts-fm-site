O.BEE is Omer Bil, a DJ and producer raised between Istanbul and New York, who co-founded the label Jigit in 2020 with [Tomas Station](/artists/tomas-station) and plays house, minimal and electro. fabric London lists him as a resident. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 13 March 2024, has 33,974 views on YouTube.

## Where is O.BEE from and how did he start?

fabric London gives his real name as Omer Bil and says he was raised between Istanbul and New York. The club's profile says he honed his craft in New York's underground scene and graduated from The New School's Contemporary Music program. The DGTL festival's artist page and Insomniac's listing repeat the New School detail, and Insomniac lists his origin as Turkey, calls Istanbul his hometown, where he is a regular, and gives New York as his base.

His route into the city's scene ran through ReSolute, the New York party series. DGTL says he became part of the ReSolute community, and Insomniac says he hosted and played alongside Zip, Chez Damier and [Sonja Moonear](/artists/sonja-moonear). When XLR8R covered the launch of Jigit in October 2020, it called him a ReSolute affiliate.

None of these pages gives a birth date, a birth year or a first release. We could not find a long-form interview, and the biographies we opened are club, festival and listing pages rather than editorial profiles, so the early part of his story is thin.

## What does O.BEE sound like?

DGTL says he plays across genres, mainly house, minimal and electro, drawing on years of record digging. fabric describes a sound that bridges cultures, mixing tradition with the avant-garde. A Vitruta magazine page says it blends Eastern melodies with Western rhythmic structures, and Insomniac says he builds musical trips instead of purely functional dance sets. fabric adds that he plays solo or back-to-back with Tomas Station, in formats from long club sets to festival mainstages.

## What are the career highlights of O.BEE?

### Jigit, 2020 onward

XLR8R reported on 23 October 2020 that O.BEE and Tomas Station had launched Jigit as a record label and creative project. It is wrapped in a fictional story about a spiritual entity called Jigit, each release is a chapter and the cover art, by Asli Tunaman, reads as a comic strip. Trommel, in a premiere dated 15 October 2020, called it the brainchild of Station and O.BEE. Jigit's Bandcamp page lists the label in Brooklyn and dates its first record, JIGIT001, to October 2020.

In April 2023 Trommel announced a Jigit showcase at the H0L0 venue in New York for 22 April, billed as a 40-hour event across three floors, with O.BEE and Tomas Station among the DJs. Trommel contrasts the label's slower, funkier records with the peak-time material it is also known for.

### Residencies and club bookings

fabric lists him as a resident, along with ReSolute. DGTL says his residencies also include Club der Visionaere in Berlin and Circoloco at DC-10 in Ibiza. We did not open a source for the Club der Visionaere residency beyond that page.

Resident Advisor reported on 4 March 2025 that O.BEE and Tomas Station would play a back-to-back set during Circoloco's 2025 season at DC-10. The season ran on Mondays from 28 April to 13 October 2025 and RA's announcement gave no set date. Other back-to-backs announced for the season included [Arapu](/artists/arapu) and [Priku](/artists/priku).

Berghain's own listing puts him and Tomas Station on the bill of PARAMIDA's Love On The Rocks at Panorama Bar on Friday, 9 May 2025, playing back-to-back from 06:00. The listing says it was their second time at that party. Insomniac adds that he has played clubs in New York, London, Berlin, Paris and Barcelona, and festivals including Okeechobee, Comunite and UVA.

### A guest on SIT's Synaptic

The Bucharest duo [SIT](/artists/sit) released the album *Synaptic* on Sushitech on 6 January 2026. Its Bandcamp page lists O.Bee among the guests, as we note on that artist's page. We did not find which track he appears on.

## What is O.BEE's Yoyaku instore session?

O.BEE's Yoyaku instore session was published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 13 March 2024. It runs about 79 minutes and has 33,974 views, 710 likes and 109 comments as of 24 November 2026. The video description is blank, so it gives no tracklist, recording date or location. Among the 178 instore sessions we counted on the channel it ranks 67th by views, well outside our top 10, whose cutoff is 179,421 views. The shop connection runs deeper than the video: XLR8R reported in 2020 that Yoyaku is Jigit's distributor. ## Which labels is O.BEE associated with?

- **Jigit.** The label and creative project he runs with Tomas Station and Asli Tunaman, based in Brooklyn according to its Bandcamp page, and distributed by Yoyaku according to XLR8R.
- **Enstantane.** Insomniac says he runs this label with Rona Budak and that it focuses on experimental music from around the world. We found no second source and no release list.
- **ReSolute.** A party series and community rather than a label. fabric and DGTL both list it among his affiliations.
