Tomas Station is a Bogotá-born DJ and producer who now works from Brooklyn, according to fabric London, and who records as Irie Nation and ISOR29. He co-founded the label Jigit with O.BEE and plays house, minimal and deep techno. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 8 March 2024, has 58,715 views on YouTube.

## Where is Tomas Station from and how did he start?

fabric London, which lists him among its residents, says he grew up between Bogotá and Chicago, a mix of cultures and sounds that fed an early musical obsession. The fabric profile and his agency biography agree on the rest of the early story. He played in bands that ran from hardcore punk to jazz, trained on piano and drums, and as a teenager started visiting Gramaphone Records in Chicago, which introduced him to underground electronic music.

Back in Colombia he co-founded Downpitch Recordings, according to fabric and the agency biography, which name his partners as Dorado and Bruno Anthony. The label pressed vinyl, favoured quality over quantity and linked Bogotá with Chicago and Detroit. The biography credits it with helping to spark a dance music scene in Colombia.

He later moved to New York. The same biography says he worked as a music buyer for the shop Halcyon and as a booker and resident for the parties Kolekti and Zodiac Social, then returned to Brooklyn as a resident DJ for the ReSolute party and agency.

The sources do not give a birth date, a birth year or the dates of these moves, so we cannot say when each step happened.

## What does Tomas Station sound like?

Insomniac describes him as a DJ and producer working in house, minimal and deep techno, and his agency biography calls him a house DJ with an outsider approach. The set archive set79 lists 12 of his recorded sets spanning deep house, minimal and electronica.

His own productions lean in other directions. The Irie Nation EP on Jigit mixes acid, breakbeat, dub, electro and trance styles, according to Yoyaku's release page, and Trommel's premiere describes one track as bouncy, with old-school acid touches and 1990s rave stabs. ISOR29 is different again, a six-track mini-album that Yoyaku tags as ambient and house, built from a microphone, a Korg MS20, a hang drum and a field recorder.

## What are the career highlights of Tomas Station?

### Jigit and Irie Nation (2020)

Jigit launched in October 2020 as a record label and creative project run by Station and O.BEE, with visual artist Asli Tunaman creating its story, according to XLR8R. It is built around fiction about a spiritual entity, with each release a chapter and the cover art a comic strip. Its first record, JIGIT001, is the Irie Nation 12" *5.1398° N, 3.3238° W*, which XLR8R says he produced during travels to the Ivory Coast. XLR8R says it drew early support from Raresh, Magda, Lamache, Varhat and Laylla Dane. Trommel premiered "Vibrations & Soul" on 15 October 2020.

### ISOR29

Yoyaku's and Rush Hour's descriptions of ISOR29 call it a project that follows his 2020 Irie Nation debut. The mini-album *Moon Phase Gardening* (Second Circle, SC020) was made in a Lisbon living room during the first Covid lockdown, according to both shops. Rush Hour lists it with a 2022 date and as a repress, and we could not find the original pressing date.

### Club and radio work

Back-to-back sets with O.BEE are what fabric calls his best known work, and set79 shows Lot Radio broadcasts of "Can You Jigit?" with O.BEE and others. Its most viewed listing is a Magda b2b Tomas Station set at Floyd from May 2024. His agency biography says that Danny Krivit, [Raresh](/artists/raresh), [Magda](/artists/magda) and Douglas Sherman of The Loft have supported his work. For the 2025 Circoloco booking, see the latest section above.

## Which labels is Tomas Station associated with?

- **Downpitch Recordings** is the Colombian vinyl label he co-founded with Dorado and Bruno Anthony.
- **Jigit** is the label and creative project he runs with O.BEE and Asli Tunaman.
- **School of Rock** is named by set79 as a third label he co-runs. We could not open a second source on it.
- **Low To High, Second Circle, Dailysession NY, Descendants Of The Deep, Moody Recordings and Future Funktion** are labels his releases have appeared on, per the shop and agency pages above.
- **ReSolute** is the New York party and agency where fabric and his biography list him as a resident. XLR8R calls O.BEE a ReSolute affiliate.
