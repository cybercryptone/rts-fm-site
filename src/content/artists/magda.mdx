Magda (Magdalena Chojnacka) is a Polish-born DJ and producer who grew up in Detroit and became one of the best-known names on [Richie Hawtin's](/artists/richie-hawtin) Minus label before co-founding Items & Things. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 23 February 2024, has 200,786 YouTube views as of 24 November 2026.

## Where is Magda from and how did she start?

German Wikipedia says she was born in Żywiec, Poland, in 1975, and that her real name is Magdalena Chojnacka. French Wikipedia says "around 1975". That makes her 50 or 51 in November 2026, but no source we opened gives a full birth date, so treat the exact age with care.

Sources disagree on when she left Poland. A 2017 XLR8R interview says she arrived in America at age nine, and French Wikipedia puts that move in 1984, first to Texas and then to Hamtramck, Michigan, in 1986. German Wikipedia says 1986, at age eleven. All of them agree she was raised in Detroit.

In a 2015 Electronic Beats interview she said she started going out in Detroit in 1994 and later digitised the Minus catalogue as a novice DJ. An interview republished on Paperblog says she bought Underground Resistance's "Acid Rain" that year at the Record Time shop, where she later worked, and began DJing in 1996. LA Weekly and German Wikipedia also give 1996. French Wikipedia adds that she persuaded a bar to run a monthly techno night with [Daniel Bell](/artists/daniel-bell) and Claude Young as residents. Our [Detroit techno guide](/blog/detroit-techno-guide-2026-clubs-labels-history) covers the city.

Her path to Hawtin is dated differently by source. Resident Advisor's 2007 interview says he gave her a first residency in 1997, Mixmag and 909originals say she joined the Minus family in 1998, and LA Weekly says he spotted her in 1999.

She moved to Berlin from New York around 2003, according to LA Weekly and 909originals. In 2012 she told XLR8R she had moved to Switzerland while keeping her Berlin studio. Electronic Beats called her Berlin-based in 2015 and Techno Airlines gives Berlin now, so we do too, with that caveat.

## What does Magda sound like?

LA Weekly described her in 2009 as a dark but groovy "minimal disco" DJ who blends laptop programming with effects, loops and samples. She called her own sets "ghoul funk". The Amsterdam Dance Event biography lists space disco, Detroit's metallic precision, stripped-down techno and Italian horror soundtracks. Mixmag's 2015 In Session feature praised a range from "fidgety minimal-techno" to swampy tech bangers.

She has pushed against the minimal label. She told 909originals in 2024 that it became limiting and did not reflect her broader taste. Resident Advisor's 2015 review of her Balance 027 mix said she had moved away from the "mistress of minimal" tag.

XLR8R's 2012 studio feature says her sounds are mostly analogue, run through an AD converter into Ableton, and that she rarely uses samples. She told XLR8R in 2017 that her longest set was 14 hours at Club der Visionaere in 2004.

## What are the career highlights of Magda?

### Minus and the Run Stop Restore years

German Wikipedia says she, Marc Houle and Troy Pierce released as Run Stop Restore from 2003, and Resident Advisor calls them fellow members. The English Wikipedia page on Houle describes it as his moniker, so sources differ. Her first solo release, the Stop EP, came out on Minus in 2005, according to German Wikipedia and Mixmag.

### She's A Dancing Machine and fabric 49

Her 2006 mix CD She's A Dancing Machine on Minus raised her profile sharply, according to Resident Advisor's 2007 interview, and she played Europe, the US, Japan, Russia and Australia that year. Fabric 49 followed on 16 November 2009. Resident Advisor said she was the third woman in the series after DJ Heather and Ellen Allien, and that it drew on 1970s Italian horror soundtracks.

### From The Fallen Page

Her debut album came out on Minus in October 2010 with ten tracks. Resident Advisor's reviewer found it distinctive for the label but uneven, singling out "Get Down Goblin" and "Doom Disco". German Wikipedia also lists the 2010 release Making Contakt, credited to Hawtin featuring Magda and Troy Pierce.

### Berlin, remixes and Blotter Trax

German Wikipedia lists her 2009 remix of Depeche Mode's "Wrong". With T.B. Arthur (Jay Ahern) and the bassist Hannes Strobl she records as Blotter Trax, whose debut album Superconductor came out on Optimo Music in June 2023. Optimo's Bandcamp page gives both 23 and 30 June as the date.

In March 2024 she told 909originals about early Berlin sets back-to-back with [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos), Hawtin, Zip and Vera.

## Which labels is Magda associated with?

Minus is the label that made her name. Items & Things began in 2006 as a Minus sub-label, according to the English Wikipedia page on Marc Houle and French Wikipedia. On 4 August 2011 XLR8R reported that Magda, Houle and Pierce had left Minus to run it themselves, wanting to "release what we want", and German Wikipedia agrees on 2011. Resident Advisor reviewed its Berlin launch party at Prince Charles in November 2011.

XLR8R said in October 2017 that she had launched a PERM event series in Berlin and planned a PERM label. We could not confirm that the label ever released records.

## FAQ

### How old is Magda?

German Wikipedia gives 1975 as her birth year and French Wikipedia says around 1975, which makes her 50 or 51 in November 2026. No source we opened states a full birth date.

### Where is Magda from?

She was born in Żywiec, Poland, and raised in Detroit after her family moved to the United States, according to German and French Wikipedia and XLR8R. Sources differ on whether the move came in 1984 or 1986.

### Who is Magda?

Magda is the stage name of Magdalena Chojnacka, a DJ and producer known for her work on Richie Hawtin's Minus label and for co-founding Items & Things with Marc Houle and Troy Pierce.

### Where does Magda live?

Techno Airlines gives Berlin, and Electronic Beats called her Berlin-based in 2015. In 2012 she told XLR8R she had moved to Switzerland and kept a Berlin studio.

### What is Magda's Yoyaku instore session?

It is a 61 minute, 36 second set published on 23 February 2024, with 200,786 views as of 24 November 2026. Yoyaku's description calls it the shop's first in-store session of the year.

### What label is Magda on?

She released her early solo work on Minus, including From The Fallen Page in 2010. In 2011 she, Houle and Pierce left to run Items & Things, and she has also released on Balance Music and Optimo Music.

### What is Items & Things?

It is the label Magda runs with Marc Houle and Troy Pierce. It started in 2006 as a Minus sub-label and became fully independent when the three left Minus in 2011.

### What is Blotter Trax?

It is a trio of Magda, Jay Ahern (T.B. Arthur) and Hannes Strobl. Their debut album Superconductor came out on Optimo Music in June 2023.
