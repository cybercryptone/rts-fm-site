Vincent Lemieux is a Montreal DJ, producer, record dealer and [MUTEK](/festivals/mutek) programmer who co-founded the label Musique Risquée in 2003 and records as half of the duo Flabbergast with Guillaume Coutu Dumont. He has two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, from 2019 and 2024, and Flabbergast's first album came out on Yoyaku x Chapelle XIV in 2026.

## Where is Vincent Lemieux from and how did he start?

The archive of Berlin's [CTM festival](/festivals/ctm-festival) calls him a native of Montreal and says he has co-programmed MUTEK since 2000 and been its official DJ since 2002. MUTEK's own artist pages also date his work on the festival's sound and image to 2000. One MUTEK page, for Flabbergast, instead says he joined the programming team in 2001, so the start year differs by source.

According to the CTM archive, in 2000 he was the main buyer at Rotation, a record store in Montreal, where he was credited with setting a minimal house and techno tone for the city. MUTEK's Flabbergast page says he began as a DJ and studied electroacoustic composition at Concordia University for a short time. In early 2003 he launched a club night called HUB_musiques, which CTM says was a reference point for forward-looking Montreal artists for two years.

We could not find his real name, birth date or age in any source. MUTEK's artist pages list him as Canada/Quebec.

## What does Vincent Lemieux sound like?

Descriptions agree on range. MUTEK says he prefers techno and house and also likes disco, funk, soul and hip-hop. The Montreal outlet PAN M 360 calls him a versatile, eclectic and refined DJ who started in techno and house and widened out through disco, funk, soul, hip-hop and groove.

Musique Risquée had a similar attitude. In PAN M 360's Flabbergast interview, he describes the label as a way to support the local community with a playful, humorous take on electronic music, away from strict, serious minimal.

As Flabbergast, the sound is more experimental. Circus Company describes the duo's music as leftfield club music, heady and playfully unhinged. The Yoyaku x Chapelle XIV listing for their album mentions electronics, jazz, acid house and futuristic grooves. 

## What are the career highlights of Vincent Lemieux?

### MUTEK and Montreal nights

MUTEK is the constant. Beyond programming, MUTEK's profile says he has helped shape the festival's sonic and visual identity since 2000. In April 2024 the festival put out a set he recorded at the Rinse France studios in Paris, as part of a MUTEK residency on the station.

At home, his Soirée Risquée and Disco Dessert nights are named in MUTEK's profile. PAN M 360 says he has organised more than 20 years of nights in Montreal, including a 110th Soirée Risquée, and has a long link with the club Salon Daomé. The CTM archive mentions Tuesday nights there alongside [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) and [Luciano](/artists/luciano).

### Abroad

MUTEK's profile lists Berlin's Get Perlonized nights at Panorama Bar, which it says have run every year since 2001, and a long list of countries, including Japan, Brazil, Russia, Denmark and the United States, plus London's Fabric. CTM's archive lists him on the programmes of the CTM.09 and CTM.11 editions.

Piknic Électronik, Montreal's outdoor Sunday series, lists appearances on 27 August 2017, 31 July 2021 and 26 May 2024. PAN M 360 says he played the 26 May 2024 edition to a full lawn, with a set that grew more atmospheric as evening fell.

## Which labels is Vincent Lemieux associated with?

**Musique Risquée.** CTM says he founded it in 2003 with Marc Leclair (Akufen), Scott Monteith (Deadbeat) and Stephen Beaupré, and that he still manages it. MUTEK names only Leclair as co-founder and describes Lemieux as head boss, A&R man and all-rounder. The CTM archive lists releases from Akufen, Crackhaus, Atom TM, Philippe Cam, The Mole and Jichael Mackson, and counted close to twenty singles at the time. MUTEK adds Bruno Pronsato, Stephen Beaupré and Guillaume & the Coutu Dumonts. PAN M 360 says he later ran the label largely alone.

**Flabbergast releases.** The duo has put music out on Circus Company, Copier/Coller, Yoyaku and Chapelle XIV Music, according to Circus Company and the Yoyaku listing. Copier/Coller is the duo's own label, per PAN M 360.

**Other projects.** CTM lists Brandy Snifter, a project with Stephen Beaupré, and PAN M 360 mentions solo releases under the name CCCLTD .CA. We did not find release pages for either.

## FAQ

### How old is Vincent Lemieux?

We could not find his birth year or date in any source we opened, so we do not state an age. The CTM archive says he was already buying records at Rotation in 2000.

### Where is Vincent Lemieux from?

The CTM archive calls him a native of Montreal, and MUTEK lists him as Canada/Quebec. He is still active in Montreal, where he runs club nights.

### Who is Vincent Lemieux?

He is a DJ, record dealer, producer and MUTEK programmer who co-founded Musique Risquée, according to MUTEK's artist page. He also records as Flabbergast with Guillaume Coutu Dumont.

### What is Vincent Lemieux's Yoyaku instore session?

There are two on YouTube. One was published on 5 October 2019 and runs about 124 minutes, the other on 23 July 2024 and runs about 60 minutes. As of 24 November 2026 they have 25,767 and 33,947 views.

### Which label does Vincent Lemieux run?

He co-founded Musique Risquée in 2003 and, per CTM and PAN M 360, has managed it since, largely on his own in later years. The label has released Akufen, The Mole and Atom TM, among others.

### What is Flabbergast?

Flabbergast is the duo of Vincent Lemieux and Guillaume Coutu Dumont. Its releases include *No 1* and *Weirdo Active* on Circus Company and the album *Consolation in Constellation* on Chapelle XIV Music via Yoyaku.

### What does Vincent Lemieux play?

His sets cover techno and house, with disco, funk, soul and hip-hop alongside, according to MUTEK and PAN M 360.

### Does Vincent Lemieux work with MUTEK?

Yes. MUTEK and the CTM archive describe him as part of its programming team since 2000 and its official DJ since 2002, though one MUTEK page says he joined in 2001.

### Has Vincent Lemieux played Piknic Électronik?

Yes. PAN M 360 reported his set at Piknic Électronik on Sunday 26 May 2024, and the festival's artist page lists earlier dates in 2017 and 2021.
