Atree is the stage name of Oliver Gleinser, a German DJ and producer based in Berlin and a co-founder of the label Smoove Mocean. He makes funk-infused house on vinyl, and his two hour Yoyaku instore session, published on 7 November 2024, has 80,124 YouTube views and ranks 30th among the 178 sessions we tracked.

## Where is Atree from and how did he start?

His booking agency, Rings of Neptune, says he comes from the countryside of Southern Germany and is now Berlin-based. Mixmag also calls him a Berlin-based producer, in its report on the closure of the Piros label. Trommel, in a 2024 premiere, describes him simply as a German artist.

The agency biography says he has been DJing for about 20 years, the first seven of them devoted to reggae and dub. The page is undated, so we cannot say which year the 20 years are counted to. It puts the start of his steady vinyl output at 2015, and Discogs agrees: its earliest entry for him is *Uwaga 002* on the Uwaga label in 2015.

Discogs also lists an alias, Smoove & Mocean, and Yoyaku's artist page for Atree carries the same "aka". That is the name used on his own label.

## What does Atree sound like?

The agency biography calls his signature sound funk-infused, spacey and laid-back house built around groove, with syncopated drum programming, bouncy basslines and tumbling synth leads. Trommel's description of his *Happy Bout That* EP is more dancefloor-minded: it mentions snare rolls, a deep bassline, shimmering pads, a touch of 303 and roots in electro and French Touch. The funkON Bandcamp page for *Spirit of the Hawk* calls the record a mix of funk-infused electro and house.

Retailers file his records under deep house, house, tech house and electro. The announcement of his Komplices EP calls that record funky and light-hearted, made for cosy dancefloors.

## What are the career highlights of Atree?

### Vinyl output from 2015

Discogs lists a run of 12-inch records on small labels: *Pambore* on Pluie/Noir Recordings in 2017, *Fresh Like A Gardon* on Shotgun in 2020, and *Arp 003* on Arpège Records in 2021. Rings of Neptune names Tresydos, Pluie/Noir, Shotgun and AirFunk as the labels he has released on since 2015.

### Tresydos, AirFunk and Smoove Mocean

*Time For Spirits EP* was Tresydos's seventh release, a four-track 12-inch with a listed release date of 24 June 2022, according to the KMA60 shop. He appeared on AirFunk 002 in 2023, a record the agency announced on 20 June 2023. KMA60 lists Smoove Mocean as founded in 2020, and the agency says he shares it with his wife.

### A busy 2024

The agency says *Start to Relax* came out on 9 February 2024 as the debut release of Jo SZT's new Komplices imprint. *Spirit of the Hawk* followed on funkON on 12 April 2024, per its Bandcamp page, and Trommel premiered "Righteous Funk" from *Happy Bout That*, the first vinyl release on a new Kumquat imprint, on 19 May 2024. Discogs also lists a *Dreams EP* on Piros that year. Mixmag says his Piros release was the label's last, after roughly seven years of output.

### Mixes

Rings of Neptune's news feed lists a mix for Sonder London (12 May 2022), Manifesto Mixtapes 001 (25 November 2022) and Komplices Podcast 02 (18 June 2024). We list them from the agency's headlines only and did not open the mixes.

### How do we know this is the same Atree?

The name is short and easy to confuse, so we checked it in steps.

1. The video description links two accounts under "follow the artist": Instagram at atree.ollie_ and SoundCloud at atree-music.
2. The SoundCloud profile text names the Smoove Mocean label account and a booking contact at Rings of Neptune.
3. The Rings of Neptune artist page lists those same Instagram and SoundCloud accounts, names Oliver Gleinser, places him in Berlin, and carries a news item for the Yoyaku session.
4. That page, Yoyaku's own artist page and Discogs list the same releases, among them Tresydos, Kumquat, funkON and Lost In Sound records.

One source disagrees on geography. A SoundCloud mix description, seen through the tracklist site Set79, calls Smoove Mocean a Marseille label, while the agency and Mixmag place Atree in Berlin. We follow the agency and Mixmag.

## Which labels is Atree associated with?

His own label is Smoove Mocean, which also issues records as Smoove & Mocean. He has released on Tresydos, Komplices, funkON, Kumquat Records, Lost In Sound Recordings, Piros, AirFunk, Pluie/Noir, Shotgun, Arpège Records and Inside Universe, according to Discogs and the shop and label pages we opened.
