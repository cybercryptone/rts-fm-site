Cabanne is the French DJ and producer Jean-Guillaume Cabanne, based in Paris, who runs the Minibar label and has released on Perlon. He made the first release on Yoyaku's main label, YOYAKU001, in 2018, and has three instore sessions on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel, from 2017, 2018 and 2025.

## Where is Cabanne from and how did he start?

XLR8R's November 2016 interview, written around his debut album, gives his name as Jean-Guillaume Cabanne and describes him as a French producer based in Paris. It says he grew up in Brétignolles sur Mer and later moved to Dijon, formed a first band at 14 after learning guitar, and began learning electronic production at 17 with David Gluck. He told the magazine that he comes from soul, jazz and funk, where an LP is a rite of passage. Yoyaku's artist page also calls him a trained jazz guitarist.

XLR8R dates his first DJ lessons to 1995 and his first international booking to New Year's Eve 2000 in Zurich, for Thomas Brinkmann. His first recorded appearance, per the same interview, was a 1997 various-artists EP on Logistic Records, followed in 1998 by a track with John Thomas on the same label. In 2003 he performed at Mutek in Montreal under his Narod Niki alias, which XLR8R describes as a fully improvised laptop act.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state his age. The sources do show releases from 1997 onward.

## What does Cabanne sound like?

Yoyaku's page for his debut album lists the styles as minimal, minimal house, jazz, downtempo and ambient, and says he is considered one of the main players on the minimal house scene. His Bandcamp bio goes further and calls him the French reference for underground house, which is his own claim.

His club records are lean and groove-led. The two sides of his 2018 Yoyaku EP show the range: Trommel described "Erobique" as a punchy minimal track built on bassline, claps and synth pads, and "Terepho" as a mellower piece with piano-like synths. Discopathy, in contrast, was made largely for home listening, drawing on jazz, soul, funk and hip-hop, and XLR8R reported that most of it does not suit the dancefloor.

## What are the career highlights of Cabanne?

### Telegraph, Perlon and early records

XLR8R says he founded Telegraph in 1999 for his micro house productions and released there from 2000, and that he lost that outlet in 2003. He released on 7th City in 2002. Perlon ties date from 2001, when he and Ark put out the Copacabannark split EP on the label, followed by To Beach Or Not To Beach in 2004. XLR8R's 2018 news item still introduced him as a Perlon-affiliated producer, and Trommel lists Perlon among the labels he is a regular on.

### Minibar and Discopathy

According to XLR8R, he started Minibar in 2005 with Eric Vence as a vinyl-only label, after losing his Telegraph outlet and needing somewhere to release his music. Discopathy, his debut album, came out on Minibar in 2016 on CD and triple vinyl. XLR8R says it took about ten years to finish and calls it a prism of the previous 15 years of his music.

### Concrete, Club der Visionaere and Berlin

The same interview says Concrete, the Paris booking agency and label, has represented him since 2011, that he holds a residency there and that his studio sits below its Paris headquarters. It also lists a residency at Club der Visionaere in Berlin and regular guest slots at Get Perlonized events. Trommel adds Rex Club, Panoramabar and Club Guesthouse to the venues that regularly host him, and notes that Concrete has since closed.

Resident Advisor reported on 4 November 2026 that Cabanne is on the first-wave lineup of Dimensions 2027 and will make his festival debut. Our [Dimensions Festival guide](/festivals/dimensions-festival) has the dates and the full first wave.

## Which labels is Cabanne associated with?

- **Minibar.** His own vinyl-only label, founded in 2005 with Eric Vence. XLR8R listed Fumiya Tanaka, [Rhadoo](/artists/rhadoo), Audio Werner, Lowris and Ben Vedren among its artists in December 2018. For the scene around Rhadoo, see our [Romanian minimal history](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno).
- **Polyson.** A second vinyl-only label he launched, announced by XLR8R on 5 December 2018 with the Hokenkok EP by Varhat, which includes a Cabanne remix. Yoyaku's catalogue lists later Polyson releases, including a Cabanne EP, Ecouvillon.
- **Yoyaku.** He made the first release on the label's main imprint, YOYAKU001, the Stereophobique EP. XLR8R posted the news on 19 September 2018, and Yoyaku lists the record as a 2018 release with the tracks "Erobique" and "Terepho".
- **Perlon and Telegraph.** Perlon since 2001 and Telegraph, which he founded in 1999, per XLR8R. Trommel also names Hello? Repeat among the labels he appears on regularly.
- **Aliases and projects.** Trommel lists John Hut, K.O.D. and Narod Niki as other names, and Yoyaku's artist page adds Interlude. XLR8R also credits collaborations including Ultrakurt with David Gluck and Copacabannark with Ark.

## What is Cabanne doing in 2026?

- **4 November 2026.** Resident Advisor lists him in the first wave for Dimensions 2027, due 26 to 31 August 2027 in Tisno, Croatia, per our festival page.
- **Rhabanne, B/C EP.** Yoyaku lists this two-track Minibar record, MB060, as a collaboration between Cabanne and Rhadoo under the joint name Rhabanne. It is a pre-order with an estimated arrival of February 2027.
- **C.C.C. EP.** Yoyaku lists a Minibar 12", MB054, with the tracks "Karan" and "Stwaight". The page says the release date is to be confirmed, so we have not dated it.
