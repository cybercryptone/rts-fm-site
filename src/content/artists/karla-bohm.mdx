Karla Böhm is a Belgian DJ, producer and label owner who grew up in Liège and is based in Brussels. She runs the vinyl label SOUS:SOL Records and plays house, minimal and techno with a dub streak. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 1 March 2024, has passed 458,000 views on YouTube.

## Where is Karla Böhm from and how did she start?

Her Mixcloud profile says she was born in Belgium and grew up in Liège, where she first played records. The booking agency Interwave also calls her a Belgian DJ and producer from Liège and describes her as well known in Brussels. Her Mixcloud profile gives Brussels as her base.

The same profile says she was fascinated by rhythm from childhood and bought her first drum machine at 18. It says she then travelled around Belgium and Europe, visiting clubs to refine her style. In her own account she began as a producer "for fun", then started DJing, first in Liège and later in Brussels. Interwave adds that she is also a graphic designer, works on furniture and has been active for about 10 years, a figure it does not date.

No source we opened gives a birth date or birth year, so we cannot state her age. Interwave's "about 10 years" is a rough guide to when she began, not a birth year.

## What does Karla Böhm sound like?

Her Mixcloud profile lists techno, house, dub and minimal as influences. It names Brouquade, Melliflow, Minibar, Cabinet, Perlon, Finest Hour and Moshitaka as labels that shaped her taste. That points to the groovy, stripped back end of European house and minimal rather than peak-time techno.

The Yoyaku session fits that picture. The set79 listing of the Yoyaku SoundCloud upload describes the set as minimal and deep house, and its automated tracklist includes Mat Carter, Neurotron, Jovonn and Chris Fortier's 20 year dub of Fade. Set79 is automated analysis, with several tracks unidentified, so treat the track names as a guide. A 30 April 2025 back-to-back with Yentl on GIMIC Radio is described on set79 as a groove-heavy blend of deep house and minimal cuts.

## What are the career highlights of Karla Böhm?

### Label and release work

The clearest public record of her work is SOUS:SOL Records. According to her Mixcloud profile she started it as the next step after DJing, so that her favourite artists could show their skills. In August 2020 the music site Trommel described it as a Belgian, vinyl-only imprint that had put out untitled tracks by artists with no names, and named Karla Böhm as the person behind it.

### Festivals and radio

The Dutch listing site Festivalfans shows her under past appearances at CORE Festival in Brussels, Dour Festival and Paradise City Festival in 2022, WECANDANCE and Wildeburg in 2023, and Wildeburg in 2024. It is a fan-maintained listing without dates or stage details, so we would not treat it as a full touring record.

Her own Linktree lists Horst Arts and Music Festival in 2021, a Planet Show at Kiosk Radio on 19 October 2022 and a night called Molly invite Karla Bohm on 27 May 2023. We could not find independent pages for these dates.

## Which labels is Karla Böhm associated with?

Her main label is SOUS:SOL Records, written Sous:sol on some stores. Trommel lists Arapu, Herodot and tINI as previous supporters. Trommel calls the John Manhard EP SOS004 the label's fourth release and the first to bring an artist forward, after earlier anonymous releases. It adds that she found Manhard and secured the EP, with a remix from Lowris. For more on Arapu, see [his artist page](/artists/arapu).

The newest release we could verify is "The Alternative EP" by Zgelt, catalogue SOS006. Yoyaku's page says Böhm met Zgelt through a shared line-up in Lyon in 2024, admired his precise, restrained mixing and agreed the release after a few conversations. Do not confuse the label with a different producer called Sous Sol, who appears on Traxsource and SoundCloud with Bondage Music and other labels.

## What is Karla Böhm doing in 2026?

Yoyaku lists Zgelt's "The Alternative EP" (SOS006, 12 inch) as a 2026 release on SOUS:SOL. The page gives the year only. Its four tracks are "Deep Inside", "I Feel", "Modern Poetry" and "The Alternative", and Yoyaku describes it as hypnotic, tool-like club music with foggy atmospheres. We found no verified 2026 tour dates, so check her booking page at Interwave or her social accounts for current dates.
