Sapunov is the working name of Sergey Sapunov, a Moscow house DJ whose career runs through the same circle as [Sergey Sanches](/artists/sergey-sanches) and [Anton Kubikov](/artists/anton-kubikov). He plays groove-led, melodic house, he has been on the Russian club circuit since the mid 1990s, and he is a regular name in the RTS.FM archive.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

In a 2023 [interview with Moskvich Mag](https://moskvichmag.ru/lyudi/mne-nravitsya-muzyka-v-kotoroj-est-nerv-dj-sergej-sapunov/), Sapunov says he started learning to DJ around 1995 under Sergey Sanches, who at first would not lend him records. His first public set, played on his own records, came at a birthday party for Sanches in 1996. He lists Luch, Substance and Krysha Mira among the places he has played and says he worked at Luch. By his own account he was part of the team that opened Ptyuch in 1994, though he never got to play there. His first records came from Ivan Illesh, a resident at the club Justo.

## Sound and Style

Sapunov describes his taste as groove-driven, melodic and minor-key, shaped by Ninja Tune, Mo' Wax, Depeche Mode and minimalism. In the same interview he is openly critical of the current Bosnian-school style associated with Solomun. He says he is now booked mostly for summer pre-parties and outdoor events, and that he has no plans to leave Moscow.

## Labels and Releases

The earliest release [Discogs lists](https://www.discogs.com/artist/1791621) for him is The Ancient Future (2000), issued through 20:20 Vision and Only Records. Later mix releases include Live @ Mix on Deus Vox Records (2004), Fashion Only Presents: Dj Sapunov on Fashion Only Records (2006) and Red Club 12 on MacroVision Records and Electroluxe (2007).

A large part of the catalogue comes through collaboration:

- With Sanches, Bell and Djungl, he appears on the House Music.Ru releases HM Tour Collection (2005) and Housemusic.ru: Djungl / Sapunov / Sanches / Bell (2006).
- With Kubikov, he released 3fatmen on World Club Music (2006) and Nu:Gravity (2007), and appears on the Solyanka compilations, including Solyanka 2007 and Solyanka 2009.
- Sapurra is his house alias, listed on Discogs and named in the interview. The same interview mentions a second project, Trevozhny Tip Lichnosti, with a darker, more alternative sound.

As Sapurra, [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/4251229) lists 14 main releases between 2015 and 2026, beginning with *Cheese No More* on Pro-tez Records (2015). In 2019 he shared the *Paperman EP* with Defaultman on Poker Flat Recordings, and later releases include *Never Been To Detroit* on MixCult (2022), which [Genning](/artists/genning) remixed, and *Half Dead EP* on Muted Noise (2026). In the Moskvich interview he calls Sapurra the house project where he puts his more commercial music.

## On the RTS.FM label

RTSFM017, released on 12 June 2026 as a digital release under the Sapurra name, is on the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm017). It has two tracks, "Acid Burn" and "Crash Override", and Bandcamp tags it deep house, minimal house, house and electronic.

## FAQ

### How old is Sapunov?

He says in a 2023 interview that he was born in Moscow in 1980, which makes him 45 or 46 as of October 2026. No full birth date is published. He says he played his first public set in 1996.

### Where is Sapunov from?

Sapunov is a Moscow DJ and says in a 2023 interview that he has no plans to leave the city. He says he was born in the city in 1980.

### What is Sapurra?

Sapurra is Sapunov's house alias, under which he released RTSFM017 on the RTS.FM label in June 2026 and EPs on labels including Poker Flat and MixCult.

### What genre does Sapunov play?

House music, in a groove-driven, melodic and often minor-key style. His Discogs releases are mainly house DJ mixes and compilations.

### What is Sapunov's real name?

Sapunov is his own surname. He releases as Sapunov or Sergey Sapunov, and uses the alias Sapurra for house releases. No separate real name is public.

### Who taught Sapunov to DJ?

According to his Moskvich Mag interview, he learned around 1995 under Sergey Sanches, and the two later played a double set at a Ptyuch anniversary party.

### What is Solyanka?

A compilation series on which Sapunov appears with Anton Kubikov. Discogs lists editions for 2007 and 2009, and a 2014 edition on which Sapunov is also credited.

### Has Sapunov played at RTS.FM?

Yes. The RTS.FM archive has 31 videos with his name in the title, dating from 2008 to 2017.
