Danny Daze is the stage name of Daniel Gomez, a Cuban-American DJ and producer from Miami who runs the Omnidisc label and moves between electro, techno and IDM. He had a house hit with "Your Everything" in 2011 and released his debut album ::BLUE:: in 2023. His Yoyaku instore session was published in January 2026.

## Where is Danny Daze from and how did he start?

Mixmag's March 2016 profile gives his name as Daniel Gomez and places him in Westchester, a Cuban-American neighbourhood of Miami. Qobuz's biography says he was born in Miami, and the festival database Festival Abroad calls him a Cuban-American DJ and producer from the city. SPIN's February 2016 interview says he began DJing weddings and all-ages raves at 13, in the mid-1990s. Mixmag says he started making bass music at 14 in FruityLoops for Miami's car bass parties.

The same Mixmag piece lists his early scene contacts: the producer Otto von Schirach, whom he met at 15, and the turntablist DJ Craze, whom he met around 1999 or 2000. It also says he held a weekly eight-hour residency at Funktion and Monday nights at Back Door Bambi, and that DJ AM picked up his edits.

We could not verify his age. No source we opened gives a birth year or date, so we do not state one.

## What does Danny Daze sound like?

Qobuz describes a producer who resists easy categorisation, drawing on Dutch electro, Detroit techno and disco-house. Festival Abroad says his freeform sets move between electro, techno and Italo disco. In SPIN he put it more bluntly: "I don't have a sound."

On record the reference points are Miami's bass and electro tradition and the experimental end of electronic music. Mixmag's 2023 album news describes ::BLUE:: as ambient, bass and skewed IDM, while Resident Advisor's review calls it a glitchy, sci-fi long-form work built on slowed arpeggios and harps.

## What are the career highlights of Danny Daze?

### From DiscoTech to "Your Everything"

According to Mixmag's 2016 profile, he formed the remix group DiscoTech in 2006 with Joe Maz and Gigamesh, and released "Ghettofab" under his own name in 2009, a track that appeared in mixes by [DJ Hell](/artists/dj-hell) and [Loco Dice](/artists/loco-dice). Mixmag says he took a year off gigging, and that break led to "Your Everything", released in 2011 on Hot Creations. SPIN says Jamie Jones picked up the track, which led to full-time touring. Mixmag adds that the hit led some promoters to book him as a deep house DJ, a label he has not embraced.

Qobuz also credits him with a collaboration with [Maceo Plex](/artists/maceo-plex) as Jupiter Jazz, which we could not confirm elsewhere.

### Omnidisc

He founded Omnidisc in 2015. Mixmag's February 2016 news item revealed him as the person behind the label, which had kept its boss anonymous in its early run, and the March 2016 profile quoted Mixmag's electro editor calling it one of the most exciting labels in electro and techno. The label's first release was his own Four EP, with collaborators including 214, Deroboter, Drvg Cvltvre and Philipp Gorbachev, according to Ibiza Spotlight. Electronic Groove's December 2025 report describes the label as an extension of his earlier Omniamm project.

### ::BLUE:: and the audiovisual show

::BLUE:: is billed as his first full-length album. Mixmag reported on 11 October 2023 that it would arrive on 17 November 2023 on Omnidisc as a 90-minute continuous piece, with Nick León, Jonny From Space, Push Button Objects and Deroboter as guests. A 33-minute audiovisual version was first shown at Art Basel Miami in December 2022, and Mixmag listed a residency at the Frost Museum Planetarium for January 2024. Resident Advisor's review, dated 19 December 2023, gives the album 19 tracks and says it was mastered in stereo and Dolby Atmos.

## Which labels is Danny Daze associated with?

- **Omnidisc.** His own Miami label, founded in 2015. Electronic Groove says it had 70 tracks in its 10-year compilation, 11 of them by Daze, and named Wata Igarashi, Barker, Nick León, Tim Reaper and Anthony Rother among the artists.
- **Hot Creations.** Released "Your Everything" in 2011, per Mixmag and Qobuz.
- **Kompakt Extra.** Qobuz lists his Speicher 80 release from 2014 on the imprint. For the parent label, see our [Kompakt history](/blog/kompakt-records-cologne-history).
- **Schematic.** Qobuz lists Protocol 305 Variables (2020) and Broenecia (2023) on Schematic. Mixmag describes founder Romulo Del Castillo's 1996 Miami label as "America's Warp."
- **Ultramajic and Craigie Knowes.** Qobuz lists Dual (2015) and the Tumbadora EP with Jonny From Space (2024) on these labels respectively.
