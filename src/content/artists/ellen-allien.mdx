Ellen Allien (born Ellen Fraatz in West Berlin on 16 September 1968, according to Wikipedia) is a German DJ and producer who founded the Berlin label BPitch Control. She is known for techno that runs from minimal to raw rave. Her 42 minute Yoyaku instore session, published on 26 May 2026, has 63,871 YouTube views.

## Where is Ellen Allien from and how did she start?

English and German Wikipedia both give her birth name as Ellen Fraatz and her birth date as 16 September 1968, which makes her 58 in November 2026. No other source we opened states a birth date. Both Wikipedia articles say she grew up in West Berlin, and Mixmag's 2017 profile describes her as a teenager there when the Wall fell in 1989.

Her route into clubs ran through the Fischlabor bar in Schöneberg, which Mixmag and Friends of Friends say was run by Dimitri Hegemann, later the founder of Tresor. Friends of Friends says she lived in a squat in Schöneberg from the age of 18 and began experimenting with turntables there. German Wikipedia says a Fischlabor manager heard one of her mixtapes around 1991 and asked her to DJ, an offer she first turned down.

German Wikipedia says she spent a year in London and met electronic music during the Second Summer of Love in 1988, while English Wikipedia says 1989.

English Wikipedia says she became a resident DJ in 1992 at Bunker, Tresor and E-Werk. German Wikipedia says she hosted a show on Kiss FM in 1993, and ran a Sunday afterhour called Club the Rest with Tanith in Tresor's Globus room in 1994 and 1995. In a 2022 Amsterdam Dance Event interview she said she worked at Tresor on the door and behind the bar, and began DJing because friends kept asking her to play.

## What does Ellen Allien sound like?

Her own productions sit at the minimal end of techno, with a voice that is part of the sound. English Wikipedia says she sings in German and English. BPitch's description of her 2026 album New Life calls her techno a blend of minimal, dark wave and hypnotic euphoria, with spoken words that give it an otherworldly quality.

Mixmag's 2017 profile called her album Nost her most straightforward techno record, built on 1990s old school techno and classic analogue synthesisers while keeping a minimal feel. Her sets are harder and more physical than her albums suggest. She told DJ TechTools in 2020 that she plays techno alongside edits of indie tracks, avoids playlists, does not use effects, and mixes on vinyl or USB depending on whether the turntables are stable. In 2019 she said she prefers wild, rave-style clubs where the kick drum hits hard.

## What are the career highlights of Ellen Allien?

### BPitch Control and the first albums

She founded BPitch Control in 1999, according to English Wikipedia, DJ TechTools and Beatportal. Beatportal's July 2026 profile says she started it because her music did not fit other labels of the time, Tresor Records among them. Her debut album Stadtkind came out on it in 2001, followed by Berlinette in 2003 and Thrills in 2005, according to both Wikipedia articles. Friends of Friends and the Wikipedia articles name Modeselektor, Apparat and [Paul Kalkbrenner](/artists/paul-kalkbrenner) among the acts the label brought to a wider audience.

### Apparat, fabric and LISm

Orchestra of Bubbles, a collaborative album with Apparat, followed in 2006, and her fabric 34 mix in 2007. Sool (2008) and Dust (2010) came next, with German Wikipedia dating Dust to 21 May 2010. LISm (2013) grew out of her score for Drama Per Musica, a dance piece by the choreographer Alexandre Roccoli, which English Wikipedia says premiered in Paris in March 2011. She told The Fader in 2013 that she wrote the album by following her own feelings rather than the stage's timing.

### Nost, UFO Inc. and We Are Not Alone

Nost came out on 12 May 2017 on BPitch Control, according to a VICE interview, and she told VICE she recorded it in analogue. In 2019 she started UFO Inc., a label she described to DJ TechTools as a home for rough, raw techno, alongside the album Alientronic. English Wikipedia says the same year she launched We Are Not Alone, a bi-monthly rave at Griessmühle in Berlin that moved to RSO.berlin after the venue closed in 2020. AurAA followed in summer 2020. She also runs Vinylism, a series of parties in record stores that VICE described in 2017 as a celebration of vinyl culture.

### Recognition

BPitch reported on 26 March 2026 that she received a Polyton award for her performance at Berlin Beats at Hamburger Bahnhof. German Wikipedia places it in the EDM category and cites n-tv. Beatportal calls it the first Polyton Award for Electronic Music. We could not independently confirm a French honour that Beatportal's profile reports for 2023.

## Which labels is Ellen Allien associated with?

BPitch Control is her main label. Sources disagree on its start: 1999 in most, 1994 in Friends of Friends, and 2001 in a Berliner Philharmoniker biography. Before it she ran Braincandy, which German Wikipedia dates to 1995, and which English Wikipedia and Friends of Friends say she closed after distribution problems. German Wikipedia ties its end to the closure of E-Werk.

BPitch grew into a home for other artists. Her own Yoyaku chart lists BPitch releases by Gotshell and Exos, and our [Tigerhead](/artists/tigerhead) page says Tigerhead debuted on the label in July 2024. UFO Inc. is her second imprint, started in 2019. English Wikipedia also lists a 2005 sublabel, Memo Musik, which we found in no other source.

## What is Ellen Allien doing in 2026?

The dated items we could confirm:

- **6 February 2026.** The Berliner Philharmoniker's Strom festival programme listed her DJ set in the Philharmonie foyer from 01:00 to 03:00. In July she told The Berliner that she performed at the Philharmonie this year.
- **26 March 2026.** BPitch announced her Polyton award.

- **25 June 2026.** Berlin Beats: Ellen Allien was listed by the Staatliche Museen zu Berlin as a free open-air event at Hamburger Bahnhof, 19:00 to 22:00.
- **9 July 2026.** New Life came out on BPitch Control, her first album in six years according to Beatportal, recorded in Berlin, Ibiza and Miami with the co-producer Pablo Mateo Sánchez.
- **12 and 13 September 2026.** Yoyaku's SlapFunk event at Cité de la Mode et du Design in Paris listed her on its lineup. The page says the event has ended, but does not say who played.
- **7 November 2026.** Our [London clubs guide](/blog/best-london-clubs-2026-guide) notes that fabric's calendar billed her for its Saturday night.
- **10 November 2026.** Resident Advisor's feature of tributes to Juan Atkins includes her among the contributors, as noted in our [Juan Atkins report](/blog/juan-atkins-dies-detroit-techno-pioneer).

In a July 2026 interview she described New Life as a protest album about rejecting leaders and building community, and said she tests new tracks at her We Are Not Alone nights at RSO.berlin.

## FAQ

### How old is Ellen Allien?

English and German Wikipedia give her birth date as 16 September 1968, which makes her 58 as of 24 November 2026. We found no other source stating it.

### Where is Ellen Allien from?

She is from Berlin. Wikipedia says she was born in West Berlin.

### Who is Ellen Allien?

She is a German techno DJ and producer, born Ellen Fraatz, who founded BPitch Control in 1999 and UFO Inc. in 2019. The Staatliche Museen zu Berlin describe her as the matriarch of Berlin techno.

### Where does Ellen Allien live?

Her Bandcamp page gives Berlin. Mixmag reported in 2017 that she split her time roughly evenly between Berlin and Ibiza.

### What is Ellen Allien's Yoyaku instore session?

It is a 42 minute, 57 second set published on 26 May 2026, with 63,871 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 38th of 178 Yoyaku instore sessions we tracked.

### What label is Ellen Allien on?

She releases mainly on her own BPitch Control, including New Life in July 2026. She also runs UFO Inc., which released her Ufo EP in 2019.

### What is BPitch Control?

It is the Berlin label she founded in 1999, according to most sources we opened. English Wikipedia says it launched Modeselektor, Paul Kalkbrenner and Apparat.

### What is her latest album?

New Life, released on BPitch Control on 9 July 2026. Beatportal called it her first new album in six years, after AurAA in 2020.

### What is We Are Not Alone?

It is a recurring rave she started in 2019, first at Griessmühle and now at RSO.berlin, according to Wikipedia and Beatportal.

### What genre does Ellen Allien play?

Techno, from minimal and electro to dark wave and hard, rave-driven sets.
