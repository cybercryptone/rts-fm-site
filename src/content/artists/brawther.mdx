Brawther is Alexandre "Sammy" Gouyette, a French house producer and DJ who was born in Paris and is now based in Lisbon. He runs the Negentropy label, co-runs Dungeon Meat with Tristan da Cunha and records deep, soulful house. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 20 March 2024, has 42,914 views.

## Where is Brawther from and how did he start?

Mixmag's August 2018 feature says Gouyette was born and raised in Paris and was 34 at the time of writing. Yoyaku's artist page gives his name as Alexandre Gouyette and lists the aliases Izmo, Paris Underground Trax and Sammy Gouyette. NTS says he began producing at 15. The Ransom Note and XLR8R both describe him as French and Parisian.

XLR8R says that in the late 1990s he was drawn to ambient and experimental IDM, including Aphex Twin and the Rephlex label, and then found deep house on holidays in the UK. It names Pepe Bradock and St Germain as main influences. In a November 2019 Trommel interview he said he learned piano as a child and was influenced by Aphex Twin as a teenager. He released as Izmo in the late 2000s, and Trommel dates his first record under that name, "Where Is Eddie?", to 2007. Mixmag says Tony Humphries opened an online mix with it.

He left France in 2011, according to Trommel, and lived in Sweden before spending six years in Leeds. Mixmag found him in Leeds in 2018, living with his wife and young child. He then moved to Lisbon, where The Ransom Note, XLR8R and the Josh Baker Podcast (July 2021) all place him. His Bandcamp bio says he is currently based in Lisbon, though its profile still lists Paris.

## How old is Brawther?

Mixmag's piece was published on 7 August 2018 and gives his age as 34. That puts his birth between 8 August 1983 and 7 August 1984, so he is 42 or 43 as of 24 November 2026, depending on his birthday. No source we opened gives an exact birth date.

## What does Brawther sound like?

Mixmag describes deep, "proper" house with a spectral, jacking feel, with touches of broken beat, instrumental hip-hop and jazz on his album. Trommel quotes him calling his music house at its core, with influences from UK garage, broken beat, tech house and breakbeat. NTS says his work draws on garage and deep house from the UK, Chicago, Detroit, New Jersey and France, plus the Warp-style ambient electronica he grew up on. XLR8R calls it "sleek and soulful house". The Ransom Note says Kerri Chandler, Ron Trent, Chez Damier and Jeremy Underground have championed his music.

## What are the career highlights of Brawther?

### Balance and the early records, 2009 to 2015

Trommel says he debuted as Brawther in 2009 on Balance Alliance, a sub-label of Balance, followed by two more releases on Ron Trent's and Chez Damier's Balance labels. Resident Advisor says his 2015 album *Endless*, which XLR8R calls his debut album, compiled tracks from his Balance EPs. Soundshelter dates his Do It Yourself EP, the first release on secretsundaze Music (SECRET001), to 25 May 2011.

### My Love Is Underground and Dungeon Meat

Mixmag says he co-founded the Paris label My Love Is Underground with a circle of friends including Jeremy Underground, and that his EP there was credited to Paris Underground Trax. Trommel says he was also involved in running its later releases. Dungeon Meat, his raw house project with Tristan da Cunha, was marking 10 years in April 2024, according to XLR8R.

### Negentropy and Transient States, 2018

Brawther launched Negentropy with the album *Transient States*. Resident Advisor and Mixmag announced it on 6 March 2018 for release on 23 April, while Soundshelter lists 30 April 2018, so the sources differ by a week. Resident Advisor says it was his first full-length album of new material and that the label name comes from a 2015 track on Balance. Mixmag describes the eight tracks as ranging from house to dub, broken beat and downtempo. The same article lists dates in Osaka and Tokyo in March 2018, and Trommel mentions back-to-back sets with Fumiya Tanaka in both cities.

### Touring and radio

Mixmag says he played a rooftop party at Output in Brooklyn in spring 2018 and is a semi-regular at Back To Basics. Trommel mentions a 2017 guest mix for Closer in Kyiv and says the track "Fuck Off Track" was first played on the DC10 terrace. NTS says it has played him more than 20 times, starting on 5 May 2014, and that his music has featured on 26 episodes, including a Floating Points special for MAP Charity on 26 August 2024. See our profile of [Floating Points](/artists/floating-points).

## Which labels is Brawther associated with?

- **Balance.** The Chez Damier and Ron Trent label, home to his 2009 debut as Brawther, according to Trommel, and to *Endless*. Trommel also lists him as managing Courtesy Of Balance.
- **secretsundaze.** Released his Do It Yourself EP in 2011. Mixmag says it was repressed roughly nine or ten times, by his own account.
- **My Love Is Underground.** The Paris label he co-founded, per Mixmag.
- **Cabinet Records.** Soothing came out there in 2017, per Mixmag, and Yoyaku lists The Driver / Basix and Fairground / Kitten (CAB59 and CAB55).
- **Negentropy.** His own label, started in 2018, which Yoyaku shows releasing a Jaxx Freaxx 12" with Fumiya Tanaka and Zweizig remixes.
- **Dungeon Meat.** Run with Tristan da Cunha.
- **B2 Recordings and Scale Factor.** Home of Sundials and Aferrafters. Yoyaku also lists him on RAWMoments, and on Berg Audio and Sushitech compilations.
- **Remixes.** Yoyaku lists a remix on a release by [Satoshi Tomiie](/artists/satoshi-tomiie) (Abstract Architecture, AA005).

## FAQ

### How old is Brawther?

He is 42 or 43 as of 24 November 2026, depending on his birthday. Mixmag gave his age as 34 on 7 August 2018, and we found no exact birth date.

### Where is Brawther from?

He is French and was born and raised in Paris, according to Mixmag. He lived in Sweden and Leeds before settling in Lisbon, according to Trommel.

### Who is Brawther?

Brawther is the house producer and DJ Alexandre "Sammy" Gouyette. He runs Negentropy and Dungeon Meat and has released on Balance, secretsundaze and Cabinet Records.

### What is Brawther's real name?

Yoyaku, Resident Advisor and Trommel give it as Alexandre Gouyette. Mixmag and XLR8R call him Sammy Gouyette.

### What is Brawther's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 20 March 2024, runs 68 minutes and has 42,914 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 58th by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Which labels has Brawther released on?

Balance, secretsundaze, My Love Is Underground, Cabinet Records, Negentropy, B2 Recordings and Scale Factor, among others, according to the sources we opened.

### What is Negentropy?

It is Brawther's own label, launched in 2018 with his album *Transient States*, according to Resident Advisor. Yoyaku lists later Negentropy records.

### What is Dungeon Meat?

Dungeon Meat is his raw house project with Tristan da Cunha. XLR8R said in April 2024 that they were celebrating 10 years of it.

### Is Brawther the same person as Izmo?

Yes. Yoyaku lists Izmo as an alias of Alexandre Gouyette, and NTS says Brawther also records as Izmo.

### What does Brawther sound like?

Deep, soulful house with influences from UK garage, broken beat, dub and Chicago and Detroit house, according to Mixmag, NTS and Trommel.
