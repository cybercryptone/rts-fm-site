Nina Yamada is a DJ, record collector and label founder, born and raised in Kamakura, Japan, and based in London since 2022. She runs Jazzy Sport London, the first international branch of the Japanese label and shop Jazzy Sport. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 14 April 2026, has passed 400,000 views on YouTube.

## Where is Nina Yamada from and how did she start?

The Pool, the London agency that represents her, says she was born and raised in Kamakura and moved to London in 2022. Her Ella Records interview and her Worldwide FM host page give the same origin. All three credit a family vinyl collection with pulling her into music and into DJing.

The Pool's July 2026 announcement says she came up through Tokyo's hip hop, soul and nu jazz scene of the 2000s and later got to know London's club scene. The Ella interview adds that she works as a graphic designer too, making flyers for her own parties and, more recently, zines and liner notes for labels. No source we opened gives her birth date, so we cannot say how old she is.

## What does Nina Yamada sound like?

She describes deep house as her specialty, but her sets cross into Afrobeat, jazz, Latin and reggae, according to Ella Records and Worldwide FM. The Pool's list is wider still: downtempo, dub, disco, acid jazz and broken beat.

In the Ella interview she says she tends to pick one key track and build a set around it. Her house mix for Ella builds toward BFC's "Galaxy". She also says she prefers buying records in shops, especially on a trusted owner's recommendation.

Her Worldwide FM show of 13 February 2026 was billed as a two-hour journey, starting with ambient textures and moving through dub and music from Africa and Asia to deeper electronic sounds. Mixmag Asia, in August 2023, called her mix for My Analogue Journal eclectic but jazzy, downtempo and fun.

## What are the career highlights of Nina Yamada?

### Jazzy Sport London

Jazzy Sport began in Japan in the early 2000s as a shop and label, and The Pool dates it to 2002. Yamada founded Jazzy Sport London in 2022, according to The Pool, as its first international branch. The Pool says it runs events, radio shows and community projects linking the Japanese and UK scenes.

Its first label release is the compilation *Past Forward: Silver* (JSL001), which Yamada selected from the Jazzy Sport catalogue. Time Capsule's write-up of 7 October 2026 says Kuniyuki Takahashi mastered it and that it includes remixes of Cro-Magnon by DJ Mitsu the Beats and Idjut Boys.

### Venues and festivals

The Pool lists Panorama Bar, Wonderfruit, Love Supreme, Multi Multi, We Out Here, Jazz Cafe, MOT London and Yoyaku Records among the places she has played. Panorama Bar is the upper floor of Berghain, which our piece on [who runs Berghain](/blog/who-runs-berghain-ownership-operators) covers. The Pool's roster says she opened for [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) at We Out Here in the UK. The Ella interview lists France, Spain, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands and Norway.

## Which labels is Nina Yamada associated with?

Jazzy Sport London is the one we can confirm. The Pool says it is expanding into label work, and *Past Forward: Silver* is its first release, with distribution by Time Capsule, per the Time Capsule write-up.
