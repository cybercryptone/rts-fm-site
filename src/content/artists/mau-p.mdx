Mau P is the Dutch house and tech house producer Maurits Westveen, based in Amsterdam and signed across labels including Repopulate Mars, Afterlife, Black Book, Diynamic and Interscope. He broke through in 2022 with "Drugs from Amsterdam", reached No. 55 in DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs 2026, and is booked for Time Warp Mannheim in April 2027.

## Where is Mau P from and how did he start?

Wikipedia gives his name as Maurits Jan Westveen and his birth date as 26 October 1996, in Amsterdam, which makes him 30 as of November 2026. Wikipedia is our source for the exact date, so treat it as single-sourced. A Complex interview from September 2025 says he grew up in Amsterdam around house parties.

He did not start as Mau P. According to Wikipedia, his first release was "Formula" in 2014 on Spinnin' Talent Pool, under the name Maurice West. Dancing Astronaut says that as Maurice West he dominated bigroom in the second half of the 2010s, then experimented outside the genre without it advancing his career.

During the pandemic he decided to start over, per the same profile. Complex says the change came around 2022 because the new sound did not fit his earlier profile or the bookings he wanted. None of the sources we read explains what "Mau P" stands for, so we do not guess.

## What does Mau P sound like?

Wikipedia lists his genres as techno, tech house and house. In practice that means bass-heavy, club-first records built around a hook you can sing back after one pass. Beatportal describes his production style as heavy, and says his tracks have topped the Beatport, Shazam and Spotify charts.

Complex lists Armand van Helden, [Solomun](/artists/solomun), Carl Cox and Skrillex as influences. "Merther", his 2024 debut on Defected, samples Ini Kamoze's "World A Music", according to Beatportal. That mix of dance-floor sampling and hard-hitting low end is the throughline from "Drugs from Amsterdam" to his latest singles.

## What are the career highlights of Mau P?

**2022.** "Drugs from Amsterdam" came out on 19 August 2022 on Lee Foss's Repopulate Mars label and reached No. 1 on Beatport's main chart, per Wikipedia. Dancing Astronaut says he first sent the record to DJs such as John Summit and Tchami without revealing who made it. Wikipedia reports that Reinier Zonneveld and Armand van Helden made official remixes, and that Billboard named it one of the 50 best dance songs of 2022.

**2023.** Dancing Astronaut named him its Breakout Artist of 2023 and reports he played EDC Las Vegas, EDC Orlando and Tomorrowland that year. It says his Afterlife track "Metro", with Kevin de Vries, was the most-played track at Tomorrowland in 2023. Wikipedia adds that he received the SLAM! Global Breakthrough Award at Amsterdam Dance Event.

**2024.** He entered DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs at No. 91 and released "Receipts" with Diplo and Gunna on Columbia, according to Wikipedia.

**2025.** His first EP, Too Big for B-Side, arrived on Diynamic on 23 May 2025, per Wikipedia. Beatportal's 2025 year-end tech house charts put "Like I Like It" first among tracks and Mau P first among artists. DJ Mag ranked him No. 77. According to uDiscover Music, he headlined Coachella's Sahara Tent across both weekends.

**2026.** DJ Mag ranked him No. 55, which our [Chris Stussy profile](/artists/chris-stussy) also notes: he, Stussy and John Summit shared the Highest Climber distinction, each rising 22 places. The poll was revealed at [UNVRS] Ibiza, the club we covered in our [DJ Mag Top 100 Clubs report](/blog/berghain-unvrs-dj-mag-top-100).

## Which labels is Mau P associated with?

He releases across a wide spread of labels rather than one home base. Repopulate Mars issued "Drugs from Amsterdam" and "Beats for the Underground". Afterlife released "Metro", Black Book released "Dress Code" and "neck", Diynamic released his EP and "Like I Like It", and Defected released "Merther".

Insomniac, Experts Only and Nervous also appear in his discography on Wikipedia. His 2026 single "Just a Little Bit More" is on Interscope Records. Beatportal notes the Beatport listing is a Generation Rebrand BV release under exclusive license to Interscope, which explains why Wikipedia credits Generation Rebrand.

On 6 January 2026 he wrote that the year would bring "an album, a label, a clothing line", as reported by We Rave You. We could not confirm the details of any of the three from sources we opened.

## Latest: Mau P in 2026

The year started with that January announcement and the single "neck" on Chris Lake's Black Book Records in February, per Beatportal. In April, "Like I Like It" won Best-Selling Track of the Year at the 2026 Beatport Awards, Beatportal reported on 23 April.

"Just a Little Bit More" followed on 4 September 2026 on Interscope. uDiscover Music says it had been previewed at live sets and festivals beforehand, and Beatportal counts it as his 15th original production since "Drugs from Amsterdam". The same piece notes he holds residencies at Pacha Ibiza and XS Las Vegas.

His Baddest Behaviour party series launched at Pacha in 2025 as a weekly Wednesday residency, according to Complex.
