Monile is a Morocco-born DJ and producer based in Barcelona, according to Trommel and Decoded Magazine. She is known for deep, bass-heavy sets that move between acid, ghetto house, Detroit techno and electro. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 July 2026, has 58,629 YouTube views.

## Where is Monile from and how did she start?

Monile is Moroccan and describes her roots as Amazigh, or Berber, in an interview published by Jaeger in Oslo. She says her father is from the Souss region and her mother from the Middle Atlas, and that she is the youngest of three children. The page carries no publication date, though its image path points to October 2025.

She says electronic music reached her through her older brother's collection, which ran from 80s and 90s dance and trance to techno and house. Decoded Magazine, in its write-up of her Yoyaku session, tells the same story. She trained on classical piano from a young age and trained as an architect, which she says helps her structure how she makes music.

She first touched a mixer at 19, playing for friends in Morocco, and began to treat DJing seriously after her first paid gig in front of a real crowd. A producer friend introduced her to production in 2017. She moved to Spain at the end of that year and enrolled in a music production school. Her first club residencies were in Barcelona, where she says she developed as both DJ and producer.

We could not find a birth date or birth year in any source we opened, so we cannot say how old she is.

## What does Monile sound like?

Decoded describes a taste for deep grooves and heavy bass, moving through old school acid, ghetto house, minimal, Detroit techno and electro, with funk running underneath. It adds a dub influence that gives her sets weight and patience. In the Jaeger interview she names electro, techno and house, along with breaks, UK garage, trip hop, jazz, indie rock and world music, and says her music carries a hypnotic depth tied to her Amazigh roots.

Trommel's writers hear it in the records. In 2023 it called her output bass-driven house with a darker side, and a punchy, tech-leaning track with a broken piano break. In 2025 it called her Vacuity contribution a deep, bubbling techno cut with a minimal beat and pulsing bassline, suited to a dark room.

## What are the career highlights of Monile?

### Barcelona club scene

Trommel's 2024 premiere text says she plays regularly at Les Enfants Brillants and RED58 in Barcelona, as well as abroad. Earlier, in April 2023, Trommel said she was about to play her first resident event at Atlantic Club in Barcelona and a Paris date alongside [Traumer](/artists/traumer). In September 2022 the same outlet listed her as a local supporting artist for an Audiodise open-air event at Audiodise Beach Club. For the wider Spanish scene, see our [clubbing in Spain city guide](/blog/clubbing-in-spain-city-guide-2026).

### Trommel's attention, 2023 and 2024

Trommel ran a free download of her track "Belle's" in April 2023 and had her on its InSession mix series. In January 2024 it named her one of 12 artists to watch, citing premieres, its party series and a busy year of gigs, including one at a top venue on the scene. It did not name the venue in the text we could read.

### Records from 2020 onward

She remixed Red Pig Flower's "Meduza Madness" for the Work In Progress label in 2020. Her track "Who Am I" appeared on CHZDC001, the first record on the Chez Doc imprint, in a report dated December 2021. By April 2023 Trommel listed "Play" for MAAI Records and a dark track for The White Wagons Records among her recent work. In 2024 she appeared on MASS003, the third vinyl release from MASS, a Leeds and Barcelona collective. In 2025 she appeared on the Chiron Key EP from Vacuity Records. Jaeger's interview says her first solo release was due in 2025, but it names no title and we could not identify it.

### How do we know this is the same Monile?

The name could be confused with others, so we checked. The video description links an Instagram account, monile.music, which is the handle we list for her. Decoded's page for the same session describes a Morocco-born selector based in Barcelona who learned piano and grew up with an older brother's records, and the Jaeger interview gives the same background. Trommel, which has covered her since 2020, also describes a Moroccan artist based in Barcelona. Her Yoyaku link goes back to August 2021, when Trommel listed her among the residents warming up for a Yoyaku-themed party in Barcelona headlined by Zendid, [Lowris](/artists/lowris) and Varhat.
