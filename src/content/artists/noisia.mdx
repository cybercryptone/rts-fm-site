Noisia are a Dutch drum and bass trio from Groningen: Nik Roos, Thijs de Vlieger and Martijn van Sonderen. Active since 2003, they built their name on neurofunk and meticulous sound design, and run Vision Recordings. Their albums *Split the Atom* and *Outer Edges* are genre landmarks, and in September 2026 they announced a reunion after seven years apart.

## Where is Noisia from and how did they start?

Noisia come from Groningen in the north of the Netherlands. According to Wikipedia, Martijn van Sonderen, who had a background in hip hop production and piano, formed the group with Nik Roos and Thijs de Vlieger. Their debut release was the "Silicon" and "Tomahawk" 12-inch on Nerve Recordings in August 2003. Wikipedia lists their years active as 2003 to 2022 and 2026 to the present, although one of its categories dates the group's founding to 2000, so we use 2003 as the release start. Wikipedia does not explain where the name comes from, and we have not found a source that does.

Wikipedia notes that their tracks "Machine Gun" and "Could This Be" were used in the game *SSX*, and lists video game work on titles including *Wipeout HD* and *DJ Hero*.

## What does Noisia sound like?

Noisia are best known for neurofunk: dense, mechanical drum and bass with razor-cut bass design and heavy emphasis on production detail. Wikipedia lists their genres as drum and bass, neurofunk, techstep, breakbeat, electro house, dubstep and grime, and the official Noisia site describes the trio as making drum and bass, neurofunk and sound design since 2003.

Their range is wider than the label suggests. The Wikipedia article on *Outer Edges* gives drum and bass, neurofunk, dubstep and nu-jazz as the album's genres. They also scored the *DmC: Devil May Cry* game, with a 36-track soundtrack released through Division on 15 January 2013, according to Wikipedia. ## What are the career highlights of Noisia?

- **2008:** *FabricLive.40*, their mix for London's fabric, released 9 June 2008 per Wikipedia.
- **2010:** *Split the Atom*, their debut studio album, released 5 April 2010. "Machine Gun" and the title track were its singles, according to Wikipedia.
- **2013:** *I Am Legion*, a collaborative album with the UK hip hop group Foreign Beggars, released 2 September 2013.
- **2015 and 2016:** Wikipedia lists a run of Drum&BassArena Awards: Best Producer, Best Track (for "Dead Limit" with The Upbeats) and Best Live Act in 2015, then Best Video ("Mantra"), Best Album (*Outer Edges*) and Best Producer in 2016.
- **2016:** *Outer Edges*, released 5 August 2016 on Vision, their first album in six years. Wikipedia says it peaked at No. 18 on Billboard's Top Dance/Electronic Albums chart.
- **2022:** *Closer*, described by Wikipedia as their final album, announced on 28 April 2022.

We found no Grammy nomination for Noisia in the sources we checked, so we claim none.

## Which labels is Noisia associated with?

The trio own and run their own labels. According to Wikipedia, Vision Recordings is the drum and bass imprint and Division Recordings covers house, grime and other genres. Invisible Recordings launched in March 2010 for experimental drum and bass and closed in 2019. Wikipedia's infobox also lists mau5trap, OWSLA, Roc Nation and Spinnin' among the labels they have released through.

Vision keeps going. Wikipedia says Thijs de Vlieger's solo releases as Thys and Nik Roos's Sleepnet EPs (*First Light* and *II*) appeared on Vision. Beatportal reported in September 2026 that van Sonderen continues to run the label alongside his bandmates, and that Vision released Former's *Lownoise* EP earlier in the year.

## Why did Noisia split in 2019?

Noisia announced on 17 September 2019 that they would disband, and Wikipedia says they cited creative differences. In a 2021 Mixmag report they said that "nowadays we want different things." The pandemic delayed the farewell tour. Mixmag reported in August 2021 that it would run through Europe, with a December finale at Amsterdam's Melkweg. Wikipedia gives 21 August 2022 as the date of their final show, and Beatportal says the tour closed at Lowlands in 2022.

## What have the members done on their own?

Per Wikipedia, Thijs de Vlieger releases solo music as Thys and is part of VIER and ILY (with Skrillex). Nik Roos releases as Sleepnet and is part of Ring Noord (with Former) and Body Ocean (with Jeremy Glenn of The Upbeats). Martijn van Sonderen is part of the techno and house duo Zonderling with Jaap de Vries.

## FAQ

### Who is Noisia?

Noisia are a Dutch drum and bass trio from Groningen, active since 2003. They are known for neurofunk, sound design and their own labels Vision and Division. They announced a reunion in September 2026.

### Who are the members of Noisia?

Nik Roos, Thijs de Vlieger and Martijn van Sonderen. Roos also records as Sleepnet, de Vlieger as Thys, and van Sonderen is part of the duo Zonderling.

### Where is Noisia from?

Groningen, in the north of the Netherlands. The official Noisia site and Wikipedia both place the trio there.

### What genre is Noisia?

Mainly drum and bass and neurofunk. Wikipedia also lists techstep, breakbeat, electro house, dubstep and grime among their genres.

### What is Noisia's biggest track?

It depends on the measure. "Machine Gun" from *Split the Atom* is the track most often cited, and Wikipedia notes it and "Could This Be" were used in the game *SSX*. "Dead Limit" with The Upbeats won Best Track at the 2015 Drum&BassArena Awards.

### Why did Noisia break up?

They announced the split on 17 September 2019. Wikipedia says they cited creative differences, and in 2021 they told Mixmag that they now wanted different things.

### Are Noisia back together?

Yes. They announced on 17 September 2026 that they are making music together again, and their first show back is at Melkweg in Amsterdam on 4 December 2026. The Noisia website lists a second night on 5 December and further dates.

### What labels does Noisia run?

Vision Recordings for drum and bass and Division Recordings for house, grime and other styles. Invisible Recordings, launched in March 2010 for experimental drum and bass, closed in 2019, according to Wikipedia.

### What albums has Noisia released?

*Split the Atom* (2010), *I Am Legion* with Foreign Beggars (2013), *Outer Edges* (2016) and *Closer* (2022), per Wikipedia. They also made the *FabricLive.40* mix in 2008.

### Did Noisia ever get nominated for a Grammy?

We found no Grammy nomination for Noisia in the sources we checked. Their documented awards are from the Drum&BassArena Awards in 2015 and 2016, according to Wikipedia.
