Vlad Caia is a Bucharest producer whose records start as improvised takes and are then edited into arrangements. He is also a mastering engineer and a co-founder of Amphia Records, and works as half of [SIT](/artists/sit) with [Cristi Cons](/artists/cristi-cons), as one third of Amorf, and under his own name, where his 2018 album *Division I* is the headline release. He features in our history of [rominimal](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno), the Bucharest scene around Sunrise and Arpiar.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Discogs describes him as a Bucharest-based musician. According to [Bandcamp Daily's 2026 scene report](https://daily.bandcamp.com/scene-report/romanian-minimal-techno-scene-report), he stumbled upon the Rominimal sound while studying in Norway, and XLR8R reports that the first synthesizer he ever bought, a MicroKorg, came from a local music shop there around 2007. A [Bucharest club listing](https://zilesinopti.ro/evenimente/personnal-w-vlad-caia-iuly-b-personne-control-club/) says he studied Digital Media Technology in Oslo and was selected for the Red Bull Music Academy in London in 2010. In a Fabric interview with Cristi Cons, undated on the page, Cons says the pair met in 2006 and slowly started working as producers before settling on the name SIT.

## Sound and Style

[XLR8R's Studio Essentials](https://xlr8r.com/features/a-deep-dive-into-the-present-and-history-of-romanian-house-and-techno/) with Caia describes a deliberately minimal studio built around a few computers and a handful of instruments, including a Moog Sub 37, a DSI Tempest, an Elektron Model:Samples and a Eurorack system. His recordings are improvised: he programs drums and synths, hits record to capture a take of 15 to 30 minutes, then spends a long time editing and testing arrangements on club systems. Bandcamp Daily says he favours a compact process and has a mastery of Ableton, Bitwig and Max/MSP.

## Labels and Releases

Caia helped form [Amphia Records](https://amphiarecords.bandcamp.com), the Bucharest label he runs with Cristi Cons, and Discogs lists him as its co-owner and as a mastering engineer for Composure Audio Works. The Amphia catalogue on Discogs shows his early EPs *Projections* (2012) and *Codex* (2014), followed by the two parts of *Division*.

[Division I](https://amphiarecords.bandcamp.com/album/division-i) came out on 20 June 2018 as Amphia 14. It has five tracks, running from 6 to 9 minutes: "Division," "Sidepoint," "Give Receive," "Border Patrol" and "Interstice." The label's notes say he wrote and produced it, Mike Grinser mastered it at Manmade Mastering, and the structure is symmetrical, opening with dynamic harmonies, settling into a calmer middle section and ending dynamically again. Bandcamp Daily calls the album "Rominimal at its funkiest" and describes laser-like melodies mingling with sharp sequencing. *Division II* followed in 2019, according to Discogs.

## Career Highlights

Caia is half of SIT with Cons, whose 2019 album *Invisibility* Bandcamp Daily highlights, and one third of Amorf with Cons and the pianist Mischa Blanos, a project XLR8R says began in a January 2016 studio session. SIT is described by XLR8R as one of the few Bucharest acts to achieve sustained international success. Amorf's music appeared publicly in May 2016, when Rhadoo played a piano track at Sunwaves, XLR8R reports.

[Electronic Groove](https://electronicgroove.com/sunwaves-35-a-farewell-to-mamaia-the-final-romanian-chapter/) listed SIT (Cristi Cons and Vlad Caia) among the artists at SW35, the last Romanian edition of Sunwaves, held from 1 to 5 May 2025; our [Sunwaves festival page](/festivals/sunwaves) tells the festival's story. A further link to [Raresh](/artists/raresh) runs through Amphia: an Xceed bio for Cons describes the *Numiris* EP as a collaboration between Caia, Raresh and Cons under the Verico name, which Discogs lists as Amphia's fifth release, in 2013.

## FAQ

### How old is Vlad Caia?

Vlad Caia has not published his date of birth in any source we found, so we do not give an age. A club listing says he was selected for the Red Bull Music Academy in 2010, and Cristi Cons says they met in 2006.

### Where is Vlad Caia from?

Vlad Caia is Romanian and based in Bucharest, according to Discogs. Bandcamp Daily says he discovered the Rominimal sound while studying in Norway.

### What genre does Vlad Caia play?

Vlad Caia plays minimal house and techno in the Romanian minimal tradition, with a studio method built on improvised recordings. Bandcamp Daily calls his debut album Rominimal at its funkiest.

### What is Vlad Caia's real name?

Vlad Caia is the name he records and performs under. No separate legal or stage name is published in the sources we found.

### What is Division I?

*Division I* is Vlad Caia's debut full-length, released on 20 June 2018 by Amphia Records as Amphia 14. It has five tracks and, according to the label, a symmetrical structure.

### What is Amphia Records?

Amphia Records is a Bucharest label co-founded by Cristi Cons and Vlad Caia. Its catalogue runs from Cons's *Traversa* EP in 2011 to releases by SIT, Amorf, Cezar Lazar and Dan Andrei.

### Who are SIT?

SIT, short for Sideways Invisibility Theory, is the duo of Vlad Caia and Cristi Cons. Their 2019 album *Invisibility* is highlighted by Bandcamp Daily, and Electronic Groove listed them on the SW35 bill in May 2025.
