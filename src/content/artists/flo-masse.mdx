Flo Massé is a French-Brazilian DJ and former drummer, described by Time Out Bangkok as now based in Barcelona. She is known for vinyl-driven sets that mix progressive house, trance, acid and electro. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 10 November 2025, has 48,550 YouTube views as of 24 November 2026.

## Where is Flo Massé from and how did she start?

Flo Massé is French-Brazilian, according to Refuge Worldwide, the Dimensions Festival mix page as mirrored on set79, and set79's own artist page. Refuge says she started in 2018 as part of INTIMA, a house label in Rio de Janeiro. Her early work was in Brazil, in Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo.

Refuge's profile says she joined the Nobii Records crew in São Paulo as a resident in 2021, joined the 4Finest Ears party collective in Rio as a resident the same year, and launched her own label, Pulso, in Rio. The page was written around the summer of 2022 and gives no birth details, so we know her early path only through her own promotional copy.

Several pages describe her as a drummer. Refuge says that background brings the intensity and continuous groove of drumming into the booth, and the Dimensions mix page calls her a former drummer. Set79 and Time Out Bangkok say she is now based in Barcelona, though none of the pages we opened says when she moved or where she grew up. We could not find her real name, birth date or birthplace in any page we opened.

## What does Flo Massé sound like?

Her sound is percussive and built around rhythm. Set79 describes vinyl-driven sets of progressive house, trance, acid and electro, and Refuge adds breakbeat to that list. Time Out Bangkok calls her Brazil's "trance queen" and says her vinyl sets have travelled across the Americas, Europe and Asia.

Her Dimensions mix, DIM299, is a useful reference. The page describes it as percussive and full of unexpected turns, drawing on house, prog and trance. Its partial tracklist includes Michael Kruck's "Transposer" and Rannie S's "Lucid Dream", and Franco Battiato's "Meccanica" at around 44 minutes, but most of the tracks are marked unknown. Set79 presents the mix as a preview of her Dimensions debut and does not give its release date.

## What are the career highlights of Flo Massé?

### Boiler Room and European touring

Time Out Bangkok lists a Boiler Room set in 2022. Refuge's page, written during her first European summer, says she toured Europe from June to August, with dates in Paris, Berlin, London, Bordeaux and Lyon, before returning to Brazil for a first Boiler Room in São Paulo on 13 August. It does not state the year, but Time Out dates the Boiler Room to 2022. For wider context on the platform, see our report on the [Boiler Room KKR boycott](/blog/boiler-room-kkr-boycott).

### Trommel and Dimensions

Trommel included her in its 2024 "New Year's Resolution" list of 12 must-see artists on the rise. The write-up mentions her Boiler Room São Paulo set and two long European tours, and does not give dates. Time Out Bangkok says she has played [Dimensions Festival](/festivals/dimensions-festival), which fits the DIM299 mix, although we could not find the year of the festival set itself.

### Recorded sets

Set79 lists nine recorded sets for her. Dated entries include Androgyne x SUPER Daronne in Nantes on 24 June 2023, HORS-SOL with Germain Cls on 28 March 2024 and a set called NOTURNA dated 20 March 2026. Other entries include Hâws mix 098, a Trommel InSession numbered 100, a themuddshow session with Louison for Alto Artists, and a set labelled as live at Mimo Festival 2025. Refuge adds that she has recorded sets for Hâws, Conspiracy Music, Fraise Records and Casa'96.

### Bangkok, September 2025

A Time Out Bangkok listing, published on 24 September 2025, advertises a Flo Massé DJ set at Beamcube on 27 September, from 9pm, alongside Gaspray. The page does not print the year, but the publication date points to 2025.

## Which labels is Flo Massé associated with?

- **INTIMA.** The Rio de Janeiro house label she started with in 2018, per Refuge.
- **Nobii Records.** A São Paulo crew she joined as a resident in 2021, per Refuge.
- **Pulso.** A label she launched in Rio de Janeiro in 2021, according to Refuge.
- **Campana Records.** Set79 says she runs this label, and that her debut track "Prog Ritual" came out in 2025.

Refuge names Pulso and set79 names Campana Records, and we could not tell whether these are the same project, two labels or a later change. We have not seen a catalogue for either.
