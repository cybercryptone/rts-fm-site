Octal Industries is the dub techno project of Icelandic producer Jónas Thor Gudmundsson, who lives and works in Tallinn, Estonia. It began as a duo in the mid-2000s and later became his solo vehicle. Yoyaku published his 74-minute instore session on YouTube on 12 April 2024, and the shop also stocked his 2019 album *Our Seasons*.

## Where is Octal Industries from and how did he start?

Octal Industries is Icelandic in origin. A bio reproduced on Recorder.13's podcast page says the project was founded in 2003 by Gudmundsson and Mike Sickinger, founder of the Octal label, and that their debut was the *Automatik* EP on Transistor Rhythm. Other sources date the start to 2004: the Verdant Recordings page for *Our Seasons* says Gudmundsson started the project with Sickinger in 2004, and Gudmundsson's own 2023 interview with Fifteen Questions says the same year. We therefore give both years.

Gudmundsson also records as Ruxpin, according to Verdant. The Recorder.13 bio says he released a split 12" with Deepchord and then formed the trio Spectral Network, which put out a limited run of 111 copies. After a break, he went solo for a French label, which led to the *Meeting of the Waves* LP.

He came up through the Icelandic scene around Thule Records. Fellow Icelander Ohm told inn8 in November 2023 that he met Jonas at Thule's studios and that Jonas was two years younger and regarded as a prodigy. We found no birth year or birthplace for Gudmundsson, so we do not state an age.

## Where is Octal Industries based?

Recorder.13 and Volumo both say he is Icelandic but works and lives in Tallinn. Ohm repeated this in an Etui Records podcast, saying Jonas lives in Tallinn while he is in Copenhagen. In his 2023 Fifteen Questions interview, Gudmundsson described moving around often and spending time in Copenhagen, where he reconnected with Ohm.

## What does Octal Industries sound like?

Verdant's blurb for *Our Seasons* ties the album to Icelandic landscapes and shifting tempos. Yoyaku's shop listing calls it dub-infused techno with a colder, glacial atmosphere. A Trommel premiere of the Ohm collaboration "Weather Report" describes dub techno with crystalline synth sweeps and ethereal pads, built by stripping away channels until the chords carried the track.

Gudmundsson told Fifteen Questions that he has also worked in electronic pop, IDM and shoegaze, and that those experiences feed his dub techno. Volumo tags the project across dub techno, deep house and melodic techno.

## What are the career highlights of Octal Industries?

### Early years and the Entropy album

The Fifteen Questions interview mentions limited vinyl from the early period, including a split 12" with Deepchord in 2006. Apple Music dates *Meeting of the Waves*, on Entropy Records, to 12 March 2013. Recorder.13 adds that dubtechnoblog.com named his G.R.I.T. remix the best remix of 2012, a claim we could not check at the source.

### Our Seasons on Vertex

*Our Seasons* came out on 14 December 2019 as Vertex 001, the first release of a label collaboration between Verdant Recordings and Exalt Records. The ten-track double LP was mixed and mastered by José Diogo Neves. A remix double 12", *Changing Seasons* (Vertex 002), followed on 1 July 2020, with reworks by ASC, G.R.I.T., Mike Schommer and Ohm, plus a self-remix of "Forever".

### How does Octal Industries work with Ohm?

Ohm is his most frequent partner. Ohm told Etui Records that they had made more than ten EPs together, mostly over the internet by swapping loops. Trommel reported in April 2019 that they first met through Thule and had known each other for nearly 20 years, and that Jonas handled most of the mixdown while Bjarnar led the arrangement. Fifteen Questions credits their first joint release to Thule, the *Sedna* EP, and adds that they had not yet played live together as of mid-2023.

Their first full album, *Northwest Passage*, came out on Lempuyang on 20 October 2023 with eight tracks and artwork by Andrei Kedrin. Ohm said in 2023 that they have worked together since 2015, while Trommel in 2019 placed the start around 2012, after Jonas moved to Denmark. The sources disagree, so we treat the start as somewhere between 2012 and 2015.

Their *Weather Report* appeared on a Luck of Access release, LOA003, premiered by Trommel on 15 April 2019. Luck of Access is run by [Andrey Pushkarev](/artists/andrey-pushkarev), whose page on this site lists the pair on the roster. Trommel also names Rawax among their labels. Our guide to [Rawax and Robert Drewek](/blog/rawax-records-robert-drewek) covers that label.

## Which labels is Octal Industries associated with?

- **Transistor Rhythm.** Released the debut *Automatik* EP, per Recorder.13.
- **Entropy Records.** The French label behind *Meeting of the Waves*, 2013.
- **Vertex (Verdant and Exalt).** *Our Seasons*, 2019, and *Changing Seasons*, 2020.
- **Kontakt Records.** Recorder.13 says it released the *Julia Sets* EP, and Volumo lists a May 2026 release there.
- **Thule Records.** The Icelandic label where he met Ohm and where their first EP appeared.
- **Lempuyang.** Home of *Northwest Passage*.
- **Luck of Access and Rawax.** Collaborations with Ohm, per Trommel.
- **Vade Mecum and XOZ.** Named by Gudmundsson as homes for other work with Ohm.

## What is Octal Industries' Yoyaku instore session?

Octal Industries' Yoyaku instore session, titled "Yoyaku instore session with Octal Industries live", was published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 12 April 2024. It runs 74.1 minutes, a little longer than the channel's median session of about 65 minutes. As of 24 November 2026 it had 56,578 views, 1,815 likes and 137 comments, which ranks it 43rd by views among the 178 Yoyaku instore sessions we counted. ## FAQ

### Where is Octal Industries from?

He is Icelandic, according to Recorder.13, Volumo and Fifteen Questions. He is based in Tallinn, Estonia, per Recorder.13 and Volumo.

### Who is Octal Industries?

It is the dub techno project of Jónas Thor Gudmundsson, who also records as Ruxpin. It began as a duo with Mike Sickinger and later became a solo project.

### When did Octal Industries start?

Sources differ. Recorder.13 and Volumo say 2003, while Verdant and Gudmundsson himself say 2004.
