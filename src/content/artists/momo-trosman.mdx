Momo Trosman is the DJ name of Jerónimo Turek Trosman, an Argentine DJ born in Buenos Aires in 1991 who has played professionally since 2011. He is known for vinyl-led sets and for co-running Cure Music and Melcure. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 18 July 2025, has 65,675 YouTube views.

## Where is Momo Trosman from and how did he start?

XLR8R's July 2018 "Hi-Five" feature gives his full name as Jerónimo Turek Trosman and says he was born in Buenos Aires in 1991. Data Transmission, in a June 2018 piece, gives the same birth year and city and says he started DJing professionally at the end of 2011. Neither source gives a birth date, so he is 34 or 35 in November 2026, depending on his birthday.

XLR8R says he has played clubs around the world, including four European tours by 2018. A Mixmag Spain interview published on 8 July 2024, written during a European tour, calls him an Argentine DJ with about 15 years in the underground and says he has also played in Uruguay, Ecuador, the USA and Central America. The same interview says he works as an economist alongside music. We found no account of his first records, first club nights or musical upbringing.

## What does Momo Trosman sound like?

XLR8R says his natural inclinations are dub and jazz but that he plays what moves him. It adds that he studied electronic music's sub-genres closely and has built a vinyl collection that covers their past, present and future. Data Transmission also says he prefers vinyl and has widened his collection through travel, which lets him change direction within a set.

Among the records he named for XLR8R were Larry Heard's "Night Images", Aphex Twin's "CIRKLON3" and Cabaret Voltaire's "I Want You", a spread that fits the genre-blind description. HÖR's listing for his July 2024 set tags it as minimal house, tech house and liquid house, which is the closest thing we found to a current genre label. Those tags come from the broadcaster, not from him.

## What are the career highlights of Momo Trosman?

### Cure Music and Melcure

In late 2015 he and the Chilean DJ and producer Felipe Valenzuela started Cure Music, according to Data Transmission. A December 2015 interview in Buenos Aliens describes a vinyl-only label with no digital sales, no promo copies and no reissues. Its first record, the Fortu EP by the French duo Zendid, was pressed in 350 copies. XLR8R says it sold out in three days.

Mixmag Spain says Melcure was founded in 2017 by Valenzuela, Dani Casarano and Trosman, and that it grew out of Cure Music and the Melisma project run by Valenzuela and Casarano. The two were merged during OFF WEEK 2017. Abscal, a friend of the team, joined later. The label works between Berlin and Buenos Aires. Mixmag says it runs events at Club der Visionaere in summer and Hoppetosse in winter in Berlin, and a monthly night at Avant Garten in Buenos Aires, with more than 24 editions at the time of writing. Our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide) covers the Berlin venues.

### Festivals and shows

Both XLR8R and Data Transmission list him on the bill of the first Moonland Festival, held from 3 to 5 August 2018 at the Rummu quarry in Estonia. Both articles were written before the event, so we cannot confirm his set from them. [Time Warp](/festivals/time-warp)'s artist page records one appearance for him, in 2016. HÖR's page lists a Melcure set dated 3 July 2024, which had 15.6 thousand views when we checked.

## Which labels is Momo Trosman associated with?

He is tied to two labels. Cure Music, started with Valenzuela in 2015, released limited vinyl only. Melcure is the label that followed. Its Bandcamp page describes it as a hybrid label based in Berlin and curated by Valenzuela, Casarano, Trosman and Abscal. It lists a TRAX series that runs to Volume 14, three Edits volumes and a "3 Years of Melcure" compilation, but gives no release dates. Trommel's October 2021 premiere of MELCURE007 says the label returned to vinyl after a run of Bandcamp releases in the TRAX series, and that the crew is split between Buenos Aires, Chile and Berlin. It names Valenzuela as the curator. We could not find a source that lists his own productions on either label.
