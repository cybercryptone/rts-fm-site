Black Loops is the house music project of Riccardo Paffetti, an Italian producer, drummer and DJ who lives in Berlin. He is known for warm, funk and disco leaning house on Freerange and Toy Tonics, and for the 2025 album *Always Moving*. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 10 July 2024, has nearly 99,000 YouTube views.

## Where is Black Loops from and how did he start?

Black Loops is Riccardo Paffetti, according to Yoyaku, 5mag and the 15 Questions interview. He is Italian. 5mag says he was raised in Arezzo, a small town in Tuscany, and a 2021 review on We Hate Music mentions a solo set he played there. The Ransom Note, in an undated interview, quotes him speaking of the Tuscan hills of his homeland and says he splits his time between Italy and Berlin. That article spells his surname "Parfetti", while every other source we opened uses Paffetti.

His route into house music came through the drums. 5mag says he was a session drummer and a member of the band Thank You For The Drum Machine from 2006 to 2012, and that he moved to Berlin in early 2013 while working closely with Gomma Records, now Toy Tonics. In a May 2025 interview 15 Questions describes him as coming from a drumming background and living in Berlin for about 13 years, which fits that timeline.

Black Loops did not begin as a solo project. Yoyaku's artist page says it started as a duo with Gabriele Micheli and that Paffetti has run it alone since 2017. When We Dip, in April 2015, introduced it as two Italians based in Berlin. We could not find a source that says why the duo ended.

## What does Black Loops sound like?

Volumo describes his influences as funk, soul and disco, with a sound comparable to 1990s UK house and early 2000s New York house. Gina Loves Jazz, reviewing *Always Moving*, calls his style house and deep house with a breezy feel, and

Drums remain the core of the approach. 15 Questions quotes him saying rhythm is where he starts a track and that he rarely quantizes his beats. In the same interview he lists hardware including a Boss DR-202, a Roland MC-909 and a Prophet 6, alongside software such as Omnisphere.

## What are the career highlights of Black Loops?

### Toy Tonics years

When We Dip says the duo's early releases came on Exposure and Gomma, followed by Audiojack's Gruuv imprint, and that they returned to Toy Tonics in April 2015 with an EP led by "Suki", after a "Lovelite" release the previous September. A July 2016 premiere on the same site placed the track "Sex" on a four-track Toy Tonics various artists release dated 25 July 2016. 5mag says he has made seven EPs for Toy Tonics.

### Freerange and the wider catalogue

Freerange became his main home. When We Dip reports that the *7Hills* EP in 2018 was among the label's biggest recent vinyl sellers and that *Something Special* followed on 20 March 2020. Per 5mag, *These Changes* with Cody Currie was his third Freerange release. When We Dip's 2020 piece also lists Madhouse, Neovinyl and Traxx Underground among his labels, and says he had remixed Joey Negro and played in South Africa, Australia, the US, Asia and across Europe. We Hate Music reviewed the *Circulation* EP on Shall Not Fade in March 2021.

### Always Moving

*Always Moving*, his first full length album, came out on Freerange. 15 Questions gives 9 May 2025 as the release date, while the distributor Aboveboard lists 16 May 2025 for the double 12 inch, catalogue FRLP43. Gina Loves Jazz says most tracks were played live in the studio and that guests include Marlena Dae on vocals, Byron the Aquarius and Harvey Sutherland.

## Which labels is Black Loops associated with?

- **Freerange.** The label of Jimpster, home of *7Hills*, *Something Special*, *These Changes* and *Always Moving*. Apple Music also lists *Freerange Records, In The Mix 032: Black Loops* as a 2025 DJ mix.
- **Toy Tonics.** The Berlin label tied to Gomma, with seven EPs according to 5mag and Volumo.
- **Aterral.** Yoyaku lists his *Higher Remixed* on Aterral. 15 Questions says he shares a studio with Carlo of Aterral Records, and Yoyaku lists Carlo's *Versus* as featuring Black Loops.
- **Shall Not Fade.** Released the *Circulation* EP in 2021.
- **Tartelet Records.** Released *Utopia* in 2026.

## Where is Black Loops based, and what is not verified?

Every source we opened that gives a base says Berlin, including Yoyaku, Bandcamp, Gina Loves Jazz and Volumo. The Ransom Note adds that he divides his time with Italy, so we treat Berlin as his main base. For the city's venues, see our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide).

We could not verify a birth date or year, a birthplace, or the dates of his first Black Loops releases with Gabriele Micheli. Sources disagree on whether Black Loops is a solo name or a duo: Yoyaku and When We Dip describe the duo era, while later pieces credit Paffetti alone, and one 2021 review calls the name his moniker.
