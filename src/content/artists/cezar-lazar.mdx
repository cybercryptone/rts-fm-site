Cezar Lazăr is the quiet infrastructure of the Romanian scene. As a producer he is known simply as Cezar, but he is just as much a label owner and distributor, running Understand, managing Algorithm of Unknown and sharing Our Own, the distribution and booking company, with Rhadoo.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Little is published about his early life, and no date or place of birth appears in the sources we could open. [XLR8R's history of Romanian house and techno](https://xlr8r.com/features/a-deep-dive-into-the-present-and-history-of-romanian-house-and-techno/) gives his name as Cezar Lazar, a.k.a. El Cezere or Cezar, and describes him as soft-spoken and one of the oldest members of the group around the agency Sunrise, who joined after the first Sunrise artists. It says he described the early community as blank canvases and sponges absorbing new music. [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/2283688) calls him a DJ, producer and recording and mixing engineer based in Bucharest. [Bandcamp Daily's 2026 scene report](https://daily.bandcamp.com/scene-report/romanian-minimal-techno-scene-report) adds that he is a member of the band Prismatic.

## Sound and Style

Bandcamp Daily says Cezar "balances approachability with the avant-garde." That fits a catalogue that runs from solo EPs to live band recordings: Discogs lists *Prismatic Live* on Algorithm of Unknown in 2024 and the album *Deepak* in 2023, both credited to him alongside several collaborators. Understand, meanwhile, is described by Discogs as an experimental techno label, with a parallel Live Series for more acoustic and electronic crossbreeds.

## The Label Story

Understand started in 2011 with Cezar's own *Din Gând În Când EP*, according to Discogs, and ourown.ro dates the label to 2011 as well. Its early catalogue was small and focused. [Rhadoo](/artists/rhadoo) released *Arhiva EP* on Understand in 2012, Prâslesh's *Out of Time EP* came out the same year, and Discogs lists Dubtil's *Ebandogue EP* in 2015, Amorf's *Blending Light* and Arapu's *Wasted Ego* in 2017. XLR8R named the Amorf record as available on Cezar's label.

Our Own is the business side. XLR8R says Cezar runs it as a distributor and booking agency founded with Rhadoo, and Bandcamp Daily calls it a vinyl distribution service. The Our Own site's [labels page](https://ourown.ro/labels.html) lists a stable that includes [a:rpia:r] (2007), Understand and Amphia (both 2011) and several newer imprints, which makes it a map of how much of the Romanian underground funnels through one small operation. Bandcamp Daily also says Cezar manages Algorithm of Unknown. For the wider context, see our [history of Romanian minimal techno](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno).

## Career Highlights

- *Archetypes*, a double EP released in 2018 on Amphia Records in Bucharest, per Bandcamp Daily. Discogs gives the catalogue number AMP015, 180 gram vinyl and a release date of 9 April 2018.
- *The Balancing Act EP* on Amphia in 2016, according to Discogs.
- Co-founder of Our Own with Rhadoo, per XLR8R.
- Owner of Understand, and manager of Algorithm of Unknown.
- Member of the band Prismatic, with a live album on Algorithm of Unknown in 2024, per Bandcamp Daily and Discogs.

His closest professional links are within the Arpiar family. His labels sit beside the one that [Raresh](/artists/raresh), Rhadoo and [Petre Inspirescu](/artists/petre-inspirescu) run.

## FAQ

### Where is Cezar Lazăr from?

Cezar Lazăr is Romanian and based in Bucharest, according to Discogs. His exact birthplace is not published.

### What genre does Cezar Lazăr play?

Cezar plays minimal techno and electronic music in the Romanian style. Discogs describes his label Understand as experimental techno, and Bandcamp Daily says his music balances approachability with the avant-garde.

### What is Cezar Lazăr's real name?

No distinct real name is public. He records under his own name, Cezar Lazăr, shortened to Cezar. XLR8R lists him as Cezar Lazar, a.k.a. El Cezere or Cezar, and Discogs credits releases to Lazăr Cezar and Cezar Lazăr.

### What labels does Cezar Lazăr run?

He runs Understand and manages Algorithm of Unknown, according to Bandcamp Daily and XLR8R. He also manages the vinyl distributor Our Own, which XLR8R says he founded with Rhadoo.

### What is Archetypes by Cezar Lazăr?

Archetypes is a double EP released in 2018 on Amphia Records in Bucharest, according to Bandcamp Daily. Discogs lists it as AMP015, a pressing on 180 gram vinyl.

### What is Our Own?

Our Own is a Romanian distributor and booking agency founded by Cezar Lazăr and Rhadoo, according to XLR8R. Its website lists labels including Understand, Amphia and Arpiar.
