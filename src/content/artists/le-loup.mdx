Le Loup is the Paris DJ and producer Léonard Perret, known for the Hold Youth project with Seuil, his Shadow Play label and a long-running Rex Club residency. Since 2025 he has also released as AV1 with Chris Carrier. Yoyaku Record Store has two instore sessions with him on YouTube, from 2016 and 2025.

## Where is Le Loup from and how did they start?

Le Loup is French and Paris-based. In an interview with SAY WHAT Mag he gave his real name as Léonard Perret and said he has always lived in Paris or its suburbs. The same name appears in the credits for the 2025 AV1 EP *Lifeforce*, which is how we connect the DJ to the releases below. The interview is undated, but it refers to a second lockdown, which suggests late 2020.

He told SAY WHAT that friends started calling him "Leo Le Loup" at about 13, after he and his best friend began listening to house music, and the name stuck. He has been producing and playing for over a decade. A Colectivo Futuro podcast interview, which also gives his name as Léonard Perret and seems to date from around 2010, says his remix of Nicolas Jaar's "The Student" reached the Wolf + Lamb label through [Nicolas Jaar](/artists/nicolas-jaar) himself. Trommel Music dates that remix to 2009. XLR8R reported in 2017 that Wolf + Lamb signed him before his 21st birthday for his debut EP, *See Line*. We could not find a release year for it, and no source we opened gives a birth year, so we do not state an age.

## What does Le Loup sound like?

XLR8R called him one of the standout names of a new wave of French minimal talent, with warm, groove-led productions. Time Out Paris, in a September 2014 listing, described a house style that draws on jazz, funk and hip-hop and a love of Detroit techno. In the Colectivo Futuro interview he named 1980s and 1990s music, jazz, disco and hip-hop as influences and stressed sampling.

The AV1 records are different. Lumbago's Bandcamp page describes the *Lifeforce* EP as trancy tech house, dark techno beats and a slowed, electro-leaning B-side, and Yoyaku files the *Goapana* EP under neo trance, progressive house and trance at 128 to 133 BPM.

## What are the career highlights of Le Loup?

### Hold Youth with Seuil

Hold Youth is the joint project of Le Loup and Seuil. Trommel Music says they met in 2009 and began making Hold Youth tracks in 2010, while XLR8R says they co-founded the vinyl-only label in 2011. Its debut double LP, *De La Club*, carries the catalogue number HYLP001 and a release date of 14 May 2014 on the shop Oye Records. The listing describes a first half that looks back at Detroit and 1990s French house, and a darker second half of German minimal and micro house. XLR8R says the label has also released [Rick Wade](/artists/rick-wade) and S3A.

### Residencies and parties

XLR8R and Trommel both place Le Loup and Seuil as Rex Club residents in Paris, and XLR8R says Hold Youth also plays fabric in London. SAY WHAT lists residencies at Rex Club, Badaboum and the London party Half Baked. Time Out Paris listed Circle, his residency with Mouloud at Badaboum, on 26 September 2014, as best we can infer from the listing's publication date.

### Shadow Play and AV1

In 2016 he launched Shadow Play with Pura, according to SAY WHAT. Its first record, SP001L, was his solo *The Ancient Ways EP*, dated 15 May 2017 by Soundshelter. SAY WHAT says the label has also put out Scott Edward, Dawl, Daïf and Chris Carrier, and Trommel reports it reissued unreleased Scott Edward material on vinyl.

Chris Carrier's Bandcamp lists a sold-out vinyl, *Red Eyes Troopers*, credited to Chris Carrier and Le Loup, without a label or year. In 2025 the pair began working as AV1. Trommel's premiere of *Lifeforce* calls AV1 a new project of the two, and Lumbago's page calls them Parisian producers with a long career. Yoyaku's *Goapana* page, dated 2024, says AV1 follows an earlier EP on Alt.

## Which labels is Le Loup associated with?

Per the sources above: his own Hold Youth and Shadow Play, plus Wolf + Lamb and Life and Death, where he reworked dOP's "After Party" (XLR8R). SAY WHAT names SUR, Lumbago, BinarySound and Cabaret Recordings, and Colectivo Futuro names Eklo. As AV1 he has appeared on City Of 3000, Metallic States (the Lumbago sublabel) and ALT Records, a sublabel of Cartulis Music. Yoyaku sells these records, and our [guide to the best record shops in Europe](/blog/best-record-shops-in-europe-2026) covers the shop, but none is a Yoyaku label release.

## FAQ

### How old is Le Loup?

We could not find a birth year or date in any source we opened, so we do not state an age. XLR8R says he was signed to Wolf + Lamb before turning 21, but gives no year.

### Where is Le Loup from?

Le Loup is French and lives in Paris. He told SAY WHAT he has always lived in Paris or its suburbs, and he currently lives in the east of the city.

### Who is Le Loup?

Le Loup is the stage name of Léonard Perret, a Paris DJ and producer. He co-runs Hold Youth with Seuil, runs Shadow Play with Pura and records as AV1 with Chris Carrier.

### What is Le Loup's real name?

Léonard Perret, according to SAY WHAT Mag and Colectivo Futuro. The 2025 *Lifeforce* credits also give the music to Léonard Perret and Chris Carrier.

### What is Le Loup's Yoyaku instore session?

There are two on YouTube. One was published on 17 July 2016 and runs 110 minutes. The other, with Chris Carrier as AV1, was published on 25 June 2025 and runs about 91.6 minutes. They have 21,654 and 30,839 views as of 24 November 2026.

### What is AV1?

AV1 is a project of Chris Carrier and Le Loup. Its records include *Goapana* on City Of 3000, *Lifeforce* on Metallic States and *Waves + Plants Pt. 2* on ALT Records.

### Which labels does Le Loup run?

Hold Youth, with Seuil, and Shadow Play, with Pura. Hold Youth is vinyl only, according to XLR8R.

### Does Le Loup release on Yoyaku?

Yoyaku sells his AV1 records, but they are on other labels. We found no release by him on a Yoyaku label.
