Vakula is the stage name of Mikhaylo Vityk, a Ukrainian DJ and producer from Konotop who works in deep house, techno and dub techno and runs the Leleka and Bandura labels. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 23 September 2017, runs 88 minutes and has 30,027 views.

## Where is Vakula from and how did he start?

Vakula is from Konotop, a small town in northern Ukraine. The Amsterdam Dance Event profile calls him Konotop-born and gives his name as Mikhaylo Vityk. Yoyaku's artist page and Partyflock use the same name, and Bandcamp lists his location as Konotop. Resident Advisor spells the surname Vityuk in two news items, so the transliteration varies between outlets.

The name Vakula comes from a character in a novel by Gogol, according to Fact, which says Vityk calls the character a national hero of Ukraine. Fact also reports that his track titles often nod to the painter Kazimir Malevich and to his hometown. Partyflock's biography says he draws on his grandfather, a traditional instrumentalist and bandura player, and blends Ukrainian and Slavic influences with a Detroit-style sound.

Fact says he has released dozens of singles and EPs since 2010, under Vakula and other aliases. Partyflock and ADE both say he gained international recognition in 2008 through releases on Meakusma and Dekmantel. The two dates do not match, and we could not find an earlier release to settle it, so we treat 2010 as the safer start. Beat Magazine, in July 2012, counted more than 24 twelve-inch records and two albums.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year. One record-shop listing gave a different hometown and a decade of birth, but it was inconsistent with every other source and we did not use it.

## What does Vakula sound like?

His early records drew on Detroit house and soulful sample collage, which Fact compares to [Moodymann](/artists/moodymann). Fact's review of his first Firecracker album describes a mix of synth abstraction, jazz fusion, new age, broken beat and ambient interludes, with house as the common thread. If the Detroit angle is new to you, our [Detroit techno guide](/blog/detroit-techno-guide-2026-clubs-labels-history) covers the background.

Partyflock lists his genres as deep house, disco, electro, funk, house and jazz, and says he works with analogue synths, drum machines and vinyl. The Meakusma festival biography says his DJ sets move across techno, dub techno, deep house, jazz, funk, disco and psychedelic rock, and that he calls the mixer a musical instrument for a DJ. It also says his Ukrainian studio holds rare analogue gear and folk instruments, including an Oberheim OB-8, a marimba and conga drums.

Later records moved in several directions. Resident Advisor described his 2016 Bandura 12-inch as dubby and contemplative, a shift from a more direct dancefloor EP he put out earlier that year on Arma. In 2017 it reported a techno album for Arma.

## What are the career highlights of Vakula?

### Early releases and Resident Advisor podcast, 2010 to 2012

Fact names Dekmantel, Firecracker, Shevchenko and his own Leleka among the labels he worked with. Partyflock says he was the first Ukrainian producer on Resident Advisor's podcast series, with a mix made of his own productions. Beat Magazine, on 24 July 2012, mentioned a recent RA podcast and a coming Melbourne date. Partyflock lists 34 past performances, the latest on 20 July 2018 at De Zomernacht in De School, Amsterdam, though its biography page was last updated in 2013 and does not claim to be a full record.

### Albums, 2012 to 2017

Resident Advisor reported in October 2014 that he had a 2012 LP on Dekmantel under the alias Vedomir. Fact reviewed "You've Never Been to Konotop", a collection of tracks made between 2009 and 2012, in June 2013. It came out on Firecracker and takes its title from his hometown.

In October 2014 Resident Advisor announced A Voyage To Arcturus on Leleka, calling it his third LP. Its 16 tracks are named after the chapters of David Lindsay's 1920 science fiction novel, and Vakula told RA it was closer to a soundtrack than a standard album. The announcement gave no release date.

Cyclicality Between Procyon And Gomeisa came out on Dekmantel in 2016. Resident Advisor's 2017 report on Techno Game, a seven-track album scheduled for 25 November 2017 on the Moscow-based Arma label, called it his fifth. Album counts differ between RA items, so we do not give a firm number.

## Which labels is Vakula associated with?

- **Leleka.** His own imprint. Partyflock calls it vinyl-only and says it draws on Ukrainian culture and folk music. Meakusma says he runs it for experimental music.
- **Bandura.** Another label of his own. Meakusma says it is for more club-oriented releases, and Yoyaku lists Acid Tools as BANDURA008. Resident Advisor said in September 2016 that a 12-inch called A was the first Bandura release since 2014.
- **Firecracker and Shevchenko.** Fact and Beat Magazine name both. Firecracker issued his 2013 album, and Partyflock says he took part in its Shevchenko sub-label, named after the poet Taras Shevchenko.
- **Dekmantel.** Fact lists it, and Resident Advisor says Cyclicality Between Procyon And Gomeisa came out there in 2016.
- **Arma.** Yoyaku lists 108 Mysteries as ARMA025, a double 12-inch, and RA places the label in Moscow.
- **Oath and Seven Villas Music.** Yoyaku lists Time as OATH029 and Cloud Bloom as 7V200, both on 12-inch.
- **Quintessentials.** A shop listing gives Night In Konotop City as a 2018 release on this label.

## What is Vakula doing in 2026?

- **Meakusma Festival, 3 to 6 September 2026.** The festival's page lists him in its 2026 lineup in Eupen, Belgium, with the labels Leleka, Dekmantel and Firecracker. The page does not report the set, so we cannot say he played.
- **13 September 2024.** Bandcamp dates the Dinsync EP, with LG, to this day. It is his most recent dated release we could open. The page does not name the label.
- **Oath EP.** Yoyaku lists Time on Oath, and a news search turned up an article on a four-track hardware-led EP for the same label. We could not open that article or find a release date, so we do not date it.

We found no confirmed 2026 releases or tour dates beyond these. His Bandcamp page says nothing more than a one-line description and lists no upcoming dates.

## FAQ

We could not verify his age. None of the pages we opened gives a birth year or date. Fact says he had been releasing records since 2010.

### Where is Vakula from?

He is from Konotop, a small town in northern Ukraine, according to ADE, Partyflock and Fact. Bandcamp also gives Konotop as his location.

### Who is Vakula?

Vakula is a Ukrainian DJ and producer working in deep house, techno and dub techno. He runs the Leleka and Bandura labels and has released on Firecracker, Dekmantel, Arma and Oath.

### What is Vakula's real name?

ADE, Partyflock, Fact and Yoyaku give it as Mikhaylo Vityk. Resident Advisor spells the surname Vityuk.

Fact says it is taken from a character in a Gogol novel, whom Vityk calls a national hero of Ukraine.
