Mike Banks, known as "Mad" Mike, is a Detroit techno producer and musician who co-founded [Underground Resistance](/artists/underground-resistance) with Jeff Mills in the late 1980s. He also co-owns Submerge, the Detroit distribution company, and plays live in Timeline, a band that pushes techno toward what it calls "high tech jazz".

## Who is Mike Banks and how did he start?

Michael Anthony Banks is from Detroit and lives there, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Banks_(musician)). No source we checked gives a birth date or year, so we do not state an age.

He began as a studio musician on bass and guitar and, per Wikipedia, played with Parliament and Funkadelic among others. In a [2018 Red Bull Music Academy lecture](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/mad-mike-banks-tresor-berlin-detroit-lecture/) in Berlin, he described learning how the industry worked through session work in Detroit studios.

Wikipedia's production list starts with singles by the vocal collective Members of the House in 1987 and 1988. He names [Derrick May](/artists/derrick-may) and [Juan Atkins](/artists/juan-atkins) as early influences, along with Marshall Jefferson, per the same page.

## What does Mike Banks sound like?

Wikipedia says his later work shows growing interest in acid house and industrial, while the UR catalogue he steered ranges from raw techno to electro.

Galaxy 2 Galaxy and Timeline take the opposite route. Wikipedia says he and Mills believed jazz musicians could work with the same "man machine" idea associated with Kraftwerk, which became the style called hi-tech jazz. In 2017, [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/rpm-adds-underground-resistances-mike-banks-and-timeline-to-body-soul-news) said Timeline describes itself as "high tech jazz", pairing live electronics with high-energy funk.

His politics run through the records. Wikipedia says his releases often carry social and political commentary, which made him controversial within Detroit's scene.

## What are the career highlights of Mike Banks?

### Underground Resistance and Tresor

Wikipedia credits Banks and [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) with founding UR in the late 1980s, with [Robert Hood](/artists/robert-hood) producing much of the early output. After Mills and Hood left, Banks led the label. Wikipedia lists his X-102 album *Discovers the Rings of Saturn* on Berlin's Tresor in 1992, and in the RBMA lecture he describes X-101 as a Tresor release.

### The mainstream and independence

UR's 1992 *Message to the Majors* is among its best-known statements against the major labels. In the 2018 RBMA lecture, Banks criticised corporate control of art and said music cannot be made into a formula. Wikipedia adds that he refuses to be photographed in public as part of an underground ethic.

### Drexciya

Wikipedia says that after Hood and Mills left, Banks released material by Drexciya through UR, and its Drexciya page lists three UR releases from 1993 to 1996. In the RBMA lecture he says Derrick May connected him with the duo and that he first sold their music in his shop. Wikipedia gives James Stinson's death as 3 September 2002.

### Submerge and Exhibit 3000

Wikipedia says Banks co-owns Submerge Distribution with Christa Robinson, dating it to 1992. RA's 2025 article gives 1982, so the year is inconsistent in sources. The [Music Origins Project](https://musicorigins.org/item/submerge-records/) quotes him in 1994 saying small Detroit labels needed one operation to handle billing and distribution. Submerge moved to 3000 East Grand Boulevard between 2000 and 2002, and that building also houses the Exhibit 3000 techno museum. Our [Detroit techno guide](/blog/detroit-techno-guide-2026-clubs-labels-history) covers visiting, and our [independent label study](/blog/independent-label-survival-study) notes UR still moves records through Submerge.

In a 2024 Roland short film, *Somewhere in Detroit*, [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/roland-docuseries-underground-resistance-mike-banks-news) said Banks called the underground "real" and criticised much current music as overproduced.

### Live bands: Timeline, Galaxy 2 Galaxy and Los Hermanos

Wikipedia says Galaxy 2 Galaxy began as a 1993 EP and was revived from 2005 as a live ensemble of UR members. Timeline's lineup in 2017 was Banks, Jon Dixon, DeSean Jones and Mark Flash, per Mixmag.

Of Los Hermanos, member Esteban Adame told [fabric](https://fabriclondon.com/posts/in-conversation-underground-resistance-cohort-esteban-adame-looks-back) that Banks wanted a group uniting Black and Latino musicians, in the spirit of WAR and Santana. Adame called Banks "the General" who oversaw it. Wikipedia adds that Banks quit live shows in the late 1990s over customs problems with equipment and resumed in the mid-2000s.

## Which labels is Mike Banks associated with?

- **Underground Resistance:** the label and collective he co-founded.
- **Submerge:** the distribution company he co-owns, home of the Somewhere in Detroit record shop and Exhibit 3000.
- **Red Planet:** the imprint for his releases as The Martian, per Wikipedia's production list.
- **Somewhere in Detroit:** the imprint of his 2006 release *Attack of the Sonic Samurai*.
- **Tresor:** the Berlin label of the X-102 albums.

## FAQ

### Who is Mike Banks?

Mike Banks, known as "Mad" Mike, is a Detroit techno producer who co-founded Underground Resistance with Jeff Mills. He also co-owns Submerge Distribution and plays in the live band Timeline.

### What is Mike Banks' real name?

Wikipedia gives his name as Michael Anthony Banks. He is best known as "Mad" Mike Banks.

### How old is Mike Banks?

We could not find a published birth date or year, and Wikipedia lists his year of birth as missing. For that reason, this page gives no age.

### Where is Mike Banks from?

He is from Detroit, Michigan, and lives there, per Wikipedia and his 2018 RBMA lecture. Submerge, UR's base, is at 3000 East Grand Boulevard in the city.

### Did Mike Banks found Underground Resistance?

Yes, with Jeff Mills in the late 1980s, per Wikipedia. Robert Hood produced much of the early output, and after he and Mills left, Banks led the label.

### What is Submerge?

Submerge is the Detroit distribution company that Banks co-owns, which handles UR and other labels. The building also holds the Somewhere in Detroit record shop and the Exhibit 3000 techno museum.

### What is Galaxy 2 Galaxy?

It began as Banks's 1993 EP that gave the hi-tech jazz style its name, per Wikipedia. From 2005 it was revived as a live group of UR members.

### What is Timeline?

Timeline is a live band with Banks, which Mixmag said in 2017 featured Jon Dixon, DeSean Jones and Mark Flash. It describes its sound as "high tech jazz" with high-energy funk.

### What was Mike Banks' connection to Drexciya?

Wikipedia says Banks released Drexciya's music through UR, and Drexciya's own page lists three UR releases between 1993 and 1996. Banks says Derrick May introduced him to the duo.

### Is Mike Banks still active?

Yes. Detroit Electronic Quarterly listed him for Tec-Troit in June 2026, and UR was billed for Dekmantel in July 2026, per RA.
