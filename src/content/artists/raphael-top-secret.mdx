Raphaël Top-Secret is a Paris-born DJ, record digger and producer who has lived in Brussels and Tokyo, known for his releases on Antinote and for co-compiling Uneven Paths for Music From Memory. His two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, from 2019 and 2023, have 21,554 and 6,067 views as of 24 November 2026.

## Where is Raphaël Top-Secret from and how did he start?

The Montreux Jazz Festival's artist page calls him a native of Paris and a French artist. It says he moved to Brussels in 2009, where he met the Dutch DJ Loud-E (CBS), described there as a protégé of I-F. Their friendship led to long record-digging sessions that, according to the festival, deepened his interest in music. The same page says he has adopted Brussels and Tokyo as home cities and organises parties and underground festivals in places such as São Paulo, Melbourne, Seoul and Vancouver. The set-listing site set79 also calls him Paris-born.

Dekmantel, which hosted him for its Selectors podcast, describes him as a French/Belgian selector with a reputation for digging in Parisian record shops and flea markets. It says he often played with Zaltan, the head of the Paris label Antinote, in sets that moved across Balearic, boogie, reggae, world music, disco and house. Antinote is the label most tied to his name, and Mixmag's 7 June 2018 feature on the new Parisian underground names him among the artists the label supports.

We could not find a birth date, a real name or a first gig date in any source we opened, so we do not state an age or a start year.

## What does Raphaël Top-Secret sound like?

Set79 summarises his sets as a mix of house, boogie, disco and Balearic music, with reggae, R&B and hip-hop turning up in some of the recordings it lists. His Dekmantel Selectors mix is a good example of the range. The label says it covers 1990s hip-hop and R&B, downtempo, house and tropical dance, mixing recent finds with long-time favourites. In the page's account he made it in two days, so the approach was more spontaneous than usual.

Retailer Rush Hour describes his 2016 Antinote EP as a blend of synthesised European music and tropical imagery, with a percussive ambient opener, a foggy drum machine jam and a spectral reggae closer.

## What are the career highlights of Raphaël Top-Secret?

### Antinote, 2016 and 2017

Antinote released his three-track 12 inch "Love So High" with Nelson Bishop in 2016 (catalogue ATN 028, listed by Rush Hour for week 20 of 2016). Resident Advisor reported on 8 July 2017 that he is one of three members of 18 Rays, alongside Zaltan (Quentin Vandewalle) and Nico Motte. Their self-titled 12 inch came out on Antinote in summer 2017. RA says the trio recorded it over five weeks in winter at Motte's Synth City studio in Paris, and that they call the sound aquatic dream pop.

### Uneven Paths, 2018

With Jamie Tiller of Music From Memory he compiled "Uneven Paths: Deviant Pop From Europe, 1980-1991". The Vinyl Factory announced it on 26 January 2018 for a mid-March release, describing a collection of twenty musicians that included Cada Día, Pete Brandt's Method and Härte 10. Release dates in retailer listings vary, so treat the exact day with care.

### Dekmantel 2018

Boiler Room's page for the Saturday of Dekmantel Festival 2018, on 4 August 2018, bills Tiller and Top-Secret as a crate-digging pair alongside Carista. Our [Dekmantel Festival guide](/festivals/dekmantel-festival) covers the event. Dekmantel's Selectors page says he was joining the organisers for the Selectors series and for [Lente Kabinet](/festivals/lente-kabinet) later in May, but it gives no year, and the page shows 3 July 2019, which does not fit "May", so we leave the year open.

### NTS and travelling sets

NTS's artist page says he first played on the station on 15 March 2016 and lists guest spots in Melbourne on 5 December 2017 and Vancouver on 27 August 2019. It also places him in Noncollective, which it describes as a pan-European DJ group with a taste for Balearic and eclectic music. Set79 lists a set at Discos Baratos in São Paulo on 30 August 2025 and a Refuge Worldwide show on 4 June 2025.

## Which labels is Raphaël Top-Secret associated with?

Antinote is the label tied to him in every source we could open: the 2016 EP, the 18 Rays record and the Mixmag mention all run through it. Music From Memory is the home of his compilation work with Tiller, though he is a compiler there rather than a label artist. Set79 also lists Rush Hour and Macadam Mambo against his name for original tracks and edits, but it does not say which release is on which label. The Rush Hour page we opened only lists the Antinote EP, so we cannot confirm a release on its own imprint.

## FAQ

### How old is Raphaël Top-Secret?

We do not know. None of the sources we opened gives a birth date or year, so we do not state an age.

### Where is Raphaël Top-Secret from?

The Montreux Jazz Festival calls him a native of Paris, and set79 calls him Paris-born. He moved to Brussels in 2009, according to the festival.

### Who is Raphaël Top-Secret?

He is a French DJ, record collector and producer known for digging sets, Antinote releases and the Uneven Paths compilation. He also records as part of the trio 18 Rays.

### Is Raphaël Top-Secret the same as Raphaël Top Secret?

Yes. Sources use "Top-Secret" and "Top Secret" interchangeably, and the Yoyaku channel uses both spellings across its two sessions.

### What does Raphaël Top-Secret play?

Set79 describes house, boogie, disco and Balearic music. Dekmantel adds downtempo, tropical dance, reggae and world music.

### What is Raphaël Top-Secret's Yoyaku instore session?

There are two, published on 17 May 2019 and 14 December 2023. As of 24 November 2026 they have 21,554 and 6,067 YouTube views.

### Which label is Raphaël Top-Secret on?

Antinote is the label we could verify, through the 2016 Love So High EP and the 2017 18 Rays EP. Set79 also lists Rush Hour and Macadam Mambo, without release details.

### What is 18 Rays?

18 Rays is a trio of Raphaël Top-Secret, Zaltan and Nico Motte. According to RA, their self-titled Antinote EP came out in summer 2017.

### Did Raphaël Top-Secret compile Uneven Paths?

He co-compiled it with Jamie Tiller for Music From Memory, per The Vinyl Factory. It collects European pop from 1980 to 1991.

### Is Raphaël Top-Secret playing Montreux Jazz Festival 2027?

The festival lists him on its page for the 2 to 17 July 2027 edition, but gives no date or stage. We treat it as a listing, not a confirmed set.
