Mai Iachetti is an Argentine DJ and producer who works in minimal house, deep tech and techno. Argentine press places her in Córdoba, while US listings call her a Club Space resident in Miami. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 7 July 2026, has 46,639 views and ranks 55th among the channel's 178 sessions.

## Where is Mai Iachetti from and how did she start?

Argentine press and US promoters agree that she is Argentine, but they differ on the details. Córdoba Beat describes her as a DJ and producer from Neuquén who lives in the city of Córdoba, which it says has adopted her. Buenos Aliens, the Buenos Aires electronic music site, calls her a Córdoba DJ and producer in several notes, and its April 2023 note on her House Box EP describes her and her co-producer Bodeler as both from Córdoba. We could not settle whether Neuquén or Córdoba is her birthplace.

Córdoba Beat says she began producing during the pandemic, when club gigs stopped and she was listening to a lot of music. Her earliest dated credit in the sources we opened is a Buenos Aliens note from 22 October 2020 on the first release from Yellow House, a new Córdoba label. It was a pair of edits, one co-written with Brandub and one with Tomás Saenz. Buenos Aliens lists a podcast from 12 February 2021 under the line "Mirando al dancefloor", and Electronic Groove en Español published her guest mix EGE.156 on 19 January 2021. We could not open the podcast itself.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state her age.

## What does Mai Iachetti sound like?

Descriptions are consistent about the territory and vary on the label. Insomniac's artist page calls her sound eclectic and dynamic, blending house, techno, electro, minimal and breaks. Buenos Aliens describes her 2023 Gente Despierta EP as covering different strands of minimal and micro house, and the House Box EP with Bodeler as moving through house, minimal and acid. A promotional description of her DOWCAST002 mix, reproduced on the Set79 tracklist site, calls it an hour of minimal house and deep tech with rolling grooves, warm low end and stripped-back percussion. These are promotional and press summaries, not our own listening notes, so treat the genre tags as a guide.

If this corner of minimal house is your interest, the site's pages on [Arapu](/artists/arapu) and [Chris Stussy](/artists/chris-stussy) cover neighbouring ground.

## What are the career highlights of Mai Iachetti?

### Córdoba, 2020 to 2023

After the Yellow House edits in 2020, she appears on a Música Lunar showcase tour in Argentina. Buenos Aliens lists the first leg on 15 January 2022 in Misiones, organised by Void Forest and Diferente Producciones, with Mariano and Marcos Li on the bill.

Córdoba Beat's interview covers her *90s Trip* EP (WXTDL011) on WeirdTracx, a Córdoba label. It has four originals and two remixes of the title track, by LaRosa and Samos. She told the site that LaRosa's remix, not her original, was the version played in European clubs, including by Enzo Siragusa, and that nothing like it had happened to her before. The article we opened carries no date.

Buenos Aliens reported the House Box EP with Bodeler on 4 April 2023, six originals released through their Bandcamp account, and the Gente Despierta EP, four originals on Midtown House NYC, on 29 August 2023.

### Miami, 2024 to 2026

Insomniac and the Pittsburgh promoter Longturn describe her as having lived in Miami for about two years and as a resident at Club Space. Neither page is dated, so we cannot say when she moved. Both say she runs the Save Our Souls party series at Floyd Miami and has released the first vinyl on her own label, with tracks by Bodeler, Cosenza, Deep Mariano, Brandub and herself. A second EP with Bodeler, remixed by Jorge Savoretti and Juaan, is described as forthcoming. We could not find the label's name in a source we opened.

According to Insomniac, she has played for Solid Grooves, Music On, No Art and Circoloco, and at the Get Out, Sophie and Into the Woods festivals. It also lists releases on Knee Deep In Sound, the label run by Hot Since 82, and says her track "Bones" was played in several of his parties. Longturn adds Handpicked as another label. Neither page gives release years for these. These claims come from promoter copy, and we could not corroborate them in a second independent outlet.
