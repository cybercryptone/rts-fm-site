Ricardo Villalobos made minimal techno feel like a living language rather than a style. Across three decades he has stretched tracks to 37 minutes, played sets that run for most of a day, and kept a Latin percussive swing inside machine music that is otherwise austere. His records and his marathon bookings have made him one of the most discussed DJs on the underground circuit, and he remains a headline name on Ibiza's Sunday circuit.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Villalobos was born on 6 August 1970 in Santiago, Chile, and [moved to Germany with his family in 1973 to escape the Pinochet dictatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos). Discogs places the family's new home in [Darmstadt](https://api.discogs.com/artists/3057), while [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos) names the nearby Hessian town of Seeheim-Jugenheim and says he holds both German and Chilean citizenship. He started on conga and bongos at around ten or eleven, and German Wikipedia adds that he played percussion live at dances organised by friends of his father.

By the late 1980s he was making electronic music, partly in the image of his teenage obsession with Depeche Mode. German Wikipedia dates his first DJ gig in a disco to 1988 and his first single, on the Overload label, to 1992, followed by acid house-driven sets and illegal parties around Hesse. English Wikipedia lists his early label Placid Flavour (1993) as unsuccessful, and gives 1994 for his first Playhouse release and 1998 as the year he began DJing professionally. The sources differ on whether his career starts in 1992 or 1993, so treat both dates as approximate.

## Sound and Style

Villalobos is filed under minimal techno and microhouse, but the label undersells the range. His percussion background shows in the groove, and [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos) describes a mix of house, techno, minimal and South American percussive sound, and notes that his albums carry traces of acid house and Balearic colour as well as minimalism.

In the club he is known for patience and length. His 2004 session with Richie Hawtin at Robert Johnson in Offenbach, [listed on Hawtin's Wikipedia page as an 11-hour set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richie_Hawtin). The two are also members of Narod Niki, a laptop-driven live project that [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos) describes as bringing together eight well-known DJs. That long-form instinct is also why his name sits so naturally on the more patient end of Ibiza's programming, as in the [Pyramid residency at Amnesia](/blog/nina-kraviz-amnesia-ibiza-pyramid-residency) that he curates with [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) and Hawtin.

## Labels and Releases

His first widely noticed album, [Alcachofa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcachofa_(album)), came out on Playhouse on 19 September 2003 and is routinely cited as a landmark of microhouse and minimal techno. English Wikipedia notes that Resident Advisor later voted it the number one album of the 2000s, and in 2025 placed it third in a list of the best electronic records of 2000 to 2025. It is worth being precise about labels here: as RTS.FM has noted in its [Cocoon timeline](/blog/cocoon-recordings-history-timeline), Alcachofa belongs to Playhouse, while his contribution to [Sven Väth's](/artists/sven-vath) Cocoon that year was the mix CD "In The Mix: Taka Taka". For the wider picture of how Cocoon shaped the minimal wave, see [the label's 25-year story](/blog/cocoon-recordings-25-years).

"Thé au Harem d'Archimède" followed in 2004 on Perlon, the Frankfurt-born minimal label that [Wikipedia describes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perlon) as one of the most influential German dance labels. Then came the record that turned him into a reference point for length. [Fizheuer Zieheuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fizheuer_Zieheuer), released in 2006, runs 37 minutes and is built on a brass sample from a Serbian folk recording, which a Discogs forum member identified after release. English Wikipedia says critic Simon Reynolds cited it as evidence that a handful of artists were still pushing dance music into new territory.

He started his own label, Sei Es Drum, [in 2007](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos), releasing the album of the same name that November. Later records include "Re:ECM" with Max Loderbauer in 2011, a collaboration that reworked material from the ECM Records catalogue ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re:ECM)), "Dependent and Happy" in 2012 and "Empirical House" in 2017. More recently Rawax opened a dedicated Villalobos series in January 2024 with a 25th-anniversary reissue of "808 The Bassqueen", a story told in RTS.FM's [Rawax feature](/blog/rawax-records-robert-drewek).

## Career Highlights

In 2006 Villalobos, Hawtin and Luciano played the Berlin Loveparade to a crowd [Wikipedia puts at around 300,000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos). The same page records him as first in Resident Advisor's Top 100 DJs poll at the end of 2007, 2008 and 2010. His mix "fabric36" came out in 2007. On 4 October 2026 he was billed to close Pyramid's season at Amnesia in a back-to-back set with [Raresh](/artists/raresh), covered in our [Ibiza closing parties guide](/blog/ibiza-closing-parties-2026), and his Sunday role there is recapped in the [Kraviz 2026 overview](/blog/nina-kraviz-2026-recap).

A German documentary by Romuald Karmakar, "Denk ich an Deutschland in der Nacht", released in 2017, also featured him, per [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos).

## FAQ

### Who is Ricardo Villalobos?

Ricardo Villalobos is a Chilean-born German DJ and producer, based in Berlin, who became one of the central figures in minimal techno and microhouse through albums such as Alcachofa and long-form tracks such as Fizheuer Zieheuer.

### How old is Ricardo Villalobos?

Ricardo Villalobos was born on 6 August 1970, which makes him 56 (age 56 as of October 2026).

### Where is Ricardo Villalobos from?

He was born in Santiago, Chile, and moved to Germany with his family in 1973. He grew up in the Darmstadt area of Hesse and is now based in Berlin. He holds both Chilean and German citizenship.

### What genre does Ricardo Villalobos play?

His music and DJ sets are filed under minimal techno and microhouse, with a strong percussive, Latin-influenced groove and influences from acid house and techno.

### What is Ricardo Villalobos's real name?

Ricardo Villalobos is his own name. He has no separate public real name, though he has recorded under aliases including Termiten, Richard Wolfsdorf and Bispeed Black.

### What is Ricardo Villalobos's most famous album?

Alcachofa, released on Playhouse in September 2003, is his best-known album. Resident Advisor later voted it the number one album of the 2000s.

### How long is Fizheuer Zieheuer?

The 2006 single Fizheuer Zieheuer runs about 37 minutes. It is built around a looping brass sample and became a signature piece of his multi-hour DJ sets.

### What label does Ricardo Villalobos run?

He founded Sei Es Drum in 2007. He also released early work on his short-lived Placid Flavour label and has recorded for Playhouse, Perlon, Cadenza and Rawax.
