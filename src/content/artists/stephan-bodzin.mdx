Stephan Bodzin made his name with a particular kind of techno: big, singing synthesizer lines over a pulse that stays clean and unhurried. Working out of Bremen, far from Berlin's club economy, he built a reputation as a producer and live performer who plays his own material instead of leaning on other people's records. Wikipedia reports that DJ Hell dubbed the result the Bremen sound, and the label Bodzin founded in 2006 has been its home.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Stephan Bodzin was born on 4 August 1969 in Bremen, according to [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephan_Bodzin) and [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephan_Bodzin), and [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/artist/6f027cd7-c2e8-4569-8922-aa2c9a503e65) gives the same birth date and city. He is a classically educated musician and the son of the artist Herbert Bodzin, per the German article, which says the path into electronic music was set early. English Wikipedia says composing music for European theatres was his first successful platform.

Alongside theatre work he discovered a passion for danceable sounds and began to develop as a dance producer. His Bandcamp page still lists Bremen as his home.

## Sound and Style

Wikipedia describes him as a techno and trance producer who also performs as a live artist, and quotes him describing himself as a "melody man". The sound is synth-led and cinematic. When the label XLR8R announced his third album in 2021, it promised rich, synth-heavy electronic music, and noted that playing the album live was in the back of Bodzin's mind during the whole writing process.

## Labels and Releases

In the mid-2000s Bodzin released at a rapid pace and under several names. Wikipedia lists records for Systematic Recordings, Spielzeug Schallplatten, Get Physical Music, Datapunk and Gigolo, and projects with Oliver Huntemann (H-Man, Rekorder and the Black EP), Marc Romboy (the vs. series) and Thomas Schumacher. He has also remixed Depeche Mode, Booka Shade and The Knife, according to both Wikipedia articles.

In 2006 he founded his own label, Herzblut, which German Wikipedia says is run within the Bremen label group Plantage 13. Herzblut released his debut album Liebe Ist in May 2007, then Powers of Ten in 2015. He also appears on Cocoon Recordings' Compilation G, which our [Cocoon timeline](/blog/cocoon-recordings-history-timeline) notes alongside Marcel Dettmann, Jamie Jones and Cobblestone Jazz.

## Career Highlights

His third album, Boavista, arrived in October 2021, six years after Powers of Ten. According to [XLR8R](https://xlr8r.com/news/stephan-bodzin-announces-third-album-with-lead-single/), it was recorded during the pandemic: unable to travel, Bodzin looked back through his archive of unfinished music, picked 25 favourites while in Brazil, and finished the best 17 for the album. The title track was released first on Afterlife, while the album itself was scheduled for Herzblut. English Wikipedia lists the album under Afterlife and German Wikipedia under Herzblut, a discrepancy XLR8R's account resolves.

His live shows remain central.
