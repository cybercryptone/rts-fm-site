Type B. is a house producer and DJ who turned up on the RTS.FM label in December 2025 with the EP RTSFM013, and who played a Moscow night for the label's radio arm six months later. There is no published biography, birth date or interview in the sources we could open, so this page documents the release and the recordings rather than a life story.

## On the RTS.FM label

RTSFM013 was released on 26 December 2025 as a digital EP, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm013). It has four tracks: "Petage De Plomb", "Clouds", "Red Load" and a remix of "Red Load" by Kandaurov. Bandcamp tags it deep house, minimal house, house and electronic, with Moscow listed as the label's location, and offers it as a digital download.

The name Kandaurov recurs across the label. The YouTube video for "Red Load" credits mastering to Kandaurov, and the Bandcamp pages list a Kandaurov remix on [Nemec's RTSFM008](/artists/nemec) and both a remix and the mastering on [Hugobeat's RTSFM014](/artists/hugobeat). Using the same remixer across several numbers gives the catalogue a recognisable shape. The label's Bandcamp page also invites demo submissions, and the label is introduced in the [label section](/#label) of our home page.

## Sound and style

The only stylistic information the label publishes is its tags: deep house and minimal house. The four titles suggest a restrained palette, but we make no claims beyond what the label states. The format is three originals plus a single remix, a shape shared by several recent RTS.FM EPs.

## minim.all x RTS.FM, Moscow

RTS.FM's YouTube archive holds a set titled "Type B. | minim.all x RTS.FM, Moscow 12.06.2026", uploaded on 13 August 2026 and running about 1 hour 17 minutes. The description links Type B. and minim.all, each with its own SoundCloud and Instagram pages, alongside the RTS.FM label's own channels. A second set from the same night, by Farber, sits in the archive under the same minim.all x RTS.FM title, so the 12 June 2026 event is documented across at least two artists.

## RTS.FM Connection

The archive holds five videos with Type B. in the title: the minim.all set, plus one video for each of the four tracks on RTSFM013, all uploaded on 6 September 2026. The track videos give the label's own audio for "Petage De Plomb", "Clouds", "Red Load" and the Kandaurov remix.
