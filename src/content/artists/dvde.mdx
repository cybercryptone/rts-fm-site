DVDE is a Paris-born DJ and producer who runs the House of Underground and Operation Madness labels, working in house, acid house and electro. His debut solo record, the Ultralucid Paris EP, came out in late 2023 or early 2024, and Yoyaku filmed two instore sessions with him, in December 2023 and January 2026.

## Where is DVDE from and how did he start?

Refuge Worldwide describes DVDE as born and raised in Paris, a longtime record collector who runs two labels, House Of Underground and Operation Madness Records. Rinse France gives a similar profile: a Paris-based DJ, vinyl collector and producer who founded House Of Underground Records. Trommel, which premiered a track from his first EP, calls him a French artist and the head of House Of Underground.

Those biographies are short and read like artist-supplied text, and we found no long interview, so the early part of the story is thin. We could not find where he trained, when he started DJing or who his first influences were. What the sources do show is a sequence: he was running a label and hosting radio shows before he put out a record of his own. Yoyaku's description of his 2023 session says as much, calling him the boss of House of Underground who "finally" releases his first record.

## What does DVDE sound like?

Rinse France lists his DJ sets as moving between disco and funk, Chicago and Detroit house, acid house and electro. Refuge Worldwide's profile gives a nearly identical range: disco, funk, house, acid and electro.

On record, the focus is acid. Yoyaku describes the Ultralucid Paris EP as three tracks inspired by Chicago acid house with a modern touch, built on classic drum machines and 303 basslines. Trommel describes the title track as electro-leaning with a sizzling acid bassline and vocal chops that recall 1980s acid house, and "Good Morning England" as hard-hitting, with heavy kicks and a gnarly 303 line. Rush Hour's listing calls the EP an old school house workout.

His second record leans toward classic house. A review on Vaguely Offensive describes the title track of the Set Free EP, made with DMX Krew, as built around Robert Owens vocals, and a shop listing from Groove Distribution says the acid builds over the four tracks until an IDM and techno closer.

## What are the career highlights of DVDE?

### House of Underground

The label's Bandcamp page lists a catalogue that began with Boo Williams, Night Heat EP (HOU01) and Scan 7, As It Should Be EP (HOU02), then moved through Detroit's Filthiest and Byron the Aquarius. Yoyaku's label page describes House Of Underground as a Paris label run by DVDE. Trommel adds that the label's roster includes Boo Williams, Byron the Aquarius, Scan 7 and Detroit's Filthiest. The label is vinyl-focused, which makes the economics in our report on [vinyl pressing capacity](/blog/vinyl-pressing-plant-capacity-crisis) relevant to small imprints like it.

### Radio and club dates

Rinse France lists DVDE as a DJ who has played at Trésor and Renate in Berlin and at Rex Club in Paris, and says he also has a live set built for the dancefloor. Trommel says he has played for HOR and The Lot Radio as well as at many club dates. We could not verify specific dates for any of those bookings.

His Rinse France show page lists a monthly slot. The earliest entry is dated 9 December 2022 and the latest, Episode 23, is dated 13 July 2026. We read those as scheduled slots on the station's page, not as a confirmed archive of broadcasts. Refuge Worldwide also lists two House Of Underground shows with him, on 14 October 2023 and 11 April 2025.

### Ultralucid Paris and Set Free

Ultralucid Paris (HOU06) is his first solo record, a 12 inch with three tracks, including "Dramacid" featuring San Proper. Set Free (HOU08) is a four-track 12 inch made with the UK producer DMX Krew, with Robert Owens, known as a vocalist for Fingers Inc, featured on two tracks according to Groove Distribution. Vaguely Offensive disagrees on the second one, saying the Owens credit on "Lost in Music" is only a chopped sample of the song title.

## Which labels is DVDE associated with?

- **House Of Underground.** His main label, based in Paris. The Bandcamp page lists eight HOU releases, HOU01 to HOU08, with his own records at HOU06 and HOU08.
- **Operation Madness Records.** Named by Refuge Worldwide and Rinse France as his second label. Rinse France says Boo Williams, Byron the Aquarius and DMX Krew have released on his labels. The Bandcamp page also lists three OPM releases, by DMX Krew, Matt Whitehead and Tom Carruthers, though the page does not say OPM stands for Operation Madness, so we treat that link as likely, not confirmed.
- **Yoyaku.** Not a label of his, but the shop sells his records and filmed both of his instore sessions. Yoyaku's label page lists HOU06 and HOU08 under his name.
