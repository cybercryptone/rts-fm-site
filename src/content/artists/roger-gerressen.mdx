Roger Gerressen is a Dutch DJ and producer from Nijmegen who works with the Paris-based Yoyaku and runs the labels Irenic, Autodidact and OGUN. He moves between techno, dub, house and hip-hop beats. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 29 October 2016, has 68,356 YouTube views.

## Where is Roger Gerressen from and how did he start?

MEOKO's October 2017 interview gives Arnhem as his birthplace and Nijmegen as his base, and Volumo's biography says the same. Yoyaku's artist page calls him a producer and DJ from Nijmegen. Trommel's 2019 and 2022 features also name Nijmegen as his hometown and describe him as Dutch, though neither states where he was born.

He grew up on early 1990s hip-hop. Trommel says he was given an A Tribe Called Quest album at 13 and bought Wu-Tang Clan's debut with birthday money, and that he disliked the gabber and trance he heard on Dutch radio. Trommel's 2022 feature says he found techno at a rave on his 18th birthday, and its 2019 interview adds that Steve Rachmad mix compilations deepened the interest. He told MEOKO that he has always loved sample-based loops, and that the lack of equipment pushed him toward digital production.

His DJ apprenticeship was local. Trommel says that from 2004 he regularly warmed up crowds at the Planet Rose nights in Nijmegen, and the 2019 interview says he had a regular spot at Doornroosje, opening for names such as Ame, [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills), Marcel Dettmann and Mr. G. He names Mills as the influence that still resonates most, alongside [Steve Rachmad](/artists/steve-rachmad) and Dave Clarke.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot say how old he is.

## What does Roger Gerressen sound like?

He is hard to pin to one genre. Volumo lists deep house, raw, deep and dub techno, minimal and deep tech, microhouse and house. Trommel's 2022 feature calls him very prolific across techno, electro, house, dub and hip-hop, and says he gives each project its own alias so it keeps its own identity. As Sahasrahla, Trommel says, he made old-school minimal techno on Autodidact. Under Reinhardt Fötzestauer he released the Bad Girl EP on Fstauer in early 2022, which Trommel describes as moody, with sub bass and acid lines.

The hip-hop roots stay audible. MEOKO's interview describes a hip-hop attitude toward house, making music with whatever gear is to hand. His Argee alias released an instrumental beats collection on the hip-hop label Straatkunst in 2022.

## What are the career highlights of Roger Gerressen?

### Nijmegen labels, 2009 to 2014

Volumo says his first solo records came out on the local Wolfskuil label, and Shotgun's discography lists Hajastan on Wolfskuil Limited in 2009. He co-founded ESHU Records in 2011, according to both Trommel and Volumo. Volumo adds that he then built a run of solo records and collaborations on Fear Of Flying and its sub-label Sudden Drop. The Amsterdam Dance Event profile lists Nilla Records and Wolfskuil Ltd among his labels, and names the groups Dilated Pupils, ARC#, ALMA, ESHU and Novio Dub Tribe.

### Joining Yoyaku, 2016 to 2018

Trommel's 2019 interview says he met the Yoyaku team at an early party in Strasbourg and signed to its booking agency after his previous agency closed. He describes himself as the only Dutch member of a mostly French crew. His records for Yoyaku's imprints started with Joule, Aku and Tartouffe, as MEOKO put it in 2017.

XLR8R's news item of 7 September 2017 lists him on AKUVA01, the first various-artists release on Aku, with Difid, Boniface and Robert David. XLR8R's November 2018 feature on [Janeret](/artists/janeret) says Janeret heads Joule Imprint, which released music from Teluric and Gerressen. Trommel's September 2018 profile of Yoyaku groups him with [Lowris](/artists/lowris) among artists who had proven their value. XLR8R's announcement for the Barcelona Off-Week showcase of 15 June 2018 listed him with [Cabanne](/artists/cabanne), Varhat, Janeret, Zendid and Oshana. We did not find a report of the night itself, so we cannot say he played it.

### His own labels

MEOKO's 2017 interview shows him launching Irenic and Autodidact, with a Novio Dub Tribe record as Irenic's first release and a Doyle Johnson record as Autodidact's first. Trommel says the Irenic name means aiming at peace, and that each main release is dedicated to someone who did something for the greater good. Its first night was held at Rex Club in Paris with Steve Rachmad and Steve O'Sullivan, Trommel reports. Yoyaku's page also credits him with Fstauer and OGUN Records, and Trommel's 2022 feature adds Diurnal, whose first release was Bloodflow.

## Which labels is Roger Gerressen associated with?

- **Yoyaku and its imprints.** Joule, Aku and Tartouffe, per MEOKO, plus the YYK No Label series. Yoyaku lists his album Heading In A Backwards Direction as YOYAKU003LP.
- **Sushitech.** Home of his Monoaware project, per MEOKO and Trommel.
- **Irenic, Autodidact, Fstauer, OGUN and Diurnal.** Labels he runs or started, per Yoyaku and Trommel.
- **ESHU Records.** Co-founded in 2011.
- **Early labels.** Wolfskuil, Fear Of Flying, Sudden Drop and Nilla, per Volumo, Shotgun and the Amsterdam Dance Event.

## FAQ

### How old is Roger Gerressen?

We could not verify his age. No source we opened gives a birth year or date. Trommel says he got his first hip-hop albums at 13 in the early 1990s, but not the year.

### Where is Roger Gerressen from?

He is Dutch. MEOKO and Volumo say he was born in Arnhem, and Yoyaku, MEOKO and Trommel place him in Nijmegen, where he grew up and is based.

### Who is Roger Gerressen?

He is a Dutch DJ and producer working in techno, dub, house and hip-hop, and the owner of labels including Irenic and Autodidact. He is a member of the Yoyaku roster.

### What is Roger Gerressen's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 29 October 2016, runs about 117 minutes and has 68,356 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 32nd by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Is Roger Gerressen signed to Yoyaku?

Trommel's 2019 interview says he signed to the Yoyaku booking agency after meeting the team at a party in Strasbourg, and XLR8R lists him among the artists it calls the Yoyaku roster.

### Which labels does Roger Gerressen run?

Yoyaku's artist page lists IRENIC, Autodidact, Fstauer and OGUN Records, and says he co-founded ESHU Records. Trommel adds Diurnal.

### What aliases does Roger Gerressen use?

Yoyaku lists Gasometric Run, Monoaware, Doyle Johnson, Sahasrahla, Argee and Reinhardt Fötzestauer. Volumo and Trommel list others, and the Amsterdam Dance Event names group projects such as Dilated Pupils and ARC#.

### Who influenced Roger Gerressen?

He names A Tribe Called Quest as his favourite group in MEOKO, and Jeff Mills as the techno influence that resonates most in Trommel. Steve Rachmad and Dave Clarke are also named.
