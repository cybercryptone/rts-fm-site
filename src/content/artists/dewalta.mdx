DeWalta is the stage name of David Koch, a German producer and DJ based in Berlin who runs the Meander label. He trained as a jazz saxophonist before moving into minimal house and techno. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 22 November 2018, has 87,253 YouTube views and ranks 28th among the channel's 178 sessions.

## Where is DeWalta from and how did he start?

Coda Music gives his real name as David Koch and says he was born in 1985 in Heidelberg, in southern Germany. Skiddle also says he was born in Heidelberg, but we found no second source for the year, so treat it as unconfirmed. If 1985 is right he is 40 or 41 in November 2026, depending on his birthday, which no source we opened gives.

In a fabric interview, Koch says he grew up in Gauangelloch, a village about 30 km from Heidelberg, and that his family moved to Potsdam, near Berlin, when he was 15 or 16. He started on the French horn as a child (age 6 per Coda Music and ADE, 7 per NTS), took up saxophone as a young teenager and learned jazz from his grandfather's records. Vice's Thump said in 2013 that he took the DeWalta name from that grandfather, Walter. Ibiza Spotlight, in September 2012, described him playing in a student jazz club and a jazz pub in his hometown as a teenager.

Sources disagree on when he arrived in Berlin. Skiddle says around 13, Thump says 15, and NTS says 1999 for conservatory study. He studied at the Hanns Eisler music academy in Berlin, which ADE, NTS, Coda and Ibiza Spotlight all mention. NTS says that by 2003 he was building a studio and putting on electronic music events. In the fabric interview he dates his first public DJ gigs to Paloma Bar in Berlin, at around 17.

## What does DeWalta sound like?

Coda Music calls his style a trance-like, minimal blend of house and techno, and says it sits close to Romanian-style minimal. Skiddle adds that it draws on hip-hop, jazz and funk. Thump quoted other DJs comparing him to "Jaco Pastorius on acid", and Igloo's 2012 review of *Wander* heard tech house with jazz influences, analogue synths, layered percussion and live guests.

His records are long. A 2019 review of *Lyra* on The Moderns noted six extended tracks across a 56-minute double LP, and RA's 2017 review of *Dark Matter* described four side-long pieces, sparse on melody and heavy on bass. In a February 2018 Mixmag podcast he spoke about the link between minimal and jazz, and about an unusual studio method, without the page giving details.

## What are the career highlights of DeWalta?

### Early releases and Haunt

Coda Music dates his debut release to 2007, the *Nebelhorn* EP with Aquatic on Kalk Pets, which we could not confirm elsewhere. Ibiza Spotlight says his first 12-inch records came out on the techno label Vakant. His debut album, *Wander*, came out in 2012 on Haunt Music, the label run by Mike Shannon.

### DeWalta and Shannon

Fabric describes the two as meeting in Berlin and working in the studio almost at once, with Koch playing saxophone in their live set, first performed at fabric. Skiddle said in August 2016 that he had stopped solo live shows and now played live only with Shannon, and that they had a full-length that sold out quickly. Their Artificial Turf EP is on Visionquest, per Yoyaku's artist page and Coda Music, which dates it to 2019. Fabric also names him in DDMS, a collaborative project with Colin and Scott, which Mixmag's podcast page references as his crew.

### Amphia and the Romanian connection

*Dark Matter*, a double EP, was his debut on Amphia, the Bucharest label run by [Cristi Cons](/artists/cristi-cons) and [Vlad Caia](/artists/vlad-caia). XLR8R covered it on 28 June 2017. Resident Advisor's review says it was a return of a favor, because Cons had released the *Perceptual* EP on Meander in April 2017. Cons and DeWalta also share a Meander release listed by Yoyaku as *Infinity*, and a Meander record with Topper called *Family Jubilee III*. Our [rominimal guide](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno) covers the scene around Amphia.

### Festivals and club dates

Our [Waking Life festival page](/festivals/waking-life) records him on the announced bill of the first edition in August 2017 in Portugal, with Giegling's DJ Dustin and Konstantin. Skiddle reported in August 2016 that he was making his debut at the iRis night in Birmingham on 20 August, and that he ran a monthly residency at Club der Visionaere in Berlin. That residency detail dates from 2016, so check whether it still runs. ADE also keeps a profile for him.

## Which labels is DeWalta associated with?

His own label is Meander. NTS says he founded it in 2007, and ADE says he started it with Fabian Geimer and Jens Körmer. Skiddle said in 2016 that it was selective, had reached its 16th release, put out about four records a year at most, and that his childhood friend Fabian runs the business side while he handles A&R. XLR8R calls Fabian Geimer-Lorusso, also known as Jupiter, a Meander co-owner. *Illumination* in 2014 was the label's first full-length, per Coda Music, and a 2019 XLR8R article on [Christopher Ledger](/artists/christopher-ledger) places his *Dark Moon* as Meander's 27th release.

Elsewhere he has appeared on Vakant, Haunt, Cynosure (Shannon's label), Kalk Pets, Amphia, Visionquest and Indigo Raw, according to Skiddle, ADE, Coda Music and Yoyaku. Yoyaku also lists *Quadrilogy Part III / IV* on Vinylclub with Topper and [Audio Werner](/artists/audio-werner), with no date given. Skiddle says he released a record as Kool D on Tuskegee, the label of Seth Troxler and the Martinez Brothers.

## FAQ

### How old is DeWalta?

Coda Music gives his birth year as 1985, which would make him 40 or 41 in November 2026. We found no second source and no full birth date, so treat the age with care.

### Where is DeWalta from?

He is German. Coda Music and Skiddle say he was born in Heidelberg, and fabric says he grew up in a village nearby before his family moved to the Berlin area. He has been based in Berlin since.

### Who is DeWalta?

DeWalta is the stage name of David Koch, a German producer and DJ who runs the Meander label. He is known for long minimal house and techno records and for his work with Mike Shannon.

### Did DeWalta play a Yoyaku instore session?

Yes. It was published on 22 November 2018, runs about 136 minutes and has 87,253 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 28th of the channel's 178 sessions.

### Which label does DeWalta run?

Meander, which NTS says he founded in 2007. Skiddle described it in 2016 as a small, selective label with a maximum of about four releases a year.

### Does DeWalta play saxophone?

Yes. Fabric says he plays saxophone in his live set with Mike Shannon, and Ibiza Spotlight calls it his main instrument.

### Who is DeWalta's usual collaborator?

Mike Shannon, whom he met in Berlin, per fabric. They record as DeWalta and Shannon, and Skiddle said in 2016 that he played live only with Shannon. He has also worked with Cristi Cons, Topper and Audio Werner.

### What was DeWalta's debut album?

*Wander*, released in 2012 on Mike Shannon's Haunt Music, according to Ibiza Spotlight. Igloo's review calls it his debut full-length.

### What was DeWalta's latest release?

The most recent dated item we found is his guest spot on SIT's *Synaptic* album, released on 6 January 2026. Check Meander and Bandcamp for later material.
