Que Sakamoto is a Tokyo-based DJ, producer and radio host who plays disco, dub, garage and Japanese music from the 1970s to the 1990s. He co-founded the Huit Etoiles party in 2015 and records with NT. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 January 2026, runs 103 minutes and has 45,020 views.

## Where is Que Sakamoto from and how did he start?

Keyi Magazine, in an interview published on 29 October 2024, describes him as a musician and producer from Tokyo, Japan. Kiosk Radio and Subsonic Music both give Tokyo as his base, though Keyi quotes him describing a nomadic DJ life, and the Ransom Note says he is based in Japan but travels most of the year.
According to Keyi, he was a clubber and party-goer in Tokyo before he started DJing. He bought two Pioneer turntables and a mixer, began collecting vinyl and learned the craft with his partner NT, whose full name is Noriyuki Takagi. Groove's podcast write-up of 19 March 2021 adds that NT is a music teacher and sound engineer, a clubbing friend of more than ten years, who taught him production and handles mixing and mastering in a studio full of vintage analogue gear.

No source we opened gives his real name, birth date or birth year, so we cannot state his age.

## What does Que Sakamoto sound like?

His sets do not stay in one place. Subsonic Music and Alinea A both say he tailors each set to the crowd in the country he is playing, moving between disco, garage, dub, trance and experimental breakbeat. Keyi adds drum and bass to the list, and says he works with vintage equipment.

Since about 2024 the emphasis has been on Japanese music from the 1970s to the 1990s. Keyi quotes him saying he wants younger listeners, especially in Japan, to connect with their musical roots, and he names Hoodoo Fushimi, Haruomi Hosono, Osamu Sato and Mushroom Now among his favourites. In 2021 he told Groove that he approaches his work as a fusion of Japanese and European folk music with disco, built on 1970s analogue sounds and hard-to-find material that runs from post-punk and dub to city pop.

## What are the career highlights of Que Sakamoto?

### Huit Etoiles and early records, 2014 to 2019

Clubberia says he played a solo Open to Last night at 65MQ in Milan in 2014, billing him as the first Japanese DJ to do so. The Ransom Note says he founded Huit Etoiles, a Tokyo party, in 2015. Groove calls it a Japanese nu disco community that he started with Tamaru, with parties first at Origami (later renamed Arc) and then at Vent. Guests have included Hot Toddy of Crazy P, Psychemagik, Khidja and Mehmet Aslan, according to Groove and Clubberia, and Groove says it toured China and Korea before the pandemic.

His first records came out with NT. "Lalo" reached the top 5 of a Beatport nu disco chart in March 2017, according to Groove and Clubberia. Clubberia also lists two edits on the UK label To Rack & Ruin. A European tour in September 2017 took him through Berlin, Istanbul, Belgium and Italy, per Clubberia.

In 2019 came the Uchuu Hikoshi EP with NT on Not An Animal, reviewed by Test Pressing, which describes a folky Japanese sound palette over a mid-tempo disco groove. Keyi dates their collaboration "Minimüzikhol", a blend of Japanese and Turkish electronic music, to the same year.

### Radio, collaborations and touring, 2021 to 2024

He hosts a show called Thoughts and Ideas on Mutant Radio, which Groove and Keyi describe as built around guest selectors and, more recently, Japanese music. Keyi says he has released mixes on Red Light Radio, Rinse FM and NTS Radio, and Kiosk Radio adds The Lot Radio.

In 2023, he and NT contributed "Murasaki" to a 44-track Keyi Magazine charity compilation on Keyi Records, released on 29 June 2023. Mixmag Asia reviewed it and said the vocals were sampled from a Kabuki drama. Keyi says he met Brazilian musician Paulo Santos of Uakti at Bruma Festival in 2023, and that the two planned a vinyl EP with NT. It also says he toured 22 countries in 2022 and played an all-night set at Womb with [Satoshi Tomiie](/artists/satoshi-tomiie).

Kiosk Radio lists Panorama Bar, Fuji Rock, Mamba Negra, [Garbicz](/festivals/garbicz-festival) and [Waking Life](/festivals/waking-life) among the clubs and festivals he has played. Keyi adds Waha Festival in Romania in 2023 and 2024.

## Which labels is Que Sakamoto associated with?

- **Not An Animal.** Run by Andy Bainbridge and Chris Stoker, according to Test Pressing. It released Uchuu Hikoshi with NT, and Groove lists it as a home for "Lalo".
- **Roam Recordings.** The San Francisco label that Clubberia and the Ransom Note name as an outlet for his work with NT. Groove and Clubberia disagree on which label put out "Lalo", so we do not assign it to one.
- **Hard Fist.** The Lyon label named by the Ransom Note, which says his track "Istanbul", named after a residency there, came out on it.
- **To Rack & Ruin.** Clubberia lists two edits on the UK imprint.
- **Keyi Records.** The label behind the 2023 charity compilation that included "Murasaki".
- **Hivern Discs.** Groove says he and NT appeared on a compilation from John Talabot's label.
