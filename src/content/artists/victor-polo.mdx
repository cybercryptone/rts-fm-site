Victor Polo is a house DJ and producer from Valencia who has run his own labels since the early 2010s and who appears twice in the RTS.FM label catalogue: with the solo EP RTSFM003 in October 2024 and with "Philip Of The Field" on the RTSFM011 compilation a year later. Most of what is published about him comes from his [Discogs profile](https://www.discogs.com/artist/2645040), which is community edited, so claims here are attributed to it.

## Early life and path into music

According to Discogs, Victor Polo is a Spanish DJ born in Valencia in 1991. The same profile says he started his career in 2010 as the boss of Chola Records Group, which [Discogs describes](https://www.discogs.com/label/568912) as a label and studio founded in 2007 and based on Lanzarote, Spain, and that he is a resident DJ at Mogambo in Valencia. It also lists the alias Dok Jebeni and two group credits, Pilk Meier and Techbrothers.

Discogs ties the profile to the RTS.FM artist through a shared SoundCloud account: the profile links to soundcloud.com/victorpolodj, the same page RTS.FM credits in its YouTube description for "Philip Of The Field".

## Labels and releases

Discogs says Victor Polo owns Wireless Marionettes, a Valencia label, with Carlos Moreno, and Eccentric, also from Valencia, with Raul Rubio, whose own entry describes him as a Valencia-area DJ born in 1986. The Eccentric label profile says it focuses on house, deep, tech and techno.

Discogs lists main releases from 2012 onward: *It Down* on Bronzai Records, the *Name Job EP* on Wireless Marionettes with The Smilers, and the *In The Kitchen EP* with Raul Rubio and Javier Alemany on Sempai Music, all in 2012. Later entries include *All Days* (2014) and *The Funk* (2015) on 808 Recordings, *Diédrico EP* on Innocent Music and *Self Mode* on Cientoonce (both 2019) and *Deep End* on Platform 7even (2020). He also remixed Arkady Antsyrev's "Zero" on Tres 14 Music and "Terrible" on Cientoonce in 2017.

## On the RTS.FM label

RTSFM003 was released on 12 October 2024 as a digital release, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm003). It has three tracks: "Bubble Four", "Contactless" and "Desert Sunset". Bandcamp tags it deep house, minimal house, house and electronic, with Moscow as the label's location.

On 17 October 2025 Victor Polo opened the track list of [RTSFM011](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm011), which the label describes as a compilation of resident artists from the RTS.FM label. His contribution is "Philip Of The Field". The other three tracks are by [Genning](/artists/genning), [Nemec](/artists/nemec) and [Nicolas Barnes](/artists/nicolas-barnes).

## Sound and style

The label's tags are deep house and minimal house, and Discogs shows his own labels pitched at house, deep and tech. He has not published a statement about his sound that we could find, so we describe only the tags and label focus rather than the music itself.
