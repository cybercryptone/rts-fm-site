Lamache is Thibaut Machet, a DJ from Toulouse, France, who is based in Berlin and runs the Discobar label. He is known for stripped-back minimal house and techno sets. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 6 December 2018, runs about two hours and has 37,427 views.

## Where is Lamache from and how did he start?

XLR8R's April 2017 feature names him as Thibaut Machet and says he is from Toulouse, in the south of France. He told the magazine that he began organising parties at about 16 and taught himself to mix at 17. He played bars in the city and took a residency at a gay club there. In the Meoko interview from November 2017 he names that first residency as Beaucoup, a Toulouse venue that has since closed. A Jaeger Oslo interview adds that he started on a pair of Technics turntables at his father's apartment and still owns them.

After a university degree in economics, XLR8R says, he moved to Paris in 2009 to study sound engineering, partly because he thought production skills would help his DJing. He built a name through pre-parties and warm-up sets at Rex Club, though he told Jaeger he was never a Rex resident. In 2012, according to XLR8R, Isis Salvaterra and Claus Voigtmann brought him to London as a resident of Toi Toi, the event series that also runs a label and booking agency. He was represented by Toi Toi until August 2016 and has since lived in Berlin.

XLR8R's feature was mostly about anxiety and panic attacks that began in March 2013 and disrupted his travel for years. By 2018 the same magazine said he had managed them for about three years and was touring again.

## How old is Lamache?

No source we opened gives a birth date or year. XLR8R's feature, published on 5 April 2017, gives his age as 29. That points to a birth year of 1987 or 1988 and an age of 38 or 39 on 24 November 2026, but this is our own estimate, not a stated fact.

## What does Lamache sound like?

The set79 database describes him as a Toulouse-born, Berlin-based selector focused on stripped-back minimal house, techno and classic house grooves. XLR8R's 2018 podcast page calls his sets warped, groove-driven minimal, and its 2017 mix page describes stripped-back house and beat-driven oddities. Trommel's page for its December 2023 podcast adds acidic tones, punchy percussion and a mystical vibe.

In the Jaeger Oslo interview he calls himself a DJ first and says he is often labelled a minimal DJ. He lists [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos), [Rhadoo](/artists/rhadoo), Shonky and tINI among his influences. Meoko records that seeing Villalobos play at Razzmatazz in Barcelona was a turning point. He also told Meoko that his style is versatile and shaped by travel.

## What are the career highlights of Lamache?

### Toi Toi, Rex and the early touring years, 2012 to 2016

The London move in 2012 raised his profile and led to wider touring, according to XLR8R. Set79 lists a recorded set from ReSolute in New York dated 13 February 2016, though we did not verify more about it.

### United States, 2017

XLR8R's mix page says he played the XLR8R and Cyclone pool party in Los Angeles on 23 April 2017, his first appearance in the city. He took an early midday set because he had a flight later that day, and the live recording was posted on 23 June 2017.

### Yoyaku and Club der Visionaere, 2018

XLR8R's Podcast 545, posted on 5 June 2018, says he had recently joined the French agency Yoyaku. It also ties him to the Club der Visionaere sessions and mentions festival dates and a Discobar anniversary tour that year. Trommel's feature on Yoyaku's rise, published on 8 September 2018, lists him with [Lowris](/artists/lowris) and [Roger Gerressen](/artists/roger-gerressen) among artists who had already proven their worth to the Yoyaku group. See our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) for the shop and label background.

### Discobar showcases, 2020

Trommel's 18 February 2020 article announced a five-year Discobar tour. The list included Nodd Club in Paris on 21 February, Hoppetosse in Berlin on 22 March, The Lion and Lamb in London on 27 March and Roots in Madrid on 28 March. It also listed fabric's Room 3 in London on 25 April, Wildt in Prague on 9 May, and Veniceberg in Verona on 15 May with [Craig Richards](/artists/craig-richards). These were announced dates, and we did not confirm which went ahead. Set79 separately lists a Slwdnc x Yoyaku set at Mutabor in Moscow dated 22 February 2020.

## Which labels is Lamache associated with?

- **Discobar.** His own label. Trommel's 2020 article names Guillaume Taillieu, also known as Sir Gui, and Lamache as its founders, and calls the label French. Meoko says he named it after a DJ booth in Belgium. XLR8R's 2018 pages count three years of the label in 2018 and Trommel counted five in 2020, which points to a start around 2015, though no source we opened gives the year. Trommel's 2023 podcast page lists Toba, Odd Soul, Alex & Digby, Zendid, Robin Ordell, Federico Molinari and Len Lewis as artists the label has welcomed.
- **Toi Toi Musik.** The London collective, label and agency where he was resident and was represented until August 2016.
- **Yoyaku.** XLR8R describes it as a French agency he joined in 2018. We did not find a Yoyaku label release credited to him.
- **Amorfo.** XLR8R mentions it as a new label with a first release in his 2018 mix. We opened no further source on it.

## FAQ

### How old is Lamache?

No source we opened gives a birth date. XLR8R gave his age as 29 in April 2017, which suggests he is 38 or 39 now, but this is an estimate.

### Where is Lamache from?

He is from Toulouse, France, according to XLR8R, Meoko and Trommel. He lives in Berlin, where XLR8R interviewed him at home in Kreuzberg in 2017.

### Who is Lamache?

Lamache is Thibaut Machet, a French DJ known for minimal house and techno, and a co-founder of the Discobar label. He was a Toi Toi resident in London before moving to Berlin.

### What is Lamache's real name?

XLR8R, Jaeger Oslo and XLR8R's podcast page give it as Thibaut Machet.

### What is Lamache's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 6 December 2018, runs about 121 minutes and has 37,427 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 63rd by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Is Lamache signed to Yoyaku?

XLR8R said in June 2018 that he had joined the French agency Yoyaku, and Trommel listed him among its proven artists that September. We found no Yoyaku label release by him.

### What is Discobar?

Discobar is a label Lamache co-founded with Guillaume Taillieu, according to Trommel. It has released Zendid, Robin Ordell, Alex & Digby, Toba and others, and it runs club showcases across Europe.

### Has Lamache released any records?

We found no solo release. XLR8R noted in 2018 that he had toured widely without one, and his label work appears to be curation and A&R.

### What does Lamache's music sound like?

Stripped-back minimal house and techno with classic house grooves, per set79, XLR8R and Trommel. He cites Villalobos, Rhadoo and Shonky as influences.

### Where has Lamache played?

Sources place him at Rex Club in Paris, Toi Toi events in London, and a Los Angeles pool party in 2017. Trommel's 2020 article listed Discobar dates in Berlin, Madrid, Prague and Verona.
