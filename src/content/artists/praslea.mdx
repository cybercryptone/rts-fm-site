Praslea is the kind of name that rarely trends and is always on the bill. A Bucharest DJ who came up inside the Sunrise agency and the a:rpia:r circle, his name was already on major Bucharest events around 2007, and he was still on the lineup when the Sunwaves festival played its last edition in Romania in 2025.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Praslea is the stage name of Florin Cunțan, according to [Discogs](https://api.discogs.com/artists/1179863), which describes him as a Romanian DJ and producer from Bucharest. His date of birth has not been published by any source we found, so there is no reliable figure for his age.

[Trommel's profile](https://trommelmusic.com/music/podcast/trommel-190-praslea) says Bucharest is his hometown and that his name was appearing on major events there around 2007. It adds that he was inducted into the a:rpia:r clique from an early age. A 2009 event listing for forbiddEn fruit #10 in Leuven, Belgium, includes a profile that lists him on the Sunrise and a:rpia:r roster and calls him one of the ["most promising artists of the young Romanian generation"](https://www.beyondgaming.be/archive/music.54/forbidden-fruit-10-silo-leuven.640762).

## Sound and Style

No source we opened sets out his sound in detail, but the context is clear. The people around him, Raresh, Rhadoo and Petre Inspirescu, are the core of the a:rpia:r label, and [Bandcamp Daily's July 2026 scene report](https://daily.bandcamp.com/scene-report/romanian-minimal-techno-scene-report) describes the wider rominimal sound as a regional style that blends folk and classical elements into patient dance music. Our [history of rominimal](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno) traces how that scene formed in Bucharest in the 2000s.

Trommel describes him as someone who performs alongside his peers even at large events. It also says he forms a duo, Praslesh, with another artist from his circle, but does not name the partner, so we do not either.

## Releases and Radio

His recorded output is small, and Trommel calls him hardly prolific since 2009. Discogs lists him on the 2009 compilation EP *Jurnal De Bord* on a:rpia:r, on the 2008 Moon Harbour Recordings compilation *Circoloco@DC10 10 Years Anniversary (Part 2 Of 3)* and as the main artist on the *Habitual Psychosis EP* on Spacebased Recordings, which Discogs lists without a year.

That 2009 profile says he co-owns the online live DJ broadcasting project Audiomatic Net Radio with [Raresh](/artists/raresh), the a:rpia:r co-founder. It also names Circoloco nights at DC10 in Ibiza among his gigs, along with Chalet Torino for a Time Warp club edition, Ambasada Gavioli in Slovenia and Nana Club in Rome.

## Career Highlights

In April 2023 Praslea played Sunwaves at Mamaia, on Stage 4 on a Monday evening, sharing the stage with Kozo and a DJ listed as SIT, according to Trommel. Trommel published an extract of that set as its Trommel.190 podcast on 5 April 2024.

When Sunwaves announced that its 35th edition would be the last in Romania, [Electronic Groove](https://electronicgroove.com/sunwaves-festival-announces-its-final-edition-in-romania/) listed him among the regular artists expected from 1 to 5 May 2025 at Sunwaves Beach in Mamaia, alongside [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos), Sonja Moonear, Seth Troxler, tINI, G76 and [Priku](/artists/priku). Our [Sunwaves festival page](/festivals/sunwaves) tells the festival's story, and our [Sonja Moonear profile](/artists/sonja-moonear) covers another name from that bill.
