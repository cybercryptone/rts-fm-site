Maxime dB is a Paris-based DJ and producer who co-runs the AirFunk record label with Aline Brooklyn and plays a mix of house, disco, funk and electro. His two-hour Yoyaku instore session, published on 24 January 2025, has 67,118 views on YouTube as of 24 November 2026.

## Where is Maxime dB from and how did he start?

Public information about Maxime dB is thin, and nothing we opened gives a birth year, real name or birthplace. What the sources agree on is that he is rooted in Paris. The Amsterdam Dance Event artist directory calls him a dedicated digger rooted in the Paris music community and lists his country as France. Set79 also describes him as Paris-based.

MDLBeast's profile says he first studied music through the piano at a conservatory. It adds that he had 12 years of experience at the time of writing and had built a record collection with no stylistic boundaries. The same profile says he began producing in 2020, with a first release on Rings of Neptune. We could not find that record in any catalogue we could open, so we treat it as his own account rather than a confirmed credit.

Mixes and radio shows give a few dated markers. Set79 lists a "Home Diggin'" invitation from [D'Julz](/artists/d-julz) on Rinse France dated 10 December 2022, a Kiosk Radio show on 16 February 2024 and a set for a1recordsparis on 20 April 2024. It names the Rex Club in Paris and the Hoppetosse in Berlin among the venues he plays. Set79 is an aggregator of DJ sets, so we use it for these dates only.

## What does Maxime dB sound like?

Descriptions vary slightly between sources, which is common for a DJ with a wide collection. The ADE biography says his sets blend genres while keeping a clear touch of funk, electro and house. Set79 says house, disco and funk with 80s and 90s influences. MDLBeast's profile calls his signature sound a mix of modern sophistication and 80s and 90s influences, with a particular groove.

Retail pages for his records point the same way. Dancing Vinyl describes the third AirFunk record as leaning toward pop and disco influences, and Sound Shelter files most of his releases under house, with a few under electro. His 2023 collaboration track "Disco Sex Machine" with Aline Umber is a representative title: Dancing Vinyl lists it at 129 BPM.

## What are the career highlights of Maxime dB?

### AirFunk and the Paris scene

His main project is the AirFunk label, which ADE says he runs with Aline Brooklyn. Sound Shelter dates its first record, AF001, to 2022. The Yoyaku store page calls it a French electronic music label, and Yoyaku stocks the catalogue. The release credits on AirFunk use the name Aline Umber, and we could not confirm from an opened page whether this is the same person as Aline Brooklyn.

AirFunk records are mostly numbered various artists 12-inch records. Maxime dB appears on AF003, AF004, AF005, AF006, AF007 and AF008 and on the 2026 special edition AFSE04, according to Yoyaku and Sound Shelter. AF007 Episode 1 is a four-way split with Paul Cut, FÆR and D'Julz.

### Playing abroad

MDLBeast's profile says he has played clubs and festivals in France, the USA, Japan, Portugal, Germany and the Czech Republic. We could not confirm individual dates for those shows.

### Amsterdam Dance Event

ADE's directory lists him as an artist and speaker entry for France. The page does not name a venue, stage or date for him, and ADE 2026 ran from 21 to 25 October, so we cannot say he played ADE. For the event itself, see our [ADE 2026 30th anniversary preview](/blog/ade-2026-30th-anniversary).

## Which labels is Maxime dB associated with?

- **AirFunk.** The Paris label he co-runs. Its first record, AF001, dated 2022 by Sound Shelter, features Sweely, Arnic and Aline Umber, and Maxime dB first appears in its listings on AF003 in 2023.
- **Pager.** Sound Shelter lists "Silky Layers", a 2024 house release credited to Maxime Db with Phil Evans, Aline Umber, Markus Sommer and Rocky, on this label. Its profile text says he has released vinyl on labels including AirFunk and Pager.
- **Rings of Neptune and Velvet Spirit.** Set79 and MDLBeast say he has released on these labels, with Rings of Neptune given as his first release in 2020. We could not confirm either from a catalogue page, so we leave them out of our label facts.

## What is Maxime dB doing in 2026?

- **2026.** Echoes of Marco Attali (AFSE04), an AirFunk special edition credited on Sound Shelter to Maxime Db, David Triana and Marco Attali. Clone lists it with a date of 17 June 2026.
- **2026.** AirFunk 007, which Clone lists for 13 May 2026. Yoyaku shows Maxime dB on AF007 Episode 1, alongside Paul Cut, FÆR and D'Julz.
- **Upcoming.** Yoyaku shows AirFunk 008 (AF008) with Maxime dB among the credited artists. Clone shows it as upcoming and gives no date.
