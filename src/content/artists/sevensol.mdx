Sevensol is the artist name of Alexander Neuschulz, a house DJ and producer from Leipzig who co-founded the KANN label in 2008 and is a founding member of Long Vehicle. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 November 2024 and about 156 minutes long, has 54,585 YouTube views and ranks 47th of 178.

## Where is Sevensol from and how did he start?

Sevensol is the alias of Alexander Neuschulz, whom he calls Alex in a May 2020 interview with It's Delayed. The Ransom Note's label profile of KANN gives his name as Alexander Neuschulz, and says he started the label with two school friends, Jan Barich (map.ache) and Dennis Bender (Bender). In the It's Delayed interview he says he grew up in the 1990s on the outskirts of Leipzig and never left the city. He and friends threw small parties in their teens, most of his friends liked techno, and he began collecting CDs and records.

Set79 says he learned about records working behind the counter at Leipzig record shops and cites Chicago house and Detroit techno as his DJing influences. The Ransom Note says the KANN founders bonded through the Hochschule für Grafik und Buchkunst Leipzig community, and that they were booked by Nachtdigital's Steffen Bennemann to open the festival's Tent Stage in 2008. Our [Nachtdigital festival page](/festivals/nachtdigital) covers that event. If you want the wider picture of the shop culture he came up in, see our [guide to techno and electronic record shops](/blog/best-techno-and-electronic-record-shops-2026).

Base is not entirely settled. Set79, It's Delayed and The Ransom Note's KANN pieces place him in Leipzig, but a Ransom Note mix profile, which carries no date, says that mix was recorded in his Kreuzberg apartment in Berlin. We use Leipzig, and note that he appears to spend time in Berlin as well.

## What does Sevensol sound like?

In the It's Delayed interview he describes his house as repetitive, built on simple percussive rhythms, usually between 120 and 138 BPM, with a fondness for 2000s records with UK bleep and electro influences, cinematic samples and a psychedelic streak. The Ransom Note says his productions are often deep, pensive and emotional, and that with Bender he makes stripped-back, textural music. As a DJ, it says, he moves through several genres with ethereal undertones. His own one-line summary of his sound is a box of drums and stabs glued together with swaying basslines and effects.

Resident Advisor's 2012 review of his record with Bender links their sound to Smallville and Aim as well as to KANN: deep, swirling house with interlocking basslines and bubbling synths.

## What are the career highlights of Sevensol?

### Producing with Bender

Sevensol's best-documented production partnership is with Bender, a school friend who is the producer on their joint tracks, according to It's Delayed. Their Sleepers / Poland 12" came out on Stretchcat in 2010, per the Oye Records listing, and How Not to Lose Things appeared on KANN in August 2012. Resident Advisor reviewed that record in the same month. Set79 singles out "Live At Coliseum" as the pair's standout track, while The Ransom Note spells it "Live at Coloseum" and counts it among KANN's first releases.

### Manamana with map.ache

With map.ache he plays as the duo Manamana, which The Ransom Note calls a KANN project. The It's Delayed interview mentions a five-hour Manamana set at Objekt Klein A in Dresden on 7 March 2020, and says several later dates that spring were cancelled because of COVID-19. The same interview names De School and several Nachtdigital festivals among his gigs, and Set79 describes him as a Nachtdigital resident. We could not find a festival source that states the residency, so treat that as Set79's claim.

### Mixes and interviews

The Ransom Note ran a Sevensol mix in its series, with Regal86's "La Onda" named as his favourite track in it. In the interview he says the first 15 minutes of a set matter most to him.

## Which labels is Sevensol associated with?

**KANN** is his main label. Its founders are Sevensol, map.ache and Bender, and The Ransom Note says it was run from Leipzig and also operated a record shop, taking over the premises of a store called Freezone. KANN's Bandcamp page says it was established in 2008 and lists Berlin as its base, which differs from the Ransom Note's Leipzig. The label's first release in 2008 was a double 12" of six tracks, according to The Ransom Note, while Set79 says the first release was The Big Easy. The sources do not agree on that point, and we have not tried to settle it.

**Long Vehicle** is a second label that Sevensol founded with Marc Schnubbzer, per It's Delayed. Its first release arrived in April 2020, with tracks by Harry Wills, Mbius and Tim S, and the label also runs a monthly radio show and events. The spelling of Schnubbzer varies between sources.

**Stretchcat** released his Sleepers / Poland 12" with Bender in 2010. We did not research the label further. The It's Delayed interview adds that map.ache works closely with the Giegling family, a collective covered on our [Edward Giegling page](/artists/edward-giegling).

He is Alexander Neuschulz, a Leipzig house DJ and producer, co-founder of KANN and a founding member of Long Vehicle. He also plays with map.ache as Manamana.
