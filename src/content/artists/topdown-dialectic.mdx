Every Topdown Dialectic track since 2013 has followed the same rule: no title, no credit, and for most of that decade-plus run, exactly five minutes long. Resident Advisor's reviewer of the project's second Peak Oil record put the effect plainly: "We don't know how much of this music is manual, deliberate, or the result of some complex piece of programming." That uncertainty is not an accident. It is the entire design.

## A Decade Without a Face

Topdown Dialectic's first release was a self-titled cassette on the Chicago label Tailings in 2013, described at the time as sitting somewhere between Basic Channel, Actress, and Jan Jelinek's Faitiche project. More tapes followed through the mid-2010s on Aught, a small, DIY-leaning crew imprint, before the project settled at Peak Oil from 2018 onward: a self-titled album, then Vol. 2 in 2019 and Vol. 3 in 2021. Every one of those records shipped with the same absence of information, no artist photo, no interview, no confirmed hometown. In 2024, the project co-founded a new label, False Aralia, alongside Brian Foote of Peak Oil.

## A Name, Attached Almost as an Afterthought

In 2026, alongside the announcement of a new album, press coverage confirmed the person behind the alias as Izaak Schlossman, a producer who reportedly performed live under the Topdown Dialectic name for the first time in 2025. But the reveal did not function as a conventional unmasking. Inverted Audio's review called it "more of a red herring than revelation," a framing that matches the music's own logic: knowing a name attached to the project changes almost nothing about how it sounds, because the sound was never built around a personality in the first place.

## False LP A

Released July 3, 2026 on False Aralia, False LP A gathers sixteen untitled tracks, numbered one through sixteen, running exactly 80 minutes across a double LP. Shawn Reynaldo's First Floor newsletter described the record as work that "blithely defies even loosely defined convention," pulling together material that reportedly spans roughly a decade of production into one sustained, unbroken listen. The label credits on the release itself are almost as spare as the tracklist: sound by "IS," design by Nick Almquist.

## FAQ

### Who is Topdown Dialectic?

Topdown Dialectic is an anonymous techno project that has released untitled, uncredited music since 2013. Press coverage in 2026 identified the producer behind it as Izaak Schlossman, though the project's public identity remains deliberately minimal.

### Why doesn't Topdown Dialectic use track titles?

The project has never used song titles or public credits on any release, a consistent design choice across more than a decade of output that keeps the listener's attention on the sound itself rather than on the artist behind it.

### What is False LP A?

False LP A is a 2026 album released July 3 on False Aralia, sixteen untitled tracks across a double LP running exactly 80 minutes, gathering material that reportedly spans roughly a decade of work.

### What labels has Topdown Dialectic released on?

Tailings (2013 debut cassette), Aught (mid-2010s cassette releases), Peak Oil (three albums, 2018 to 2021), and False Aralia, a label the project co-founded in 2024.

### Has Topdown Dialectic performed live?

According to press coverage from 2026, the project's first live performance took place in 2025, more than a decade after its first release.
