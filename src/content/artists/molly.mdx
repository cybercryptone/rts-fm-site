Molly is the stage name of Emeline Ginestet, a French DJ and producer who became a Rex Club resident in Paris, runs the RDV Music label and has released on AKU, Giegling and Groovement. Her Yoyaku instore session, published in June 2017, has more than 108,000 views on YouTube.

## Where is Molly from and how did she start?

Molly is French. Yoyaku's artist page lists her as Emeline Ginestet, also known as Emeline, and XLR8R's 2020 podcast notes and Groove's 2022 podcast interview use the same name. Groove says she began buying vinyl in Toulouse, where she studied before moving on. XLR8R's 2018 interview says she studied law and economics there.

Her route into dance music ran through London. XLR8R says she discovered house and techno on a summer trip, going to the club The End, and Groove names Erol Alkan's TRASH night as a draw. Back in France, she and a friend bought turntables and played bars with a mix of rock, disco and electronic records. Groove adds that she later found minimal techno through [Richie Hawtin](/artists/richie-hawtin).

Her Mixcloud page shows sets from as early as 2012 and a Concrete recording dated 17 March 2013. Trommel's July 2018 profile says she never set out to become a DJ and began by playing for friends in Paris bars, keeping music as a weekend activity. She then worked at Rex Club in Paris, where XLR8R says she was head of communications until 2017. Groove describes a wider role covering press, communication and, for her last three years, bookings only.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state her age. A 2020 interviewer remarked that 23 was young, but that does not fit the timeline of sets and jobs above, so we have not used it.

## What does Molly sound like?

MUTEK's artist page describes her work as house and deep techno. Piknic Electronik's bio adds that her sets are shaped by digging for rare records and mix house with techno. XLR8R's notes for her podcast 630 say she selects by feeling rather than genre, and the mix runs through deep house, house, deep techno and breaks.

Her records show the same range. The Delayed premiere of her 2023 Introspections EP describes breakbeat rhythms, acid bass lines and two ambient pieces. Her 2022 Favorite Cocktails EP is described by the Kilómetro 4.5 Bandcamp page as deep house with lush, smooth textures. In a 2020 interview she said she uses the name Emeline for techno releases on the AEX label.

## What are the career highlights of Molly?

### Rex Club, HEAD_ON and the Paris years

XLR8R's May 2018 interview calls her a long-running Rex Club resident and says she hosts a monthly party there called HEAD_ON. Trommel's profile says she plays back-to-back sets with tINI at Rex, Robert Johnson, [Sonar](/festivals/sonar) and in Chicago, and that the pair were also making tracks together. Piknic Electronik's artist page lists her shows on 17 June 2018 and 6 September 2015.

### Remixes, AKU and RDV Music

XLR8R's May 2017 announcement of her debut says she had already built a name through remixes for Rekids, Karlovak and All Inn. Her debut solo EP, SB Relief, came out on Yoyaku's AKU sub-label as AKU007, which XLR8R dated to 16 June 2017. She launched her own label, RDV Music (Récit de Voyage), in 2017, according to XLR8R. Trommel says it signs music from artists she meets while travelling, and Groove's October 2022 piece gave it nine releases and three compilations.

### Giegling and Groovement

XLR8R says Giegling signed her after she sent them music, and the four-track Waves EP followed in 2018. Torturetheartist's 2020 interview ties it to her breakthrough. She has since released on Groovement in Lisbon, with Introspections premiered on 4 July 2023, which the Delayed premiere calls her first release on the label in five years.

### Chapelle XIV and the MUTEK showcase

Resident Advisor reported on 9 October 2020 that Yoyaku's new Paris space, Chapelle XIV, had opened the week before with a [MUTEK](/festivals/mutek) Montreal showcase featuring Bambounou, Molly and NSDOS. RA's article does not describe which Molly this was. MUTEK's own artist page for Molly describes a French DJ and producer who has released on Yoyaku, Giegling and Groovement, which matches the artist profiled here.

### Cargese Sound Festival and Corsica

Trommel says she was artistic director of the Cargese Sound Festival in Corsica in 2018, her second year in the role. In 2018 XLR8R said she split her time between Corsica and Paris. By 2020 she was living in Barcelona, according to torturetheartist and XLR8R. Groove's October 2022 interview says she had become a mother and opened a hotel in Corsica with her husband, and the label description for her March 2024 stólar EP says she now lives on the island. We have left out further family details.

## Which labels is Molly associated with?

- **RDV Music.** Her own label, launched in 2017. Yoyaku lists releases including a remix of Dana Ruh's 4 Leaf Clover on RDV04 and her own Thoughts of a day on RDV09.
- **AKU.** Yoyaku's sub-label, home to her debut EP SB Relief, AKU's seventh release per Yoyaku.
- **Giegling.** Released Waves, catalogued GLG22 by Yoyaku.
- **Groovement.** A Lisbon label, with Introspections and Apostrophe listed by Yoyaku.
- **Kilómetro 4.5.** A Mexican label, home to Favorite Cocktails in 2022.
- **Stólar.** A Berlin label, with On The Road Again and Island Flow.
- **Remix credits.** XLR8R lists Rekids, Karlovak and All Inn. On her 2023 RDV EP, [Fred P](/artists/fred-p) and Koreander remixed her tracks.
- **Emeline.** Her alias for techno, with AEX named by torturetheartist.

## FAQ

### Who is Molly?

Molly is Emeline Ginestet, a French DJ, producer and label owner. She was a Rex Club resident in Paris and runs the RDV Music label.

### How old is Molly?

We could not verify her age. None of the sources we opened gives a birth year or date, and a passing remark in one 2020 interview does not fit the rest of her timeline.

### Where is Molly from?

She is French, according to XLR8R and MUTEK. Groove says she began buying vinyl in Toulouse, and she later lived in Paris, Barcelona and Corsica.

A March 2024 stólar release description says she lives in Corsica. Groove's 2022 interview says she opened a hotel there. Earlier sources placed her in Paris and Barcelona.
