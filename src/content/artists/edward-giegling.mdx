Edward Giegling is the name Yoyaku used for Edward, the Berlin-based DJ and producer Gilles Aiken, a long-standing member of the Weimar collective Giegling. He is known for psychedelic, krautrock-tinged house and minimal records, and his Yoyaku instore session, published on 22 August 2025, has 161,201 YouTube views.

## Where is Edward Giegling from and how did he start?

"Giegling" is not a surname. It is the collective and label he belongs to, and Yoyaku put it in the video title after his name. We matched the person in three ways. Yoyaku's own artist page for Edward credits him as Gilles Aiken, based in Berlin, and lists his Giegling records. The video title carries the label name. Decoded Magazine's write-up of the session describes the same artist: a Berlin producer born Gilles Aiken, whose work sits at the psychedelic end of the Giegling sound.

XLR8R's podcast page of 23 August 2016 says he was working from "his native Germany" and that it was coming up to a decade since his first production, which puts his start at roughly 2006. It calls him a Giegling and White artist. Resident Advisor's podcast page for RA.458, published on 9 March 2015, says he became friends with the Giegling crew through music and plays back-to-back sets with his friend Oskar Offermann. We found no birthplace, birth date or account of his first records and first DJ sets.

## What does Edward Giegling sound like?

Resident Advisor describes dark, dreamlike club music built mostly on house and techno, with cosmic and experimental touches. On RA.458 he named Brian Eno and Robert Fripp and krautrock producers such as Conny Plank among his reference points, and said repetitive and ambient qualities draw him in across genres, from jazz to disco.

Kiosk Radio calls his tracks dark club music with a surreal edge and his sets groove-heavy, and lists kraut, minimal and house as his genres. Set79, a tracklist site, adds deep techno and ambient and says his music leans on manipulated samples and edits. Decoded Magazine says he has cited krautrock as a guide, tracing a line from Can's repetitive grooves to techno loops, and that his early DJing drew on Latin jazz, funk and soul. XLR8R in 2016 described his other alias, Desert Sky, as more repetitive and grounded than Edward.

## What are the career highlights of Edward Giegling?

### Giegling and Panorama Bar

XLR8R said in 2016 that he played regularly at Giegling's Panorama Bar residency and at Robert Johnson, as well as clubs outside Germany. Resident Advisor's 2017 announcement of Waking Life, the Portuguese festival where Giegling curated a stage, names him in the lineup. Our [Waking Life festival page](/festivals/waking-life) covers the event. Set79 lists recordings from Waking Life in 2022 and 2024, Fusion in 2024 and [Houghton](/festivals/houghton-festival) in 2025. Those are listing titles, not event confirmations.

### Mixes and radio

RA.458 is a 2015 studio mix built from records he sampled and edited while making his album. XLR8R's 2016 podcast is a 75-minute home mix recorded on two turntables and two CDJs, moving from broken beats and house to acid rave and techno. Set79 also indexes a Boiler Room Berlin studio set, which it describes as trippy and hypnotic. It gives no date. Set79 lists radio sets for The Lot Radio on 5 July 2025 and Kiosk Radio on 14 September 2025.

### Conny Plank stems

Resident Advisor says he remixed material from Conny Plank's archive of original tape stems, and that the work shaped his later music. We did not open a release page for that project, so we do not name it.

## Which labels is Edward Giegling associated with?

Giegling is his home. Yoyaku describes it as a Weimar label founded in 2009 that designs its own limited covers. Inverted Audio says the collective began with parties in an abandoned building, that the club was demolished in 2008 and that the label grew out of that loss. It calls Edward a long-standing member.

He also records for White, which RA calls a Berlin label and Set79 ties to Oskar Offermann, and for Die Orakel, which Kiosk Radio places in Frankfurt. Other labels on Yoyaku's artist page include Trelik, Delsin, Running Back, Club Blanco, Smallville Records, Kalahari Oyster Cult and Needs, but the page gives no release years, so we do not date them. XLR8R mentioned a track on Traffic Records in 2016.

## FAQ

### How old is Edward Giegling?

We could not find a birth year or date in any source we opened, so we do not state an age. XLR8R said in 2016 it was coming up to a decade since his first production.

### Where is Edward Giegling from?

XLR8R described him in 2016 as working from his "native Germany". Resident Advisor, Kiosk Radio and Yoyaku all place him in Berlin.

### Who is Edward Giegling?

He is Edward, the Berlin DJ and producer Gilles Aiken, a long-standing member of the Weimar collective Giegling. Giegling is the label and crew, not a surname.

### What is Edward Giegling's real name?

Resident Advisor, XLR8R, Kiosk Radio and Yoyaku all give his real name as Gilles Aiken.

### What is Edward Giegling's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 22 August 2025, runs about 70 minutes and has 161,201 views, 3,630 likes and 250 comments as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 11th by views among 178 sessions.

### Which label is Edward Giegling on?

Mainly Giegling, with records on White, Die Orakel and Trelik too. Yoyaku lists Giegling albums from Into A Better Future, from 2014, to Turning, from 2021.

### What does Edward Giegling play?

Psychedelic, krautrock-influenced house, minimal and deep techno, with ambient and experimental touches, according to Resident Advisor and Kiosk Radio.

### Who does Edward Giegling play with?

Resident Advisor names Oskar Offermann as a friend and back-to-back partner. Resident Advisor also calls him a regular on the White label.

### Is Edward Giegling the same as Edward Maya?

No. Set79's "Stereo Love" entries on his page belong to Edward Maya, a different artist.
