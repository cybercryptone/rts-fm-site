Indira Paganotto is a Spanish DJ and producer, born in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, who plays a driving, psytrance-tinged style of techno she and her press call psy-techno. She runs the ARTCORE label, released an album, Arte Como Amante, in 2026, and is listed on the bill for [Time Warp Mannheim 2027](/blog/time-warp-mannheim-2027-lineup).

## Where is Indira Paganotto from and how did she start?

Wikipedia and Wikidata give her birth date as 30 May 1992 and her birthplace as Las Palmas de Gran Canaria in the Canary Islands, which makes her 34 as of November 2026. We found no second source for the exact date, so treat it as Wikipedia's figure. Amsterdam Dance Event's profile independently says she was born in 1992 and describes her as a Spanish artist.

Music came from home. According to Noctis Magazine, her father was a DJ in the 1980s who collected disco, acid house, jazz and Goa music, and in an interview with FAZEmag she said he had a strong influence on her.

ADE's profile says Ian Pooley discovered her at a warm-up set in Gran Canaria and that she signed three vinyl releases to his label, Pooled Music, at 17. After those first releases she moved to Madrid to work at a larger scale as a DJ and producer, per the same profile. Femmusic adds that the work that became her 2026 album began in Madrid in 2012, when she was 19.

## What does Indira Paganotto sound like?

Her sets are energetic and danceable. ADE's profile calls her the driving force behind a new sound it labels psy-techno, and Noctis says she has spent more than a decade blending psytrance with techno. Wikipedia describes her sets as drawing on 1990s elements, with traces of psytrance alongside underground techno.

She does not stay in one lane. In the FAZEmag interview she said she tends to focus on one style for a while before moving on, and at that point was drawn to 1980s synthesisers, metal and rock. 

## What are the career highlights of Indira Paganotto?

Wikipedia lists Tomorrowland in 2022, Ikarus and Exit in 2023 and a headline slot at Airbeat One in 2024. It also records a third-place finish for "Breakthrough International" in the FAZEmag poll in 2022 and first place in 2023, and entries in the DJ Mag Top 100 DJs at No. 97 in 2023 and No. 49 in 2024.

### Collaborations

With Nina Kraviz she released "White Horse" on Kraviz's Trip label in 2023, her first collaboration with Kraviz, according to ADE. See our [Nina Kraviz profile](/artists/nina-kraviz) for that side of the story.

"Mantra", a two-track EP with [Joseph Capriati](/artists/joseph-capriati) on ARTCORE, appeared in June 2024 with the tracks "Ananda" and "Mantra", according to Beatportal, which calls it their first collaboration.

"Dragon", a single with Armin van Buuren, came out on Armada Music in 2025, as Mixmag NL reported on 13 June 2025. The coverage tied it to their first back-to-back set at [Sónar](/festivals/sonar) in Barcelona.

## Which labels is Indira Paganotto associated with?

ARTCORE is her own label, launched in 2022 according to Wikipedia, which cites The Groove Cartel's launch coverage. She told FAZEmag it is a platform for younger artists who do not want to be confined to one genre, and the brand also has a fashion line. ADE credits it with shaping psy-techno across its first 15 releases. Beatportal and Noctis both name "Mantra" as an ARTCORE release, and Noctis calls it the label's eleventh.

Other labels in her catalogue, per Wikipedia and ADE:

- **Pooled Music:** Ian Pooley's label, home to her first vinyl.
- **Frequentza:** her 2018 album The Imaginarium.
- **Octopus Recordings:** the 2021 album Invisible Connections.
- **KNTXT:** [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte)'s label, for the Himalaya (2021) and Lions Of God (2022) EPs.
- **Trip:** Nina Kraviz's label, for "White Horse".
- **Armada Music:** the Armin van Buuren single "Dragon".
