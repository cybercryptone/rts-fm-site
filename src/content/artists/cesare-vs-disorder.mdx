Cesare vs Disorder is the solo name of Cesare Marchese, an Italian-Polish house and breaks producer and DJ based in São Paulo, who runs Serialism Records and has a residency at D-Edge. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 14 November 2023, runs 157 minutes and has 36,016 views.

## Where is Cesare vs Disorder from and how did he start?

Despite the "vs" in the name, this is not a duo. XLR8R calls Cesare vs Disorder the solo alias of the Italian producer Cesare Marchese, and Volumo, Partyflock and Bandcamp all give the real name as Cesare Marchese. Sources disagree slightly on nationality. Partyflock and Bandcamp say Italian. Volumo, Yoyaku and the Brazilian site DJ News describe him as Italian-Polish.

According to Volumo and DJ News, he lived in Milan, then London for almost a decade, then Berlin, before settling in São Paulo. Partyflock says he moved to Berlin in 2010 after many years in London and held a residency at the Berlin club Kater Holzig. An older biography on the Amsterdam Dance Event artist page, which does not date itself, agrees on London and Berlin. It also credits him with a debut at the Detroit Electronic Music Festival and showcases at [Sónar](/festivals/sonar) Barcelona, without giving years. We found no source saying when he moved to Brazil.

Electronic Groove's premiere text for his debut album says he trained in classical music and began DJing in the late 1990s, taking in hip hop, indie rock, reggae and drum and bass before modern jazz, bossa nova, dub and electronica shaped his taste. DJ News adds that he studied classical piano and live drums.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state his age.

## What does Cesare vs Disorder sound like?

The sound has changed with the cities. The Suleiman EP of April 2022 was described by When We Dip as mixing UK garage-style two-step, acid bass and a dubby downtempo piece. Flow Music, covering the 2023 follow-up, called his approach a forward-thinking and quirky take on house, with jazzy chords, looped piano, shuffling percussion, warped synths and breakbeats over stripped-back grooves.

Resident Advisor's review of his 2018 Vakant EP placed it in house and minimal, and heard [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) in the track "Desorientado". For the debut album Antidote, Palms Out Sounds heard UK-inspired tracks, deeper club music, acid jazz and a Brazilian-tinged Detroit electro cut. His Bandcamp tags include house, techno, minimal, breakbeat and downtempo.

## What are the career highlights of Cesare vs Disorder?

### London and Berlin years, 2009 to 2015

The ADE biography says Beatport named him one of its "Ones to Watch" in 2009 and that his track "Mean Land" (Thema) appeared that year on the Cocoon compilation Sound of the Tenth Season, mixed by [Sven Väth](/artists/sven-vath). See our [Cocoon Recordings timeline](/blog/cocoon-recordings-history-timeline) for the label. In March 2010 he released the Battle For Mouths EP with the Catalan producer Rainer on Mean, which Oye Records lists as the label's ninth release and as a return to his home imprint. The Yoyaku listing for his Wide Close Up EP, Serialism's 21st release, includes a collaboration with Quenum and a remix by [Maayan Nidam](/artists/maayan-nidam).

In May 2015 XLR8R premiered "Vortex" from Vortex To Unreason, a four-track Vakant EP that came out in late April, with remixes by [Cristi Cons](/artists/cristi-cons) and Fango.

### Ararapira Jazz and the move into film work, 2018 to 2022

XLR8R covered the Ararapira Jazz EP on 16 February 2018 as his first Vakant release since 2015, with a Spacetravel remix on the vinyl. Resident Advisor reviewed it on 26 February 2018. DJ News says he also composes for film, television, shorts and documentaries, and that some of that work has won awards at film festivals. We did not verify which ones.

### Suleiman, Antidote and D-Edge, 2022 to 2024

Two EPs on the Canadian label Suleiman followed, in April 2022 and April 2023, the second being New Order. In February 2023 he and Anderson Noise released the two-track Quartel on Bandcamp under a D-Edge Rec title. On 17 July 2023 Serialism issued Antidote, his debut album. Electronic Groove lists collaborators including Cristi Cons, San Proper, Rockey Washington, Mike Ladd and Weg, who is Serialism's A&R. DJ News billed him in May 2024 for a night at D-Edge in São Paulo on 29 May, in a back-to-back set with Lu.cian.

## Which labels is Cesare vs Disorder associated with?

- **Serialism Records.** The label he founded. Bandcamp and Yoyaku credit him as founder, and Yoyaku lists his Wide Close Up EP (SER021), Broken EP with Onirik (SER017) and the Antidote double LP (SERLP002).
- **Mean.** The earlier imprint behind Battle For Mouths. Partyflock says Serialism was previously Mean Records.
- **Morning People.** A second label he founded, per Volumo, Bandcamp and Yoyaku. We found little detail on its output.
- **Vakant.** The Berlin label of Vortex To Unreason (2015) and Ararapira Jazz (2018).
- **Suleiman.** The Canadian label of his 2022 and 2023 EPs.
- **D-Edge Records.** His Bandcamp lists Sonhando Acordato, with a Ratier remix, and Quartel on D-Edge labels.
- **Others.** Bandcamp also lists Robot Backpack on [Rawax](/blog/rawax-records-robert-drewek), Golden Gate Club Records, Seaweed Records, Sight Records and Synthetic Gold. Volumo, Partyflock and press bios name Bpitch Control, Get Physical, Transmat, Crosstown Rebels, Bedrock, Ninja Tune's Big Dada and Watergate, without dating those releases.

## FAQ

### How old is Cesare vs Disorder?

We could not verify his age. No source we opened gives a birth date or year. Electronic Groove says he began DJing in the late 1990s.

### Where is Cesare vs Disorder from?

He is Italian, and several outlets call him Italian-Polish. He lived in Milan, London and Berlin and is now based in São Paulo, per Volumo, DJ News and Bandcamp.

### Who is Cesare vs Disorder?

Cesare vs Disorder is the solo project of Cesare Marchese, a DJ, producer, composer and sound engineer. He founded Serialism Records and Morning People, and is a D-Edge resident, per DJ News.

### Is Cesare vs Disorder a duo?

No. Despite the name, XLR8R describes it as a solo alias. He has a separate project, Azimute, with Quenum.

### What is Cesare vs Disorder's real name?

Cesare Marchese, according to Volumo, Partyflock, Bandcamp and Yoyaku. He also records as Queen Atom, Cez, Sire Cactus and Strangers in Heaven.

### What is Cesare vs Disorder's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 14 November 2023, runs 157 minutes and has 36,016 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 66th by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Which label did Cesare vs Disorder start?

Serialism Records, plus Morning People, per Bandcamp and Yoyaku. His earlier Mean imprint also released his records, including Battle For Mouths in 2010.

### What is Cesare vs Disorder's debut album?

Antidote, released on Serialism Records on 17 July 2023, per Electronic Groove. Palms Out Sounds called it his first full-length.

### What does Cesare vs Disorder play?

Stripped-back house, breakbeat and minimal, with jazz, dub, downtempo and Brazilian touches, per Flow Music and When We Dip.
