Nicole, credited as Nicole Popkhadze, is a Georgian DJ and producer born in Batumi and listed as based in Berlin. She is known for acid techno, minimal and electro, and released her debut EP on Marco Shuttle's Eerie label in 2020. Her Yoyaku instore session, published on 28 November 2024, has 36,486 views.

## Where is Nicole from and how did she start?

Several sources agree on the basics. Yoyaku's artist page gives her name as Nicole Popkhadze and calls her a Georgian DJ and music producer based in Berlin. The Eerie Records Bandcamp page for her debut says she was born in Batumi, the Black Sea port in western Georgia, and describes her as a Tbilisi-based debut artist, around 22 at the time, with a residency at the club Cafe-Gallery. Refuge Worldwide and the HÖR Berlin artist page both repeat the Batumi origin.

Those two profiles also give the early story. She took music lessons as a child and played drums in a band. At 17 she began performing in a duo called Body Thrills, then went solo and built her own DJ style. Refuge Worldwide adds that she worked as a promoter at Cafe Gallery in Tbilisi.

Her base is not stated consistently. Eerie (2020) and Patterns of Perception (September 2021) place her in Tbilisi. Yoyaku, Refuge Worldwide and Trommel (December 2025) say Berlin, and Trommel adds that she plays Berlin clubs including Kater and Tresor. HÖR's page says Kyiv, which appears to be an outlier. For the city's venues see our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide).

## What does Nicole sound like?

Refuge Worldwide and HÖR describe her sets as shaped by the energy of the dancefloor, drawing on old-school acid techno and minimal sounds. Patterns of Perception, which published her mix in September 2021, adds intelligent minimal and experimental styles and calls her sets energetic and emotive.

Her records are broader than a techno DJ's. XLR8R and Eerie describe her debut EP as four tracks that blend Detroit and IDM elements, and Eerie adds that they are more like songs than conventional tracks. Oye Records says her contribution to Partisan's third split EP includes her own vocals, over stripped-back electro with leftfield turns. Trommel's December 2025 free download is billed as "moody electronics", with deep bass, menacing synths and sultry vocals. Yoyaku tags her releases as electro, minimal, techno and ambient.

## What are the career highlights of Nicole?

### Bassiani, Eerie and Hot Steel, 2018 to 2021

Refuge Worldwide, HÖR and Patterns of Perception all tie her breakthrough to a Boiler Room set at Bassiani, held when the Tbilisi club reopened after the 2018 rave protests that followed police raids on the city's clubs. They credit that set with making her one of Georgia's leading DJs and producers. We did not find a recording or press report of the set itself, so we cannot give its date or length.

XLR8R announced her debut EP, *Inside*, on Marco Shuttle's Eerie imprint on 10 December 2019. It describes the record as her first official release and gives release dates of 28 January 2020 on vinyl and 5 February 2020 on digital. Bandcamp lists 3 February 2020, so the sources differ by a few days. Patterns of Perception, Refuge Worldwide and HÖR also say her track "Luna" appeared on Hot Steel, the compilation series that [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) runs on her трип label. Patterns of Perception places that in 2021.

### Vacuity Records, 2023 to 2024

Her track "Lies in Your Head" appears on *Synchronicity* (VACU001), the first release on Vacuity Records, which Sound Shelter dates to 7 December 2023. Her track "Jasmine" is on the follow-up, *Order Behind Chaos* (VACU002), dated 6 September 2024 on Bandcamp and 7 September on KMA60. Both pages call her a resident artist of Vacuity and link her to "Satz", though they do not explain whether that is an alias, a collective or something else.

### Cath and Partisan, 2025

Trommel premiered "Woo Woo" from the *Out of Control* EP, shared with Troy Vladislavovich and the second release on Cath Records (CATH002), on 7 March 2025. She also shares *Split EP 3* (PTN015C) on Partisan with the Ukrainian producer, listed by one retailer on 3 October 2025. Trommel says her music has drawn praise from [Sonja Moonear](/artists/sonja-moonear), Marco Shuttle and Tunik.

## Which labels is Nicole associated with?

- **Eerie Records.** Marco Shuttle's imprint, launched in 2012 according to XLR8R, which released her debut EP *Inside* (EE15).
- **Vacuity Records.** A small French electro and minimal label. Nicole contributed to its first two releases and is called a resident artist.
- **Cath Records.** Trommel describes it as a home for the darker end of minimal techno and electro. She released the *Out of Control* EP there with Troy Vladislavovich.
- **Partisan.** Her split with Troy Vladislavovich is the third EP in the label's split series. Trommel connects the imprint to Anthea.
- **трип.** Her track appeared on a Hot Steel compilation, which is a compilation appearance and not a label release of her own.
