Satoshi Tomiie is a Tokyo-born DJ and producer, based in New York, known for the 1989 house record "Tears", made with Frankie Knuckles, and for running the label Abstract Architecture. His Yoyaku instore session with Tomoki Tamura, published in December 2024, has 782,636 YouTube views and ranks second among all Yoyaku sessions.

## Where is Satoshi Tomiie from and how did he start?

Wikipedia says he was born on 22 November 1966 in Tokyo and calls him a Japanese DJ and record producer. A 1994 DJ Mag interview, reprinted by 909originals, also gives Tokyo as his birthplace and describes him as an accomplished pianist and jazz musician. Wikipedia carries maintenance tags for sourcing and promotional tone, and no other source we opened states a birth date. That would make him 60 as of 24 November 2026, if the date is right. Other sources fit the year: XLR8R says he was about 20 when "Tears" was made, and a 2019 IU Magazine interview says he moved to New York in the early 1990s at 24.

In that interview he traced his start to hip-hop around 1983, when he learned scratching and drum machines, then to house, which he taught himself to mix. He still owns the TR-909 he bought. His route to Knuckles ran through Japan: XLR8R says he made theme music for Knuckles' party series during a Japan tour around 1987 or 1988, and the 1994 interview says a promoter asked for the theme while Tomiie was working with the composer Ryuichi Sakamoto. Wikipedia adds that he toured as keyboard player for Sakamoto in the early 1990s.

## What does Satoshi Tomiie sound like?

His catalogue runs from vocal house to dub techno. Yoyaku tags his 2023 album Magic Hour as dub, dub techno and house, and Mixmag Asia describes his earlier releases as Sato as dub and ambient. The joint records with Tomoki Tamura sit closer to deep and minimal house, according to the Yoyaku listings.

He also works live. In a 2019 Magnetic Mag interview he described a mostly improvised, computer-free hour on a Eurorack modular setup at Paris's La Gaîté Lyrique. The A_A project with Nao Gunji, billed as dub techno and ambient experimental, is built from studio jams reworked on stage, according to 909originals.

## What are the career highlights of Satoshi Tomiie?

### "Tears" and the Def Mix years

According to XLR8R's oral history, Tomiie wrote and recorded the instrumental in Tokyo on two-inch tape and sent it to New York, where Knuckles directed the vocal session and Robert Owens sang. It came out on FFRR in 1989. Wikipedia says it was co-produced with Knuckles and calls it an instant club hit, a claim it does not source. The 1994 interview says he then joined the Def Mix roster alongside David Morales and Hector Romero. Wikipedia and ADE both say he has remixed U2, Mariah Carey and David Bowie.

### Albums and mixes

XLR8R's 2015 Q&A calls New Day his first artist album since Full Lick, which Wikipedia dates to 2000 and XLR8R places in 1999. Wikipedia lists a Global Underground Nu Breed 6 mix from 2002 and Renaissance: The Masters Series, Part 9 from 2007. Magic Hour followed in November 2023, billed as his first solo album under his own name in eight years.

### Labels of his own

SAW Recordings, which he started with Hector Romero in 2001 according to XLR8R, came first. Abstract Architecture was brand new in July 2015, and Tomiie described it to XLR8R as a platform for projects rather than a conventional label. A_A is the duo project with Nao Gunji.

## Which labels is Satoshi Tomiie associated with?

His own imprints are Abstract Architecture, SAW Recordings (with Hector Romero) and A_A. Yoyaku lists him on its Yoyakuza series, with YOYAKUZA002 and YOYAKUZA003, and on 20/20 Vision, Luck Of Access, Kaoz Theory, DubWax and Vatos Locos. The TOMTOM series is a joint release with Tamura's Holic Trax. Chapelle XIV Music issued his 2022 Sato album, and Artificial Owl Recordings his A_A live EP.

## FAQ

### How old is Satoshi Tomiie?

Wikipedia gives his birth date as 22 November 1966, which makes him 60 as of 24 November 2026. No other source we opened states a full date, so treat the exact age with care.

### Where is Satoshi Tomiie from?

He was born in Tokyo, according to Wikipedia and a 1994 DJ Mag interview. His Bandcamp page lists New York as his location, and he told IU Magazine in 2019 that he moved there in the early 1990s.

### Who is Satoshi Tomiie?

A Japanese DJ and producer who co-created the 1989 house record "Tears" with Frankie Knuckles. He runs Abstract Architecture and co-runs SAW Recordings.

### What is Satoshi Tomiie's Yoyaku instore session?

His best known is the 7 December 2024 session with Tomoki Tamura, which has 782,636 YouTube views as of 24 November 2026. A second joint session followed on 10 July 2026.

### Who made "Tears" with Satoshi Tomiie?

Frankie Knuckles worked with him on it, and Robert Owens sang the vocal, according to XLR8R. It was released on FFRR in 1989.

### Which labels does Satoshi Tomiie run?

Abstract Architecture, A_A, and SAW Recordings, which he started with Hector Romero in 2001 according to XLR8R.

### Does Satoshi Tomiie work with Tomoki Tamura?

Yes. They have released joint 12-inch EPs in the TOMTOM series: Dream Sketches (2024), Indian Summer (2026) and Unfinished, due in February 2027.

### What is A_A?

A_A is a project with Nao Gunji, billed in 2024 as dub techno and ambient experimental sound. Its releases include the live EP Diskordant.

### What is Satoshi Tomiie's latest album?

Magic Hour, on Abstract Architecture, released in November 2023 according to XLR8R. It was his first solo album under his own name since New Day in 2015.

### Has Satoshi Tomiie released on Yoyaku?

Yoyaku lists him on its Yoyakuza series, and Soundshelter dates YOYAKUZA002 to 25 January 2018. Yoyaku also lists the TOMTOM EPs and Magic Hour.
