Admo is Adrien Mora, a producer and DJ born in Grenoble, France, and a member of the Ordinateuf collective. He is known for tech house and minimal house 12-inch records on Haŵs, Automatic Writing and Limousine Dream. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 October 2025, has 33,264 views.

## Where is Admo from and how did he start?

Yoyaku's artist page names him as Adrien Mora, a producer and DJ born in Grenoble, France, who is a member of the Ordinateuf collective and also records as VNZT. Discogs gives the same real name and alias. The credits on his 2023 Automatic Writing EP also read "Written & produced by Adrien Mora", which ties the stage name to the person.

The name Admo is short and easy to confuse with others, so the identity checks matter. The Yoyaku video description links the Instagram account @__admo, and Yoyaku's own artist page links the same account next to the instore session. That is how we connect the video to the Grenoble producer and not to any other act of that name.

Resident Advisor describes him in one line as a composer, producer and record player who has been playing "from Grenoble to Prague". Its page lists 2022 as his first event on RA. Discogs lists an earlier digital EP, Zajigalka, on the Grenoble student label Nymphony Records in 2018, and the Nymphony Records Bandcamp page names Admo among the label's artists. No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state his age.

## What does Admo sound like?

His records sit between tech house, minimal house and breakbeat. Yoyaku's store listings tag his releases with tech house, minimal house, breakbeat, trance and electro, depending on the record. The retailer 3345 in The Hague, repeating the label's press text, describes the Elysus EP as tech house built on minimal elements and classic vocal hooks, with acid lines, IDM-like clicks and a heavy bassline.

The press line for his 2022 debut on Ordinateuf, as reproduced by Coldcut Hotwax, calls it a "stomping" three-tracker that goes straight to the point. Resident Advisor lists live sets alongside DJ sets in his bookings, including a live billing at Hive in Newcastle in May 2025, so he performs both formats.

## What are the career highlights of Admo?

### Early records, 2018 to 2020

Discogs dates three early records to this period: Zajigalka (Nymphony Records, 2018), the Cyber Romance EP with Gab Jr. (Phonik, 2020) and Modern Issues (Substra, 2020). Yoyaku sold Modern Issues as an exclusive, and its listing tags the record with breakbeat, drum and bass, electro, jungle, UK garage and techno as well as house.

### Ordinateuf, Automatic Writing and Haŵs, 2022 to 2023

Burn The Soundsystem (ODTF001) was the first release on Ordinateuf. Sound Shelter gives its date as 13 June 2022, while Coldcut Hotwax's listing shows 01-08-2022, so the exact day is not settled, but the year is. Ordinateuf's SoundCloud page lists its locations as Paris, Prague, Berlin and São Paulo.

A Visible Spectrum (ATWT015) followed on Automatic Writing on 3 March 2023, according to Bandcamp. It has four tracks, was mixed and mastered by Digby Smith and was distributed by YYdistribution. Elysus (HAWS021) came out on Haŵs on 24 May 2023, per 3345. The label's text says it marked the start of a new phase for Haŵs and describes Admo as out of Grenoble but based in Berlin. Sasha's Spaceship (TSOL009) on Limousine Dream, a four-track 12-inch, is listed by Sound Shelter as a 2023 release.

### Bookings, 2024 to 2025

Resident Advisor's page, in a version archived in 2025, lists him on the bill at Gene On Earth's Rave 'n' Cruise at Zenner in Berlin on 8 February 2025, alongside [Craig Richards](/artists/craig-richards) and [Danny Daze](/artists/danny-daze). It also lists Ordinateuf's Alpyren release party in Paris on 30 November 2024, 303 in Barcelona on 22 February 2025 and Planet Paradigm in the Netherlands on 31 May 2025. These are event listings, and we did not find reports confirming each set.

## Which labels is Admo associated with?

- **Ordinateuf.** The collective and label he belongs to, per Yoyaku. His Burn The Soundsystem was its first release. He was also on the bill of its Alpyren release party in Paris, according to Resident Advisor.
- **Automatic Writing.** The Paris minimal house label that issued A Visible Spectrum. Resident Advisor lists it among his labels.
- **Haŵs.** Elysus is HAWS021, and Yoyaku lists his remix of Tifra's Terra Incognita as HAWS028.
- **Limousine Dream.** Sasha's Spaceship is TSOL009, a release in the label's numbered TSOL series.
- **Substra and Phonik.** Modern Issues and the Cyber Romance EP with Gab Jr., both listed by Discogs for 2020.
- **Nymphony Records.** The Grenoble student label that released Zajigalka in 2018.

Resident Advisor also lists Unsilenced Music among his labels. We could not find a release of his on it.

## What is Admo doing in 2026 and 2027?

The first lineup wave for the 2027 edition of [Dimensions Festival](/festivals/dimensions-festival), announced on 4 November 2026, names Admo among its live acts, according to Electronic Groove. The festival runs from 26 to 31 August 2027 in Tisno, Croatia. We found no new Admo release dated 2026 on a page we could open.
