Craig Richards is a British DJ, born in 1966 in Bournemouth, who made his name on the Saturday nights at London's fabric, where he has been a resident since the club opened in 1999. His sets are long, patient and genre-blind, moving between techno, dubby house, electro and breaks. Since 2017 he has also been the creative director of Houghton Festival in Norfolk.

## Where is Craig Richards from and how did he start?

Wikipedia gives his birth year as 1966 and his birthplace as Bournemouth, England. Apple Music and Shazam list 1965, so sources differ, though Mixmag's March 2018 age of 51 fits 1966. That makes him 59 or 60 in November 2026, depending on his birthday, which no source we checked gives.

Wikipedia says he studied illustration at Saint Martins School of Art in London, and describes him as a painter and photographer as well as a DJ. In a March 2018 Mixmag interview he said he moved to London in 1987, when acid house was still in its infancy, and that his outlook was shaped by acid house and art school.

## What does Craig Richards sound like?

fabric's own biography describes his sets as a mix of techno, dubby house, electro and breaks, played with a deep-digging approach.

Length matters as much as genre. At the first Houghton in 2017 he played more than 20 hours across the weekend, according to Mixmag and Trommel, including an eight-hour back-to-back with [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) and a back-to-back with Nicolas Lutz. That format, extended sets with room to change direction, is the thread running through both his club work and his festival.

## What are the career highlights of Craig Richards?

According to Wikipedia, he was part of the duo Tyrant with Lee Burridge, and initially Sasha. Tyrant's releases include a 2000 album on Distinct'ive Breaks and No Shoes, No Cake, a 2002 double CD on fabric.

### fabric and the Saturday residency

fabric says he has played over 700 Saturday nights at the club since 1999, and Wikipedia names him as a resident alongside Terry Francis and as one of the club's directors of music programming. Mixmag described the residency in 2017 as 17 years long. fabric's Wikipedia entry records that Islington Council revoked the club's licence on 7 September 2016 and that an agreement to reopen on stricter conditions was reached on 21 November 2016. Our [London clubs guide](/blog/best-london-clubs-2026-guide) and the piece on [UK nightclub closures and licensing reform](/blog/uk-nightclub-closures-licensing-reform) cover that history.

Mixmag reported that the closure was "devastating on many levels" for him and that it pushed him towards a festival of his own. In March 2018 he announced he was stepping back from the weekly slot. fabric said he remained "the cornerstone of the club" and a resident, with a monthly night in which he invites peers into the booth. Nicolas Lutz and DMX Krew were the first guests, per Mixmag. fabric is still running a full programme, including its July 2026 day at Junction 2, covered in our [Nina Kraviz and Jeff Mills fabric report](/blog/nina-kraviz-jeff-mills-fabric-junction-2), but check the club's listings for Richards's own dates.

### Other festival and club appearances

Our festival pages place him on a 2015 Sunwaves bill alongside Marco Carola and Ricardo Villalobos, and note that [Ben UFO](/artists/ben-ufo) played a seven-hour back-to-back with him at Gottwood in 2015. Mixmag adds that they played Sunday marathon sets on the Walled Garden stage there in 2015 and 2016.

## How is Craig Richards connected to Houghton Festival?

Houghton is an art and music festival at Houghton Hall in Norfolk. Craig Richards curated the first edition in August 2017, and the festival is produced by the team behind Gottwood, according to Mixmag. Gottwood approached him in autumn 2016, he was unsure at first, and the fabric closure made a new project close to home more appealing. Gottwood's Tom Elkington told Mixmag that the team let him run the curation completely.

Huck described him in August 2025 as the festival's founder, creative director and longtime fabric resident. He told Huck that his aim was to give visual art equal standing with music, and that "going home with discovery is better than going home with reassurance."

Mixmag's 2017 write-up called it a near-flawless debut, with a capacity of 11,000 and a 24-hour music licence. According to Mixmag's March 2018 interview, Houghton won its festival of the year award for that edition. Richards also released a book featuring all 92 artists who played the 2017 festival. Our [Houghton Festival guide](/festivals/houghton-festival) has the full history, the opening lineup, stages and travel details.

## Which labels is Craig Richards associated with?

Mixmag reported in December 2017 that he had revived his label The Nothing Special after a three-year break and run Tuppence, a 7" label, with a release involving Howie B and Shaun Ryder. fabric's biography also calls him a label boss.

## FAQ

### How old is Craig Richards?

Wikipedia gives his birth year as 1966, which makes him 59 or 60 in November 2026. No source we checked states a full birth date, and Apple Music and Shazam list 1965, so treat the exact age with care.

### Where is Craig Richards from?

Wikipedia says he was born in Bournemouth, England. He moved to London in 1987, according to a 2018 Mixmag interview, and has been associated with the city's club scene ever since.

### Who is Craig Richards?

He is a British DJ, producer and artist who has been a resident at London's fabric since 1999. He is also the creative director of Houghton Festival, which he curated from its first edition in 2017.

### Is Craig Richards still a resident at fabric?

When he stepped back from the weekly Saturday slot in 2018, fabric said he remained a resident and "the cornerstone of the club". Huck still called him a longtime fabric resident in August 2025. His dates are less regular now, so check fabric's listings.

### How many Saturday nights has Craig Richards played at fabric?

fabric's own biography says over 700 Saturday nights since 1999. Mixmag described the residency as 17 years long in 2017.

### Did Craig Richards found Houghton Festival?

Huck calls him the festival's founder and creative director. Mixmag says he curated the first edition in 2017 after the Gottwood team approached him, and that Gottwood produces it.

### When is Houghton Festival 2027?

### Who is Craig Richards' Tyrant partner?

Wikipedia says Tyrant was a project with Lee Burridge, and initially Sasha. Their releases include a 2000 album on Distinct'ive Breaks and No Shoes, No Cake on fabric in 2002.

He told Mixmag it was "devastating on many levels" and his longest break from the booth. The same article says it pushed him towards making a festival, and Trommel quotes him calling Houghton a "calling" that fabric's closure forced.
