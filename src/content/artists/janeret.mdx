Janeret is the French DJ and producer Mickael Jeanneret, who grew up in Grenoble, has lived in Paris since 2010 and directs the Joule imprint on Yoyaku. He is known for deep house, breaks and dub. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 11 May 2016, is the earliest on the channel and has 142,745 views.

## Where is Janeret from and how did he start?

XLR8R's "20 Questions" feature, published on 19 November 2018, names him as Mickael Jeanneret, a French producer who grew up in Grenoble in the south-east of the country. Yoyaku's own artist page gives the same name and adds the alias J. He told XLR8R that he fell for electronic music at about 17, after hearing Mr. Fingers' "Can You Feel It" on a local radio show.

He began DJing at home as a hobby, then played bars and small clubs from around 2009, and played weekends with the residents of the Vertigo club in Grenoble. He moved to Paris in 2010, which he calls the point where he started taking production seriously, though he had experimented earlier. His first big Paris booking, he said, was at Concrete in 2014, on the invitation of Nick V.

No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we cannot state his age. We also could not find where he was born.

## What does Janeret sound like?

XLR8R describes his sets as energetic blends of minimal house and breaks, and says his production is mainly deep house and minimal house, with jungle, hip-hop, ambient and dub or reggae on the side. He credits old-school atmospheric jungle, especially LTJ Bukem's Good Looking Records, as an influence. In a 2017 Meoko interview he named dub techno, deep house, reggae and dub and atmospheric jungle as his main sources, and said he aims for strong beats mixed with dreamy breaks.

Mixmag Italy's April 2024 profile adds the late-90s atmospheric drum and bass link, plus the Chicago and New York deep house scenes. It also records that he describes himself as a "purchase-on-sight" artist who releases sparingly. Trommel's review of his Uchüsen EP called it a move toward electro and breakbeat from his usual deep house pads and dubby sound.

## What are the career highlights of Janeret?

### Early records and Yoyaku, 2014 to 2016

XLR8R says the first track he was happy with, "Skyward", came out on Courtesy of Balance, a sub-label of Chez Damier's imprint, though the article gives no year. It dates the Rutilance EP, his first solo EP, and the Heat EP to 2015. He said he met Benjamin Belaga that year, when Belaga was about to start Yoyaku and invited him into the project, and met Varhat at the first Yoyaku party in Paris a few months later.

His JNRT444 EP on Aku, which he calls the record where he found his signature style, was reviewed by XLR8R on 23 February 2016. The reviewer called it vinyl-only, deep and dubby, with "Ether" on the A-side and "Rown" on the B-side.

### Joule, Aku and the Yoyaku roster

Trommel's January 2019 review says he is in charge of artistic direction for Yoyaku's Joule imprint, and the Amsterdam Dance Event profile calls him one of the key artists on the Yoyaku roster and the AD of Joule. XLR8R lists Joule releases by Teluric and Roger Gerressen under his direction. In his 2017 Meoko interview he called Yoyaku "kind of a second family". See our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) for the wider shop and label story.

### Barcelona and Les Enfants, 2021 to 2024

Trommel announced him for a headline set at the Breathedeep and Chez Doc night at Atlantic Club, Barcelona, on 11 December 2021, billing it as an extended 12-hour session. Trommel's July 2024 premiere describes him as based in Spain and running curated nights at the Input venue in Barcelona. Mixmag Italy, in May 2024, said he was in Barcelona for a few months after living in Paris since 2010, and that he had recently moved to the booking agency Pieces. XLR8R says he has toured Japan, Australia and the Americas, which is his own account. Yoyaku's press release, as summarised in our guide, lists a 2017 Barcelona white label with Varhat, Zendid, Janeret and Oshana among its records from the city, which it ties to Off Week, the programme around [Sónar](/festivals/sonar).

## Which labels is Janeret associated with?

- **Yoyaku and its sub-labels.** XLR8R says he signed to Yoyaku and has released on its sub-labels YYK no label and Aku. Trommel describes YoY as a Yoyaku sub-label, and his Uchüsen EP is YoY.08. Yoyaku's artist page lists 23 releases of his plus remixes.
- **Joule.** The imprint he directs. Yoyaku lists his collaborations with Miroloja on Joule, including Bridge of Spirit and Expoz.
- **Les Enfants Records.** His Shadows EP, LER004, which Trommel premiered in July 2024 and Mixmag Italy previewed as a solo EP for around April 2024.
- **Omike.** Mixmag Italy calls it his second label, oriented toward house and breakbeat. Yoyaku lists Critical EP, OMK001.
- **Others.** Yoyaku's catalogue also shows records on Rawax, 20/20 Vision, Berg Audio, Fuse London, Up The Stuss and Olo Records. Meoko's 2017 interview mentioned upcoming remixes for Scenario, Open and All in.

## FAQ

### How old is Janeret?

We could not verify his age. No source we opened gives a birth year or date. XLR8R says he was about 17 when he first fell for electronic music and began DJing around 2009.

### Where is Janeret from?

He is French and grew up in Grenoble, according to XLR8R, which also says he has lived in Paris since 2010. His Bandcamp page lists Paris. Trommel and Mixmag Italy reported him in Barcelona in 2024.

### Who is Janeret?

Janeret is Mickael Jeanneret, a French DJ and producer working in deep house, minimal house, breaks and dub. He is a Yoyaku roster artist and directs the Joule imprint.

### What is Janeret's real name?

XLR8R and Yoyaku's artist page both give it as Mickael Jeanneret. Some outlets spell the surname in other ways.

### Is Janeret signed to Yoyaku?

XLR8R says he signed to Yoyaku and has released on its sub-labels Aku and YYK no label. He met founder Benjamin Belaga in 2015, according to the same feature.

### What is Janeret's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 11 May 2016, runs 77 minutes and has 142,745 views as of 24 November 2026. It is the earliest of the 178 instore sessions we counted and ranks 15th by views.

### Which labels has Janeret released on?

Yoyaku's catalogue lists his records on Yoyaku sub-labels such as Aku, Joule and YoY, and on Les Enfants Records, Omike, Rawax, Berg Audio and Fuse London, among others.

### What does Janeret run?

Trommel and the Amsterdam Dance Event say he is the artistic director of the Joule imprint, a Yoyaku sub-label. Mixmag Italy also calls Omike his label.

### What does Janeret's music sound like?

He plays deep house, minimal house and breaks, with strong influences from dub, reggae and atmospheric jungle, by his own account to XLR8R and Meoko.

### What was Janeret's first release?

XLR8R calls the Rutilance EP, dated 2015, his first solo EP. He told the magazine that "Skyward" on Courtesy of Balance was the first track he was happy with, but gave no year.
