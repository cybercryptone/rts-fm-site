Einzelkind is Arno Völker, a German DJ and producer from Frankfurt who co-runs the Pressure Traxx label and works in stripped-back house, raw minimal and techno. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 29 January 2017, runs 98 minutes and has 33,081 views on the Yoyaku Record Store channel.

## Where is Einzelkind from and how did he start?

Einzelkind is the working name of Arno Völker. A 15 Questions interview published on 2 July 2018 gives that name and calls him German, and fabric's profile uses the same name. In a Trommel city guide published on 7 August 2018 he says he is from Frankfurt am Main, which he describes as a mix of small town and big city.

In the 15 Questions interview he says he started DJing at 15, playing hip hop, after seeing a DJ mix live at around 13. He was soon drawn to drum and bass, and later moved into house and techno. He names bass and drums as his main influence and says he gets ideas from other artists' arrangements and does not copy them.

The project began as a duo. A short biography on the NTS Radio artist page, taken from Last.fm and describing the 2007 period, names Völker and Miguel Ayala, his drum and bass partner from earlier years. Yoyaku's artist page also says Einzelkind began as a duo and is now led by Völker. We found no second source on Ayala, so treat that name as single-sourced.

We could not find a birth date or birth year for him in any source we opened, so we cannot state his age.

## What does Einzelkind sound like?

Several sources describe the same core sound. Set79 and fabric both call his music stripped-back house and techno, and Set79 adds raw minimal. fabric describes Pressure Traxx as mixing nostalgic 1990s acid sounds with raw minimal and breakbeats in a dubby house style. Electronic Beats, in a September 2017 piece on Frankfurt labels, said the label was one of the few in the area with a real interest in rominimal, the sound built on Romanian minimal. For the background, see our [history of rominimal](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno).

His own account is looser. In the 15 Questions interview he says that when he sets out to make a party track, it tends to end up ambient. He says he mainly uses Ableton for recording and editing and has moved toward hardware. In 2019 the label behind his debut album described it as dancing music with emotion, made for small, intimate clubs, which is a promotional description and not a critic's view.

## What are the career highlights of Einzelkind?

### Early records, 2010 to 2013

XLR8R says Völker released a collaborative album with Christian Burkhardt in 2010, without naming the title. The NTS biography dates "Blues For Yous" to 2012 on Cécille Records and "Clubnight" to 2013, and Set79 lists Roman Flügel as a remixer of "Blues for Yous". The same NTS page lists Einzelkind guest shows on NTS in 2013. XLR8R also says he has released on Playhouse, Aku and Kindisch, among others. The Last.fm text says the duo's first release on Get Physical's sub-label Kindisch, "Marsha", drew wide attention.

### Pressure Traxx and Frankfurt, 2012 to 2018

Pressure Traxx's Bandcamp page says the label was founded in 2012 by Frost and Arno and lists its location as Offenbach am Main. According to fabric, the name came from a studio session with Christian Burkhardt, when Frost suggested "pressure tracks". Electronic Beats called Einzelkind a local hero of the Frankfurt scene in September 2017. Set79 and Yoyaku both describe him as Frankfurt-based, and Set79 lists him as a resident at Robert Johnson in Offenbach.

fabric's profile, which is undated, says he had released seven Pressure Traxx records by then, mostly with Frost, and that he had worked with Ricardo Villalobos on the Arnorac EP the year before. It also names him as part of a small group of Frankfurt producers and DJs, alongside the crews Traffic and HardWorkSoftDrink. The same page calls his coming Cocoon appearance his third at fabric, and mentions a Cocoon promo mix. [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) is also named there as an affiliate of the label. Set79's discography lists tracks tied to Cocoon Recordings, including a Rhythm Factory release with Robin Scholz, but gives no years. For the label's own story, see our [Cocoon Recordings timeline](/blog/cocoon-recordings-history-timeline).

### The 2019 album as Arno

XLR8R reported on 4 June 2019 that Völker would drop the Einzelkind name for his debut solo album, "We Perfectly Understand Each Other Until We Start to Talk", due on 17 June as a 12-track vinyl release. It was the first album on Dana Ruh's Brouqade label. This matters for how to read the name: since then, Pressure Traxx's Bandcamp lists tracks under "Arno", for example "Do You Realize ?!" (PT X Files 009) and "Coast 2 Coast" with DJ Dope.

## Which labels is Einzelkind associated with?

- **Pressure Traxx.** Co-founded with Frost, per Bandcamp. The label also runs a Silver Series, and its Bandcamp page lists artists such as [Dan Andrei](/artists/dan-andrei), Kuyateh and Elia Nafzger, plus a "10 Years Pressure Traxx" compilation (PTX030) with no date shown.
- **La Peña.** fabric calls it his first label. Electronic Beats says its records sell well in minimal-focused shops.
- **Harlo.** Run with Charlotte T., per fabric, with a release that includes an [Audio Werner](/artists/audio-werner) remix.
- **Jax.** Yoyaku's artist page says he owns and founded Jax, La Peña, Pressure Traxx and Harlo. We found no other detail on Jax.
- **As an artist elsewhere.** XLR8R says he has released on Brouqade, Playhouse, Aku and Kindisch. Yoyaku's artist page also lists releases on Naural, Curtea Veche, Housewax and Adult Only.

We did not find any dated 2026 release or booking for him in a source we opened, so we do not report a latest item.

## FAQ

### How old is Einzelkind?

We could not verify his age. None of the sources we opened gives a birth date or year. He told 15 Questions in 2018 that he started DJing at 15.

### Where is Einzelkind from?

He says he is from Frankfurt am Main, in Germany, according to a Trommel guide from August 2018. The same article says he moved to Berlin in 2018, while Yoyaku's page calls him Frankfurt-based.

### Who is Einzelkind?

Einzelkind is the DJ and producer Arno Völker, a German artist known for stripped-back house, minimal and techno and for co-running Pressure Traxx.

### What is Einzelkind's real name?

Arno Völker. The name appears in the 15 Questions interview, on fabric's profile, in XLR8R and on Yoyaku's artist page.

### What is Pressure Traxx?

A label founded in 2012 by Frost and Arno, according to its Bandcamp page, which lists Offenbach am Main as its location. It focuses on acid, house and techno.

### What is Einzelkind's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 29 January 2017, runs 98 minutes and has 33,081 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 70th by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Did Einzelkind work with Ricardo Villalobos?

Yes. The Arnorac EP, a four-track double 12-inch with Villalobos, came out on Pressure Traxx on 16 January 2017, according to Sound Shelter.

### Was Einzelkind a duo?

Originally, according to Yoyaku and the NTS biography taken from Last.fm. That text names Miguel Ayala as his partner, and we found no second source for it.

### Why did Arno Völker drop the Einzelkind name in 2019?

XLR8R said his debut solo album would appear under his first name, but did not give a reason. Pressure Traxx's Bandcamp page now lists some tracks under "Arno", though without dates.

### What labels does Einzelkind run?

According to Yoyaku, he owns and founded La Peña, Jax, Pressure Traxx and Harlo.
