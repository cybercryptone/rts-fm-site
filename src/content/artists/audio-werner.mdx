Audio Werner is the alias of Andreas Werner, a German house and minimal producer based in Berlin, known for co-founding the label Hartchef Discos in 2004 and for records on Perlon and Sushitech. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 19 April 2018, has more than 122,000 views on YouTube.

## Where is Audio Werner from and how did he start?

XLR8R and Yoyaku both describe him as a German artist, and give his real name as Andreas Werner. His Bandcamp page lists Berlin, Germany as his location, and a June 2017 profile on the blog Beats Beyond Borders says he lives in Berlin, where many of his early DJ and live sets took place. We could not find his birth date, birth year or hometown in any source we opened.

XLR8R says he began his career in 2004, the same year Beats Beyond Borders says he set up Hartchef Discos with friends. In March 2006, Resident Advisor reported that his track "Zwtshak Drive" on Hartchef was his most successful release so far. RA said it had been played heavily by [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos), [Richie Hawtin](/artists/richie-hawtin), Guido Schneider and Andre Galluzzi, and had reached number one in the Groove magazine charts in Germany. Beats Beyond Borders spells the title "Zwrtshak Drive" and says it brought him many requests for DJ and live sets.

## What does Audio Werner sound like?

Descriptions differ by period. XLR8R calls his productions wonky, jazz-influenced minimal, and its 2016 feature places his sound between house and minimal. Beats Beyond Borders describes his production as simple, slick and complex, tied to the Berlin minimal deep tech sound.

In a May 2019 review of his AKU 12-inch, Resident Advisor's Mark Smith wrote that his catalogue of the previous 15 years was largely minimal in the European sense. He added that classic house elements became more prominent as the 2000s went on, and that Werner had more recently settled into a warmer house style with wider appeal. The review compared the lead track to late 1990s UK tech house. His Bandcamp tags run from minimal and house to broken beats and ambient.

## What are the career highlights of Audio Werner?

### Early years: Hartchef and Traum

By March 2006, RA said Triple R (Riley Reinhold), head of Traum, had supported him for a year. Triple R signed two of his tracks and put "Just Get Down" on the Traum compilation "Selection 4". He had also released on Traum's sub-label Trapez, Matthias Schaffhauser's Ware label and Matthias Tanzmann's Moon Harbour Recordings. The same report said he had played at Studio 672, Watergate, Panorama Bar and the C/O Pop festival, and was booked to headline a Traumschallplatten showcase at Phuture in Manchester on Good Friday 2006. (Watergate has since closed as a club, as noted in our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide).)

### Perlon and the minimal years

XLR8R says he signed with Perlon in 2006, and Beats Beyond Borders lists "Onandon" and "Base" on Perlon from that year. Later releases on Minibar, Galdoors, Cabinet and Hello?Repeat, among others, are mentioned across XLR8R and Beats Beyond Borders. In early 2014 he put out a jazzy, dubby house record on Finest Hour Recordings (FH01), which Beats Beyond Borders says sold out in under a week.

### Aliases, Sushitech and recent records

In April 2019 he released the three-track WRNR222 on Yoyaku's AKU sub-label, and in November 2019 XLR8R reported that he had signed with Yoyaku for three vinyl records under other names: DYKH, Audient and Andrew Renew, each on a sub-label of the same name, due on 2 December. On 31 October 2019 XLR8R announced his first album, "On A Different Note", on Sushitech, describing it as an anthology of previously unreleased tracks recorded over about a decade. In October 2024 the double LP "Soul" appeared on Metereze, the Romanian label run by [Raresh](/artists/raresh). Yoyaku's listing says it collects studio jam sessions recorded in Berlin between 2015 and 2020, with the cover drawn by his friend Melanie Kasper.

### Production advice

In a March 2016 XLR8R feature he gave production tips, including treating the room acoustically, mixing at moderate volume, recording your own samples rather than relying on libraries, and finishing each track before starting the next.

## Which labels is Audio Werner associated with?

- **Hartchef Discos.** The label he co-founded in 2004, which Bandcamp says released many of his classics.
- **Perlon.** He signed in 2006, per XLR8R.
- **Sushitech.** Home of "On A Different Note".
- **Minibar, Galdoors, Cabinet Records, Hello?Repeat, Finest Hour.** Named by XLR8R and Beats Beyond Borders as places his records have appeared. Yoyaku lists a Minibar release (MINIBAR018) and a Cabinet Records release with Daniel Paul (cab39).
- **Metereze.** Raresh's label, home of "Soul".
- **AKU, DYKH, Audient and Renew.** Yoyaku-linked sub-labels and aliases from 2019 onwards.
