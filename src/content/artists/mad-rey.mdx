Mad Rey is the alias of Quentin Leroy, a Paris house producer and DJ who co-founded the publishing house and label Red Lebanese. He came up on D.KO Records and joined Ed Banger in 2021. He has two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, from June 2023 and June 2026, and his *nomad* 12" appeared on Yoyaku in 2026.

## Where is Mad Rey from and how did he start?

Mad Rey is a Paris artist, and his real name is Quentin Leroy, according to Tsugi, the Warehouse club in Nantes and Yoyaku. The Warehouse biography says he started on percussion as a child, began making beats on an MPC at 16 and studied at the Beaux-Arts de Paris, where he also makes collages and event posters. It lists further aliases, L Rey, Bad Rey and Le Nettoyeur. Tsugi adds that he also raps under L Rey, and Yoyaku's artist page lists "Mad le Barge".

His first public step in house came with the label D.KO Records. Tsugi, in a May 2016 article, calls him the figurehead of the Paris label and says his track "Coeur d'Artichaut" appeared on its first compilation in 2013. Yoyaku's Rekids release page says he began releasing as Mad Rey in 2014, with his second release on D.KO, after contributing under his real name.

Alongside the music, Leroy belongs to the Red Lebanese platform. i-D's May 2015 feature lists a Quentin Leroy among its seven photographers, and Carhartt WIP says he founded it with Pablo Jomaron in 2012. No source we opened gives his birth date or birthplace, so we cannot say how old he is.

## What does Mad Rey sound like?

Yoyaku describes his music as warm house with raw electronic textures and influences from the international club scene. The Warehouse biography is wider: house, ghetto house, techno, hip hop, footwork, pop and what it calls cosmic breakbeats, shaped into a live performance. The festival listing site festapp calls his work sample-heavy and nostalgic, pairing lo-fi house, jacking rhythms and disco-leaning grooves with a French accent. That is promotional copy, so treat it with care.

His newest record leans harder toward the floor. Yoyaku's text for *nomad* says the EP shows a more club-focused and percussive direction, with tech house productions made for powerful sound systems. Tsugi, writing in 2016, filed him with the new wave of Paris house.

## What are the career highlights of Mad Rey?

### D.KO Records years

Tsugi mentions "Salon de Thé" on D.KO in late 2015 and "Hôtel La Chapelle" on the label Vertv in January 2016. Yoyaku's artist page lists *Bon Souvenir* (DKORL01), the *Spliff Shit EP* with Mézigue (DKO22) and the double release *Please Don't Wait* (DKORLP02) on D.KO. It gives no dates for them, so we place none. D.KO also marked its ten years with a night at Wonder on 23 June 2023, according to the Warehouse event list, with Mad Rey on the bill.

### Rekids and Ed Banger

*L'orage* came out on Rekids in 2018, per Yoyaku's release page, with "In My House No 6" on the A side and a dub on the B side. In June 2021 The Fader premiered "Joe Da Zin" with the Paris MC Jwles and reported that Ed Banger had added Mad Rey to its roster. The track was set to appear on his *B.R.O.* EP. Tsugi lists the Ed Banger signing as 2021. The Warehouse page lists an Ed Banger night on 1 October 2022 with Mr Oizo and Busy P.

### Red Lebanese and Studio X8

Carhartt WIP's label feature describes Red Lebanese as an independent publishing house married to a music label, based in Montreuil. It says Leroy set up Studio X8 there in 2014 as a place for meetings and production, usually making the sounds while Jomaron makes the artwork. The label put out three EPs in 2023, including Mad Rey's *Good Love*, which Tsugi dates to 2023. The feature, undated but pointing to autumn 2024, says a Mad Rey LP was scheduled for October 2024. We found no confirmation that it came out. Tsugi also credits him with the score for the film *Bientôt la lumière* in 2022.

### Live work

The Warehouse biography says he has played Weather Festival alongside Lil' Louis, Kenny Dope and Mr Tiers, and has appeared at Concrete and at Astropolis. Tsugi had him booked for Astropolis in Brest on 1 July 2016.

## Which labels is Mad Rey associated with?

Five labels are documented on Yoyaku's artist page and the sources above.

- **D.KO Records.** His home base for the early years, with *Bon Souvenir*, *Spliff Shit* and *Please Don't Wait*.
- **Red Lebanese.** The Paris publishing house and label he co-founded in 2012, home of *Good Love* (RLM008).
- **Ed Banger Records.** Signed in 2021, with *B.R.O.* (catalogue BEC5907199).
- **Rekids.** *L'orage* (REKIDS115), 2018.
- **Yoyaku.** *nomad* (YYKMADREY01), 2026.
