Shaun Reeves is a Detroit-born house DJ and producer who co-founded the Visionquest collective and label with [Seth Troxler](/artists/seth-troxler), Ryan Crosson and Lee Curtiss, and spent roughly two decades based in Berlin. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 February 2025, has 30,045 views and ranks 73rd of 178 on the channel.

## Where is Shaun Reeves from and how did he start?

Mixmag's "In Session" feature, published on 28 September 2021, says Reeves was born in Detroit, Michigan, and raised in the state. It says he first played records as a child on his parents' vinyl, and moved into Detroit itself in the 1990s. The Amsterdam Dance Event profile says he grew up in and around the city and began by putting on parties in small bars and attic spaces, booking [Magda](/artists/magda), Dan Bell and John Tejada. Electronic Groove's bio puts his move to Detroit in his late teens, and Trommel says he had about 20 years of DJing behind him by 2021.

A 2011 fabric interview adds detail: his first party was in an art gallery with Rob Hood, and he later ran club nights, bar events and loft parties with a former roommate, Matt Abbott. He named the record shop Freebase, Melodies and Memories as the place where his circle met Troxler, who worked there as a teenager. He also recalled a free after-party in an abandoned loft with [Richie Hawtin](/artists/richie-hawtin) and [Marco Carola](/artists/marco-carola).

The move to Berlin is dated differently depending on the source. XLR8R, in March 2020, says he moved there in 2003 aged 22. ADE, Mixmag and Trommel say 2004, and Electronic Groove says a holiday in the summer of 2004 turned into about ten years. XLR8R also says the group moved to Berlin together in June 2007, and that he later lived in Ibiza for a time.

## What does Shaun Reeves sound like?

ADE describes his taste as American house, classic and contemporary, alongside post-minimal techno and tech house, and credits early Perlon records and sets by Zip as inspirations. Above Board Distribution's notes on his Symbols EP call the tracks dubby, with driving drums and psychedelic synth and arp lines. Mixmag describes his debut album as eight darker, Detroit-influenced deep house tracks, made partly on a Eurorack modular system with 808 and 606 drum machines and an Elektron Digitone.

## What are the career highlights of Shaun Reeves?

### Berlin and the Visionquest years

ADE lists his Berlin residencies as Panorama Bar, Watergate, the Beatstreet loft parties and a summer "Soul Shower" residency at Club Der Visionaere, and other stops including DC10 in Ibiza, fabric in London and Robert Johnson in Frankfurt. Resident Advisor reported in August 2010 that he was about to make his first Australian tour, with dates in Melbourne and Sydney, and that Visionquest was a new label he ran with Troxler, Crosson and Curtiss. Mixmag says the label launched at the end of 2010.

A 2011 fabric interview describes the group's fabric Room One debut in March 2011 and the fabric61 mix CD, which he called their most important joint project apart from the label. He also named Detroit's Movement festival and DC10 as favourite gigs. For the festival itself, see our [Movement Detroit page](/festivals/movement-detroit) and our report on [Detroit's 20th Movement](/blog/detroit-movement-festival-20-years).

### The label years, 2012 to 2019

XLR8R says Visionquest has put out around 100 records, 12 of them in 2012 and 16 in 2013. Mixmag says things shifted after Troxler stepped away to focus on his own work, and our [Seth Troxler page](/artists/seth-troxler) cites Wikipedia dating that to the start of 2014. Vice, in March 2015, said Reeves became more prominent on the label afterwards. XLR8R says he co-managed Visionquest with Crosson after the group stopped touring in 2015.

### The 2020 reunion and the debut album

XLR8R reported on 6 March 2020 that all four members would reunite for the label's tenth anniversary, starting with a series of anniversary parties. Trommel says a planned 2020 to 2021 tour as a four was cancelled by the pandemic. Trommel also says he moved to Los Angeles in late 2020 to set up a studio, and Mixmag says he had returned to the United States by then, without naming a city.

His first album, *The Eye That Sees Us All*, was due on 23 July 2021 on Visionquest, according to Trommel. Mixmag says most tracks were finished in Berlin in 2019 and many began as jam sessions at Club Der Visionaere, and that he called it a statement of gratitude to the city.

## Which labels is Shaun Reeves associated with?

- **Visionquest.** The label he co-founded. He has released his own EPs and the album there, and remixed other artists on it.
- **Wolf + Lamb.** ADE lists solo work and collaborations with Guti and Miss Fitz. The 2011 fabric interview mentions "Hold Me Tight" with Guti for the label.
- **Raum...musik, Catwash and No. 19 Music.** Named by Electronic Groove as labels he has released on.
- **Crosstown Rebels.** ADE credits him with releases as The Royal We, with Troxler.
- **Sunday Money Music.** Yoyaku lists *Day Before Last* with Pablo Marco, SM002.

## FAQ

### How old is Shaun Reeves?

He is 45 as of 24 November 2026, if Trommel is right that he turned 40 on 24 July 2021. That implies a birth year of 1981, and Mixmag's September 2021 feature also gives his age as 40. XLR8R's statement that he moved to Berlin at 22 in 2003 fits the same year.

### Where is Shaun Reeves from?

He is from Detroit, Michigan. Mixmag says he was born there, and ADE says he grew up in and around the city before moving to Berlin.

### Who is Shaun Reeves?

Shaun Reeves is an American DJ, producer and label co-founder who built his career between Detroit and Berlin. He co-founded Visionquest with Troxler, Crosson and Curtiss.

### What is Visionquest?

Visionquest is the collective and label of Reeves, Troxler, Crosson and Curtiss. XLR8R says the label started in 2010, has around 100 releases, and marked its tenth anniversary with a 2020 reunion.

### Did Shaun Reeves release an album?

Yes. *The Eye That Sees Us All* is his debut album, released on Visionquest as VQLP010. Trommel gave its date as 23 July 2021.

### What is Shaun Reeves's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 21 February 2025, runs about 67 and a half minutes and has 30,045 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 73rd of the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Which labels has Shaun Reeves released on?

Visionquest, Wolf + Lamb, Raum...musik, Catwash, No. 19 Music and Sunday Money Music, according to Electronic Groove, ADE and Yoyaku.

### Is Shaun Reeves based in Berlin?

He lived in Berlin from 2003 or 2004, depending on the source. Yoyaku still lists him as Berlin-based, but Mixmag and Trommel said he moved to the United States at the end of 2020, so we cannot confirm his current base.

### Is Seth Troxler still part of Visionquest?

Troxler stepped away from the label around 2014, per Mixmag and Wikipedia as cited on our Seth Troxler page. XLR8R reported in March 2020 that he rejoined Reeves, Crosson and Curtiss for the tenth anniversary reunion.
