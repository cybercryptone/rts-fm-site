Sergey a.m. is a Moscow club DJ whose recorded footprint is a string of house and techno mixes made for Propaganda, the city's long-running club on Zlatoustinsky Lane, plus dozens of sets for RTS.FM. He publishes very little about himself, so this page is built from release data and says where nothing has been confirmed.

## Name and Identity

Discogs lists the artist as Sergey a.m., with the variant spelling Sergey A. M., and links the profile to a second entry under the name [Sergey Timoshov](https://www.discogs.com/artist/1206802-Sergey-Timoshov). The two entries are marked as aliases of each other, and the Timoshov entry carries a link to a Facebook page under that name. We have not found an interview or official biography that states the connection in the artist's own words, so we treat Timoshov as the name Discogs gives, not as a confirmed real name.

## Propaganda Mixes

Propaganda is the club that anchors most of his documented work. According to [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/3661814-Sergey-a-m), his earliest listed release is Voices (2009), a promo-only mix issued under the Propaganda label to mark the club's birthday, tagged deep house, tech house and minimal techno. It was followed by Propaganda. New Year 2012 and Propaganda. 15 Years (also 2012), both self-released, and by Propaganda. 17 Years, which carries no year in the listing and is tagged techno and deep house. Together they cover several years of club mixes, although Discogs does not say whether he held a formal residency.

## Other Credits

Under the Timoshov name, Discogs credits him on Salat, Moskva! and the compilation Dyshite Glubzhe, both from 2009, the latter on Algorythmik Records and Shanti. A 2018 self-released set is listed as DJ Set at Mechatronica. Newer entries include compilation tracks on Welofi (2023), System 108 (2023) and Electro Music Coalition (2025).

## Sound and Style

Reading across the release tags, his sound moves with the room: deep and tech house in 2009, nu-disco and house on the 2012 New Year mix, and techno by the 17-year mix. That range fits a club DJ who plays long sets and adjusts to the night.
