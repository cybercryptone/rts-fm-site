Maceo Plex is the house and techno alias of Eric Estornel, an American producer who works from Barcelona and has spent three decades moving between deep house, tech house and techno under several names. He runs two labels, Ellum Audio and Lone Romantic, and has released on Drumcode.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

Maceo Plex's real name is Eric Estornel, according to [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maceo_Plex) and [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maceo_Plex). Both give 1978 as the year of his birth, with a day that differs by one (6 November on the English page, 7 November on the German one) and Flower Mound, Texas as the birthplace. [Beatportal's artist profile](https://www.beatportal.com/features/artist-of-the-month-maceo-plex/) says he was born in Miami to Cuban parents and raised in Dallas from his early teens, so sources disagree on where he was born. They agree that he began DJing around 1993, which the profile marks as 30 years in the game in 2023.

Beatportal says he first performed as Eric Entity, then as Maetrik for techno and Mariel Ito for IDM and electro, and adopted Maceo Plex toward the end of the 2000s. It dates his move to Valencia to 2009, as European bookings grew, and describes him as living mostly in Barcelona now.

## Sound and Style

Wikipedia lists his genres as deep house, tech house, house, techno and progressive house. The aliases split that range, with Maetrik used for techno and Mariel Ito for IDM and electro, per Beatportal. His first album as Maetrik, Quality Exertion, came out on Treibstoff in 2002, and My Cyborg Depths appeared in 2005 as Mariel Ito.

## Ellum Audio and Lone Romantic

Ellum Audio was founded in 2011, according to [Dancing Astronaut's 2017 profile](https://dancingastronaut.com/2017/09/ellum-maceo-plex-label-interview/), and its first release was his own High and Sexy EP. The article describes a label that began as a platform for his own music and grew to release other artists, including Shall Ocin, Gardens of God, Dino Lenny and Fiberroot, in a sound that moves between melodic and sparse with a dark, futuristic mood. Beatportal says he later launched Lone Romantic for more experimental and emotive music, leaving Ellum Audio to handle dancefloor releases.

## Career Highlights

Beatportal ties his early 2010s breakout to house singles on Crosstown Rebels, "Vibe Your Love" and "Your Style," which led to his debut album Life Index in 2011 and then Ellum. English Wikipedia lists further albums: Journey to Solar (2016, Ellum Audio), Solar (2017, Lone Romantic), Mutant Series (2019, Ellum Audio) and '93 (2023, Lone Romantic). It also lists a 2012 DJ Award for Best Deep House DJ, a 2018 fabric 98 mix and a long Ibiza history at Space and Amnesia, plus Mosaic by Maceo at Pacha every summer since 2016. In 2021 his remix of Faithless's "Insomnia" reached number one on Beatport, per Beatportal.

Wikipedia lists Drumcode Records among his labels, the imprint run by [Adam Beyer](/artists/adam-beyer), and the 2014 Conjure series included a Drumcode release. He is named among the techno and tech house acts confirmed for [Amsterdam Dance Event's 30th anniversary edition](/blog/ade-2026-30th-anniversary), and a 2015 Sea You Festival set is the source of the photo above; see our [Sea You Festival page](/festivals/sea-you-festival).
