Flabaire is the name Ralph Maruani uses for his solo work, a French house producer and DJ based in Paris who co-founded D.KO Records in 2013. He makes deep house, with ambient, breaks and electro touches, and has released on Housewax, Cyphon and Skylax. He has two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, from 2024 and 2026.

## Where is Flabaire from and how did he start?

Flabaire is from Paris. In a June 2023 interview with When We Dip he named the city as his hometown and gave his real name as Ralph Maruani. Yoyaku's artist page uses the same name and calls him a DJ, producer, multi-instrumentalist and songwriter from Paris.

According to that interview he began piano lessons at the age of 3 and moved to guitar at around 8. His father and uncle had a band in the late 1960s, and his father introduced him to classic and experimental records. As a teenager he lived in Brussels, where he went to parties he named as High Needs Low and Catclub. He started making electronic music around 2007 or 2008, and the Flabaire name appeared around 2010.

Attack Magazine profiled him in its "Introducing" series on 20 January 2017, when it described him as a 23-year-old producer and DJ from Paris. It says he learned music by playing Beatles songs on piano and guitar, and that he was already running D.KO with friends. A Nightclubber announcement of his Cyphon EP gave his age as 29, and a Volumo biography gives 31. None of the pages we opened states a birth date or year.

## What does Flabaire sound like?

In the Attack interview he described his aim as house music with an emotional charge and some depth. The palette is wide. Yoyaku's listings tag his D.KO albums with ambient, leftfield, breakbeat and jungle as well as deep house.

His Cyphon Recordings EP, Entre Deux, was described by the label announcement as a mix of electro, IDM and deep house with a nostalgic feel. His 2025 Skylax EP, At Last, is a more straightforward deep house record. HHV's listing, which is the label's own copy, names Black Jazz Consortium, the late Mike Huckaby and Soul Capsule as its reference points and calls the closer "La Haye" a dreamy ambient house piece.

A Volumo biography says [Move D](/artists/move-d) and Point G, also known as DJ Gregory, have praised his music, and that his My King Is Light EP drew attention from [Raresh](/artists/raresh) and Axel Boman. These are the bio's claims, and we have not checked them with the artists.

## What are the career highlights of Flabaire?

### D.KO Records

Maruani co-founded D.KO Records in Paris in 2013. In the When We Dip interview he said it began as a party collective with high school friends before turning into a label. Its first release was a four-track various-artists EP called Coeur d'Artichaut, which included a track of his. He credits Mad Rey's "Quartier Sex" as the label's breakout record and says Mike Huckaby championed it. The label turned 10 in 2023 and has issued a D.KO 10 Years compilation.

Yoyaku's artist page and his Bandcamp page say he co-ran the label from 2013 to 2024. We found no source explaining what changed in 2024, so we do not call it a departure.

### Albums and EPs

His first album, It's Just A Silly Phase I'm Going Through, came out on D.KO in 2016, per Deezer. Attack's January 2017 profile says an album had appeared the previous March. Bandwidth followed in 2018 and Layers in 2020, both on D.KO. By 2023 When We Dip counted three albums and several EPs on the label, plus releases on Organic Music, Deeppa and South Street. From 2023 his solo EPs moved to other labels: Cyphon Recordings, Housewax, Bass Culture and Skylax.

### Secret Value Orchestra and Aboukir

Maruani plays bass in Secret Value Orchestra, a five-piece live house band with friends from the D.KO circle, which he said in 2023 was inactive. Volumo's undated bio says he has played across France and abroad, naming Tresor, Hoppetosse and Pratersauna. Separately, he launched the psychedelic rock project Aboukir in 2018, according to Yoyaku, which lists its debut album Digital Introversion on Rotary Phono Lab. When We Dip reported in 2023 that a second Aboukir album was due that autumn on the same label.

## Which labels is Flabaire associated with?

- **D.KO Records.** The Paris label he co-founded in 2013 and co-ran until 2024, per Yoyaku and Bandcamp. His three albums Silly Phase, Bandwidth and Layers are on it, with catalogue numbers DKOLP01, DKOLP04 and DKOLP07 on Yoyaku.
- **Cyphon Recordings.** Released Entre Deux. Nightclubber called it his fourth release on the label, and Yoyaku lists it as CYPHN04R.
- **Housewax.** The Rawax offshoot, per Volumo's biography. Posture, Eclosion and Change Of Scenery are on it.
- **Bass Culture.** The label of D'Julz, per Volumo. Hidden Funk is BCR073 on Yoyaku.
- **Skylax.** At Last is on the Skylax Extra Series. HHV says it is his return to the label after the Skylax House Explosion compilation.
- **Others.** Organic Music, Popcorn Records, Temas Records, Departure Lounge and My King Is Light also appear on his listings.
