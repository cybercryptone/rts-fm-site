Cinthie is a Berlin-born DJ and producer, named Cinthie Christl by Mixmag, who runs the 803 Crystal Grooves label and the Elevate record store in the city. She is known for bassline-led house, her 2020 debut album on Aus Music and a 2022 DJ-Kicks mix. Her joint Yoyaku instore session with Meat has 52,848 views.

## Where is Cinthie from and how did she start?

Mixmag's March 2019 profile says she was born in Berlin and that her family moved to Saarbrücken, near the French border, soon after the fall of the Berlin Wall. XLR8R, in October 2017, also says she was born in Berlin but spent much of her early life in Saarbrücken. Mixmag and 15 Questions both give her full name as Cinthie Christl, without saying whether it is her birth name.

### What do we know about Cinthie's age?

No source we opened gives a birth date. Mixmag called her 38 in March 2019, and an Apple Music editorial note on her debut album says she timed it for her fortieth birthday in 2020. Together those point to 1980, which would make her 45 or 46 in November 2026. That is our inference, not a stated figure.

### The Saarbrücken years

She learned to DJ as a teenager while working at Humpty Records in Saarbrücken. The sources disagree on dates. MusicRadar says she worked at the shop from 1996, XLR8R says she took the job at 16, and 15 Questions says 1999. Mixmag says she was a weekend resident at Flash, near Frankfurt, by 1997, playing broken beats and electro, and in March 2022 she told Mixmag she had just marked 25 years as a DJ.

She told MusicRadar that a cousin gave her a tape by [Sven Väth](/artists/sven-vath) around 1994, and that she began making tracks in the late 1990s on an Atari running Cubase.

## What does Cinthie sound like?

The sound is house in the Chicago tradition, built on bass and drums. XLR8R describes stripped-back, vinyl-based sets that run from deep openings to more driving endings, and says she owned more than 6,000 records in 2017. In the 15 Questions interview she called herself "first and foremost a club DJ" who plays mostly vinyl.

Her production follows the same logic. She told MusicRadar that she usually starts with a Roland 909 drum pattern and then the bassline and kick, and she called those the most important parts of dance music. She also names heavy saturation on drums as a signature, taken from Chicago house. Mixmag Germany summarised her style in 2025 as basslines, warm synths and rich chords.

An Apple Music editorial note on *Skylines: City Lights* adds New York house and UK garage to the mix and names [Todd Edwards](/artists/todd-edwards), Jason Jinx and [Kevin Saunderson](/artists/kevin-saunderson) as reference points.

## What are the career highlights of Cinthie?

### Low Spirit, Vinyl Princess and the Berlin parties

Mixmag says Westbam signed her to Low Spirit in 1999 and that she released electro singles as Vinyl Princess. XLR8R says she played her first international gigs through the label. 15 Questions, however, dates two Vinyl Princess 12-inches to 2002, made with Adrian Misiewicz and Uwe Dockhorn, and MusicRadar says her first gigs and records came in 2000. We cannot reconcile those dates.

Mixmag and XLR8R agree on what came next. According to Mixmag she moved back to Berlin in 2001 and left the label about two years later. XLR8R says she then struggled to get booked and built her name through illegal parties. Mixmag dates her Rafgier party series to a 2004 birthday event and lists an abandoned power plant and a slaughterhouse among the venues.

### Beste Modus and the Watergate years

XLR8R dates the vinyl-only label Beste Modus to 2013, run with Diego Krause, stevn.aint.leavn, Ed Herbst and Albert Vogt, and says her international breakthrough followed. MusicRadar says she worked a 40-hour week at Ableton while running the label, and quit in 2014 to focus on music.

Mixmag also reports a weekly Watergate residency that lasted four years and ended in October 2018.

### Skylines: City Lights, 2020

Her debut album came out on Aus Music in 2020, with Will Saul's label backing it after she told him she was planning one, per Apple Music's editorial note. Release dates differ: Electronic Groove gave 15 June 2020, Apple Music lists 17 July 2020.

### DJ-Kicks, 2022

15 Questions says her DJ-Kicks entry was released on 1 April 2022 through !K7. Mixmag's 25 March 2022 interview, written before release, lists Terrence Parker, Paul Johnson, Anna Wall and Logic1000 among the artists on the mix, and says she works without a manager.

### Heist, Ostgut Ton and recent releases

Mixmag Germany reports that she released the *Piano Heaven* EP on Dutch label Heist Recordings in 2023, and followed with *Get Up* on Heist in May 2025. In 2025 she also appeared on *Klubnacht 01*, the compilation that marked Ostgut Ton's return, according to the tracklist we summarised in our [Ostgut Ton history](/blog/ostgut-ton-berghain-label-history).

## Which labels is Cinthie associated with?

- **Her own labels.** XLR8R lists Beste Modus (2013), the Beste Freunde sub-label (2015) and the we_r house sub-label (2017). Her current main outlet is 803 Crystal Grooves. The Elevate store site lists Beste Modus, Unison Wax, Crystal Grooves, We R House, Beste Freunde and Collective Cuts as its in-house labels.
- **Elevate.** Mixmag says the Berlin record shop opened in 2018 with her Beste Modus partners.
- **Aus Music, !K7 and Heist.** Home to her 2020 album, the 2022 DJ-Kicks mix and two EPs.
- **Low Spirit and Electric Kingdom.** Her early label home, from 1999 by Mixmag's account.
- **Others.** XLR8R lists a 2017 remix on Watergate Records, and her Bandcamp page lists a 2018 single on Muna Musik.
