S.A.M. is the stage name of Samuel André Madsen, a Danish producer and DJ who runs the label Delaphine and is one third of the trio Mandar. He is best known for his Yoyaku instore session of 2 November 2017, a 109 minute house set that has 259,335 YouTube views and ranks eighth among the channel's most watched sessions.

## Where is S.A.M. from and how did he start?

Trommel's July 2018 interview gives his name as Samuel André Madsen and says S.A.M. is formed from the initials of his full name. A November 2019 interview on 15 Questions calls him a Danish producer and DJ. XLR8R, which covered his debut album in 2017 and a second album in 2018, describes him as a Copenhagen-based producer and says his first EPs date from 2007. Yoyaku's shop pages list his Delaphine records under Denmark.

Before music became his work, he told Trommel, he studied theology at the University of Copenhagen and worked in a church as a drummer and incense deacon. He says his record collection began with a Kraftwerk album, *Ralph & Florian*, given to him by his sister.

Mixmag reported in May 2016 that Mandar is a Paris trio formed in 2013 with Lazare Hoche and Malin Genie. In the Trommel interview he says the three met in Paris and finished two tracks on their first day working together.

## What does S.A.M. sound like?

XLR8R says he first built a following with classic, dubbed-out house, before moving into minimal, experimental and ambient work. The shop listings for his Delaphine records tag them as deep house, dub techno, minimal and tech house. A tracklist page for his Boiler Room set in Milan calls it warm, swing-heavy deep house. MixesDB tags his Yoyaku session as house and deep house.

He told Trommel that silence and solitude are his main sources of inspiration and that he produces almost every day. He treats each EP as having its own purpose: some personal, some for the dancefloor, some built for DJ sets.

## What are the career highlights of S.A.M.?

### Delaphine and the albums

XLR8R says his debut album, *Dream State of a Bellmaker*, was the eighth release on Delaphine, with a February release date reported in 2017. A second album, *Retrospect One*, was announced by XLR8R on 14 March 2018 for 21 March 2018, and XLR8R notes it was the label's first digital release after a run of vinyl only records.

### Mandar

Mandar's output includes a five-vinyl album announced by Mixmag in May 2016 on Oscillat Music, and the *String Theory* EP, which Trommel called the trio's latest in July 2018. In 2025 the trio returned with *Lose Your Way*, catalogued YOYAKU011 on Yoyaku's own label, according to Newtone Records, which says it was their first EP in about two years.

### Sets and broadcasts

MixesDB lists a run of recorded sets after the Yoyaku session. They include a 17 June 2018 set at ipsə in Berlin that became Trommel's 33rd podcast, a 29 July 2018 set at De School in Amsterdam, a 29 April 2019 set at Organik Festival in Taiwan, and a 27 May 2021 session for HÖR in Berlin. It also lists an 11 August 2018 set under the title RTS.FM, MTM, Ibiza. These are community entries, not official bookings, so treat the dates as indicative.

## What is S.A.M.'s Yoyaku instore session?

S.A.M.'s Yoyaku instore session is a 109 minute set published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 2 November 2017. As of 24 November 2026 it has 259,335 views, 5,469 likes and 385 comments. The video description contains only the address of the shop's streaming site, yoyaku.live, with no tracklist or biography. A tracklist posted on the mnml.nl forum on 4 November 2017, and a MixesDB entry, both place one of his own records, Delaphine 006, fifth in the set. MixesDB gives the length as 1 hour 49 minutes and tags the music as house and deep house. The session is the eighth most watched on the channel and the oldest in the top ten, averaging about 78 views a day nine years after upload. One viewer comment with 105 likes calls it one of the best Yoyaku sessions ever.

### How do we know this is the same S.A.M.?

The name is common, so we checked it. The video carries no credit beyond its title, but the tracklists for the session include Delaphine 006, a 2017 12-inch that Yoyaku's own shop lists as S.A.M. from Denmark, and Delaphine is the label Madsen says is his own. Trommel's profile of Yoyaku, published on 8 September 2018, also names S.A.M. alongside [Cristi Cons](/artists/cristi-cons) and [Daniel Bell](/artists/daniel-bell) as an in-store guest, and Trommel covers Madsen under that exact name. Yoyaku also lists his Delaphine 011 and released a Mandar EP on its own label.

## Which labels is S.A.M. associated with?

In the Trommel interview he names three labels. Oscillat Music is Mandar's main platform. Delaphine is his own label, where he says he likes to surprise and take risks. DAHLIA takes demos from friends and submissions. Delaphine's catalogue numbers run at least to DELAPHINE011, and Yoyaku's page for that release says it marks the label's tenth year and that S.A.M. was back after a four year break.

His other credits include a remix of Malin Genie and Per Hammar's *Scania*, and releases under a DJ Ronnie alias on Alec Falconer's Punchline label, according to Trommel's S.A.M. tag page. Trommel's coverage, which names him as a Mandar member and Delaphine head, also mentions a free sample pack he made for Isla Instrument's S2400.
