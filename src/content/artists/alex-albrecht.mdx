Alex Albrecht is an Australian producer, DJ and live performer based in Melbourne, and a co-founder of the Analogue Attic label. He blends ambient, deep house, jazz and field recordings, and also records as Melquíades. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 24 September 2024, has 39,006 views.

## Where is Alex Albrecht from and how did he start?

Truancy's Volume 359 interview, published on 21 January 2026, describes him as Australian, from Perth originally but raised in Melbourne, which he calls home. That is the only source we opened that mentions Perth, so treat it as single-sourced. Other pages, among them Anjunadeep, the Inverted Audio newsletter and Mixmag, place him in Melbourne.

In that interview he recalls jazz records, ambient music and late-night radio from childhood, and says he started piano young. He got into electronic music and DJing at around 16. He names Terre Thaemlitz, Susumu Yokota, [Move D](/artists/move-d) and early releases on Kompakt (see our [Kompakt history](/blog/kompakt-records-cologne-history)) as influences.

The Analogue Attic imprint is the thread through his career. Anjunadeep and Inverted Audio credit him with founding or co-running it, and 4ZZZ says he has made records for it since 2014. A Strangely Isolated Place's 2023 interview post says it began as a home for his own music and later widened to other Australian ambient artists.

We could not verify his birth year, birth date or real name. Yoyaku's artist page also lists "A. Albrecht" and "Alexander Albrecht" as credited names, but no biography we opened confirms a legal name.

## What does Alex Albrecht sound like?

Press descriptions agree on a soft, organic palette. Anjunadeep calls it dreamy and lush electronic music and says he often builds tracks from field recordings of his surroundings. Truancy quotes him saying those recordings are a big part of how he processes places, and he names Sri Lanka, Japan and the Victorian bush as sources.

A Strangely Isolated Place described his 2023 album Violet Visionary as an evolution of downtempo and hazy, percussive deep house. Refuge Worldwide's page for his guest mix of 7 April 2021 calls him a Melbourne improviser who modernises the classic jazz format and says his live sets are improvised and never repeated. The Ransom Note's profile adds balearic, electronica and a nineties sense of breaks to the mix. His debut solo album, Campfire Stories, was reviewed by 4ZZZ as a blend of ambient, jazz and dance, built from pads, live piano and field recordings, with guests including Allysha Joy and Adam Haliwell of Mildlife.

## What are the career highlights of Alex Albrecht?

### Melquíades, Albrecht La'Brooy and Analogue Attic, 2014 to 2021

According to Mixmag in May 2018, Albrecht released the four-track ambient EP The Bluff on Analogue Attic, and the same article notes the debut Albrecht La'Brooy album Tidal River on Apollo (R&S) and the debut Melquíades EP Blue Caves on Scissor and Thread, both from early 2018. XLR8R adds that the duo with Sean La'Brooy followed with Healesville in 2019, also on Apollo. Resident Advisor's news page says most of his output since 2018 had come as Melquíades until Campfire Stories, his first solo release under his own name, which it dates to 10 March 2021 on Analogue Attic.

### Violet Visionary and Allambie, 2023 to 2024

A Strangely Isolated Place released Violet Visionary on 29 September 2023, his debut on that California label. XLR8R reported on 20 December 2023 that Analogue Attic would open 2024 with his Allambie LP on 18 January, a mix of house, downtempo and ambient with field recordings and piano.

### Mule Musiq and Anjunadeep

Yoyaku's artist page lists two Mule Musiq 12-inches, A Clearing and In The Turpid Heat Of The Afternoon, though it gives no release years and we did not open a page that confirms them. His Someday, Sara EP came out on Anjunadeep on 14 February 2025, per the label's own product page, with five tracks and extended mixes. Anjunadeep's undated artist spotlight says he wrote the title track while travelling in Sri Lanka, and mentions Japan dates in May and a European tour in June, which we cannot place in a year.

Truancy names two newer records without dates: The Arboretum, a five-track EP on Butter Sessions, and Tangible Dreams, a collaboration with Grant on Last Year in Marienbad. A listing for a Brunswick Music Festival event says he has played Tresor in Berlin and Bassiani in Georgia. That is the promoter's claim, and we did not find a second source.
