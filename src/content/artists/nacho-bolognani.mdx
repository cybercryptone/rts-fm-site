Nacho Bolognani is an Argentine house DJ and producer from Villa Carlos Paz, in the province of Córdoba, who has toured Europe, the Caribbean and Latin America. He is known for groove-led house with minimal and electro touches. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 20 August 2024, has 47,022 views.

## Where is Nacho Bolognani from and how did he start?

Two Argentine outlets place him in Villa Carlos Paz. Vicious Magazine, in an interview published on 1 October 2021, quotes him calling it his home town, and La Arena, in April 2024, says he was born there. According to La Arena, he began playing at 15 at the Molino Rojo club in Córdoba and was working professionally by 18. Vicious says he started with opening sets, initially as a duo with his cousin, after years as a collector of electronic music on vinyl and digital formats.

His earliest dated residency evidence is Zebra Club in Córdoba. In 2021 he told Vicious he had worked there for 11 years, alongside Thursday dates at Posta Sunsets in Pinamar. By then he said he had spent about 14 years working full time in music. In March 2026, DJ Mag Latinoamérica put it at more than 16 years.

How old is he? No source we opened gives a birth date or year. La Arena put his age at 32 in April 2024, and Vicious put it at 29 in late 2021. Those two figures point to a birth between late 1991 and spring 1992, which would make him 34 or 35 in November 2026. Treat that as our estimate from press-reported ages, not a confirmed date.

## What does Nacho Bolognani sound like?

DJ Mag Latinoamérica describes a blend of house, funk and electro touches built around groove, and quotes him calling house the musical language he feels most comfortable with. He told the magazine he played around 120 BPM a decade earlier and now often sits between 128 and 131. He says he moves between groovier, minimal, deep and darker moods.

Other outlets use similar terms. A Buenos Aliens tour diary from 16 August 2019 lists minimal house, house, deep house, acid and electro. Mejor Informado, in September 2023, calls him a house artist with an underground edge who prefers room for improvisation over a fully planned set.

He is also a producer who, according to Vicious, teaches production, sings and plays piano. La Arena reported in 2024 that he was planning a live show with synthesisers.

## What are the career highlights of Nacho Bolognani?

### Europe and early records, 2017 to 2020

A Buenos Aliens interview published on 6 July 2017 says he had roughly ten years of steady work behind him. After a tour through Berlin, France and the Netherlands he settled in Paris, where he played a Montpellier showcase, a Nanterre party and a No Club Paris date. The same piece says a vinyl record, *Hidden Gems*, came out on Myhouseyourhouse that year. 

Trommel's premiere of 17 January 2019 says Fun Records, run by the Argentine producers Barem and Alexis Cabrera, picked him for the twelve-inch FUN008. It says he played two or three times in Berlin with Cabrera in 2018 and showed the label about ten unreleased tracks. The premiere carries a [Priku](/artists/priku) remix of "KB3", which Trommel calls minimal and percussion-driven compared with the warmer original.

Buenos Aliens' summer 2019 tour diary lists fabric in London on 2 June, Atípico in Barcelona on 8 June, Nouveaux Casino in Paris on 5 July, Yamba in Lisbon on 12 July and The Zoo Project in Ibiza on 21 July. He called Paris his usual base at the time. See our guides to [London clubs](/blog/best-london-clubs-2026-guide) and [Berlin clubs](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide) for venues on that circuit that still operate.

### Own label, Argentina and festivals, 2020 to 2024

Vicious says he launched his own label, CLABEL Records, in late 2020 after about seven years of planning it. In 2021 he also named agency ties with BAN for Argentina and nearby countries, Dreams Om Wax for Europe and Island Beats for the Dominican Republic. A 2023 article in Mejor Informado says he started another label, Tool Recordings, that year and lists Música Lunar, run by Deep Mariano and Jorge Savoretti, among his releases. It also names him as a headliner of the Vision Festival in Neuquén on 9 September 2023.

La Arena reported that he was due to play Madero in Santa Rosa on 19 April 2024, at a night promoted by Zyklus, and then travel to Romania for [Sunwaves](/festivals/sunwaves) two weeks later. It also says he had recently opened and closed a night for [Peggy Gou](/artists/peggy-gou) in Córdoba.

## Which labels is Nacho Bolognani associated with?

- **Fun Records.** The label run by the Argentine producers Barem and Alexis Cabrera, home of FUN008.
- **Island Beats.** An Argentine label that issued his *Sun* EP, three house tracks, with the digital version out on 9 December 2019, per Buenos Aliens.
- **Bien Aimer Records.** The London label behind *Nowadays*, in 2020.
- **Dreams On Wax.** Buenos Aliens lists *Alone* on it in 2020. Vicious, in 2021, instead names a Dreams Om Wax as his European agency, so the two accounts may refer to the same outfit in different roles.
- **Myhouseyourhouse and Audionik Ltd.** Myhouseyourhouse issued *Hidden Gems* in 2017. Vicious says a vinyl release with Yaya on Audionik Ltd, with remixes by Steve O'Sullivan and Mahony, was close to sold out in 2021.
- **Own labels.** CLABEL Records (launched late 2020, per Vicious) and Tool Recordings (2023, per Mejor Informado).
- **Others.** DJ Mag Latinoamérica says UVAR, Tamango, Denser and Music For Freaks have released his work.

## What is Nacho Bolognani doing in 2026?
