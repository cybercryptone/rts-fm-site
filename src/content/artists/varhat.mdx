Varhat is the French DJ and producer Vincent Lubelli, a partner in the Paris shop and label Yoyaku who runs the Aku imprint. He is known for minimal and house records and a steady stream of alias projects. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 17 May 2019, has 52,702 YouTube views.

## Where is Varhat from and how did he start?

XLR8R's "Bubblin' Up" feature, published on 30 June 2016, names him as Vincent Lubelli and says he was 24 at the time. He grew up in Strasbourg in eastern France and still lived in the countryside on the city's outskirts then. He traces his first memories of electronic music to the age of five, when his older brother DJed at home for friends. He began experimenting with Ableton at 18, played small Strasbourg clubs, and took both seriously from about 20. At 21 he left a technical diploma course after missing the required grades, and decided on music. He told XLR8R he was entirely self-taught, learning online and from equipment manuals.

The Varhat name, he said, joins a "V" to "Arhat", and the project started in February 2015 as a reset. By XLR8R's January 2020 podcast profile he had moved to Paris, built a studio and spent time at the Yoyaku shop.

## How old is Varhat?

No source we opened gives a birth date or year. Because XLR8R said he was 24 in mid 2016, he was probably born between mid 1991 and mid 1992, which would make him 34 or 35 on 24 November 2026. Treat that as an estimate, not a stated fact.

## What does Varhat sound like?

XLR8R described his output in 2016 as mostly minimal, often built like house, with both full tracks and short "tools" for his own sets. A Trommel podcast page from December 2022 places him in a "Parisian minimal tech house" style and describes a two hour set moving through deep grooves, breaks and peak time material. Trommel's review of his Hokenkok EP called him a prolific producer of great eclecticism, mostly of minimal and house records, with that EP leaning toward minimal techno. Trommel in 2024 called him known for infectious, floor focused grooves.

## What are the career highlights of Varhat?

### First records, 2015 and 2016

XLR8R says his first Varhat release was "Mantra" on a Crocus compilation in August 2015. In early 2016 he put out three EPs, two on his own Aku imprint and others on Mulen Records and Howl, followed by another two track Aku release in June 2016. That Aku run includes VRHT222, which XLR8R later named as his last Aku record before 2020.

### Yoyaku partner and artistic director

XLR8R says Yoyaku began in early 2013 as a booking agency set up by Benjamin Belaga for friends, and that Lubelli, a friend since 2012, is one of its artists and has become a partner. The feature says he supervises the artistic direction of the Yoyaku labels and shop, with Yoyaku handling his bookings and management. Trommel's September 2018 history of the company singled him out as the artist who best embodies its ethos and said he sends a track to Belaga at the end of each working day, with only the strongest released. See our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) for the shop and label story, and the profiles of fellow roster artists [Cabanne](/artists/cabanne) and [Janeret](/artists/janeret).

### Hokenkok and Lieu des Herons

XLR8R reported in December 2018 that Cabanne would launch Polyson with Varhat's Hokenkok EP, with a Cabanne remix of "Chhulub", due in early February. Trommel reviewed it in February 2019. The Yoyaku catalogue also lists Lieu des Herons, a 12 inch with Akufen, catalogue YYKRSDAY4, described as a special Record Store Day release limited to 300 numbered copies with hand silkscreened sleeves. It carries an Akufen remix, and the other tracks are "Singe Aztèque" and "Huitz".

### Quiet years and a return, 2020 to 2024

XLR8R announced the Telharmonic EP on Aku in March 2020 as his first release of that year. In November 2023 it covered Breaking Out on Chris Stussy's Up The Stuss imprint, his first record there, with a remix by Paolo Rocco. See [Chris Stussy](/artists/chris-stussy). Trommel's January 2024 premiere of his remix of Max Ulis on VRNT said he had returned after a short break and called it his North American debut, and in May 2024 it premiered "Doggo" on MUZI Cartel.

## Which labels is Varhat associated with?

- **Aku.** XLR8R calls it his own imprint and lists it among the labels Yoyaku owns. VRHT222 (2016) and Telharmonic (2020) appeared there.
- **Yoyaku and its sub-labels.** Yoyaku's artist page lists a split with Janeret, the YYK no label series, YoY and Yoyaku itself. It also credits remixes for Flabbergast on Yoyaku and Janeret on Joule.
- **Polyson.** Cabanne's vinyl only label, which opened with his Hokenkok EP.
- **Up The Stuss, MUZI Cartel and VRNT.** Outside labels for Breaking Out, "Doggo" and the Max Ulis remix.

## What aliases does Varhat use?

XLR8R's 2020 podcast profile lists Niwa Tatsui, Vincen, YYY, Volière and Hostom among his names, though it adds that Hostom was not yet officially linked to him. In 2016 the same publication said the link was widely believed but not confirmed by him or his representatives. Trommel's 2022 page also lists Axelle Stone and Boniface. As Vincen he is the in-house mastering engineer for the Yoyaku labels, according to XLR8R, which also covered a Vincen EP on YYY in November 2016. Trommel's Niwa Tatsui note calls that alias more electro and techno.

Varhat is Vincent Lubelli, a French DJ and producer who works in minimal and house. He is a partner in Yoyaku and runs the Aku imprint, according to XLR8R.
