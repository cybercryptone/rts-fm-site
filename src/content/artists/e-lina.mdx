E.lina is a Ukrainian DJ and multidisciplinary artist who grew up in Kharkiv and is based in Berlin, according to her own 2022 interview and press coverage. She plays deep house, groovy bass-led house and hypnotic techno, comes from a hip-hop and dance background, and has two Yoyaku instore sessions on YouTube, published in 2024 and 2026.

## Where is E.lina from and how did she start?

In a February 2022 interview for the Berlin blog Sundowner, she said she grew up "on the streets of Kharkiv", in eastern Ukraine, and began dancing hip hop and freestyle at 15. Her route in was old-school rap and boom bap. She also said she had played piano and sung since childhood, started music school at seven but did not finish it, and recorded a Ukrainian neo-soul EP at 20, before she knew house or techno.

That changed after she moved to Kyiv. She told Sundowner that hearing electronic music there for the first time amazed her, and that she bought a Korg Electribe 2 groove box to try making something similar. Her first rave, she said, was CXEMA, where the dark techno and strobes scared her, and a week later she went to Closer, the Kyiv club, and fell for the first track she heard. She named Closer, Hoppetosse and Brave! Factory as her three favourite clubs and parties.

Promoter bios for her Bucharest and Washington shows repeat this account: hip-hop and freestyle dance first, then electronic music, then Berlin. The Bucharest listing says she DJed at Closer, Port, Arsenal and the ICKRA festival in Ukraine before moving. We could not find a source for when she moved to Berlin or for her birth date.

## What does E.lina sound like?

Her own description in 2022 was simple: music "should make you move and groove". The promoter bio for her March 2026 show at Flash in Washington, DC calls her style eclectic, built on heavy bass lines, swinging rhythms and carefully curated melodies, and says she moves from deep house on a beach to hypnotic techno at a night rave. It also says her dancing background shapes the flow of her sets. These are promoter claims.

The Club Der Visionaere mix of 9 August 2023, indexed by the set analysis site set79, runs more than 80 minutes of house records. ShallowBunny, a festival-guide site, describes her as a DJ, singer and producer whose sets mix electronic and pop elements, with a melodic edge.

## What are the career highlights of E.lina?

### Berlin clubs and festivals

The bio supplied for her Bucharest date lists Watergate, Club Der Visionaere and RSO in Berlin, and the festivals Dimensions and Sziget. The Flash bio adds Hoppetosse, Lo-Fi and NODD, and the Butik festival. All of these are promoter claims, and we have not checked each against a primary listing.

ShallowBunny lists dated appearances: the Wintergarten at Sisyphos on 18 November 2024, the Seebühne at Fusion in 2025 and Heideglühen #33 on 20 September 2026. It also lists a 2024 slot at [Garbicz Festival](/festivals/garbicz-festival). The site reads like an aggregator, so treat the dates as indicative. Two YouTube uploads titled with her name and HÖR carry the dates 6 July 2022 and 13 March 2024.

### Control Club, Bucharest, 1 November 2024

The club's own event page lists her in Room 1 on 1 November 2024, as part of its ctrl x Personnal series, alongside Victoria and Baron P. It calls her an eclectic Ukrainian DJ and notes her past as a dancer.

### Boiler Room Lisbon, October 2025

Resident Advisor reported on 28 October 2025 that fake blood was thrown into the DJ booth during Boiler Room Lisbon at 8 Marvila on Friday 24 October. E.lina was playing and posted about it on Instagram the next day. RA quotes her belief that the act "was meant as a protest for Palestine", and says she called it disrespectful and pointed out that she is an artist from Ukraine, where blood is still being shed. RA adds that it was unclear who was responsible, that a group called BOYCOTT BOILER ROOM LISBON had urged artists to pull out, and that the party continued afterwards. Roman Flügel, Margaret Dygas and [Sonja Moonear](/artists/sonja-moonear) were also on the bill.

Four/Four Magazine ran the same story and noted that her statement drew criticism online; Midnight Rebels reported that she kept playing for another hour. The wider dispute is covered in our [Boiler Room boycott explainer](/blog/boiler-room-kkr-boycott).

## Which labels is E.lina associated with?

We could not verify a label home for her. In 2022 she told Sundowner she was working on an EP with Hopper F. of Criminal Practice, a Kyiv label, combining electronic music with her vocals. We found no release listing for it, nor any Bandcamp, Beatport or Juno artist page in her name, so we cannot say it came out. Her Yoyaku appearances are as a guest DJ, not a label release. She also plays as half of a duo called Attechtion please!, per the Flash bio.

## What is E.lina's Yoyaku instore session?

E.lina has two instore sessions on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel. The first, "Yoyaku Instore Session with E.LINA", was published on 27 November 2024, runs 58 minutes 30 seconds and had 47,224 views, 1,437 likes and 172 comments as of 24 November 2026. The second, "Yoyaku Instore Sessions with E.lina", went up on 17 September 2026, runs 62 minutes 48 seconds and had 100,114 views, 1,960 likes and 164 comments on the same date. Both descriptions are short. They link Yoyaku's shop, events and social accounts and point to her Instagram, and the 2024 one also links her SoundCloud. Neither lists a tracklist. Yoyaku's instore sessions are long DJ sets played on vinyl and recorded in the Yoyaku shop. The analysis site set79 describes a Yoyaku SoundCloud upload of her session as a smooth, groove-led selection of deep, rolling house.

## FAQ

### How old is E.lina?

We could not find her birth year or date in any source we opened, so we do not state an age. She said in 2022 that she recorded her debut EP at 20, which does not fix a birth year.

### Where is E.lina from?

She told Sundowner in 2022 that she grew up in Kharkiv, Ukraine. Resident Advisor and Four/Four call her a Ukrainian artist, and RA calls her Berlin-based.

### Who is E.lina?

E.lina is a Ukrainian DJ, singer and producer based in Berlin, per ShallowBunny and Resident Advisor's reporting. She came to electronic music from hip-hop and dance, and plays house and techno.

### What is E.lina's Yoyaku instore session?

There are two on YouTube. One was published on 27 November 2024 and runs 58:30, the other on 17 September 2026 and runs 62:48. As of 24 November 2026 they have 47,224 and 100,114 views.

### What does E.lina play?

Promoters describe an eclectic mix of deep house, bass-led grooves and hypnotic techno. Set79 describes her Yoyaku set as deep, rolling house.

### Which label is E.lina on?

We could not verify one. She mentioned an EP with Hopper F. of Criminal Practice in 2022, but we found no release listing.

### Is E.lina playing Dimensions 2027?

Yes, according to the first-wave announcement as reported by Electronic Groove. She is listed in a back-to-back with Dyed Soundorom at the 26 to 31 August 2027 edition in Tisno, Croatia.

### What happened to E.lina at Boiler Room Lisbon?

Resident Advisor reported that fake blood was thrown into the booth during her set on 24 October 2025. She said she believed it was a protest for Palestine and criticised it, and no one has been identified as responsible.

### Is E.lina the same person as Alina?

We found nothing linking the two. Yoyaku's channel lists a separate instore session, published on 7 November 2024, under the name Alina, and we have not treated it as hers.
