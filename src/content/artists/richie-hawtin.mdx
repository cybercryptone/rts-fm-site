Few figures in electronic music have shaped both the sound of the dancefloor and the tools used to play it as thoroughly as Richie Hawtin. As Plastikman he made some of the most austere and hypnotic records in techno. As a label owner he built Plus 8 and M_nus into reference points. As a DJ he was an early adopter of laptops and digital control, and he is still a headline name at festivals and on Ibiza's Sunday circuit.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richie_Hawtin), Richard "Richie" Hawtin was born on 4 June 1970 in Banbury, Oxfordshire, and moved with his parents at the age of nine to LaSalle, Ontario, a suburb of Windsor. [Discogs](https://api.discogs.com/artists/835) lists his full name as Richard Michael Hawtin. Windsor sits directly across the river from Detroit, and the same page says his father, a robotics technician at General Motors, introduced him to Kraftwerk and Tangerine Dream.

Hawtin started DJing in clubs at 17, was mentored by Scott "Go-Go" Gordon at The Shelter in Detroit, and dropped out of film studies at the University of Windsor to concentrate on music. That cross-border upbringing is why he is usually filed with Detroit techno's second wave, alongside names such as [Daniel Bell](/artists/daniel-bell) and [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills). RTS.FM's coverage of [Detroit's Movement festival](/blog/detroit-movement-festival-20-years) is a good primer on the city he grew up next to, and the 2026 edition lists him on the bill.

## Sound and Style

Hawtin's sound is built on repetition and subtraction. Wikipedia describes him as a leading exponent of minimal techno since the mid-1990s, and the [Discogs profile](https://api.discogs.com/artists/835) sums up a range running from harder, noisier techno to deep, abstract acid, especially under the Plastikman name. Wikipedia notes that his 1996 "Concept 1" series of monthly 12-inch singles explored minimal techno, a direction his M_nus label later pursued.

His method of playing matters as much as the records. Hawtin helped develop Final Scratch digital DJ technology, according to Discogs, and RTS.FM's piece on [Ableton's first 25 years](/blog/ableton-25-years-independence-gerhard-behles) notes that MusicRadar counts him among the early high-profile users of Live. In 2016 he launched the technology company PLAYdifferently and a MODEL 1 mixer co-designed with Andy Rigby-Jones, and in 2019 the CLOSER app, according to Wikipedia.

## Labels and Releases

Wikipedia's [Plus 8 page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus_8) says Hawtin and John Acquaviva founded the label in 1990 in Windsor, naming it after the pitch-control range on a turntable. It started with faster industrial hardcore, then slowed down and moved toward funk and soul after 1992. It released early 12-inch singles by Speedy J as well as many of Hawtin's own aliases.

His alias F.U.S.E. produced the debut album Dimension Intrusion in 1993 as part of Warp's Artificial Intelligence series. The same year he adopted the Plastikman identity with the single "Spastik" and the album "Sheet One". The follow-up "Musik" (1994), the 1998 album "Consumed" and 2003's "Closer" make up the core of a catalogue that [the official Musik reissue page](https://plastikman.com/) says was remastered from the original tapes for a 30th anniversary edition in 2024.

In 1998 he started M_nus (also written Minus), which [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minus_(record_label)) describes as based in Berlin and Windsor and created when Plus 8 was put on hold. The label's roster over the years included Gaiser, Marc Houle, Troy Pierce and Magda, according to the Hawtin article. Together with Probe Records, which Wikipedia calls a sister label of Plus 8, these imprints give a clear map of his taste.

## Career Highlights

In 2006 Hawtin played the Berlin Loveparade with [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) and [Luciano](/artists/luciano) to an audience that [Wikipedia puts at about 300,000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos). The same year he scored music for the Winter Olympics opening ceremony in Turin with choreographer Enzo Cosimi. In November 2013 he performed as Plastikman at the Guggenheim Museum in New York, a recording of which appeared on the 2014 album "EX". From 2012 to 2015 he ran the weekly ENTER. party at Space in Ibiza, per Wikipedia. In July 2015 the University of Huddersfield awarded him an honorary doctorate of music technology, and Wikipedia says he has won the DJ Awards' Best Techno DJ prize three times.

His records also frame current festival bills: Hawtin was among the headliners of Junction 2's first edition in 2016, as our [Junction 2 feature](/blog/nina-kraviz-jeff-mills-fabric-junction-2) records. In 2026 he is one of the three names curating [Pyramid at Amnesia Ibiza](/blog/nina-kraviz-amnesia-ibiza-pyramid-residency), alongside Villalobos and [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz), a season covered in the [Kraviz 2026 recap](/blog/nina-kraviz-2026-recap).

## FAQ

### Who is Richie Hawtin?

Richie Hawtin is a British-born Canadian DJ, producer and label founder best known as Plastikman and F.U.S.E. He co-founded Plus 8 Records, started M_nus and has been a leading figure in minimal techno since the mid-1990s.

### How old is Richie Hawtin?

Richie Hawtin was born on 4 June 1970, which makes him 56 (age 56 as of October 2026).

### Where is Richie Hawtin from?

He was born in Banbury, Oxfordshire, England, and raised from the age of nine in LaSalle, Ontario, near Windsor and across the river from Detroit. He is usually described as British-Canadian.

### What genre does Richie Hawtin play?

Hawtin plays techno, particularly minimal techno, with roots in Detroit's second wave. His Plastikman records lean toward stark, acid-tinged minimalism.

### What is Richie Hawtin's real name?

His full name is Richard Michael Hawtin, and Richie Hawtin is the name he uses as a DJ and producer. Plastikman, F.U.S.E. and Concept 1 are among his aliases.

### What is the difference between Plastikman and F.U.S.E.?

Both are Hawtin aliases. F.U.S.E. released the debut album Dimension Intrusion in 1993, while Plastikman debuted the same year with the single Spastik and the album Sheet One.

### What labels did Richie Hawtin found?

He co-founded Plus 8 with John Acquaviva in 1990 and launched M_nus in 1998. He also ran the Probe Records label.

### What is the Pyramid party at Amnesia Ibiza?

Pyramid is a Sunday-night party at Amnesia Ibiza that Hawtin curates with Ricardo Villalobos and Nina Kraviz. In 2026 it ran weekly from 14 June to 4 October.
