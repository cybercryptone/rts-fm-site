DJ Duhov belongs to the generation of Moscow house DJs who built their names on mix CDs and radio sets in the early 2000s. Public information about him is thin: there is no official biography, so this page sticks to what releases and catalogue entries record, and says plainly where nothing has been published.

## Early Releases

The earliest records of Duhov's work are mix CDs. [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/258374-DJ-Duhov) lists Bedroom Specialist and Summer In Space, both issued in 2003 by GR Records, and tags both as deep house and tech house. The cover of Bedroom Specialist, as transcribed on Discogs, credits the artist as "DJ Duhov, Moscow", and the same listing says the recording and mastering were done at a studio in Murmansk. In 2004 came Snezhnost', a compilation on Deus Vox Records. [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/artist/c4ce363a-f732-4255-a3de-de3f185e5ad2) lists Summer In Space, dated 2003, as the only release group under his name.

Discogs also lists Bulletproof, a mix released through Deep Mix Moscow Radio, under the Russian-language spelling of his name.

## Sound and Style

Everything catalogued under his name points the same way: deep house and tech house, delivered as long, continuous mixes. Discogs gives those two styles for each of his solo mix CDs. Later compilations broaden the picture slightly. Five Years Of Life, a 2011 digibook on Krysha Mira Music shared with Spy.Der, Nils, Daman and X-Trainer, carries Discogs style tags that run from deep house to downtempo and ambient.

## Later Compilations

His later credits are group projects. Slowdance, a CD in a digipak format, appeared in 2010 with Nils and DJ Arshanitsa, and a second Slowdance edition followed in 2013 with Gavrilius and Dan Jamkinsun. Origin. 7 Years, a mix credited to Duhov and Tea Wavesword, is also listed, without a year or label. Nothing in the catalogue shows a solo release after 2004, and no label, residency or birth information has been published by the artist that we could verify.

## FAQ

### How old is Duhov?

His age is not public. Neither a birth date nor a birth year has been published by the artist, so no age can be given.

### Where is Duhov from?

The cover of his 2003 mix CD Bedroom Specialist credits him as DJ Duhov from Moscow, and his sets in the RTS.FM archive were recorded at the Moscow studio. His birthplace has not been published.

### What genre does Duhov play?

Deep house and tech house. Discogs tags both of his 2003 mix CDs with those two styles.

### What is Duhov's real name?

He records under the name Duhov. Discogs lists a first name of Alex, but this comes from a user-edited database and we could not confirm it from an interview or an official source.

### Which labels has Duhov released on?

Discogs lists GR Records (2003), Deus Vox Records (2004) and, as part of a group release, Krysha Mira Music (2011). Slowdance, a 2010 and 2013 CD series, appears under its own name.

### Has Duhov played at RTS.FM?

Yes. The RTS.FM archive has 25 videos with his name in the title, the oldest dated 10 September 2008 in its title.
