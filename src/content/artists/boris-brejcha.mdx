Few producers in electronic music are as easy to recognise from the back of a festival field as Boris Brejcha. The German artist built a career on a mask and a sound he named himself. On 24 October 2026 he told fans that the story has an end date: 2027 is his last festival year, and the book closes at the end of 2028.

## Ludwigshafen, Thunderdome and a Day Job

Brejcha was born on 26 November 1981 in Ludwigshafen am Rhein, according to English and German Wikipedia and the MusicBrainz database. At six he was in the crowd at the 1988 Ramstein air show disaster and was badly burned, an experience he has spoken about in interviews. Both Wikipedia pages note that he still carries scars.

The route into music was ordinary. At about twelve, a classmate brought him a CD from the Dutch hardcore event Thunderdome, and he began making tracks at home, mostly trance at first, according to German Wikipedia. He trained as a freight forwarding clerk and later worked part-time at Telekom, which left him time for production. He has said the mask is unrelated to the accident.

## The Mask, Autist and Harthouse

His first releases arrived in 2006 on the Berlin label Autist Records. German Wikipedia names the singles "Monster" and "Yellow Kitchen". That same year, per English Wikipedia and DJ Mag Germany, he made his first festival appearance at Universo Paralello in Brazil, and the joker mask became his trademark. Beatportal's retelling places the Brazil invitation a year later, so the exact year of the debut is one of the points on which sources differ. What is consistent is the mask itself: carnival-inspired, chosen to stand out, and, in his own words, a way to give audiences two shows in one.

The German magazine Raveline named him an "Exceptional Talent 2007", and albums on Harthouse followed. His discography from those years includes *Die Maschinen sind gestrandet* (2007), *Mein Wahres Ich* (2008) and *My Name Is* (2010), before the *Feuerfalter* albums of 2013 and 2014 and *22* in 2016.

## High-Tech Minimal: The Sound

Brejcha describes his music as High-Tech Minimal. In a 2020 interview, Attack Magazine reports, he said he coined the name because he dislikes music being put in boxes, and the magazine places the sound across techno, tech house, minimal, melodic techno and progressive. RTS.FM's [report on his retirement announcement](/blog/boris-brejcha-retirement-2028-joker-career-story) collects the interviews behind that description, including the note that he plays only his own productions in his sets.

His process is equally self-contained. Beatportal reports that he works from a basement studio, mostly in the box with a MIDI keyboard, and quantises to a fine grid to keep a shuffle in the groove. In 2014 the track "Purple Noise", from *Feuerfalter Part 02*, was the one he credits with reaching a wider audience.

## Fckng Serious and the Cercle Breakthrough

In 2015 Brejcha founded his own label, Fckng Serious, with Ann Clue and Deniz Bul. The label's own history page lists Theydream among the early artists and says Moritz Hofbauer joined in 2020, Frieder & Jakob in 2023 and Julian Wassermann in mid-2024. It describes two European bus tours and a 2023 North American tour, and claims to be the first electronic label to run such tours, which is the label's own claim and not independently checked.

The wider breakthrough ran through video. According to Beatportal and German Wikipedia, he played Cercle sets at the Château de Fontainebleau near Paris in 2017 and the Grand Palais in Paris in 2019, and German Wikipedia adds a third at the amphitheatre in Nîmes in 2022. German Wikipedia reports more than 40 million views for the Fontainebleau film as of April 2025. In 2018 his Tomorrowland set on the Atmosphere stage spread widely on YouTube, and the same year he made his Time Warp debut in Mannheim, covered on RTS.FM's [Time Warp festival page](/festivals/time-warp).

## Ultra, Arenas and an Orchestra

In July 2019 Brejcha released "Gravity" with the vocalist Laura Korinth on Ultra Music, and in August 2019 he made his Ibiza debut at Hï Ibiza. His sixth album, *Space Diver*, came out on 24 January 2020 as his first for Ultra. *Never Stop Dancing* followed in 2021, the year he placed 42nd in DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs, and *Level One* in 2024.

The venue sizes grew with the catalogue. In April 2023, Beatportal reported that he was booked for two weekends at Coachella and a headline show at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, part of a North American bus tour. The 2025 summer included Kappa FuturFestival, the Italian festival also covered on RTS.FM's [Kappa FuturFestival page](/festivals/kappa-futurfestival).

In 2026 the format changed. The Hollywood Bowl listed Brejcha with the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra on 26 July 2026, conducted by Derrick Hodge, billing it as his first worldwide orchestra show. He was also announced for a headline date at Alexandra Palace in London on 19 September 2026. Fans looking ahead can find his name among the first announcements for [Sea You Festival](/festivals/sea-you-festival), which is set for 16 to 18 July 2027 at the Tunisee. RTS.FM's [report on EXIT's return to Serbia](/blog/exit-festival-returns-serbia-2027-petrovaradin) also lists him on the bill at EXIT's Sea Star in Umag, Croatia, according to the festival's tour page.

## The Farewell: 2027 and 2028

On 24 October 2026 Brejcha posted a video saying he will end the chapter of Boris Brejcha and The Joker at the end of 2028, according to DJ Mag Germany and EDMTunes. 2027 will be his last year of festival performances, and 2028 brings a farewell headline run billed as "Last Chapter: Becoming the Joker". In his accompanying comment, as Time To House renders it, he wrote: "I'm not sick, nor have I lost my joy in making music."

No tour dates, cities or ticket details had been published in the coverage we read, and he has not said what he will do afterwards beyond wanting to write a new story. Whether he will keep releasing music after 2028 is open. RTS.FM's [full report on the announcement](/blog/boris-brejcha-retirement-2028-joker-career-story) tracks what is confirmed and what is not.

## FAQ

### How old is Boris Brejcha?

Boris Brejcha was born on 26 November 1981, according to English and German Wikipedia and MusicBrainz, which makes him 44 as of early November 2026. He turns 45 on 26 November 2026.

### Where is Boris Brejcha from?

Brejcha was born in Ludwigshafen am Rhein, in Rhineland-Palatinate in western Germany.

### What nationality is Boris Brejcha?

German. Both Wikipedia and the festival and label sources describe him as a German DJ and producer.

### Who is Boris Brejcha?

Boris Brejcha is a German DJ and producer, born in Ludwigshafen in 1981, known for the carnival-inspired joker mask and for the sound he calls High-Tech Minimal. He founded the label Fckng Serious in 2015 and has released albums on Ultra Music since 2020.

### Is Boris Brejcha retiring?

Yes. He announced on 24 October 2026 that he will end his career at the end of 2028, after a final festival year in 2027 and a farewell headline tour billed as "Last Chapter: Becoming the Joker". Dates have not been published.

### Why does Boris Brejcha wear a mask?

He adopted a carnival-inspired joker mask for his first big festival gig, at Universo Paralello in Brazil. He has described it as a way to stand out and as giving audiences two shows in one, and he has said it is not connected to the Ramstein accident.

### What is Boris Brejcha's record label?

He runs Fckng Serious, which he founded in 2015 with Ann Clue and Deniz Bul. His albums since *Space Diver* (24 January 2020) have come out through Ultra Music.

### What genre is Boris Brejcha?

He calls it High-Tech Minimal. It draws on techno, tech house, minimal and melodic techno, and is built from his own productions, which he plays in his sets.

### What is Boris Brejcha's best-known track?

"Purple Noise" (2014), from the album *Feuerfalter Part 02*, is the track he credits with reaching a wider audience. His Cercle and Tomorrowland sets are the performances most associated with his online following.
