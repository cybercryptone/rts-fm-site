Lowris is a French producer, DJ and sound engineer based in Paris, a former resident of the Concrete parties and the owner of the label Æternum Music. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 9 December 2016, has 132,079 YouTube views and ranks 16th among the channel's 178 sessions.

## Where is Lowris from and how did he start?

XLR8R's June 2022 podcast feature gives his name as Loris Pugnet and calls him a Paris-based producer and DJ. It says he grew up in the Parisian suburbs and found electronic music at about 15, starting with hardcore before moving to house and techno. He credits a friend, Le Loup, with introducing him to Luciano and [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos). Trommel's 2019 premiere calls him a French artist and a Concrete resident.

A booking profile on Regoon describes a self-made sound engineer who moved from producing into running labels. It names Perlon, Minibar and a:rpia:r as influences, along with Zip and Villalobos. The earliest record we could place is the Carambar De Toi EP on Popcorn in 2011, per Sound Shelter's catalogue. No source we opened gives a birth date, so we cannot say how old he is.

## What does Lowris sound like?

XLR8R's subtitle for his podcast is "rolling stripped-back house", and the article elsewhere calls his sound wonky minimal. Trommel described his Setup 6.0 as playful and unpredictable, with bass lines over crunchy drums. Of his K.O.D. project with [Cabanne](/artists/cabanne) it wrote about repetitive, meditative grooves, fragmented vocal snippets and a dark after-hours feel, which it called psychedelic minimal.

His Bandcamp tags his own music as house, microhouse and minimal. The Hypebot artist page describes his track selection as careful and record-digging, though it is an aggregator and gives no source for the line.

## What are the career highlights of Lowris?

### Concrete

Concrete was the Paris collective and club where he held a residency. XLR8R says he played there until the venue closed in 2019, sharing decks with Onur Özer, Cabanne, [Sonja Moonear](/artists/sonja-moonear) and Zip. Trommel's 2018 profile of the K.O.D. release says he and Cabanne spent many hours together behind the Concrete decks.

### Æternum Music and Aeternum LTD

Lowris runs Æternum Music, a French label. Trommel reported on 22 September 2018 that it was launching a sub-label, Aeternum LTD, built around a series in which a different artist joins Lowris on each release. The first was K.O.D. (Kings of Delay), his duo with Cabanne, catalogued AEMLTD001. XLR8R adds that he releases under other names on the label and was preparing a second imprint with a partner, Adema of Full Scale.

### Minibar and the wider catalogue

His solo records appear on Minibar, the label Cabanne founded, and on Crazy Jack, Castanea and Ottavia, among others. XLR8R lists Minibar and Crazy Jack as homes for his stripped-back records, and Sound Shelter's catalogue runs from 2011 to 2026. Our [Karla Böhm page](/artists/karla-bohm) notes his remix of John Manhard on Sous:Sol.

### Studio and remixes

XLR8R says he works as a sound engineer at Eye Sound Studio, where it says he has worked with Steve O'Sullivan and [Rhadoo](/artists/rhadoo). It lists remixes for Tommy Vicari Jnr, Saudade and Pheek and Kike Mayor, and a new alias, Labello, a collaboration with Abé for the label Rotate.

## Which labels is Lowris associated with?

- **Æternum Music and Aeternum LTD.** His own French label and its limited sub-label, per Trommel and XLR8R.
- **Minibar.** The Paris vinyl label run by Cabanne. Sound Shelter lists two Lowris records on it. Cabanne's page covers its history.
- **Crazy Jack Records.** He released the Apollo EP there in 2014, and Regoon says he managed the label for a friend. Sound Shelter's bio says he did so until 2015.
- **Ottavia.** The Budapest vinyl label whose first release, OTTVA001, opens with his track, per Trommel.
- **Reciproc.** The Bucharest agency XLR8R says he was joining in 2022, a booking link rather than a label. For the Romanian scene around it, see our [Romanian minimal history](/blog/rominimal-history-romanian-minimal-techno).
