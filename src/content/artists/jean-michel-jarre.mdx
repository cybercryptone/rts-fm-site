Jean-Michel Jarre, born in Lyon on 24 August 1948, is a French composer and electronic music pioneer best known for his 1976 album Oxygène, one of the best-selling instrumental records ever made, and for open-air concerts staged at the scale of a city. As of 13 November 2026, the Oxygène 50th anniversary edition is due on 20 November 2026 from BMG, his first book, *Machines: A History of Electronic Music*, is due on 24 November 2026, and he opened Amsterdam Dance Event's 30th edition on 21 October 2026.

## Where did Jean-Michel Jarre come from?

Jarre was born on 24 August 1948 in Lyon, according to English and German Wikipedia and the MusicBrainz database. He is the son of the film composer Maurice Jarre, who left for Hollywood when his son was about five, and he was raised mainly by his mother and maternal grandparents. English Wikipedia describes a boy who struggled with classical piano but was drawn to Paris jazz clubs, and to the painter Pierre Soulages, whose layered canvases gave him the idea that a composer could work like a painter with frequencies.

He studied at the Lycée Michelet in Vanves, took harmony, counterpoint and fugue lessons with Jeannine Rueff, and in 1967 played guitar in a band called The Dustbins. In 1969 he joined the Groupe de Recherches Musicales in Paris, led by Pierre Schaeffer, the pioneer of musique concrète. The tape loops and found sounds of that environment, along with his early synthesizer experiments, shaped everything he later did.

## How did Oxygène come about?

Oxygène was assembled in a makeshift studio in Jarre's Paris apartment, a converted kitchen, with EMS VCS 3 and EMS Synthi AKS synthesizers and a Scully eight-track recorder. It was turned down by several labels before Francis Dreyfus agreed to release it, and it came out in France in December 1976 on Disques Motors and internationally in 1977 through Polydor. His 1972 album Deserted Palace had been an experimental record intended for film and television use, and from 1972 to 1975 he mainly wrote music and lyrics for other artists at Disques Motors, the label Dreyfus ran.

"Oxygène (Part IV)" reached number four on the UK singles chart and the album topped the French chart. Wikipedia gives an estimated 18 million copies sold as of 2016, a figure that RTS.FM's [Oxygène at 50 article](/blog/jean-michel-jarre-oxygene-50-years-ade) treats as an estimate because other sources cite lower totals. Equinoxe followed in 1978 on Disques Dreyfus, the label Francis Dreyfus created next.

## What does Jean-Michel Jarre's music sound like?

Jarre's music is melodic and sequence-driven, and he was never only a synth player. Wikipedia quotes one description of Oxygène as "bouncy, bubbling analog sequences and memorable hook lines", and a Qobuz retrospective cited in our Oxygène coverage frames it as the more romantic alternative to Kraftwerk's colder sound. Wikipedia lists Moby and Brian Canham of Pseudo Echo among the artists it influenced, and RTS.FM's piece on [the best ambient albums of 2026](/blog/best-ambient-albums-2026) points to Oxygène as a reference point for listeners tracing the genre's popular roots.

On Zoolook (1984) Jarre built tracks from fragments of human voices speaking in many languages, an early case of sampling driving an entire album. Oxygène 7-13 (1997) returned to the analogue instruments of the 1970s and was dedicated to his mentor Schaeffer. That record also pulled him into dance culture: The Orb's "Toxygene", commissioned as a remix of "Oxygène 8", reached number four on the UK singles chart in February 1997.

Jarre has also served as a UNESCO Goodwill Ambassador, is a Commander of the Legion of Honour (21 June 2021) and received the MOJO Lifetime Achievement Award in 2010 and the Stephen Hawking Medal for Science Communication in 2017.

## How is Jean-Michel Jarre connected to dance music?

Jarre turned collaborator in the 2010s and has strong ties to club culture, mainly through admiration and collaboration. Electronica 1: The Time Machine (2015) paired him with acts including Gesaffelstein, M83, Tangerine Dream, John Carpenter and Armin van Buuren, and Electronica 2: The Heart of Noise (2016) added Pet Shop Boys, Hans Zimmer, Yello and Gary Numan. Oxymore, released on 21 October 2022 by Sony Music and Menart Records as a tribute to Pierre Henry, came with remixes from Brian Eno, Nina Kraviz, Armin van Buuren and Martin Gore, tying his catalogue to [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) and the club world.

His links to techno run mainly through admiration. As RTS.FM's Oxygène coverage reports, [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) told Dazed in 2016 that he grew up listening to Jarre, and Jarre called Mills an architect of electronic music. We found no documented technical lineage from Oxygène to Detroit or Berlin techno, and the article says so.

## What is Jean-Michel Jarre's book Machines about and when is it out?

*Machines: A History of Electronic Music*, Jarre's first book, is due on 24 November 2026 from Thames & Hudson. It is a 368-page hardcover listed at $65 by the publisher's US page, built around 150 instruments from his own collection and 800 illustrations. Contributors named by the publisher include:

- Armin van Buuren
- Vince Clarke
- Gillian Gilbert
- Gary Numan
- Johnny Marr
- Hans Zimmer
- [Richie Hawtin](/artists/richie-hawtin)

Outlets quote differing instrument and illustration counts, so we use the publisher's figures. Our [Oxygène at 50 report](/blog/jean-michel-jarre-oxygene-50-years-ade) sets out the differences and the release date.

## What is Jean-Michel Jarre doing in 2025 and 2026?

Jarre has had a busy run since 2024. He performed at the closing ceremony of the 2024 Summer Paralympics on 8 September 2024, and his official biography lists a remastered Zoolook for its 40th anniversary on 21 February 2025, with a new track, "Moon Machine". German Wikipedia records a show at Stuttgart's Jazzopen on 11 July 2025, and the photo above comes from a June 2025 concert in Budapest. On 1 November 2025 he performed at Registan Square in Samarkand, his first concert in Uzbekistan, tied to the 43rd UNESCO General Conference, according to announcements from his own site and Sonic State.

## FAQ

### How old is Jean-Michel Jarre?

Jean-Michel Jarre was born on 24 August 1948, according to English and German Wikipedia and MusicBrainz, which makes him 78 as of November 2026.

### Who is Jean-Michel Jarre?

Jean-Michel Jarre is a French composer and electronic music pioneer, born in Lyon on 24 August 1948, best known for the 1976 album Oxygène and for large outdoor concerts with lasers, projections and fireworks. Wikipedia describes him as a French composer, performer and record producer. He made Oxygène in his Paris apartment.

### What is Jean-Michel Jarre's best-known album?

Jean-Michel Jarre's best-known album is Oxygène (1976). It was released in France in December 1976 on Disques Motors, reached number one in France, and is estimated by Wikipedia to have sold 18 million copies as of 2016.

### What label is Jean-Michel Jarre on?

Oxygène came out on Disques Motors, with Polydor handling international release. In 2022 BMG bought his publishing, and on 17 September 2026 it announced an exclusive licence for his recorded catalogue and future work.

### When is Oxygène's 50th anniversary?

Oxygène was released in France in December 1976, so 2026 is its 50th anniversary year. The Oxygène 50th anniversary edition is due on 20 November 2026 from BMG, with a half-speed vinyl cut and a Blu-ray carrying the album's first Dolby Atmos mix.

### Is Jean-Michel Jarre releasing a book?

Yes. *Machines: A History of Electronic Music*, his first book, is due on 24 November 2026 from Thames & Hudson, as a 368-page hardcover. See our [Oxygène at 50 report](/blog/jean-michel-jarre-oxygene-50-years-ade) for the details and the differing counts in press coverage.

### What did Jean-Michel Jarre play at ADE 2026?

Jean-Michel Jarre opened Amsterdam Dance Event's 30th edition on 21 October 2026 with the concert "Oxygène & Beyond" at AFAS Live, as the festival's guest of honour. A 3voor12 review, summarised in our [ADE 2026 wrap-up](/blog/ade-2026-wrap-up-30th-edition), praised the stylish opening and the laser harp in the encore, but found the music hard to dance to.

### Who is Jean-Michel Jarre's father?

The film composer Maurice Jarre, who left for the United States when Jean-Michel was a young child. They did not meet again until Jarre was 18, according to English Wikipedia.
