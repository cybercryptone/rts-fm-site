Oshana is an American DJ and producer based in Berlin who runs the Psionic label and works in house, techno and acid. She released the Ametisu EP on Yoyaku's YoY imprint in 2017 and joined the Yoyaku Agency that year. Her Yoyaku instore session, from 30 November 2017, has 41,761 views.

## Where is Oshana from and how did she start?

Oshana is American, and the sources we opened disagree on where exactly. Trommel's January 2018 feature says she was born in New York. MEOKO's December 2021 interview says she was born in the United States and raised in Ohio,. Electronic Groove, in April 2017, says she was American-born and had left a finance job in New York to become a full-time artist. She moved to Berlin roughly a year before that interview, and MEOKO says she built up savings and touring work first.

According to the 2019 MEOKO interview, she began collecting records at 11, learned to DJ properly in 2003, started producing around 2007 and committed to it fully in 2011. She ran local club nights in her Midwest city. Her influences include Sasha and Digweed, Carl Cox, Richie Hawtin and Magda. Electronic Groove calls her self-taught as a producer.

Trommel dates her activity from 2012. No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we do not state an age.

## What does Oshana sound like?

In 2017 Electronic Groove tied her new work to French house and described raw drum patterns and saturation for an analogue feel. MEOKO, in 2019, listed retro acid, Italo, electro and old-school house. Trommel's mix feature calls her sound funky, cosmic house and techno that works at peak time but is not a single style.

Trommel described the Astral Travel debut as spacey synths, an acid bassline and an early Detroit feel, and her album as moving through downtempo, breakbeats and techno edged with trance. XLR8R, in 2016, called her Arcadia EP dancefloor music built on synths, groove-driven basslines and sharp percussion.

## What are the career highlights of Oshana?

### Early releases and Partisan, 2014 to 2016

Trommel says her track "I See Through You" was included on [Raresh](/artists/raresh)'s 2014 Fabric mix. XLR8R, on 3 November 2016, listed earlier releases on Brouqade, Body Parts and Rue De Plaisance, and announced her Arcadia EP as the first release on Partisan, Anthea's new imprint. Trommel's 2020 label announcement calls the 2016 Missionary EP on Rue De Plaisance her debut, though XLR8R lists earlier records. Electronic Groove adds that [Ricardo Villalobos](/artists/ricardo-villalobos) and Raresh had supported her tracks.

### Yoyaku and the live show, 2017 to 2018

Electronic Groove says she joined the Yoyaku Agency in early 2017 through Anthea, whose Partisan label had a distribution deal with Yoyaku. She debuted a new live set at Rex Club in Paris in February 2017. Ametisu followed on YoY on 24 March 2017, according to Soundshelter.

Trommel's January 2018 feature says she played Robert Johnson in Frankfurt, Concrete in Paris and Club der Visionaere in Berlin on three days in July 2017, then dates in Malaysia and South Korea. It also listed a live slot at UP Festival in Prague for May 2018. A September 2018 Trommel profile of Yoyaku names her among the roster's emerging artists, with Zendid, and says she felt a natural pull to the agency. MEOKO says she was a resident at the Partisan parties at Club der Visionaere.

XLR8R announced her twice for Yoyaku's Barcelona Off Week programme: a Red58 showcase in May 2017 with [Varhat](/artists/varhat), [Janeret](/artists/janeret) and Zendid, and a 12-hour party on 15 June 2018 with Cabanne, Varhat, Janeret, Zendid and Roger Gerressen. We found no report of either night, so we cannot say how they went.

### Psionic and the album, 2020 to 2021

Trommel reported on 1 June 2020 that she was launching Psionic, with the As One EP by Astral Travel, her project with Anthea. In the 2021 MEOKO interview she said she started the label to control when and how her music came out, and that her independent period after Yoyaku shaped her music more than the agency years. Her debut album, *Disciples of Dystopia*, followed in autumn 2021. MEOKO says most of it was written over six to twelve months from the first Covid lockdown.

Trommel's March 2022 preview of the RSO.Berlin opening night said her set there would be her first live performance in several years.

Oshana's Yoyaku instore session was published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 30 November 2017. It runs 91 minutes and has 41,761 views, 812 likes and 80 comments as of 24 November 2026. The description lists only the shop's two web addresses and its Paris street address, so it gives no tracklist, recording date, venue detail or equipment note. ### The Yoyaku Barcelona white label

Yoyaku's own listing for YOYAKU.2017.BARCELONA is a four-track 12 inch with Varhat, Zendid, Janeret and Oshana. Her track, "It Is What It Is", is B2. The page says 300 stamped copies were pressed, 150 sold at Barcelona OFF on 16 June and 150 offered through Yoyaku from 26 June. The page gives no year; it comes from the catalogue number and from Yoyaku's June 2026 Barcelona press release, as summarised in our guide.

## Which labels is Oshana associated with?

- **Psionic.** Her own label, launched in 2020. Its Bandcamp page lists seven releases, including her own As One, Back to the Source, *Disciples of Dystopia* and Don't Give No Fux, plus records by Reade Truth, N-Gynn and Marcos Coya.
- **Partisan.** Anthea's imprint, which issued Arcadia and, per Yoyaku's artist page, Inner Dimension and Know Thyself EP.
- **YoY and Yoyaku.** Yoyaku's artist page lists Ametisu (YOY02) and Iwa (YoY.06) on YoY.
- **Others.** Rue de Plaisance, Brouqade, Body Parts, Altered Circuits and Acidisco.

## What is Oshana doing in 2026?

## FAQ

### How old is Oshana?

We could not verify her age. No source we opened gives a birth date or year. MEOKO says she began collecting records at 11 and went full time in music in 2011.

### Where is Oshana from?

She is American and has lived in Berlin since about 2016. Trommel says she was born in New York, while MEOKO says she was born in the United States and raised in Ohio.

### Who is Oshana?

Oshana is a DJ, producer and live performer in house and techno. She runs Psionic, records as Astral Travel with Anthea, and has been on the Yoyaku Agency roster since 2017.

### What is Oshana's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 30 November 2017, runs 91 minutes and has 41,761 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 59th by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### Did Oshana release on Yoyaku?

Yes. Ametisu, her three-track EP on Yoyaku's YoY imprint (YOY02), is dated 24 March 2017 by Soundshelter. She also has a track on the 2017 Yoyaku Barcelona white label.

### What is Oshana's label called?

Psionic, which launched in 2020 with the As One EP by Astral Travel.

### What is Astral Travel?

Astral Travel is Oshana's collaboration with Anthea. Trommel's 2025 feature says Anthea leads on direction and curation while Oshana leads on technical production.

### What is Oshana's debut album?

*Disciples of Dystopia*, a nine-track double LP on Psionic from autumn 2021. Trommel and Soundshelter give different release dates.

### Who has supported Oshana's music?

Electronic Groove says Ricardo Villalobos and Raresh supported her tracks.
