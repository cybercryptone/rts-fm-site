Raresh is one of the quietest names to reach the top of the minimal circuit. He has released little as a producer, gives few interviews and posts almost no online mixes, yet he is one of the three founders of the Romanian label a:rpia:r and a regular partner of Ricardo Villalobos in long back-to-back sets. His reputation was built in clubs, one night at a time.

## Early Life and Path Into Music

According to the [Amsterdam Dance Event artist profile](https://www.amsterdam-dance-event.nl/artists/raresh/2375/), Raresh was born in Bacău, Romania, and began playing at a small club in his home town at 15. He later moved to Bucharest, where joining an agency brought regular gigs at Sunrise events at the seaside. [Discogs](https://api.discogs.com/artists/777431) lists his full name as Rareș Ionuț Iliescu and describes him as a Romanian techno, house and minimal DJ. His date of birth is not published, so there is no reliable figure for his age.

His career took off in the mid-2000s. The [BLITZ club's artist bio](https://www.blitz.club/program/20260130/) says Ricardo Villalobos discovered and championed him in that period. An [XLR8R feature from 2015](https://xlr8r.com/features/romania-exquisitely-elusive/) says only that Villalobos has been a long-time patron of the trio, and notes that one rumor about how they met, with Raresh supporting Villalobos at a Bucharest show, is not confirmed. Crack Magazine's review of Petre Inspirescu's work also called the trio's sound [Villalobos-inspired](https://crackmagazine.net/article/music/petre-inspirescu) and called Villalobos Inspirescu's mentor.

## Sound and Style

The BLITZ bio describes his style as minimal, dubby and nuanced, and says he focuses on DJing rather than on releasing many productions. XLR8R describes the wider Romanian minimal sound as groovy, hypnotic, loopy techno, with long tracks often around seven to eight minutes that suit extended mixing, and with parties that can run for very long hours.

The same long-form approach runs through his shared sets with Villalobos. If you want the Chilean-born producer's side of the story, read our [Ricardo Villalobos profile](/artists/ricardo-villalobos); for the Amnesia party where the two share bills, see the [Pyramid residency guide](/blog/nina-kraviz-amnesia-ibiza-pyramid-residency).

## The Label Story

a:rpia:r was founded by Raresh with his friends Rhadoo and Petre Inspirescu, according to Discogs. The founding year is reported as 2007 by Discogs and as 2006 by XLR8R, so treat the date as approximate. MEOKO's 2013 piece on Romanian house, which spells the name Arpiar, [says the label has released its music on vinyl only](https://meoko.net/news/how-romania-help-change-the-face-of-house-music) and notes that the founders once had to take a 24-hour train to Prague to find the nearest record shop. The three also DJ together as RPR Soundsystem, which XLR8R describes as the front line of the Romanian movement.

a:rpia:r is also the label on which Villalobos released his 2017 album "Empirical House", according to the discography on [German Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Villalobos).

He also played the closing slot, from 3 a.m., at a BLITZ night in Munich on 30 January 2026, according to the club's programme page, a few months before the venue's farewell run described in our [BLITZ closure report](/blog/blitz-munich-closure).

## FAQ

### Who is Raresh?

Raresh is a Romanian DJ from Bacău who co-founded the vinyl-focused label a:rpia:r with Rhadoo and Petre Inspirescu. He is known for minimal, dubby DJ sets and for his long-standing connection with Ricardo Villalobos.

### How old is Raresh?

Raresh has not published his date of birth or year of birth, so his exact age cannot be verified.

### Where is Raresh from?

Raresh is from Romania. He was born in Bacău and later moved to Bucharest, where his career grew through the local club and agency scene.

### What genre does Raresh play?

He plays minimal techno and microhouse, in a dubby, hypnotic style that comes out of the Romanian minimal scene.

### What is Raresh's real name?

Discogs lists his full name as Rareș Ionuț Iliescu. He performs and records as Raresh.

### What is a:rpia:r?

a:rpia:r is a vinyl-focused Romanian label founded by Raresh, Rhadoo and Petre Inspirescu around 2006 or 2007, depending on the source. The trio also performs together as RPR Soundsystem.

### Does Raresh play with Ricardo Villalobos?

Yes. Villalobos is described as Raresh's long-time champion, and the two were billed together for the closing of Pyramid at Amnesia Ibiza on 4 October 2026.
