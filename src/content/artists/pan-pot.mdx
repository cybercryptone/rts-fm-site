Pan-Pot are a Berlin techno duo made up of Thomas Benedix and Tassilo Ippenberger, who have been releasing music since 2005. They broke through on Anja Schneider's Mobilee label, then built their own imprint, Second State, in 2014. In 2026 they are on the bill for [Time Warp Mannheim 2027](/blog/time-warp-mannheim-2027-lineup).

## Who is Pan-Pot and how did they start?

Pan-Pot are Tassilo Ippenberger and Thomas Benedix, two German DJs and producers based in Berlin. According to Wikipedia, they met in 2003 at the SAE Institute in Berlin, where both studied audio engineering and sound production, and the duo launched in 2005. Wikipedia's infobox gives 2004 as the start of their activity, which is why we cite 2005 as the first year of releases and treat the rest as approximate.

The break came through a booking. Wikipedia says that in 2005 they booked Anja Schneider for one of their own events, which led to a connection with her Mobilee label. Mobilee became the home of their early records, and Wikipedia's discography lists their Mobilee releases from 2005 through at least 2012.

## What does Pan-Pot sound like?

Wikipedia files their output under techno, tech house and experimental techno, with IDM influences in the earlier work. That description fits the Mobilee years better than their current sound, which has moved toward harder, more club-focused material.

In an August 2025 interview, Dirty Epic described a shift from minimal techno to peak-time techno, with progressive and occasional tech-trance elements still rooted in warehouse-style techno. The same interview says they play back-to-back, track for track, layering loops and using live effects and filters, with fluid roles in the studio.

Beatportal described their 2021 EP *Voodoo Signs* as adding breakbeat elements to their style. Vocal features have also been part of their albums, such as "Charly" with Hugh Betcha in 2007.

## What are the career highlights of Pan-Pot?

The first landmark was *Pan-O-Rama*, released on Mobilee in October 2007. Resident Advisor's review called it the first artist album on the label, with Hugh Betcha on "Charly" and Vincenzo on "Faces". Reviewer Paul Corey gave it a measured verdict, saying he liked it without being won over.

Wikipedia lists around 30 remixes between 2005 and 2015, including Booka Shade's "Regenerate" in 2010, Slam's "Lifetimes" in 2011 and [Stephan Bodzin](/artists/stephan-bodzin)'s "Zulu" in 2015. In 2012 they presented *Mobilee Back to Back Vol. 6*.

Their second album, *The Other*, arrived on Second State in 2015, eight years after the first, according to coverage from THUMP. In 2023 they marked 20 years of the project with *FORTE*, released on Second State on 17 November 2023 with ten tracks, per the tracklist published by EDM Identity.

A 2015 photograph on Wikimedia Commons shows them performing at the Sea You Festival in Freiburg on 19 July 2015, a small marker of the festival circuit they were already working. Wikipedia's entry for [Adam Beyer](/artists/adam-beyer)'s Drumcode lists Pan-Pot among that label's artists, and our [Drumcode history](/blog/drumcode-records-history-adam-beyer) covers the label's wider roster.

## Which labels is Pan-Pot associated with?

**Mobilee Records.** Anja Schneider's Berlin label released most of their early catalogue, including *Pan-O-Rama*.

**Einmaleins Musik.** Wikipedia lists some of their earliest singles here, including "Maffia" and "Pious Sin".

**Watergate Records.** Wikipedia lists two 2014 releases here: *Watergate Compilation 17* and *Conductor*.

**Second State.** Their own label, launched in 2014 according to Wikipedia, with *Cells* and *Grey Matter* as early releases. It became a platform for like-minded artists. Mixmag reported in June 2019 that [Amelie Lens](/artists/amelie-lens) was returning to the imprint with the *Hypnotized* EP after three earlier EPs there during her breakout year. In the 2025 interview, the duo said Second State paused signing new artists in 2024 after a change in its administrative structure, and had restarted.

**HUMAN.** A newer sub-brand, described in the same interview as primarily an event brand that tours internationally, with a limited roster. 909originals describes its aim as bridging raw techno energy with something deeper, and lists *Kalstrom*, *Opera (Street Parade Anthem)*, *Nightcode* and *TDD* as recent HUMAN releases before *Phantaxxxy* closed 2025 on 22 December.

## FAQ

### Who are the members of Pan-Pot?

Pan-Pot are Thomas Benedix and Tassilo Ippenberger, German DJs and producers based in Berlin. Wikipedia says they met in 2003 at the SAE Institute in Berlin.

### When did Pan-Pot start?

Wikipedia says the duo launched in 2005, although its infobox lists 2004 as the start of their activity. Their first Mobilee releases date from 2005.

### Where are Pan-Pot from?

They are a Berlin-based German techno duo. They met while studying at Berlin's SAE Institute and have worked from the city since.

### What genre is Pan-Pot?

Wikipedia lists techno, tech house and experimental techno. A 2025 Dirty Epic interview describes a move from minimal to peak-time techno with progressive and tech-trance touches.

### What is Pan-Pot's most important album?

*Pan-O-Rama*, released on Mobilee in October 2007, was the first artist album on the label, according to Resident Advisor. Later albums include *The Other* (2015) and *FORTE* (2023), both on Second State.

### What is Second State?

Second State is Pan-Pot's own label, launched in 2014. It has released music by Amelie Lens and others, and it put out the *PLANET 1* and *PLANET2* compilations in 2026.

### What is HUMAN by Pan-Pot?

HUMAN is a sub-brand of Pan-Pot, described in an August 2025 interview as primarily an event brand with a limited artist roster. It also releases music, including *Phantaxxxy* in December 2025.

### Are Pan-Pot playing Time Warp 2027?

### What did Pan-Pot release with Eli Brown?

"Coming In Heavy", released on Arcane on 3 April 2026, which Beatportal reports was the label's 100th release. It was made from stems Brown sent to the duo.

### Did Pan-Pot release anything on Mobilee?

Yes. Mobilee released most of their early catalogue, including the 2007 album *Pan-O-Rama*. Wikipedia's discography lists Mobilee releases from 2005 through at least 2012.
