Tomoki Tamura is a Berlin-based DJ, producer and label owner from Japan, who runs Holic Trax and makes deep, rolling house. He is best known for his Yoyaku instore session, an 87-minute set from June 2024 that has 3.24 million YouTube views, the most of any instore session on the channel, and for joint EPs with [Satoshi Tomiie](/artists/satoshi-tomiie).

## Where is Tomoki Tamura from and how did he start?

Attack Magazine's June 2016 "Introducing" profile says he is from Japan, lived in London for a long time and now lives in Berlin. His Bandcamp page also gives Berlin. Booking agency BUGECE says he is from Osaka and began by playing major clubs there. XLR8R, which ran his podcast in October 2017, says his dance music journey began in Japan.

Both XLR8R and BUGECE say he moved to London and started a party series called Holic in 2004. Attack says he went to London intending to stay two years and ended up staying much longer. XLR8R names The End, fabric, Ministry of Sound and Plastic People among his London venues, and a residency at The End. BUGECE says the Holic parties came before the label, which launched in 2012.

No source we opened gives a year for the move to Berlin, a birth date or a birth year. We therefore do not state an age.

## What does Tomoki Tamura sound like?

He describes his own sound to Attack as "Funky and fat driven drums with emotional Japanese synth sounds." He says he loves old-school house and samples vinyl on an MPC. BUGECE says he is influenced by black roots house from the United States, and adds that he also plays jazz, ambient and downtempo sets.

His recent releases lean deeper. Yoyaku's listing for the 2024 EP Dream Sketches tags it with acid, ambient and deep house styles at 120 to 128 BPM, and the follow-up Indian Summer is tagged deep house and dub house at 124 to 128 BPM. XLR8R says his 2017 podcast mix was less funky and dirty than a regular club set, by his own account.

## What are the career highlights of Tomoki Tamura?

### Holic nights and Holic Trax

XLR8R and BUGECE both date the Holic parties to 2004 in London and the label Holic Trax to 2012. XLR8R names the first release as Mr. G's Danceholics EP (HT001). It also lists Doublet's Tees 5 EP (HT004) and Trial Terror by Ekkohaus and Rufus (HT010). The Holic Trax Bandcamp now lists the label as Berlin-based and founded by Tamura, with a catalogue running past HT037.

### Doublet, HÖR and Tokyo

BUGECE and Attack say he is one half of Doublet with Tuccillo. His Mixcloud lists a Doublet set recorded live at Womb Tokyo on 26 March 2016. HÖR's site lists four of his shows between August 2021 and August 2023. The most viewed, from 1 August 2023, shows 271,300 views on that site.

### The Tomiie collaboration

With Tomiie he has released two joint 12-inch EPs in a series catalogued TOMTOM, a joint venture between Tomiie's Abstract Architecture and Holic Trax. Yoyaku calls Dream Sketches (TOMTOM001) their first collaboration and says it was recorded in Berlin. Indian Summer (TOMTOM002) is the second, and Yoyaku says it was jammed and recorded between New York and Berlin.

## What is Tomoki Tamura's Yoyaku instore session?

Tomoki Tamura's Yoyaku instore session is an 87-minute set published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 25 June 2024. As of 24 November 2026 it has 3,242,829 views, 104,721 likes and 2,604 comments. It is the most watched instore session on the channel, and in our data the most viewed video of any kind there, at more than four times the runner-up. The description lists the shop's store, social and event links, plus the artist's Instagram, and gives no further detail about the set or its tracks. Yoyaku is a record shop, label and distributor, and the instore series films DJs playing in the shop. Tamura has since appeared in two joint sessions with Tomiie, in December 2024 and July 2026. ## Which labels is Tomoki Tamura associated with?

His own label is Holic Trax, which its Bandcamp page lists in Berlin. BUGECE names the sub-labels Boothoic, Monoklo and Tom Tam Club. The Tom Tam Club name also appears on a compilation series on his Bandcamp, with volumes 2 and 3 compiled by him.

Attack says he has released EPs and remixes on Four:Twenty, SAW, Classic, Nite Grooves and Unblock. XLR8R lists releases on 89:GHOST and Unblock, including Retro City and Quiet Air Gun. The TOMTOM series is shared with Abstract Architecture.

Several Holic Trax records pair Tamura with Tuccillo or Takecha, including Salvadanaio EP (HT032), Super Colour EP (HT030) and Kansai EP (HT026), per the label's Bandcamp.

## FAQ

### How old is Tomoki Tamura?

We could not find his age. No source we opened gives a birth date or year, so we do not state one.

### Where is Tomoki Tamura from?

Attack Magazine and XLR8R say he is from Japan, and BUGECE says Osaka. He moved to London and later to Berlin, where his Bandcamp page says he is based.

### Who is Tomoki Tamura?

He is a Berlin-based DJ, producer and the owner of the label Holic Trax. He started the Holic party series in London in 2004, according to XLR8R and BUGECE.

### What is Tomoki Tamura's Yoyaku instore session?

It is an 87-minute set posted to the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 25 June 2024. It has 3,242,829 views as of 24 November 2026, the most of any instore session on the channel.

### How many views does the Tomoki Tamura Yoyaku session have?

3,242,829 views, 104,721 likes and 2,604 comments as of 24 November 2026, according to the YouTube Data API. Those counts change daily.

### What label does Tomoki Tamura run?

He runs Holic Trax, launched in 2012 according to XLR8R and BUGECE. Its Bandcamp lists it in Berlin and founded by Tamura.

### Has Tomoki Tamura worked with Satoshi Tomiie?

Yes. They released the EPs Dream Sketches (TOMTOM001) and Indian Summer (TOMTOM002) on Abstract Architecture and Holic Trax, and played two Yoyaku instore sessions together.

### Who is Doublet?

Doublet is Tamura's duo with Tuccillo, per Attack Magazine, XLR8R and BUGECE. They released the Tees 5 EP on Holic Trax and played Womb Tokyo in March 2016, according to his Mixcloud.

### What does Tomoki Tamura play?

Mostly house. He describes his own style as groovy, drum-led house with Japanese synth sounds, and BUGECE says he also plays jazz, ambient and downtempo sets.
