Ilya Schulz is a house producer whose EP RTSFM015 was the 15th release on the RTS.FM label, out in March 2026. Published information about him is limited: no biography, birth date or interview turned up in the sources we could open, so this page sticks to what the label's own Bandcamp page and the public databases actually record. It is a profile of a catalogue rather than a life story.

## On the RTS.FM label

The release is catalogued as RTSFM015 and went out on 27 March 2026 as a digital EP, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm015), which describes it as the 15th EP on the label from Ilya Schulz with a remix by Nico Castoldi. It has four tracks: "Obsession", "Brother Charlie", "Expectancy" and a Castoldi remix of "Obsession". Bandcamp tags it as deep house, minimal house, house and electronic, with Moscow as the label's location. The page also offers the release as a downloadable digital album, with the label's demo contact listed on its [main page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/).

RTSFM015 sits in the sequence the label has run since 2024, a numbered series of mostly four-track digital EPs that also includes [Hugobeat](/artists/hugobeat), [Type B.](/artists/type-b) and [Soultape's album](/artists/soultape). The label itself is the record arm of the RTS.FM internet radio project, introduced on the [home page](/#label).

He returned to the label as a remixer on RTSFM019, the [Techu](/artists/techu) EP out on 13 October 2026, where his remix of "Katzen" runs 6:40, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm019). Kandaurov remixed the other original, "We Ever Gonna".

## Earlier releases

[Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/7978167) holds an artist profile named Ilya Schulz, and [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/artist/16840419-11b4-490b-8ed0-6d360aa6fa94) places an artist of that name in Moscow. We found no source that explicitly ties these entries to the RTS.FM release, so treat the following as the catalogue of that profile rather than a confirmed biography.

Discogs credits him with the *Zabava EP* on Zingiber Audio in 2020 and *Fun Trip* on the Purple Box label in 2021, released as a 12-inch. It also lists remixes for Legit Trip on Purple Box (2020), for Ki.Mi. on Kashatskikh Records (2020), for Natural Piece of Aloe on MixCult (2021), for DP-6 on DP-6 Records (2021) and for Anton Lanski on Farmat (2025). Track appearances include compilations on Relikt, Indeep and, in 2023, a limited 12-inch on Panna Cotta Music. The same MixCult EP by Natural Piece of Aloe was also remixed by [Genning](/artists/genning), who appears elsewhere in the RTS.FM label family.

## Sound and style

The label's tags describe house in its deeper and more minimal forms, and the track titles on RTSFM015 give little else away. We have not attributed any stylistic claims that a source does not make. What the record shows is a short-form approach: three originals and one outside remix, a format the label has repeated across its recent numbers.
