Delano Smith was a Chicago-born, Detroit-raised DJ and producer who began DJing in 1978 and ran Mixmode Recordings. His family announced his death on 23 November 2026. He was known for deep house and techno rooted in the Detroit Beatdown sound. His 90-minute Yoyaku instore session, from October 2017, has 37,850 views.

## Where was Delano Smith from and how did he start?

Resident Advisor and Mixmag both say he was born in Chicago and raised in Detroit, and Mixmag places his upbringing on the city's West Side. In a March 2017 Skiddle interview he said his family moved to Detroit when he was about five. Resident Advisor and Mixmag give his start as 1978 (Skiddle says the late 1970s), and Mixmag says he was 16 and helping DJ friends carry equipment before they let him play. Mixmag dates his first residency to 1981, at the club L'Uomo.

He credited Ken Collier, the Detroit DJ who Skiddle says introduced mixing to the city, with inspiring him, and told Skiddle he once shared a residency with him. In a 2018 interview for The Standard's Le Bain series he said he played three Detroit clubs from 1980 to 1984.

He then stepped away. Skiddle says he did so in the mid-1980s for education and a steadier career, and Mixmag says he went to study computers in Kalamazoo, Michigan, before returning to DJing in the 1990s. The Standard interview puts his return in the early 1990s. Two other profiles, Amsterdam Dance Event and a 2012 review on MN Shift, describe the break as falling in the 1990s, so the sources disagree on timing.

Production came later. Amsterdam Dance Event says he began producing in 2002, with "Tribunal of Souls" on Psychostasia Recordings as his first track, and that he also appeared on Detroit Beatdown Vol. 1 on Third Ear Recordings that year, which inspired him to start his own label. In a 2016 Detroit Electronic Quarterly interview he named [Derrick May](/artists/derrick-may), Mike Slade and Norm Talley as the people who inspired him to start making records.

We could not verify a birth date. The Detroit News, as republished by Yahoo, says he was 62 when he died, but Mixmag has him helping DJ friends at 16 in 1978, which would suggest an earlier birth year. We do not state one.

## What does Delano Smith sound like?

His sets drew on a period before house and techno had firm boundaries. The Bandwagon listing for a 2014 Singapore date says his sound reflects that, and Resident Advisor's podcast notes describe the Detroit Beatdown style he helped pioneer as slower and more soulful, later widening into ambient, dub and contemporary techno.

His records ran along two tracks. He told Skiddle that his Sushitech output was more experimental, closer to modern dub techno, while Mixmode stayed rooted in traditional house. In the Detroit Electronic Quarterly interview he described his Sushitech material as influenced by dub, disco, funk and soul. MN Shift's review of his debut album says it drifts from deep house toward house-tinged techno and plays like a DJ mix.

[Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) said in a statement quoted by Mixmag that Smith "influenced either all or most of the Techno generation". MN Shift's review also quotes Derrick May as saying, "Without Delano there would be no Derrick."

## What are the career highlights of Delano Smith?

### Detroit Beatdown and Mixmode, 2002 to 2009

Resident Advisor names him a founding member of the Detroit Beatdown collective with Norm Talley and Mike "Agent X" Clark. He co-ran Mixmode Recordings: Skiddle and DoubleClap say it was founded in 2003, Resident Advisor's 2009 review calls him a co-owner, and MN Shift says he ran it with Tony Foster. Skiddle said in 2017 that it had put out more than 15 EPs. MN Shift lists earlier records on Kolour Recordings, Third Ear and Still Music. The Resident Advisor review dates Deep Fundamentals, Mixmode MM-08, to September 2009.

### The Sushitech albums, 2012 to 2017

Resident Advisor announced on 21 December 2011 that Sushitech, the Berlin label, would release his debut album An Odyssey in February 2012. It collected 11 unreleased tracks from the previous two years. DoubleClap lists the follow-up, Twilight, as a 2014 Sushitech album, and also points to remixes of Odyssey tracks by [Carl Craig](/artists/carl-craig) and Mike Huckaby. In March 2017 Skiddle reported he had just finished a triple-record compilation for Sushitech called The Lost Tapes, which appears to be the Detroit Lost Tapes set. A record shop listing gives its release as 16 November 2017 on Sushitech, catalogue SUSH39. DoubleClap dates it to 2019, another point where sources differ.

### Touring and collaborations

Resident Advisor says he played Panorama Bar, Rex Club, fabric and Contact. A listing for the Darker Than Wax showcase in Singapore billed him for 27 December 2014 and Skiddle's 2017 piece mentions planned spring touring in the UK and France. He also made a track, "Lost in Detroit", for the July 2011 Krypton Faktor EP with Gavin Herlihy on Apparel Music, and "Essence" with [Traumer](/artists/traumer), which appeared on Berg Audio's Directions compilation. XLR8R reported on 17 May 2018 that the label gave away an unreleased reduced mix of it.

### Detroit recognition

In 2016 he told Detroit Electronic Quarterly that he admired how professionally [Movement](/festivals/movement-detroit) was run and that the Charivari festival, which showcases Detroit artists, was close to his heart. WDET listed him for a set at Movement's Hart Plaza on Sunday 26 May 2024 at 4 p.m. Our [Detroit techno guide](/blog/detroit-techno-guide-2026-clubs-labels-history) notes that the 2026 "Respect the Architects" exhibit at Movement honoured him alongside others. Resident Advisor says he was also inducted into its own Respect The Architects series. His RA podcast, number 914, was published on 10 December 2023.

## Which labels is Delano Smith associated with?

- **Mixmode Recordings.** The label he ran, with Mixmode MM-08 Deep Fundamentals among its records. Sources differ on whether he founded it alone or with Tony Foster.
- **Sushitech.** The Berlin label that released An Odyssey, Twilight and Detroit Lost Tapes, and where Resident Advisor's podcast notes say he became a regular.
- **Third Ear Recordings.** The label that released Detroit Beatdown Vol. 1, per Amsterdam Dance Event, and where Resident Advisor and Mixmag say his music also appeared.
- **Apparel Music, Psychostasia, Kolour Recordings and Still Music.** Each appears in at least one profile or review we opened.
- **Berg Audio.** Released "Essence" with Traumer on a compilation.

## What happened with Delano Smith in 2026?

- **23 to 25 May 2026.** Movement ran at Hart Plaza, per our Detroit guide. Mixmag reports that in summer 2026 he played Movement Detroit, the Great Beyond Festival in Minnesota and Refuge in New York.
- **Before November 2026.** The Detroit News says he played the Lincoln Factory and TV Lounge in Detroit in the months before he died.
- **23 November 2026.** The Detroit festival Charivari confirmed his death to Resident Advisor on behalf of his family, and Mixmag published a family statement the same day. The Detroit News says he was 62. No cause of death has been given and funeral arrangements had not been announced when these reports ran. Several outlets report that he had been treated for a rare cancer.
- **Tributes.** Kevin Saunderson wrote "Rest in Power Delano Smith", per the Detroit News. The death came a day after a celebration of life for Juan Atkins, who died in October, covered in our [Juan Atkins report](/blog/juan-atkins-dies-detroit-techno-pioneer).

## FAQ

### How old was Delano Smith?

The Detroit News, as republished by Yahoo, says he was 62 when he died on 23 November 2026. We could not find a birth date or year in any source we opened.

### Where was Delano Smith from?

He was born in Chicago and raised in Detroit, on the city's West Side, according to Resident Advisor and Mixmag. He told Skiddle his family moved to Detroit when he was about five.

### Who was Delano Smith?

Delano Smith was a Detroit DJ and producer who started playing in 1978, ran Mixmode Recordings and released albums on the Berlin label Sushitech. Resident Advisor calls him a founding member of the Detroit Beatdown collective.

### When did Delano Smith die?

His family announced his death on 23 November 2026, via the Charivari festival, according to Resident Advisor. No cause has been given.

### What was Delano Smith's Yoyaku instore session?

### Which labels did Delano Smith release on?

Mixmode Recordings, Sushitech, Third Ear Recordings, Apparel Music and Berg Audio, plus Psychostasia, Kolour and Still Music per other profiles.

An Odyssey, his debut album, released on Sushitech in 2012. Resident Advisor and MN Shift both covered it.
