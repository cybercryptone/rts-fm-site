Nemec is a house producer and DJ who released the EP RTSFM008 on the RTS.FM label in April 2025, contributed to the label's resident compilation that October and played an RTS.FM session in Alicante, Spain, in between. The sources we could open publish no biography, birth date or interview, and a search of the usual databases did not turn up a profile we could confidently match to this artist. This page therefore covers the label releases and the recorded session, and nothing else.

## On the RTS.FM label

RTSFM008 was released on 11 April 2025 as a digital release, according to the [RTS.FM Bandcamp page](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm008). The label describes it as three original soundscapes complemented by a remix from Kandaurov. The four tracks are "MicroPlastic", "Obssesion", "Weaving Clouds" and the Kandaurov remix of "MicroPlastic". Bandcamp tags it deep house, minimal house, house and electronic, with Moscow as the label's location. The title "Obssesion" is spelled that way on the label's pages.

Kandaurov also remixed or mastered [Hugobeat's RTSFM014](/artists/hugobeat) and remixed [Type B.'s RTSFM013](/artists/type-b), per the Bandcamp pages.

On 17 October 2025 Nemec appeared on the [RTSFM011](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/album/rtsfm011) compilation with the track "Outside". The compilation, which the label describes as a collection of its resident artists, also features [Victor Polo](/artists/victor-polo), [Genning](/artists/genning) and [Nicolas Barnes](/artists/nicolas-barnes).

## Sound and style

The label calls the three originals on RTSFM008 "soundscapes", and tags the release deep house and minimal house. Titles such as "Weaving Clouds" and "MicroPlastic" fit the soundscape description, though that is as much as the published material supports. No interview or artist statement is available to say more.

## RTS.FM Connection

RTS.FM's archive holds six videos with Nemec in the title: one for each of the four tracks on RTSFM008, uploaded on 1 and 2 May 2025, one for "Outside" from RTSFM011, and a full session titled "Nemec | RTS.FM Alicante, Spain 11.09.2025". That session, uploaded on 11 September 2025, runs about 1 hour 15 minutes. Its description links his SoundCloud and Instagram pages next to the label's own channels. The wider archive is covered in our look at [what RTS.FM has recorded](/blog/rtsfm-archive-data-2009-golden-era).
