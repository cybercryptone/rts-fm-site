Jen Cardini, also credited as Jennifer Cardini, is a French-born DJ, producer and label founder based in Berlin. They run Correspondant and Dischi Autunno and started the queer party series Nightclubbing. Their Yoyaku instore session, published on 21 May 2026, has 65,328 YouTube views.

## Where is Jen Cardini from and how did they start?

Cardini is French. The Vinyl Factory's December 2022 profile says they grew up in Monaco and began DJing in Nice in 1994, and a 15 Questions interview from July 2015 also dates the start to 1994 and describes years in the south of France before a move to Paris in 1998. Qobuz's biography calls them "originally from Nice". Sources therefore differ on the hometown, and we found no birthplace or birth date in any page we could open.

In Paris they became a resident at Le Pulp and at the Rex Club, according to the Vinyl Factory, and CTM Festival says they spent more than ten years building a name in Paris before playing major clubs abroad. Qobuz says they later moved to Cologne and then to Berlin. MUTEK's Montreal artist page gives Berlin as their base today.

The Vinyl Factory also notes an early electro-punk duo, The Pussy Killers, formed with the late DJ Sextoy (Delphine Palatsi), which shared a French festival bill with Aphex Twin in 2000. Older coverage, including that profile, uses "Jennifer" and she/her. Metal Magazine says Cardini announced the name Jen and they/them pronouns as a political as well as personal step, and we use that here. The page does not give the year.

## What does Jen Cardini sound like?

Qobuz places them in electro, house and minimal techno and says their reputation rests mainly on DJ sets. CTM says their weekday slot at Le Pulp mixed experimental techno, acid and nu-disco. The Amsterdam Dance Event artist page, which reads as promotional copy, describes house with gothic and wave-influenced textures.

On record, Electronic Groove describes the 2026 EP *petit monstre* as sparse and percussion-driven, in line with their recent DJ sets, with an acid version that draws on the sounds of their early years as a DJ.

## What are the career highlights of Jen Cardini?

### Clubs and festivals

The Vinyl Factory lists Panorama Bar, fabric, Lux, De School and Good Room among the clubs they have played. CTM's programme page confirms a Panorama Bar night at Berghain on Friday 27 May 2022. Metal Magazine says they have played Sónar Barcelona, Sala Apolo and Fusion, and the ADE page adds Glastonbury, Primavera, Field Day, Mutek, Bassiani and Circoloco, plus art venues such as MoMA PS1 and the Centre Pompidou. We treat the ADE list as a self-description, not an independently checked tour history. Our [MUTEK festival page](/festivals/mutek) covers one of those events.

### Kompakt and Feeling Strange

Qobuz says the 2008 mix CD *Feeling Strange* made them Kompakt's first female signee. CTM calls the Cologne link with Kompakt a turning point and the mix a landmark. For background on the label, see our [Kompakt history](/blog/kompakt-records-cologne-history). Berghain's home label has its own story in our [Ostgut Ton history](/blog/ostgut-ton-berghain-label-history).

### Nightclubbing

Metal Magazine describes Nightclubbing as a queer party series with residencies at RSO in Berlin and Outra Cena in Lisbon. Resident Advisor reported in October 2025 that Cardini was launching a Nightclubbing festival in Paris from 24 to 27 September 2026, centred on La Villette and with MUTEK as a partner.

## Which labels is Jen Cardini associated with?

Cardini founded Correspondant in 2011, per Qobuz and CTM, whose roster there includes Agents Of Time, Man Power and Andre Bratten. Qobuz says they co-founded Dischi Autunno with Noura Labbani in 2017, and Metal Magazine, whose interviewer also gives 2017, says it began as an indie label for bands and moved toward dance music. Its releases include a Dollkraut album and the debut album *Curses* by Luca.

MUTEK's page adds Færies, described as a recent project for transdisciplinary and post-genre artists, and lists Crosstown Rebels, Kompakt and Mobilee as associated labels. Sources also name Kill The DJ, though we did not find release details. The 2026 EP appeared on SMIILEWAX, the new vinyl imprint of Chloé Caillet's SMIILE Records.

## FAQ

### How old is Jen Cardini?

We could not confirm an age. None of the pages we opened gives a birth date or year. Sources date their DJing to 1994 and describe about 30 years in club culture.

### Where is Jen Cardini from?

They are French. The Vinyl Factory says they grew up in Monaco and started DJing in Nice, while Qobuz says they are originally from Nice. They are now based in Berlin, per MUTEK.

### Who is Jen Cardini?

A French-born DJ, producer and label founder in Berlin who runs Correspondant and Dischi Autunno and created the Nightclubbing party series. They are also credited as Jennifer Cardini.

### What pronouns does Jen Cardini use?

They/them, according to Metal Magazine and the Amsterdam Dance Event artist page. Older coverage uses Jennifer and she/her.

Correspondant, founded in 2011, and Dischi Autunno, co-founded in 2017. MUTEK also credits them with Færies.

A queer party series created by Cardini, with residencies at RSO in Berlin and Outra Cena in Lisbon. A Paris festival of the same name was planned for 24 to 27 September 2026.
