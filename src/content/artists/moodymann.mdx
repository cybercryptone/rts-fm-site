Moodymann is Kenny Dixon Jr., a Detroit house producer and DJ who owns two independent labels, KDJ Records and Mahogani Music. He is best known for soulful, sample-heavy, lo-fi house, including the albums Silence in the Secret Garden (2003) and Black Mahogani (2004), and for DJ sets in which he often wears a mask or veil.

## Where is Moodymann from and how did he start?

Kenny Dixon Jr. is based in Detroit. Wikipedia says he was born in Los Angeles and that his mother brought him back to Detroit when he was three weeks old. That is a single-source claim, and Roland's history of his labels does not give a birthplace, so treat it with care. No source we opened gives a birth date or birth year, so we do not estimate his age.

According to Wikipedia, he played drums at his grandfather's jazz club as a teenager and, before house, produced beats for local hip-hop acts. Roland says that uncredited rap work shaped his later insistence on being credited. Wikipedia dates his first record under the Moodymann name to 1992.

In the mid-1990s he was a resident DJ at the Outcast Motorcycle Club in Detroit, per Wikipedia.

## What does Moodymann sound like?

Roland describes a blend of sampling and live musicians that draws on Detroit techno, disco, funk and R&B, made on older analog gear and described by Dixon himself as warm and hazy. He has said early techno felt more soulful and was less about building to a peak, and that he prefers music that feels good throughout.

The records are loose and rough-edged on purpose. Tracks stretch over samples from Chic and Mass Production, as on the 1996 single "I Can't Kick This Feelin When It Hits", then shift into gospel, jazz-tinged, acid or blues-influenced territory. Roland names Prince as his main inspiration, which shows in later albums where he sings more and uses live backing players.

That makes him a different kind of Detroit house artist from the machine-funk lineage of [Juan Atkins](/artists/juan-atkins). His closest peer is [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish), whose early records appeared on KDJ.

## What are the career highlights of Moodymann?

### The first records and Planet E

KDJ Records dates from 1994, per Wikipedia and Roland. Roland notes that two records are credited as "KDJ 1", which Dixon puts down to a pressing-plant mix-up. Early runs were small, about 150 copies, according to Roland. KDJ also released early records by Theo Parrish (1995), Rick Wilhite (1996) and Andres (1997).

His debut album, Silentintroduction (1997), came out on [Carl Craig](/artists/carl-craig)'s Planet E and collected early KDJ singles.

### The Peacefrog albums

Roland lists four albums on Peacefrog: Mahogany Brown (1998), Forevernevermore (2000), Silence in the Secret Garden (2003) and Black Mahogani (2004). These are the records most often cited as his core. The title track of Forevernevermore was a 1998 single, according to Wikipedia.

### 3 Chairs and Dekmantel

Moodymann is a member of 3 Chairs, which Wikipedia says formed in Detroit in 1997 as a trio with Theo Parrish and Rick Wilhite, with Marcellus Pittman joining in 2003. At Dekmantel in Amsterdam in 2014, The Quietus reported that the four began a six-hour set on the woodland stage on the Saturday, joined by Jay Daniel and Kyle Hall. Our [Dekmantel Festival page](/festivals/dekmantel-festival) has the wider history of that edition.

### Soul Skate and Movement

Dixon oversees Soul Skate, a roller-skating party held in Detroit around Memorial Day weekend, which Wikipedia says has run since 2007. Roland says it has grown from one night to a week of events. Wikipedia adds that he partnered with Red Bull Music Academy for a Soul Skate in South London in 2010.

Roland also reports that Detroit police warned him not to play "Freeki Mutha F*cker" at Movement in 2007 and that he played it anyway. That track was a white label in 2003 and was officially released in 2011, per the same article. For the festival itself, see [Movement Detroit](/festivals/movement-detroit).

### Later albums and Grand Theft Auto

Later albums include Picture This (2012), DJ-Kicks (2016, !K7), Sinner and Taken Away (KDJ, 21 May 2020). Roland says Sinner was sold mainly at a backyard barbecue.

## Which labels is Moodymann associated with?

KDJ Records is his own imprint, founded in 1994. Mahogani Music followed in 2002, according to Roland, as a sister label for other artists. Dixon describes KDJ as his personal output and Mahogani as the outlet for everyone else.

Roland lists Amp Fiddler, Monica Blaire and Sheefy McFly among the Mahogani artists, plus archival material by J Dilla. Outside his own labels, his albums appeared on Planet E and Peacefrog.

## Why does Moodymann wear a mask or veil?

Roland says he often DJs in a mask or veil, and his face appears in the KDJ Records logo. In the photo at the top of this page, taken at Sónar in 2010, he wears a white head covering and sunglasses. We did not find a source in which he explains the look, so we do not offer a reason.

## FAQ

### Who is Moodymann?

Moodymann is the stage name of Kenny Dixon Jr., a Detroit house producer, DJ and label owner. He runs KDJ Records and Mahogani Music and is known for soulful, sample-based house.

### What is Moodymann's real name?

His real name is Kenny Dixon Jr. Wikipedia also lists the aliases Moody, KDJ and J.A.N., and Roland notes that KDJ is the name he uses for his own label.

### How old is Moodymann?

We could not find a birth date or birth year in any source we opened, so we do not give an age. If a verifiable source appears, this page will be updated.

### Where is Moodymann from?

He is based in Detroit, Michigan. Wikipedia says he was born in Los Angeles and moved to Detroit as a newborn, but we found that only in one source.

### What genre is Moodymann?

Moodymann makes Detroit house with strong soul, funk, disco and jazz influences. Roland describes a blend of sampling and live musicians, made on older analog gear.

### What label is Moodymann on?

He releases through his own KDJ Records and runs Mahogani Music for other artists. His 1990s and 2000s albums appeared on Planet E and Peacefrog.

### What are the best Moodymann albums?

Roland names Silentintroduction, Mahogany Brown, Forevernevermore, Silence in the Secret Garden and Black Mahogani as core releases. His most recent original album that we could verify is Taken Away (2020, KDJ).

### Why does Moodymann wear a mask?

Roland says he often DJs wearing a mask or veil. We found no sourced explanation for it, so we do not guess.

### What is Soul Skate?

Soul Skate is a roller-skating party that Dixon oversees in Detroit around Memorial Day weekend. Wikipedia says it has run since 2007, and Roland says it has grown into a week of events.

### What is 3 Chairs?

3 Chairs is a Detroit house group of Moodymann, Theo Parrish, Rick Wilhite and Marcellus Pittman, according to Wikipedia. It formed in 1997 as a trio, with Pittman joining in 2003.
