Zendid is a French DJ and production duo, Adrien Doumenge and Lenny Mailleau, from Toulouse. They are known for minimal house, wonky breaks and back-to-back sets, released the In The Shell LP on Yoyaku, and recorded a Yoyaku instore session published on 8 February 2018 that has 31,276 YouTube views.

## Where is Zendid from and how did they start?

XLR8R's April 2016 feature "Merging of Minds" describes Zendid as a French duo from Toulouse, in the south of the country. Adrien Doumenge was 25 and Lenny Mailleau was 24 when it ran. Yoyaku's artist page also calls them a DJ and producer duo from Toulouse. Their Bandcamp page lists Berlin instead, and Refuge Worldwide's bio says Lenny has since moved to Berlin, so where the duo is based today is unclear.

According to the same feature, they met around six years before the interview, when a mutual friend introduced them at a party where Adrien was DJing. Both were already producing on their own, and the project started in 2011. The name joins two nicknames: "Zen" for Lenny and "Didou" for Adrien. XLR8R says Lenny had played piano since he was 12, while Refuge Worldwide's bio says he started at 8, so the sources disagree on that detail.

The feature says their first release was a single track on a Park and Ride Records compilation in December 2014, and it notes that its own timeline has inconsistencies. They kept a low profile at the time and gave few interviews. They told XLR8R they planned to spend a few months in Berlin that summer and to build their own label over the following year.

## What does Zendid sound like?

XLR8R calls them well-known and widely respected names in microhouse circles and describes their later album as drawing on breaks and wonky minimal. Trommel's May 2022 podcast page praises their "reductionist bangers" and a clear symbiosis behind the decks. Their 2016 Understellite EP for Infuse, reviewed by XLR8R, ranged from groove-driven main-room house to a quirkier track with a snaking bassline and a funk-influenced closer.

On process, they told XLR8R they work mainly with hardware, using a computer as a sequencer and for final processing, and that they produce only together. They said they DJ exclusively back to back, usually for two hours. They release sparingly and show unreleased material only to close friends and labels. Yoyaku's listing for their AM Isobho EP gives a tempo range of 124 to 127 BPM and files it under house, minimal and techno.

## What are the career highlights of Zendid?

### Early support and Cure Music, 2014 to 2016

In 2016, XLR8R said Zip, [Sonja Moonear](/artists/sonja-moonear), Margaret Dygas, Rhadoo and [Raresh](/artists/raresh) had played their tracks. Raresh received "OMW" from their manager and became what the feature calls their strongest advocate, later inviting them to open for him at a gig in Montpellier.

Their Fortu EP was the first release on Cure Music, the vinyl-only label started in Buenos Aires by [Momo Trosman](/artists/momo-trosman) and Felipe Valenzuela. Buenos Aliens, in an article dated 3 December 2015, reports that it was pressed in 350 copies with no reissue planned. In June 2016 they made their debut EP for Infuse, Understellite, per XLR8R's post of 30 June 2016.

### Yoyaku, Barcelona and the album, 2017 to 2020

Zendid appears on the 2017 Yoyaku Barcelona white label with [Varhat](/artists/varhat), [Janeret](/artists/janeret) and Oshana. Yoyaku's page lists Zendid's track as "Chambre 202" at A2 and describes a pressing of 300 stamped copies, half sold at Barcelona OFF on 16 June and half online on 26 June. XLR8R listed them as a scheduled performer and a member of the Yoyaku roster for the Yoyaku and XLR8R Off-Week showcase in Barcelona on 15 June 2018, an event around [Sónar](/festivals/sonar). We did not find a report confirming the night.

XLR8R's announcement of 11 February 2020 said the In The Shell LP, their first album and eight tracks long, would come out on vinyl on 17 March. It says the record had been in the works for several years and that the artwork came from Yoyaku's Belastro studio. See our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) for the shop and label background.

### Later bookings, 2021 and 2022

Trommel announced a Yoyaku showcase by Reverse and LA ASO in Barcelona on Saturday 21 August 2021, with Zendid, [Lowris](/artists/lowris) and Varhat billed as headliners. We found no post-event report. Trommel's May 2022 podcast page lists festivals including Sunwaves, Epizode and Caprices, and clubs including Guesthouse, ReSolute, Concrete, Fuse and Mutabor among their regular stops. It says their remixes include tracks for Rowlanz, Alci and Guillermo Jamas.

## What is Zendid's Yoyaku instore session?

Zendid's Yoyaku instore session was published on the Yoyaku Record Store YouTube channel on 8 February 2018. It runs about 107 minutes and has 31,276 views, 524 likes and 68 comments as of 24 November 2026. The upload came about four months before the June 2018 Barcelona showcase that listed them as roster artists. In 2016 the duo told XLR8R that they DJ only back to back, usually in two-hour sets. ## Which labels is Zendid associated with?

- **Yoyaku and its sub-labels.** The In The Shell LP is YOYAKU004LP. Yoyaku's artist page also lists Hiyoko on YoY (YOY07) and Magycops on Aku (AKU010). XLR8R's Off-Week 2018 announcement called them a member of the Yoyaku roster.
- **Timeframe.** Their own label, which XLR8R says launched in 2017. Its first release was the AM Isobho EP (TMFR001), according to Yoyaku's listing.
- **Cure Music.** The label whose first record was their Fortu EP.
- **Infuse.** The London imprint of the Fuse collective, home of their Understellite EP.
- **Others.** XLR8R names Body Parts as their 2014 debut and Lamache's Discobar. Trommel adds Disdat and Elephant Moon. Yoyaku's artist page also lists Metereze, High Ends and a Green Curry record with a catalogue number but no label.

## FAQ

### How old is Zendid?

We could not verify birth dates. XLR8R's April 2016 feature gave Adrien Doumenge as 25 and Lenny Mailleau as 24, which would make them roughly 34 to 36 in late 2026, depending on birthdays.

### Where is Zendid from?

They are French and come from Toulouse, according to XLR8R and Yoyaku. Their Bandcamp page lists Berlin, and Refuge Worldwide says Lenny moved there.

### Who is Zendid?

Zendid is a DJ and production duo made up of Adrien Doumenge and Lenny Mailleau. They make minimal house and breaks and are on the Yoyaku roster.

### What does the name Zendid mean?

XLR8R says it joins Lenny's nickname "Zen" with Adrien's nickname "Didou".

### What is Zendid's Yoyaku instore session?

It was published on 8 February 2018, runs about 107 minutes and has 31,276 views as of 24 November 2026. It ranks 71st by views among the 178 instore sessions we counted.

### What is the In The Shell LP?

It is the duo's first album, eight tracks on Yoyaku as YOYAKU004LP. XLR8R announced it in February 2020 for a 17 March vinyl release.

### What was the first release on Cure Music?

Zendid's Fortu EP, pressed in 350 copies, according to Buenos Aliens in December 2015.

### Does Zendid run a label?

Yes. XLR8R says they launched Timeframe in 2017, and Yoyaku lists AM Isobho as its first release, TMFR001.

### Is Zendid on the Yoyaku Barcelona 2017 white label?

Yes. Yoyaku's page lists their track "Chambre 202" alongside Varhat, Janeret and Oshana, on a 300-copy pressing.

### Do Zendid play together or separately?

XLR8R says they produce only together and DJ exclusively back to back. Lenny Mailleau also performs solo, per Refuge Worldwide.
