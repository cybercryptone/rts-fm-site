Superpoze is the stage name of Gabriel Legeleux, a French electronic musician and film composer born in Caen and working in Paris. He is known for atmospheric albums such as Opening and Siècle and for scoring French features. His Yoyaku instore session, published on 6 June 2025, has 43,355 views.

## Where is Superpoze from and how did he start?

French Wikipedia gives his name as Gabriel Legeleux, born in Caen, Calvados, and says he studied percussion at the conservatoire there. Its infobox dates his birth to 26 June 1992 and lists piano, percussion and synthesizers as his instruments. Qobuz calls him a Caen native, and the Amsterdam Dance Event artist page says he rose quickly on the Caen electro scene.

Numéro's interview of 20 February 2017 adds that he is self-taught in electronic music, though he says he sang in a conservatoire choir. It says he founded his own label at 19. Wikipedia dates his first EP, From the Cold, to November 2012, and lists him as active since 2012. Bandcamp lists Paris as his base, and Qobuz interviewed him in his Paris studio in 2022.

## What does Superpoze sound like?

Numéro describes his music as airy, atmospheric and cinematic, closer to a cerebral kind of pop than to dancefloor electronics. His second album, For We the Living, is almost entirely instrumental, according to the same piece, and he told the magazine he thinks of a record as a journey from one point to another. Kiosk Radio's 2025 listing calls it emotive, cerebral electronic music that blends synths, drum machines and acoustic textures, and tags it ambient, trip hop and electronic.

Qobuz says the third album, Nova Cardinale, moved away from future beats toward lyrical, organic sounds, with piano, cello, viola da gamba and vibraphone, and names Philip Glass, Renaissance music and Nick Cave and Warren Ellis as reference points. A SoundCloud feature from December 2025 has him describing Siècle as built from machines first, with piano and synth melodies added and string and wind parts written by Leonardo Ortega.

## What are the career highlights of Superpoze?

### Early records, 2012 to 2017

According to French Wikipedia, From the Cold (November 2012) and Jaguar (September 2013) were followed by his first album, Opening, on 6 April 2015, and by For We the Living in February 2017. Numéro lists that second album, due on 24 February 2017, as Combien Mille Records/A+LSO, and says he had by then worked with DJ Pone, Nekfeu and Fakear. The Amsterdam Dance Event profile, which is undated, lists a live tour with festival appearances, DJ sets across Europe, an EP and tour with his band Kuage, a joint project with Stwo and a maxi with Kitsuné.

### Producer and film composer

Wikipedia credits him with producing "Reuf" on Nekfeu's album Feu (2015), composing DJ Pone's Radiant (21 October 2016), co-producing four tracks on Lomepal's FLIP (2017) and co-composing on Lomepal's Jeannine (2018). With Ambroise Willaume he produced Alex Beaupain's Pas plus le jour que la nuit (4 October 2019), and he made the EP Endless Cultural Turnover with Jacques in 2019.

On screen, Wikipedia says his score for the documentary À voix haute (2017) was nominated for the César for best documentary in 2018. It names Frères ennemis (2018) as his first fiction score, then La Source (2019), L'Astronaute and Petites (both 2023), Les Derniers Hommes (2024) and Louise (2025).

### Stage and live work

Wikipedia lists music and a role in Hunter at the Théâtre national de Chaillot (2018) and a piece for automated instruments at the Grand Palais (2018). Fnac announced a Fnac Live Paris set for 30 June 2022.

### Siècle and the last two years

Bandcamp dates the Opening 10 year anniversary edition to April 2025. Internet Tattoo says it adds an unreleased track, "Wait (2015 Archive)", and a Rival Consoles remix of "Unlive". Siècle, his fourth album, followed on 3 October 2025 per Wikipedia, Bandcamp and his official site. Wikipedia says he presented it at the Salle Pleyel on 21 March 2026. Our sources are announcements of that date, so we have no review of the show.

## Which labels is Superpoze associated with?

- **Combien Mille Records.** French Wikipedia names it as his label, the Amsterdam Dance Event profile describes his early EPs and album as released on it, and Internet Tattoo says the 2025 Opening anniversary edition came out through it. Numéro says he founded it.
- **A+LSO.** Numéro credits it next to Combien Mille for For We the Living, without saying which role it plays. We read it as a distribution or partner credit, but the article does not spell this out.
- **Banville.** Yoyaku's artist page lists the Opening 10 Year Anniversary vinyl as BAN001LP on Banville. This conflicts in part with Internet Tattoo, which names Combien Mille, so the two may describe different editions or different roles.
- **Kitsuné.** The ADE profile mentions a maxi there, with no title or year.

## FAQ

### How old is Superpoze?

He is 34 if French Wikipedia's birth date of 26 June 1992 is right. Kiosk Radio also says he was born in 1992, though a search snippet suggested 1993, which we could not check.

### Where is Superpoze from?

He is French and was born in Caen, Normandy, according to French Wikipedia, and he works in Paris. Qobuz also calls him a Caen native.

### Who is Superpoze?

Superpoze is Gabriel Legeleux, a French electronic producer, composer and live performer. He has released four albums under the name and scored several French films.

### What is Superpoze's real name?

Gabriel Legeleux, according to French Wikipedia, Numéro, Qobuz, Fnac and the Amsterdam Dance Event.

Mostly atmospheric, cinematic electronic music that mixes synths and drum machines with acoustic instruments, according to Numéro, Qobuz and Kiosk Radio.
