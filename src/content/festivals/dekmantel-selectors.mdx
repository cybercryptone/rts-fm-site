## What is the history of Dekmantel Selectors?

Dekmantel Selectors is the Croatian summer offshoot of the Amsterdam collective behind [Dekmantel Festival](/festivals/dekmantel-festival). Dekmantel's own editorial describes Tisno locals who have attended "since the first Selectors in 2016", and Resident Advisor and Mixmag both reported the 2026 edition as the festival's tenth anniversary. Dekmantel's 2024 review calls it a festival about its musical choices first and foremost, from the tracks played to the people playing them, and quotes Croatian DJs who say the early editions were looser, with the Beach Bar sometimes hitting peak time at midday and the late-night Voodoo stage swerving towards disco.

Dekmantel's recaps place the festival in Tisno in 2019 and at The Garden Resort on Croatia's Dalmatian coast from the 2020 announcement onward. The 2020 line-up announcement, which named [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) among others, was overtaken by the pandemic: after The Garden announced that its summer events would be postponed, Dekmantel moved Selectors 2020 to 26 to 30 August 2021, and the team's own recap confirms that the 2021 edition went ahead. Pre-registration for 2022 opened straight afterwards, and the 2022 programmers, Boye 't Lam and Lisa Molle, described the newly added Magnolia stage as a place centred on music for listening. By 2025 Dekmantel reported 22 boat parties hosted by labels, clubs, radio stations and parties, and said the 21 to 25 August 2025 edition had sold out.

The format has stayed deliberately small. The official site describes a limited-capacity, five-day festival with a small number of stages, where the day program sits by the sea at the Beach Bar and the Magnolia, the evenings move to Voodoo, The Nest and the Beach Main, and the night program runs at Barbarella's. Boat parties are hosted by outside labels, radio stations and collectives, and an island party takes some of the line-up's biggest names to a remote spot. Dekmantel's 2022 programmers also stressed that the festival is built around DJs rather than live sets.

Dimensions Festival uses the same Tisno site at the end of August; see our page on [Dimensions Festival](/festivals/dimensions-festival) for its separate history and dates.

## How do you get to Dekmantel Selectors?

The address for The Garden Resort, Petrića Glava 34, 22240 Tisno, is the one given on the FAQ page of [Love International](https://www.loveinternationalfestival.com/faqs), another festival held on the same site. Dekmantel's own Selectors pages name The Garden Resort in Tisno and give no street address, though the official FAQ now carries travel notes

- **Airports and distances:** Love International's FAQ gives Zadar Airport as the nearest, about 40 minutes by car, with Split about 60 minutes and Zagreb about three hours. The Selectors FAQ now gives the same Zadar, Split and Zagreb figures.
- **Tisno and Barbarella's:** the same FAQ puts the town of Tisno a 20 to 25 minute walk from the resort and describes Barbarella's as an open-air nightclub in Pirovac, a short drive away.
- **Tickets:** the official site links to its ticket shop, and the FAQ lists prices (see the top of this page). Dekmantel's event page has also listed an Entrance Ticket, a Day and Night Program ticket and optional CO2 compensation add-ons for travel from Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Berlin, Brussels, Paris and London to Split.
- **Line-up:** announced on 27 October 2026; the full list is on the official program page.
- **Transfers and accommodation:** the official FAQ says airport transfers from Split and Zadar are offered by Shuttle Tours, with booking opening closer to the festival. It does not mention camping and refers onsite accommodation questions to the resort, and the About page describes 80 air-conditioned apartments for about 300 people plus a limited number of camp pitches.
- **Not yet published:** boat party schedule and set times.

Dates carry a small conflict among third parties. The official site, Dekmantel's announcement and Resident Advisor give 19 to 23 August 2027, but some listings, including Music Festival Wizard, show 20 to 24 August, which were the 2026 dates according to Resident Advisor and Mixmag. This page follows the official site.

## FAQ

### What are the dates of Dekmantel Selectors 2027?
19 to 23 August 2027, according to the festival's official site, with a wristband-only pre-party on Wednesday 18 August. Some third-party listings show 20 to 24 August, which matches the 2026 dates and is not the official 2027 range.

### Where is Dekmantel Selectors held?
At The Garden Resort in Tisno, Croatia, on the Adriatic coast. Night sessions run at Barbarella's, an open-air club.

### How do I get to Dekmantel Selectors?
The nearest airport is Zadar, about 40 minutes by car, with Split about 60 minutes and Zagreb about three hours, according to the official Selectors FAQ. It says airport transfers from Split and Zadar are offered by Shuttle Tours, with booking opening closer to the festival.

### How much are Dekmantel Selectors 2027 tickets?
The official FAQ lists four tiers including service fees: Community €209.90, Early bird €239.90, Regular €259.90 and Final €279.90. Boat parties are a separate €28.50 excluding fees. Dekmantel's 27 October 2026 announcement said regular tickets were on sale in limited numbers, and the pages we read do not say which tier is currently live.

### Has the Dekmantel Selectors 2027 line-up been announced?
Yes. The Day and Night program and 23 boat party hosts are listed on the official site, following the announcement of 27 October 2026. The bill includes Seth Troxler, Steffi, Ron Trent, Luke Slater, SHERELLE and Paranoid London Live.

### What is the age limit at Dekmantel Selectors?
The minimum age is 18, according to the official FAQ, and staff may ask for a passport or ID at the entrance.

### Is Dekmantel Selectors in Croatia?
Yes. It is held at The Garden Resort in Tisno, on the Dalmatian coast, with night sessions at Barbarella's.

### Who has played Dekmantel Selectors?
The 2027 line-up is listed at the top of this page.
