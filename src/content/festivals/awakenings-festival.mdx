## History

Awakenings started as an Easter techno party, and the timing wasn't incidental: founder Rocco Veenboer named the event after the religious idea of resurrection, then delivered on it in about the least churchy way possible. The first edition landed on March 30, 1997, inside the Gashouder, the domed former gas holder at Amsterdam's Westergasfabriek complex in Westerpark, with Angelo, Billy Nasty, Derrick May, Dimitri, Godard, Nick Rapaccioli and Zen on the bill. Monumental Productions BV, Veenboer's events agency, has run the brand ever since, though ownership changed hands in 2015 when the US promoter SFX (now LiveStyle) acquired it.

The Gashouder's acoustics made it the festival's spiritual home, but Awakenings has never stayed still. When Westergasfabriek closed for renovation in 2000, the party moved to Now & Wow in Rotterdam, then alternated between Now & Wow and Amsterdam's NDSM wharf before returning to a fully restored Gashouder on February 18, 2005. A first edition at Eindhoven's Klokgebouw followed in 2008, and by the 2010s Awakenings was effectively running as two events a year: an Easter weekend gathering at Spaarnwoude, between Amsterdam and Haarlem, and a separate, growing summer festival.

That summer festival found its permanent home in 2022, when Awakenings relocated to Beekse Bergen, the safari park and recreation area in Hilvarenbeek, near Tilburg. The brand now describes the festival as a three-day weekend with more than 120 artists across eight areas, and the 2026 edition (July 10 to 12) listed more than 120 acts on its announced bill. The brand's reach now extends well past the Netherlands: a first arena show played Amsterdam's Ziggo Dome in 2021, and satellite parties have run in cities including London, New York, Manchester and Antwerp since 2014, with Awakenings Exo Colombia set for Bogota on December 4, 2026. For Amsterdam Dance Event, Awakenings has moved its shows to the SugarFactory in Halfweg, an industrial site dating from 1863 that it first used in 2025, with five days of events from October 21 to 25, 2026, while the Gashouder stays on the brand's list of home venues. The spring slot has changed too: Awakenings Upclose, an intimate two-day festival at Houtrak in Spaarnwoude-park, is booked for May 15 and 16, 2027, and the official 2027 calendar lists no Easter edition.

Awakenings' 2027 edition, running July 9 to 11 at Hilvarenbeek, comes thirty years after that first Gashouder night. The lineup is still to be announced, but the festival's recent bills show how techno-first its booking policy has stayed. [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte) was one of the lead names in the 2025 edition's promotion and is on the 2026 bill, and her KNTXT imprint has staged full takeovers on Awakenings stages. [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz), [Joseph Capriati](/artists/joseph-capriati) and [Marco Carola](/artists/marco-carola) appear on both the 2025 and 2026 line-ups, with [Chris Stussy](/artists/chris-stussy) and [Sara Landry](/artists/sara-landry) added in 2025 and [Amelie Lens](/artists/amelie-lens) in 2026. At ADE 2026 the brand's SugarFactory shows include [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills), from Detroit's founding techno generation, on Joris Voorn's Trip To Galaxy afternoon on October 24, Capriati's own Metamorfosi party on October 23, and [Traumer](/artists/traumer) at Prophecy by Prospa on October 25, all part of [ADE's 30th-anniversary edition](/blog/ade-2026-30th-anniversary).

## Getting there and practical info

The summer festival is held inside Beekse Bergen, a safari park and recreation area on the edge of Tilburg in the Dutch province of North Brabant, and the brand sells everything from tickets to camping and hotel packages through its own shop. Tilburg is the usual gateway for anyone traveling by rail.

- **Venue:** Beekse Bergen, Beekse Bergen 1, 5081 NJ Hilvarenbeek, the visit address listed by the park's own site. Hilvarenbeek is near Tilburg.
- **Getting there:** the official shop has a Transportation section for the 2027 edition. A 2026 visitor guide describes taking the train to Tilburg and then a festival shuttle bus from the north exit of the station, and says Beekse Bergen is about two hours from Amsterdam. Treat that as guidance and check the shop for 2027 times and prices.
- **Camping:** the Grand Camping runs a Night Village for wristband holders with dancing until 5 am, and there is a Comfort Camping with a pool, a sauna and ice bath area and a Comfort Train to the festival on Saturday and Sunday. A new Low Stimulus Camping sits closest to the festival site and is aimed at a quieter night. Camping tickets include festival entry from Friday to Sunday and the campsite from Friday to Monday.
- **Hotels and cottages:** the official Travel & Stay offer includes pre-set-up accommodation on both campsites and hotel packages, and the hotel packages include a round-trip Saturday transfer to and from the festival. Beekse Bergen's own accommodations were shown as sold out in the shop on October 10, 2026. Most of the park's other facilities, such as swimming, bowling and dining, close during the festival.
- **Age and entry:** the minimum age is 18 and a government-issued ID is required. Awakenings runs a zero tolerance drug policy, searches everyone at entry, does not allow re-entry, and requires bags and jackets to be stored in lockers.
- **Tickets:** sold through the official Awakenings shop (shop.awakenings.com), in price tiers (Loyalty, Early Bird, Regular, Final), with a Premium upgrade that adds fast-lane entry and a viewing deck at Area V. Resale runs through TicketSwap.
- **What sets the logistics apart:** the festival sells itself as a three-day techno holiday in the woods, with a campers-only night program and a campsite food court that serves only vegetarian food.

## FAQ

### Where does Awakenings Festival take place?
Since 2022, the main summer festival has been held at Beekse Bergen, a safari park and recreation area in Hilvarenbeek, near Tilburg. The original Awakenings parties began at the Gashouder in Amsterdam's Westergasfabriek in 1997.

### How did Awakenings get its name?
Founder Rocco Veenboer named it after the idea of resurrection, since the earliest parties were held over the Easter weekend.

### Is there a smaller Awakenings festival in spring?
Yes. Awakenings Upclose is a two-day festival at Houtrak in Spaarnwoude-park, booked for May 15 and 16, 2027. The official 2027 calendar lists no Easter edition, though Awakenings did run Easter-weekend editions at Spaarnwoude in earlier years.

### How many people attend Awakenings Summer Festival?
The brand's own site describes the festival as drawing about 80,000 visitors from more than 80 countries over a weekend, with more than 120 artists across eight areas.

### When is Awakenings Festival 2027?
The 2027 edition runs from July 9 to 11 at Beekse Bergen in Hilvarenbeek, with doors listed as 15:00 to 23:00. Tickets are on sale through the official shop and the lineup has not been announced yet.

### How do I get to Awakenings Festival?
The festival is at Beekse Bergen in Hilvarenbeek, near Tilburg. The official shop has a Transportation section, and hotel packages include a round-trip Saturday transfer to the festival.

### What is the minimum age for Awakenings Festival?
The minimum age is 18, and you need a valid government-issued ID. Awakenings also runs a zero tolerance drug policy and searches all visitors at entry.

### Can I camp at Awakenings Festival?
Yes. There are Grand Camping, Comfort Camping and a new Low Stimulus Camping option, all with festival access from Friday to Sunday and the campsite from Friday to Monday. Pre-set-up accommodation and hotel packages are sold through the official shop.
