## History

Junction 2 was founded in 2016 by London Warehouse Events (LWE), the production and promotion outfit behind a run of London club nights, under co-founder Will Harold. Harold has described the festival as "born out of our passion for music," built specifically to be, in his words, "the festival that we felt that London was missing," a dedicated home for house and techno in a city whose bigger festivals leaned toward broader, more mainstream bills. From its first announcement, Junction 2 also committed to a no-VIP policy, a deliberate stance against the segregated, tiered festival experiences that had become common across UK dance events, built instead around what the team called "one ticket, one collective experience."

The inaugural edition took place on Saturday, June 4, 2016, at Boston Manor Park in Brentford, west London, drawing around 5,000 people. The lineup paired Drumcode's Adam Beyer and Alan Fitzpatrick with Detroit originator Carl Craig, who performed his live Modular Pursuits show, alongside Ida Engberg, Pan-Pot and Boxia, split across stages hosted by Drumcode, The Hydra and LWE's own Closer brand. Beyer, whose Drumcode crew anchored that first bill, has returned for the festival's biggest editions since, including its 2026 tenth-anniversary edition a decade later.

Boston Manor Park has been the festival's home almost without interruption, and its physical geography became the festival's visual signature. The one detour came in 2022, when Junction 2 moved to Trent Park in north London for the weekend of June 18 and 19 while Boston Manor Park was unavailable after a redevelopment project, before returning to its original site in July 2023. The M4 motorway's elevated flyover runs directly through the park, and Junction 2 built its main stage, The Bridge, in the concrete space underneath it, pairing exposed structural columns and passing traffic overhead with a serious sound rig. The name itself comes from that setting, junction 2 of the M4. A second stage, The Woods, sits in a more sheltered, tree-lined pocket of the same park. The park itself is older than the festival by centuries: it surrounds Boston Manor House, a Grade II* listed Jacobean manor built in 1622 to 1623, on an estate that traces back to at least the medieval period. The Clitherow family sold the house and around 50 acres to Brentford Urban District Council in 1923, and the park opened to the public on September 11, 1924.

The bills have kept leaning on the club-first end of the spectrum. [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) and [Batu](/artists/batu) were billed for the Friday of the June 2019 edition, [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte) appeared on the 2017 bill and returned in 2023 and 2025, and [Joseph Capriati](/artists/joseph-capriati) was on the 2017 and 2025 lineups. [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) was billed in 2023 alongside Underworld and again in 2024, when [Amelie Lens](/artists/amelie-lens) was announced for a back-to-back with Adam Beyer. [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) was on the Saturday of both the 2023 and 2025 editions.

Junction 2 marked its tenth anniversary in 2026 with an edition branded J2 X, returning to Boston Manor Park across three days: Saturday, July 25, Sunday, July 26 and Sunday, August 2. The Saturday paired the festival's outdoor stages with an evening handover to [fabric](/blog/nina-kraviz-jeff-mills-fabric-junction-2) in Farringdon, billed as "Junction 2 x fabric by Night." That crossover put [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills), the Detroit originator whose career runs back to the genre's earliest days, on the same bill as [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz), alongside Marcel Dettmann b2b DJ Hell, Nicolas Lutz and other names drawn from the same club-first pool of artists Junction 2 and fabric have both booked from for years. It was less a one-off crossover than a natural fit, a decade of shared audiences and shared bookings finally sharing a single night. The festival was narrowed to two flagship stages, The Bridge and The Woods, and sat inside a wider LWE series also called The Bridge, six events across two weekends at the same park.

What comes next is still open. As of October 2026 the official site no longer leads with the Junction 2 name: it reads "The Bridge, Boston Manor Park, London, Summer 2027," with a sign-up for first access to tickets, lineups and updates from the Bridge series, Junction 2 and more. No dates, lineup or on-sale have been published.

## Getting there and practical info

Junction 2 is held at Boston Manor Park in Brentford, in the London Borough of Hounslow, west London. The park is entered from Boston Manor Road, and the M4 flyover that gave the festival its name runs across it, with The Bridge stage built beneath. The official site currently carries no travel page, so the details below come from the borough's licensing record, the festival's terms of entry and ticket-agent and transport listings.

- **Venue and address:** Boston Manor Park, Boston Manor Road, Brentford, London TW8 9JX.
- **Nearest Tube stations:** two Piccadilly line stations serve the park. Boston Manor (zone 4) sits on Boston Manor Road, the same road as the park entrance. Northfields (zone 3) is named in a 2026 ticket-agent listing as the closest Tube with a walk of about 12 minutes to the site.
- **Nearest rail station:** Brentford, served by South Western Railway, is listed as the nearest National Rail station.
- **From Heathrow and central London:** Boston Manor is on the Heathrow branch of the Piccadilly line, so the Underground links the airport and the park directly, and the same line runs on into central London. No journey times are published by the festival.
- **Driving:** parking near the park is limited, and the ticket-agent listing recommends public transport.
- **Age policy:** 18+ only. The terms of entry for the Bridge series, last updated on July 20, 2026, ask for a valid photo driving licence, passport or PASS card, and bars operate a Challenge 25 policy.
- **Tickets:** SEE Tickets is the official ticket agent. TicketSwap is the only official resale route, and tickets are not exchangeable, refundable or transferable under the terms of entry.
- **Capacity and hours:** the borough licence caps attendance at 14,999 people, with live music licensed until 22:30 and the site open from 12:00 to 23:00. Those are the licence limits agreed in December 2022, so check the current ticket page for each edition's own times.
- **Accommodation:** neither the festival nor its terms of entry mention an official campsite or accommodation package for the park.
- **What sets it apart:** a single-ticket, no-VIP policy, and a main stage under a working motorway flyover. Council records describe the site as a slight dip, with the M4 overpass above helping contain sound.

## FAQ

### Why doesn't Junction 2 have a VIP area?

The festival launched in 2016 with an explicit no-VIP policy, a decision co-founder Will Harold tied to techno's non-hierarchical culture and a preference for what the team called "one ticket, one collective experience" over the tiered access common at other UK festivals.

### What is The Bridge stage at Junction 2?

The Bridge is Junction 2's main stage, built in the open concrete space directly beneath the M4 motorway's elevated flyover as it crosses Boston Manor Park, pairing the festival's sound system with the flyover's raw, industrial structure overhead.

### Who played the first Junction 2 in 2016?

The debut edition on June 4, 2016 featured Adam Beyer, Alan Fitzpatrick, Carl Craig (performing his live Modular Pursuits show), Ida Engberg, Pan-Pot and Boxia, across stages hosted by Drumcode, The Hydra and LWE's own Closer brand.

### When is the next Junction 2?

No Junction 2 dates have been announced yet. As of October 2026 the official site lists Boston Manor Park, London for Summer 2027 under The Bridge series, with a sign-up for first access to tickets and lineups. The most recent edition, the tenth-anniversary J2 X, ran on July 25, July 26 and August 2, 2026.

### Where is Junction 2 and how do you get there?

Junction 2 is held at Boston Manor Park, Boston Manor Road, Brentford, London TW8 9JX. The nearest Underground stations are Boston Manor and Northfields on the Piccadilly line, and the nearest National Rail station is Brentford, while parking is limited.

### Why is it called Junction 2 and what does M4 have to do with it?

The name comes from junction 2 of the M4 motorway, whose elevated flyover crosses Boston Manor Park. The festival's main stage, The Bridge, is built directly beneath it.

### What is the age limit at Junction 2?

Junction 2 is 18+ only. The terms of entry require valid photo ID, either a photo driving licence, passport or PASS card, and bars run a Challenge 25 policy.

### How and where do you buy Junction 2 tickets?

Tickets are sold through SEE Tickets, the official ticket agent, and TicketSwap is the only official resale route. Tickets are not exchangeable, refundable or transferable apart from official resale, and no 2027 tickets are on sale yet.
