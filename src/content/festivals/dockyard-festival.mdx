## History

Dockyard Festival started in October 2014 as a one-day techno event timed to Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE), staged in three tents at NDSM Werf, the former shipyard on the north bank of the IJ in Amsterdam-Noord. The founders, a couple named Coen van Dongen and Tania Davison, have said they first applied for a licence for 10,000 people and settled on 5,000. The first edition sold out two weeks ahead, which they took as proof the concept worked. The idea was simple and, for ADE at the time, unusual: a festival that runs in daylight and early evening rather than the usual all-night club model, outside the summer season. [DGTL](/festivals/dgtl-amsterdam) holds its Amsterdam edition in the same NDSM docklands.

The booking leaned hard on the harder, more industrial end of techno from the start. The announced bill for the 2015 edition, the second, included RPR Soundsystem, Paco Osuna, DJ Rush, Monika Kruse and [Surgeon](/artists/surgeon) across five stages at NDSM Werf. In 2016 Dockyard added a separate Warehouse Festival at Hembrugterrein, a former Ministry of Defence industrial site just outside the city, held on 9 April with a lineup that included Dubfire, Rhadoo, Sonja Moonear, Tommy Four Seven, Surgeon and [Nastia](/artists/nastia). A second Warehouse edition followed on 6 May 2017, announced with Art Department and [Luciano](/artists/luciano) at the top. The team then paused that format while Hembrugterrein was still being developed and put its energy into the autumn ADE edition.

That edition has been held at Havenpark, near Amsterdam Sloterdijk station, in recent years. The official site documents Havenpark editions in 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 one sold out. In 2024 Dockyard marked ten years with a one-off Dockyard Warehouse Festival at Hembrugterrein on 19 October, run alongside the Havenpark day. The format now pairs Dockyard with Mystic Garden Festival, a lighter, trance and uplifting-leaning counterpart, so a single ticket covers both. The 2026 edition runs on 24 October with stage hosts HILOMATIK, Dax J presenting Empyrean, Mystic Garden and HE.SHE.THEY., plus Oscar Mulero and Planetary Assault Systems (live) on the lineup.

## Getting there and practical info

Dockyard is a single-day event inside ADE week, and the official site focuses on day-of travel to the site.

- **Venue:** Havenpark, Amsterdam. The navigation address on the official site is Wethouder van Essenweg, Amsterdam.
- **Train:** the festival says Havenpark is easy to reach from Amsterdam Sloterdijk station.
- **Shuttle:** shuttle buses run between Sloterdijk and the site from 11:30 until all guests have left, on a round-trip ticket.
- **By bike or car:** the site has a bike parking area near the entrance. Car parking tickets are sold in the festival shop, and fully electric cars park free with proof of registration.
- **Hours:** 13:00 to 23:00, last entry at 21:00.
- **Age:** 18+, with ID checked at the entrance.
- **Tickets:** sold through the festival's own shop, with TicketSwap as the official resale partner.

## FAQ

### When is Dockyard Festival 2026?
Dockyard Festival ADE 2026 is on Saturday 24 October, from 13:00 to 23:00, as part of Amsterdam Dance Event.

### Where is Dockyard Festival held?
At Havenpark in Amsterdam. The official navigation address is Wethouder van Essenweg, and the site is reachable from Amsterdam Sloterdijk station.

### How do I get to Dockyard Festival?
The festival says it is easy to reach by bike, car or shuttle bus from Amsterdam Sloterdijk station. Shuttles run from 11:30 on the day until everyone has left, on a round-trip ticket.

### How much are Dockyard Festival tickets?
The official site advertises tickets from 70 euros for the 2026 edition, with extras such as an Industry Deck upgrade sold separately.

### What is Mystic Garden Festival?
Mystic Garden is the lighter, uplifting counterpart that shares the Havenpark site with Dockyard. One ticket gives access to both festivals.

### Who plays Dockyard Festival 2026?
Announced names include Oscar Mulero, Planetary Assault Systems (live), Dax J, Blanka, Partiboi69, HI-LO, Jen Cardini and DJ EZ, across stages hosted by HILOMATIK, Dax J presents Empyrean, Mystic Garden and HE.SHE.THEY.

### Has Dockyard always been at Havenpark?
No. The first editions in 2014 and 2015 were at NDSM Werf in Amsterdam-Noord, and Dockyard also held Warehouse editions at Hembrugterrein in 2016, 2017 and 2024.
