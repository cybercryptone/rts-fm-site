## History

Dimensions launched in 2012 as the sister festival of Outlook, the bass-music gathering run by the same team, with a remit to give the deeper end of house, techno and club music its own space. The first edition ran September 6 to 9, 2012 at Fort Punta Christo, a mid-19th-century fortress built to defend Pula on Croatia's Istrian coast that had been abandoned and overgrown until the Outlook crew cleared it. Even that debut was a serious bill: [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) and [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) were both on the 2012 lineup.

The fort years set the template. Stages were carved out of the fortress itself, including a small circular open-air room called the Ballroom and a horseshoe-shaped space called the Dungeon, and the festival opened each year with a separate concert at Pula's Roman amphitheatre. Bonobo, Mount Kimbie and Portico Quartet played the 2013 opener, and Grace Jones and Moderat headlined it in 2017. Resident Advisor ran its own stage, The Clearing, in 2014, where [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) was billed to play live, Moodymann and Robert Hood were on the bill, and [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) was billed for the final night. That same year, a lightning storm pushed [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) and Floating Points to share the decks for a back-to-back set. In 2017, [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) played The Clearing, and Theo Parrish played a set of about eight hours.

In October 2019, the organizers announced that Dimensions and Outlook would leave Pula for The Garden in Tisno, in Šibenik-Knin County, after bad weather and power cuts at the end of the previous summer. Time Out reported that Pula's site could handle about 15,000 people while The Garden usually caters to about 5,000. The festival's own site dates the move to 2021 and says it has never felt a stronger connection between the music and the people since arriving. The 2026 edition, run from August 27 to 31, was its fourteenth by one listing's count, and late-night programming runs at Barbarella's, an open-air club in nearby Pirovac.

## Getting there and practical info

The Garden Resort is at Petrića Glava 34, 22240 Tisno. Tisno sits on a narrow strait between the island of Murter and the Croatian mainland, roughly halfway between Split and Zadar.

- **Airports:** Split and Zadar. Listings for the 2026 edition describe official festival transfers from both airports that could be booked alongside a ticket.
- **After-parties:** Barbarella's, the open-air club in Pirovac, hosts the late-night sessions.
- **Tickets:** not on sale yet for 2027. The organizers say they will announce the first artists before tickets open in late October or early November 2026, and a sign-up list gives first access.
- **Not yet published for 2027:** pricing, age policy and camping or accommodation details.

## FAQ

### When is Dimensions Festival 2027?
Dimensions 2027 runs from August 26 to 31, 2027, according to the festival's official site.

### Where is Dimensions Festival held?
At The Garden Resort in Tisno, on the Dalmatian coast of Croatia, roughly halfway between Split and Zadar. It was held at Fort Punta Christo near Pula from 2012 to 2019.

### How do I get to Dimensions from the airport?
The closest airports are Split and Zadar. Listings for the 2026 edition describe official transfers from both, and 2027 travel details have not been published yet.

### When do Dimensions tickets go on sale?
The organizers say tickets go on sale in late October or early November 2026, after the first artists are announced. You can sign up on the festival website for first access.

### How much are tickets?
Prices for 2027 have not been published, so check the festival website once sales open.

### Who has played Dimensions?
Past lineups include Theo Parrish, Motor City Drum Ensemble, Jeff Mills, Nina Kraviz, Moodymann, Robert Hood, Floating Points and Bonobo.

### Is Dimensions related to Outlook Festival?
Yes. Dimensions launched in 2012 as the sister festival of Outlook and is run by the same team.
