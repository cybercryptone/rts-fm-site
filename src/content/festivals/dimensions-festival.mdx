## When is Dimensions Festival 2027?

Dimensions Festival 2027 is the festival's 15th anniversary edition, running August 26 to 31, 2027 at The Garden and Barbarella's in Tisno, Croatia. The first lineup was announced on November 4, 2026, tickets opened to the festival's sign-up list from November 5, and any remainder was due on general sale on November 6 at 12:00 GMT, with availability unconfirmed as of November 13, 2026.

## Who is playing Dimensions 2027?

Dimensions announced the first wave of its 15th anniversary edition on November 4, 2026, according to [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/news/86335). Returning regulars include DJ Masda, Francesco Del Garda, [Helena Hauff](/artists/helena-hauff), [Rhadoo](/artists/rhadoo), The Ghost and Kia. Alexander Skancke, Cabanne, DJ Godfather and Dee Diggs make their festival debuts. [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) returns for the first time since the festival left Fort Punta Christo, which means his last Dimensions appearance was in the Pula years.

The first wave also includes a run of b2bs: Angel D'lite with tINI, dj sweet6teen with Katia Curie, and Dyed Soundorom with E.LINA. Live acts named so far are A Guy Called Gerald, Admo, Ciel, Mad Professor, Snad and Morgan, among others. Press coverage such as [Electronic Groove](https://electronicgroove.com/dimensions-announces-first-artists-for-15th-anniversary-edition/) and [mindmusic](https://www.mindmusic.online/dimensions-first-90-artists-for-2027/) counts more than 90 names in total. The organizers say further artists, the boat parties and the afterhours programme will be announced in early 2027.

## When do Dimensions 2027 tickets go on sale?

Dimensions 2027 tickets are available to people who signed up for access, and any tickets left would go on general sale on November 6 at 12:00 GMT through the ticket link on the festival's Resident Advisor event page, according to the official ticket page. Electronic Groove reported the private sale opening at 5 PM GMT on November 5. The page lists a main festival ticket and an after-party full pass. Day tickets, day-specific after-party tickets and boat-party tickets are due later in 2027 with no dates given.

Discounted full festival and day tickets are offered to residents of Slovenia, Serbia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Kosovo, Ukraine and Palestine, with proof of residence required on entry. We could not confirm from outside the ticket page whether any tickets remain after the general sale date, as of November 13, 2026, so check the official page before planning around it.

## History

Dimensions launched in 2012 as the sister festival of Outlook, the bass-music gathering run by the same team, with a remit to give the deeper end of house, techno and club music its own space. The first edition ran September 6 to 9, 2012 at Fort Punta Christo, a mid-19th-century fortress built to defend Pula on Croatia's Istrian coast that had been abandoned and overgrown until the Outlook crew cleared it. Even that debut was a serious bill: [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) and [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) were both on the 2012 lineup.

The fort years set the template. Stages were carved out of the fortress itself, including a small circular open-air room called the Ballroom and a horseshoe-shaped space called the Dungeon, and the festival opened each year with a separate concert at Pula's Roman amphitheatre. Bonobo, Mount Kimbie and Portico Quartet played the 2013 opener, and Grace Jones and Moderat headlined it in 2017. Resident Advisor ran its own stage, The Clearing, in 2014, where [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish) was billed to play live, Moodymann and Robert Hood were on the bill, and [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) was billed for the final night. That same year, a lightning storm pushed [Motor City Drum Ensemble](/artists/motor-city-drum-ensemble) and Floating Points to share the decks for a back-to-back set. In 2017, [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills) played The Clearing, and Theo Parrish played a set of about eight hours.

In October 2019, the organizers announced that Dimensions and Outlook would leave Pula for The Garden in Tisno, in Šibenik-Knin County, after bad weather and power cuts at the end of the previous summer. Time Out reported that Pula's site could handle about 15,000 people while The Garden usually caters to about 5,000. The festival's own site dates the move to 2021 and says it has never felt a stronger connection between the music and the people since arriving. The 2026 edition, run from August 27 to 31, was its fourteenth by one listing's count, and late-night programming runs at Barbarella's, an open-air club in nearby Pirovac.

## Getting there and practical info

Dimensions is held at The Garden Resort, Petrića Glava 34, 22240 Tisno. Tisno sits on a narrow strait between the island of Murter and the Croatian mainland, roughly halfway between Split and Zadar.

- **Airports:** Split and Zadar. Listings for the 2026 edition describe official festival transfers from both airports that could be booked alongside a ticket.
- **After-parties:** Barbarella's, the open-air club in Pirovac, hosts the late-night sessions.
- **Tickets:** the first wave of 2027 tickets went to people on the festival's sign-up list from November 5, 2026, with any remainder due on general sale on November 6 at 12:00 GMT, per the official ticket page. Day, boat-party and day-specific after-party tickets come later in 2027, and availability after the general sale date is unconfirmed as of November 13, 2026. Details are in the tickets section above.
