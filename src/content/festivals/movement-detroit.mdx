## History

Movement's roots go back further than the festival's own name. Detroit techno began in the early 1980s in Belleville, a town outside the city, where three teenagers, Juan Atkins, Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson, later known as the Belleville Three, built a sound out of Kraftwerk records, George Clinton's funk and the late-night radio mixes of Charles "The Electrifying Mojo" Johnson. Atkins released as Cybotron and founded Metroplex, May built Transmat and recorded "Strings of Life," and Saunderson launched KMS Records and Inner City, all three running their labels out of Detroit's Eastern Market. A second generation followed at the turn of the 1990s, led by Underground Resistance, the label and collective founded by "Mad" Mike Banks and [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills), whose militant, anti-commercial ethos shaped much of what came to define Detroit's sound.

That history is what the city's own festival was eventually built to honor. What is now Movement began in 2000 as the Detroit Electronic Music Festival, a free event at Hart Plaza organized by Carol Marvin's Pop Culture Media with Carl Craig as artistic director, timed to plant a flag for Detroit as techno's birthplace while the genre was still largely a European club phenomenon. Derrick May took over production and renamed it Movement in 2003, Kevin Saunderson ran it after May's departure in 2005, and Detroit promoter Paxahau has produced the festival every year since 2006, steering it from a free civic event through funding crises in the mid-2000s into the ticketed, internationally booked festival it is today.

Hart Plaza itself, the riverfront civic space where all of this has played out, is a concrete plaza built in the 1970s on the Detroit River, originally conceived as a piece of downtown civic architecture rather than a festival ground. Two decades of Movement, under its various names and operators, have turned it into something closer to techno's home stage, a place where first-generation Detroit producers and the artists who grew up on their records now regularly share a bill.

Movement marked its 20th anniversary in 2026, running over Memorial Day Weekend, May 23 to 25, with more than 115 acts across six stages, and headliners including Carl Cox, Dom Dolla and [Sara Landry](/artists/sara-landry) alongside Detroit figures like Dopplereffekt, DJ Minx and Eddie Fowlkes. The official 2026 lineup also listed [Jeff Mills](/artists/jeff-mills), [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz), [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte) and [Joseph Capriati](/artists/joseph-capriati), and Voices From The Lake, the project of [Donato Dozzy](/artists/donato-dozzy) and Neel, was scheduled for the Movement Stage on the Saturday. The same week, Detroit's mayor issued the city's first official proclamation recognizing Detroit Techno Week, a largely symbolic gesture but a telling one, given how long the city's institutions kept the genre at arm's length. The full story of that anniversary, the proclamation, and the four decades of Detroit techno history behind it is covered in more depth in [our piece on Movement's 20th anniversary](/blog/detroit-movement-festival-20-years).

The festival's archive shows how much of the Detroit and Berlin axis has passed through Hart Plaza. According to Wikipedia's festival history, [Daniel Bell](/artists/daniel-bell) and [Eric Cloutier](/artists/eric-cloutier) (with Drew Maddox) were on the 2006 bill, Jeff Mills and 3 Chairs, the group that includes [Theo Parrish](/artists/theo-parrish), played in 2007, [Rick Wade](/artists/rick-wade) appeared in 2009, Parrish returned as a solo act in 2010, and [Move D](/artists/move-d) joined Mills on the 2014 lineup.

Movement 2027 is set for Saturday, May 29 to Monday, May 31 at Hart Plaza, and passes are on sale now through the festival's ticketing partner Eventim. As of October 10, 2026 the 2027 lineup has not been announced.

## Getting there and practical info

Movement is a single-site festival: everything happens at Hart Plaza in downtown Detroit, on the Detroit River. The details below come from the festival's own travel and guide pages, some of which still carried 2026 information at the time of writing, so check movementfestival.com for any 2027 changes.

- **Address:** 1 Hart Plaza, Detroit, MI 48226.
- **Airport:** The festival's travel page connects Detroit Metropolitan Airport (DTW) to the city with the SMART FAST Michigan regional bus.
- **Transit:** The Detroit People Mover and the QLINE streetcar on Woodward Avenue are both free, and DDOT city buses stop near Spirit Plaza. MoGo bikeshare docks are available across the city.
- **Driving and parking:** Routes end on I-375, which becomes Jefferson Avenue, with Hart Plaza on the left. Huntington Place is within walking distance for parking, and the festival points to the ParkDetroit app and SpotHero for other options.
- **Rideshare:** The marked drop-off and pick-up point is the corner of Washington Boulevard and Larned Street, near Huntington Place.
- **Accommodation:** The festival links to discounted hotel packages through its travel partner Del May and Partners.
- **Age policy:** At the 2026 edition general admission was all ages and VIP was 21+, and children 12 and under got free entry with a parent.
- **Tickets:** Sold through Eventim, in 3-day GA and VIP passes with a payment plan. All sales are final, and the festival points to Tixel for resale.
- **Access:** The festival notes that accessibility is limited at historic Hart Plaza because of its terrain, and asks for questions to be sent ahead to accessibility@movementfestival.com.
- **Cashless:** The festival is mostly cashless, with the Movement app used to load funds in 2026.

## FAQ

### Is Movement the same festival as the Detroit Electronic Music Festival?
Yes, in lineage. It started as the free Detroit Electronic Music Festival in 2000, was renamed Movement in 2003 under Derrick May, and has run continuously at Hart Plaza under different operators since, produced by Paxahau since 2006.

### Why is Movement held at Hart Plaza specifically?
Hart Plaza is Detroit's riverfront civic plaza and has hosted the festival, under its various names, since 2000, making it the closest thing techno has to a home stage in the genre's birthplace.

### When does Movement take place each year?
Movement runs annually over Memorial Day Weekend in Detroit; the 2027 edition is scheduled for May 29 to 31.

### How do I get to Movement?
Hart Plaza is downtown at 1 Hart Plaza, Detroit. The festival points visitors to the free Detroit People Mover and QLINE, nearby parking at Huntington Place, and a marked rideshare zone at Washington Boulevard and Larned Street.

### What is the age limit at Movement?
At the 2026 edition general admission was open to all ages and VIP was 21+, with free entry for children 12 and under accompanied by a parent. Check the festival's guide for the 2027 rules.

### Where do I buy Movement tickets?
Official 2027 passes are sold through Eventim via movementfestival.com. The festival advertises payment plans, and all sales are final, so any resale should go through its recommended platform, Tixel.

### Which airport is closest to Movement?
Detroit Metropolitan Airport (DTW). The festival's travel page lists the SMART FAST Michigan bus as a link between the airport and the city.

### Has Jeff Mills played Movement?
Yes. Jeff Mills is on record at the 2007 and 2014 editions and was on the official 2026 lineup.
