## Which festivals are best for techno, house, experimental and modular fans?

The best festival depends on how you like to hear music. Our short answer: Awakenings and Time Warp for techno, Nachtdigital and Draaimolen for minimal and house with a small-crowd feel, CTM, Rewire and Berlin Atonal for experimental and electronic, and the modular crowd is better served by the festivals in the dedicated section further down. The reasoning follows.

### Which festivals are best for techno fans?

[Awakenings](/festivals/awakenings-festival) is the only festival in our guide tagged techno and nothing else, and it marks 30 years since the first night at Amsterdam's Gashouder in 1997. The 2027 edition is at Beekse Bergen, with doors listed as 15:00 to 23:00, and the brand's own site describes a bill of more than 120 artists across eight areas. [Time Warp](/festivals/time-warp) is the opposite proposition: one night, 3 April 2027, a 19-hour run across five stages at the Maimarkthalle in Mannheim, which the festival bills as the only German edition of the year. [Kappa](/festivals/kappa-futurfestival) is the big daytime one, but it is sold out. [Draaimolen](/festivals/draaimolen) is the one for people who want techno without a headliner arms race: individual artists and labels build entire stages in the woods of the MOB Complex. [Nature One](/festivals/nature-one) is an open-air rave first and a curated lineup second, with about 20 floors on the former Pydna missile base, and its bill runs from club techno to hardstyle, which is what a rave festival looks like when it has been running since 1995.

### Which festivals are best for minimal and house fans?

[Nachtdigital](/festivals/nachtdigital), now a biennial gathering branded "Nachti", is the most intimate: it capped attendance at roughly 3,000 tickets for most editions between 2007 and 2019, and the 2027 edition is 30 July to 1 August at Bungalowdorf Olganitz. [Sea You](/festivals/sea-you-festival) is the house and techno festival built around a lake: six stages, three of them with views of the Tunisee, and more than 150 acts. [Dimensions](/festivals/dimensions-festival) swaps the lake for the Dalmatian coast, a six-day run at The Garden in Tisno, and [Houghton](/festivals/houghton-festival) is the one for long, patient DJ sets in a Norfolk country estate, curated by Craig Richards with camping included in the general admission ticket. [Dekmantel](/festivals/dekmantel-festival) and its spring sibling [Lentekabinet](/festivals/lente-kabinet) are the pick if you want house and disco next to live acts and art. [Waking Life](/festivals/waking-life) in Portugal mixes house and techno with ambient and experimental programming over six days.

### Which festivals are best for experimental and electronic fans?

[CTM](/festivals/ctm-festival) is the one to start with: its 28th edition, themed "Process...ing", spreads across Berlin venues for ten days from 22 January, and the first acts announced on 16 September 2026 included Modeselektor and Kali Malone. [Rewire](/festivals/rewire-festival) fills The Hague with concert halls, churches and clubs over four days in April. [Berlin Atonal](/festivals/berlin-atonal) runs in the turbine halls of Kraftwerk Berlin and has been biennial since 2023, so 2027 is an on year. [Sonar](/festivals/sonar) covers the widest ground, from techno to digital art, with the Sonar+D creativity and technology congress alongside. [MUTEK](/festivals/mutek) does the same job in Montreal for live electronic and audiovisual work.

None of the festivals above has announced a modular synth lineup for 2027, so we will not promise you one. The festivals closest to the idea are the ones that book live electronic and audiovisual work; the next sections sort the verified options.

## Turin and Italy: Kappa FuturFestival

The Turin techno festival is Kappa FuturFestival, and the 2027 edition runs from Friday 2 to Sunday 4 July 2027 at Parco Dora. As of 16 October 2026 the [official ticket page](https://www.kappafuturfestival.it/en/tickets) shows general admission, VIP and Gold all sold out, with a waiting list for the next release. It is the only Italian festival in our guide, and the page you want is our full [Kappa FuturFestival guide](/festivals/kappa-futurfestival).

The setting is the draw. Parco Dora is a public park built on the site of Turin's former Fiat and Michelin steelworks, and the festival's Vitali lot still has the rust-orange support towers of the old mill standing over the crowd. The festival started in 2009 as a one-off event in a Turin city square tied to the centenary of the Futurismo art movement, took its current name and daytime format in 2012, and has used Parco Dora since 2013. It runs noon to midnight over three days, is 18+ with ID required, and Italian press reported about 125,000 attendees for the 2026 edition, according to the details on our festival page.

The bookings are broad, from house and disco to some of the hardest techno on the calendar, and recent years have included names like [Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz), [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte) and [Amelie Lens](/artists/amelie-lens). For 2027, no lineup is announced and tickets are gone, which puts you in the waiting-list queue. Per the festival's FAQ, a ticket resale and name-change window runs from 31 May to 25 June 2027, which is the realistic second chance. One honest oddity: when we read the official ticket page on 16 October 2026, it showed every category as sold out and also carried an early bird banner. Trust the sold-out status and the waiting list, not the banner.

## What changed since 2026?

The short version: some festivals went biennial, some moved, a few stopped, and most of the weather and cost trouble landed outside our list. Our [festival cancellations story](/blog/europe-festival-cancellations-2026) sourced the wider wave, and we only repeat what that post sources.

- **Pauses and exits.** Mysteryland skipped 2026 on purpose to start from scratch, and has confirmed it will return in 2027 with a new concept. Ground Zero ran its last edition on 30 August 2025. Music On's Amsterdam festival lost its permit over structural safety concerns in May 2026, while its Pacha residency in Ibiza carried on.
- **Weather.** An extreme heatwave in late June 2026 cut Defqon.1 short and led to Solidays being cancelled.
- **Moves.** Terminal V left Edinburgh after nine years and has announced Clydeside Live in Glasgow for 4 to 6 June 2027.
- **Scale of the problem.** The Association of Independent Festivals counted 78 UK festivals cancelled, postponed or closed in 2024, 43 in 2025 and 20 by 10 June 2026, per Pollstar's report.

Inside our own list the changes are more specific. [Berlin Atonal](/festivals/berlin-atonal) is now biennial, with editions in 2025 and 2027 and a one-off 2026 collaboration with Unsound. Nachtdigital became the biennial "Nachti" in 2023 and returns in 2027. Sonar merged its day and night programmes into one site in 2026, and its founders stepped away in October 2025, after which it is operated by Superstruct. Draaimolen nearly lost its site: the province of North Brabant said in September 2025 that the MOB Complex would no longer be available for festivals from 2026, a petition gathered more than 26,000 signatures, and the province later allowed the festival to stay for up to three years under conditions. Junction 2's official site now reads "The Bridge, Boston Manor Park, London, Summer 2027" instead of the festival name. And if you follow UK artists onto European lineups, our piece on [Brexit and touring for DJs in 2026](/blog/brexit-touring-uk-dj-eu-festivals-2026) covers what the 2026 touring talks mean.

## What we make of it

This section is RTS.FM's own opinion, not reporting. Dates and ticket facts above come from the sources we link; the points below are how we would read them.

1. **Book the small, dated festivals first, and treat the giant sold-out ones as a lottery.** Draaimolen, Nachtdigital and Waking Life have published precise sale mechanics, which is a better signal than a famous name. Kappa has already shown what waiting costs. *The strongest counter-argument: small independents carry the most site and permit risk, and Draaimolen's near-loss of the MOB Complex is exactly that risk made real.*
2. **Do not book flights for a TBA festival, but do join its list.** Junction 2, Sunwaves and Unsound all gate first access behind a sign-up or a later announcement, and Dekmantel's estimate is just an estimate. Hold your budget, not your dates. *The strongest counter-argument: by the time dates land, the cheapest tiers are gone, and Houghton and Kappa are the kind of festival that sells through quickly.*
3. **Treat early, specific announcements as a good sign, not a guarantee.** A festival that tells you the raffle window, the early-access day and the resale rules is organised enough to be worth your money. *The strongest counter-argument: announcements are marketing, and Summer Camp in the US had its 25th anniversary edition cancelled about two weeks before it opened.*
