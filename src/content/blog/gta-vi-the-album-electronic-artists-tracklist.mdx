As of 23 November 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is out on streaming, vinyl and CD: 34 original tracks, released through Atlantic Records with Rockstar Games. The dance names are Justice, Fred again.., Étienne de Crécy, Arca and Fcukers, and the game's CircoLoco Records Radio carries unreleased tracks from Chris Stussy, Max Dean and Chloé Caillet.

This is the follow-up to our August piece on [house music and the GTA VI soundtrack](/blog/gta-vi-soundtrack-house-artists), written before anything had shipped. The game launched, the full tracklist is public, and the radio stations have been mapped by dance press. Here is what is confirmed, what was only a leak, and what nobody has pinned down yet.

## When was GTA VI: The Album released?

The game came first. Reporting dated 19 November 2026 says Grand Theft Auto VI is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Groove magazine also dates the launch to 19 November. We found no source disputing the game date.

The album date is where sources split. When Rockstar and Atlantic announced the record on 17 September, trade coverage from [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/atlantic-records-to-release-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-featuring-travis-scott-pinkpantheress-morgan-wallen-and-keith-richards/) gave 19 November, the same day as the game. Later coverage says 20 November: Vice, in a piece dated 18 November, attributes that date to Rockstar and Atlantic, and Wikipedia, Complex and CULTR all put the actual release on 20 November, a Friday.

Our reading: the album was scheduled for launch day, moved or rolled out one day later, and was live on streaming and in shops by 20 November. Rockstar's [music page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music) gives no date at all, and Rockstar's "Now Available" newswire post, as far as we could read it, says only that the album is "available everywhere now". Complex quotes a store listing saying the album "Launched November 19", so you will still see both dates online. Treat 20 November as the reported release date and 19 November as the original plan.

## Which electronic artists are on GTA VI: The Album?

Five electronic acts are credited on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, going by the track list on Rockstar's music page and the credits Complex and Wikipedia compile. Justice open the record with "Starting from Zero" (featuring Michael McDonald) and return on "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Fred again.. and French Touch figure Étienne de Crécy appear on "Sexy Magic" with CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso and PinkPantheress. Fcukers have "Only One" at track 25, and Arca has "Sandunguea" at track 26. The other 28 tracks are mostly pop, rap, Latin and rock, so the dance content is concentrated at the top and around tracks 25 to 28. Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo of Daft Punk is credited as writer and producer of Travis Scott's "Rhyno", the only Daft Punk link any source states. Justice also composed the separate Original Score, due 11 December.

### Is a Daft Punk production really on the album?

Partly. We Rave You's headline refers to "a Daft Punk production", and the underlying fact is narrow: Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo is credited on "Rhyno". [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/tracewilliamcowen/daft-punk-gta-vi-rumor) answered whether Daft Punk are involved with "Technically, no", and none of the sources we read mention Thomas Bangalter on the project. An X post from @hasitleaked on 19 October claimed Daft Punk's management was approached about scoring the game with an "unlimited" budget. It is the only source, and Rockstar and Daft Punk have not commented. Treat it as a rumour.

## What is Justice doing with GTA VI?

Justice are the most visible electronic act in the whole release. They open the album, return on "Flower in a Hurricane" (released as a single on 15 October, per Wikipedia), and composed the game's original score, which Rockstar lists for 11 December on streaming, CD and vinyl. Rockstar's page gives the score as 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, with "Pusher (feat. Gesaffelstein)" among its singles.

The score is a separate product from the album. The album is songs made for the game; the score is the music that underpins play. CULTR also reports that Justice headlined a release takeover at Miami's Club Space, with a one-off "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival" in Miami the following day.

## Is there a house or techno station in GTA VI?

Yes. Rockstar's music page lists CircoLoco Records as an in-game station, described as underground house and techno. It names the hosts as "DJ Seth and DJ Antonio". We Rave You identifies the host as Seth Troxler, and SAMO.HOUSE and CULTR say CircoLoco co-founder Antonio Carbonaro voices the links between sets. Troxler has [an artist page here](/artists/seth-troxler).

This updates what we wrote in August. Back then Rockstar had named six stations and none was a dedicated dance station; the music page now lists seven, including CircoLoco. We Rave You counts 22 stations in the finished game. Groove names others with dance interest: Stockyard FM (indie dance), Worldwide FM (curated by Gilles Peterson), Radio On-U (dub and reggae), and a hidden station called The Vault Radio. Palms Trax co-hosts AfroBank FM with Burna Boy, and Robyn co-hosts Flash FM, per Groove and The FADER.

## Is the GTA VI album on vinyl?

Yes. Rockstar's music page lists a standard vinyl, an exclusive vinyl and a CD, sold through the dedicated album shop. Complex puts the vinyl LP at $49.98 and the CD at $19.98. The September announcement added a limited liquid-filled vinyl at $124.98, per NBC Miami and Music Business Worldwide, and Vice reported on 18 November that the limited edition had sold out.

Retail-exclusive pressings came through chains and independents, including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target, Urban Outfitters and independent record stores, per Music Business Worldwide. Descriptions of the standard colourway differ between outlets (CULTR says black, the September coverage says magenta with blue splatter), so check the listing before you buy.

## What was leaked and what is confirmed?

The tracklist leaked on 14 November, five days before launch. A record store employee reportedly filmed the back of a vinyl sleeve, and the list spread on X. Dexerto called it allegedly leaked and unconfirmed, and Notebookcheck wrote that sources close to the project had verified the running order while Rockstar had not confirmed it. Complex reports that Rockstar published the official list on 17 November, and the list Complex printed matches the leaked order.

## Why does this matter for dance music?

GTA is one of the biggest entertainment properties on earth, and a game soundtrack puts club tracks in front of people who never visit a club. Luminate's figures, which we covered in [our piece on electronic music as the fastest-growing US genre](/blog/electronic-music-fastest-growing-genre-us), show dance already gaining share. A Rockstar placement is not a business plan for an underground label, but it is a signal about where Rockstar sees dance music's credibility.

CircoLoco is an unusually direct example: a label with its own in-game station, not a licensed cameo. Whether the unreleased tracks reach release formats is not something any source we read confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When was GTA VI: The Album released?

It was announced for 19 November 2026, the same day as the game, and outlets including Complex, Wikipedia and CULTR report it arriving on 20 November. Rockstar's music page gives no date.

### Is GTA VI out, and when did it launch?

Yes. Reporting dated 19 November 2026 says Grand Theft Auto VI is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Groove also dates the launch to 19 November.

### Which electronic artists are on GTA VI: The Album?

Justice, Fred again.., Étienne de Crécy, Arca and Fcukers are credited. Justice have two tracks, "Starting from Zero" and "Flower in a Hurricane". Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo of Daft Punk is credited on Travis Scott's "Rhyno".

### Is Daft Punk on the GTA VI album?

Not as a duo. Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo is credited as writer and producer of "Rhyno", and Complex says Daft Punk are "technically" not involved. A single X post about an unlimited-budget scoring offer is an unverified rumour.

### Is there a house or techno station in GTA VI?

Yes. Rockstar lists CircoLoco Records as an underground house and techno station. We Rave You says Seth Troxler hosts, while other outlets say Antonio Carbonaro voices the links.

### Which DJs have unreleased music in GTA VI?

On CircoLoco Records Radio, outlets report unreleased tracks from Chris Stussy, Max Dean, Chloé Caillet and Myd, Michael Bibi, DJ Tennis, Mind Against, Rossi. and Demi, Seth Troxler and Rampa. Counts of unreleased tracks vary by outlet.

### Is the GTA VI album on vinyl?

Yes. Rockstar lists a standard vinyl, an exclusive vinyl and a CD, and Complex prices the LP at $49.98 and the CD at $19.98. A limited liquid-filled edition was announced at $124.98 and Vice reported it sold out by 18 November.

### What is Justice doing with GTA VI?

Justice open the album and appear on track 6, and they composed the game's original score. The 30-track Original Score is due on 11 December 2026 and includes "Pusher (feat. Gesaffelstein)" among its singles.

### Was the GTA VI album tracklist leaked?

Yes. A vinyl back cover tracklist circulated on 14 November, and Dexerto reported it as unconfirmed. Complex says Rockstar published the official list on 17 November, and the list Complex printed matches the leak.

### Who hosts the electronic radio stations in GTA VI?

Per Groove and The FADER, Palms Trax co-hosts AfroBank FM with Burna Boy, and Robyn co-hosts Flash FM. DJ Mag reports PinkPantheress hosts on Stockyard FM, though we could only read a search summary of that page.
