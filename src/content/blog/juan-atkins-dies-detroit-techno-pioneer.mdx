Juan Atkins, the Detroit producer widely credited as a co-creator of techno and the founder of Cybotron, Metroplex and Model 500, has died at 64. His booking agency, Greensmith Artists, announced his death on Sunday 1 November 2026, according to NBC News and Rolling Stone. He was in Mexico City for a show planned for Saturday 31 October, and no cause of death has been released.

## What do the reports say happened?

Resident Advisor reported that a representative of Greensmith Artists confirmed Juan Atkins was found unresponsive in his hotel room before the performance, at YuYu Cine Club, and was later pronounced dead after being taken to hospital. Rolling Stone, citing the same account, and We Rave You report it the same way. No cause has been released, and we are not going to speculate.

NBC News reported that Atkins had been due to perform on the Saturday at Yu Yu in Mexico City, and that the club cancelled the appearance and announced the cancellation on Instagram before news of his death broke. His agency then announced his death on Instagram, and his Belleville Three bandmate [Kevin Saunderson](/artists/kevin-saunderson) shared the news on Facebook. Rolling Stone dates the death to Saturday 31 October, and Resident Advisor's later feature gives 31 October 2026 as the date he died.

## Who was Juan Atkins?

Juan Atkins was a Detroit producer and DJ and a member of the Belleville Three, the trio he formed with [Derrick May](/artists/derrick-may) and Kevin Saunderson. He was born in Detroit in 1962 and grew up in Belleville, Michigan, where he met May and Saunderson at high school. Our [full profile of Juan Atkins](/artists/juan-atkins) covers the career in detail, and our report on [Movement's 20th anniversary](/blog/detroit-movement-festival-20-years) retells the Belleville story and records a 2026 set that paired him with Moritz von Oswald.

## How did Juan Atkins shape Detroit techno?

Atkins shaped Detroit techno through Cybotron, Metroplex and Model 500. With Richard Davis he formed Cybotron, whose "Clear" is one of the records most often named as a starting point for techno, and in 1985 he founded Metroplex and began releasing as Model 500 with "No UFO's".

NBC notes that Missy Elliott sampled "Clear" for her 2005 song "Lose Control", and that in 2023 Atkins revived Cybotron with the Berlin producer Laurens von Oswald, releasing new music on Tresor. For how early Detroit records fit into the history of tempo, see [our guide to techno BPM](/blog/what-bpm-is-techno-tempo-by-genre).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who was Juan Atkins?

Juan Atkins was a Detroit producer and DJ, the founder of Cybotron, Model 500 and the label Metroplex, and a member of the Belleville Three with Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson. He is widely called the godfather of techno. See [our profile](/artists/juan-atkins) for the career in full.

### How old was Juan Atkins?

Juan Atkins was 64, according to NBC News and Rolling Stone. He was born in Detroit in 1962, and sources disagree on his exact birth date, so we give only the year.

### When did Juan Atkins die?

His booking agency, Greensmith Artists, announced the death of Juan Atkins on 1 November 2026, according to NBC News and Rolling Stone. Resident Advisor gives 31 October 2026 as the date he died.

### How did Juan Atkins die?

No cause of death has been released in the reports we read. Resident Advisor, citing his agency, reports that Atkins was found unresponsive in his hotel room in Mexico City before a scheduled performance and was later pronounced dead after being taken to hospital.

### Where did Juan Atkins die?

Juan Atkins was in Mexico City for a show at Yu Yu, according to NBC News, and Rolling Stone reports he was taken to a hospital there. The hospital has not been named in the reports we read.

### What was Juan Atkins known for?

Juan Atkins was known for Cybotron's "Clear", Model 500's "No UFO's" and the Metroplex label, which released early records by Detroit artists including Eddie Fowlkes and Kreem.

### Who are the Belleville Three?

The Belleville Three are Juan Atkins, [Derrick May](/artists/derrick-may) and [Kevin Saunderson](/artists/kevin-saunderson), who met at high school in Belleville, Michigan. The trio is widely credited with creating Detroit techno.

### Is there a memorial for Juan Atkins?

As of 13 November 2026, no funeral, memorial or tribute event has been announced in the reports we read. Detroit's mayor said the city will honour his memory, but that was a statement, not an event listing.
