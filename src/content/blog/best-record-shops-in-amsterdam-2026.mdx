As of 18 November 2026, the Amsterdam shops we could place in a 2026 guide are Rush Hour, Bordello A Parigi, Zwart Goud, KillaCutz, InDeep'n'Dance and Record Mania for dance music, plus Concerto, Platypus and Red Light Records for second-hand digging. Clone in Rotterdam is the one shop worth a train ride.

Amsterdam is small enough to crawl on foot, and a good afternoon can take in five or six shops. This guide only includes shops that turned up in a 2025 or 2026 source, or in the shop's own pages. Where sources disagree, we say so. For a wider trip, our [pillar guide to the best record shops in the world](/blog/best-record-shops-in-the-world-2026) and the [European round-up](/blog/best-record-shops-in-europe-2026) put the city in context.

## What are the best record shops in Amsterdam?

As of 18 November 2026, there is no official ranking of Amsterdam's record shops, so "best" here means the ones that recur in the Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE) Record Store Day guide of 17 April 2026, the I amsterdam guide updated on 14 October 2026 and the shops' own sites. The dance-focused picks are Rush Hour on Spuistraat (company founded in late 1997), Bordello A Parigi, Zwart Goud, KillaCutz and InDeep'n'Dance, with Record Mania in De Pijp for house, techno and disco. Concerto on Utrechtsestraat (1955) and Red Light Records cover everything else, while Platypus Records, Waxwell Records and Distortion Records add second-hand funk, soul, hip-hop and alternative stock. Several addresses conflict between sources, and we flag each one. Rotterdam's Clone and a few Utrecht and Hague shops are covered as day trips, and each entry names its source.

## Where to buy techno and house records in Amsterdam?

For dance music, six shops matter. Four sit within a short walk of each other in the old centre, which makes a crawl easy. For the rooms those records end up in, see our [guide to Amsterdam's clubs](/blog/best-amsterdam-clubs-2026-guide).

### Is Rush Hour worth visiting?

Rush Hour is the best-known name for a reason. Its own site describes the business as a retailer, distributor, record label and events organiser. According to its about page, the company formed in late 1997 with a record store and mail-order service, built a distribution arm by 1999 and put out its first label release just before the millennium.

The Vinyl Factory's list of the world's best record shops dates the start to 1996 and to stock bought from a basement at Coco Records, and calls it Amsterdam's best-known record store. We use the shop's own date.

I amsterdam lists house, techno, electronic, disco, italo, funk and soul, and ADE's April 2026 guide mentions regular ADE programming. Yes, it is worth visiting if you dig electronic records, with one caveat: sources give three different street numbers, covered below.

### Bordello A Parigi

Bordello A Parigi started in 2011 as an online shop, label and clothing brand, and opened a physical shop at Oudezijds Kolk 71 on 12 March 2017, according to the Vinyl Factory's report from the time. ADE's April 2026 guide lists it as a label, bar, distributor and store with in-store DJ sets, and gives its stock as italo, synthwave, electro, disco and house.

### Zwart Goud

Zwart Goud is at Geldersekade 89s, per ADE's April 2026 guide, which also calls it a record store, label, community house and coffee bar. An ADE editorial of 17 October 2025 says founder Ilker Soylu opened the Amsterdam store about ten years earlier, carrying over a name he had used for club nights in Antwerp.

By 2018, the same piece says, vinyl sales alone could not cover costs, so he started a European distribution arm that now works with more than 100 labels.

### KillaCutz

KillaCutz is a small shop at Nieuwe Nieuwstraat 21-hs. I amsterdam says it holds more than 3,000 records and names Chicago house, Detroit techno, deep house, UK tech house, garage and drum and bass. ADE's April 2026 guide adds trance and in-store sets and ADE pop-ups.

### InDeep'n'Dance Records

InDeep'n'Dance, at Rozengracht 60, describes itself in its 2026 ADE listing as open since 1999, with a focus on techno, minimal, hardcore, early rave, house, trance and drum and bass. The same listing says it runs two record labels and the weekly Techno Tuesday night at Melkweg, and that the shop is run by Dejan Milojkovic (Dexon).

A free ADE crate-digging session was listed for 22 October 2026, with the store open daily during ADE.

### Record Mania

Record Mania, at Ferdinand Bolstraat 30 in De Pijp, is the one dance-leaning shop outside the centre. ADE's April 2026 guide places it in house, techno, disco and electro, new and second-hand. I amsterdam describes a broader stock and says it has traded for more than 25 years, about 15 of them on this street.

## Where to buy second-hand vinyl in Amsterdam?

Second-hand stock turns up everywhere, but these shops lead with it.

### Concerto

Concerto, at Utrechtsestraat 52 to 60, is the oldest shop we could source to a specific date. Its own site says it opened on 25 November 1955 as a small shop selling musical instruments, parts, record players and records, and now fills five connected buildings.

It sells new and second-hand LPs, CDs and DVDs, buys collections by appointment or by sending buyers by cargo bike, and runs a label, a graphic-novel imprint and a coffee corner. Its site lists seven-day opening.

### Platypus Records

Platypus, at Zeedijk 45A, is run by two friends, Raoul and Bas, according to a Record Shop Stories profile of September 2024. It says the shop opened about six years earlier, roughly 2018, and stocks funk, soul, disco, hip-hop, library music, Dutch records and Southeast Asian music. ADE's April 2026 guide lists it among the second-hand finds.

### Red Light Records

Red Light Records is the shop with the best origin story. The Vinyl Factory's older world list says diggers Tako Reyenga and Abel Nagengast turned a vacant space in the same building as Red Light Radio into a shop, with a focus on second-hand stock and a small selection of new releases. A 2016 short film made by Dekmantel, the organisers of [Dekmantel Festival](/festivals/dekmantel-festival), covered the shop and the station.

I amsterdam, updated 14 October 2026, lists it at Oudezijds Achterburgwal 133, says to ring the buzzer, and notes some stock is donated by regular customers. Wikipedia says the radio station broadcast from 2010 to 2020 and ended its daily shows in mid 2020, so the radio side is gone. The Vinyl Factory gives an older address at Oudekerksplein 26.

### Distortion Records

Distortion, at Westerstraat 244 in the Jordaan, is listed by ADE as second-hand across genres and known for a packed, characterful layout. I amsterdam adds punk, new wave, dubstep and house, and says owner Amond Spee has run it for more than 25 years.

### Record Palace and Discostars

Record Palace, at Weteringschans 33-A opposite Paradiso, has three floors and, per I amsterdam, was opened by Jan van Dorsten in 1988. Discostars, at Haarlemmerdijk 86, deals in second-hand records, especially Amsterdam artists such as André Hazes and Johnny Jordaan, and I amsterdam calls it one of the city's oldest surviving independent shops without giving a year.

### Record Friend Elpees

Record Friend Elpees, at St. Antoniesbreestraat 64, sells second-hand and new vinyl across rock, pop, jazz, punk and soul, and repairs record players, per I amsterdam.

## Which Amsterdam shops suit soul, funk and global records?

### Waxwell Records

Waxwell, in the Nine Streets, is described by I amsterdam as a hip-hop and soul specialist with a notable jazz selection, plus jazz-funk and Dutch breaks. ADE's April 2026 guide describes a vinyl-only shop with strength in pop, rock and funk that appeals to disco collectors. The guides give the address as Gasthuismolensteeg 8A and 8. We did not find the Amsterdam shop on waxwellrecords.com, which presents itself as an online store.

### Zap Records and Black Gold

Zap Records, at Paleisstraat 137 behind Dam Square, stocks Caribbean music, funk, South African house and heavy metal, with listening in the basement, per I amsterdam. Black Gold, at Korte Koningsstraat 13, pairs funk, soul, afrobeat, 1990s hip-hop and electronica with coffee, and hosts small concerts and DJ sessions, according to the same guide.

## How do ADE and Record Store Day shape Amsterdam record shopping?

Record shops here lean on events. ADE's guide to Record Store Day in Amsterdam, published 17 April 2026, lists eleven shops in walking order and calls out in-store sets and ADE pop-ups at several. In October, KillaCutz and InDeep'n'Dance run daytime sessions during ADE.

ADE 2026 was the festival's 30th edition and closed on 25 October, as covered in our [ADE 2026 wrap-up](/blog/ade-2026-wrap-up-30th-edition) and [preview](/blog/ade-2026-30th-anniversary).

## Which record shops are worth the train from Amsterdam?

### Rotterdam: Clone

Clone, at Raampoortstraat 12 in Rotterdam, is the Dutch electronic shop most often named outside Amsterdam in the sources we read. The Vinyl Factory says Serge Verschuur founded it in 1993 as a techno label, added a storefront and a distribution arm a few years later, and now runs more than a dozen sub-labels. The shop sells new and second-hand independent electronic records. Clone's website lists house, electro, techno, acid, disco and Detroit among its main genres. Other sources date it a year or two either side, so treat 1993 as approximate.

### Utrecht and The Hague

For Utrecht, a July 2026 guide from Keep Them Spinning lists Plato at Voorstraat 19, Dig It! at Loeff Berchmakerstraat 9 and Vinyl24 at Oudegracht 314. The guide gives no genre focus, so we cannot call them electronic specialists.

In The Hague, the city tourism site lists 3345 Vinyl Records at Noordeinde 87 G, which it describes as focused on electronic music and vinyl culture, with in-store DJ sets and a listening corner. It also lists Vinyl Grove at Boekhorststraat 10 for classic and new records.

## What we could not verify

- **Rush Hour's exact address.** Its contact page lists Spuistraat 110, I amsterdam lists Spuistraat 116, ADE's April 2026 guide lists Spuistraat 98, and the Vinyl Factory describes a move from 98 to 116. Check the shop's site before you walk over.
- **Red Light Records' address.** I amsterdam and ADE say Oudezijds Achterburgwal 133, the Vinyl Factory says Oudekerksplein 26. We found no founding year.
- **Waxwell's address and age.** Sources give 8 and 8A, and we found no founding year in a source we opened.
- **KillaCutz.** We found no founding year.
- **Opening hours.** Only Rush Hour and Concerto hours come from the shops' own undated pages.
- **Dropped.** We found no source for a Rush Hour branch in Rotterdam, and could not verify Kinky Star, Fat Beats or Hunter Records as Amsterdam shops, so they are dropped. The Financial Times list could not be read, and the Time Out summary of it contains no Amsterdam shop.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best record shop in Amsterdam?

For electronic music, Rush Hour is the most widely cited, in its own history, the Vinyl Factory's world list and ADE's 2026 guide. For second-hand variety, Concerto is the long-running pick. Which is best depends on whether you want a curated dance shop or a deep general stock.

### Where can I buy techno records in Amsterdam?

InDeep'n'Dance, KillaCutz and Zwart Goud are the clearest techno and house picks in 2026 guides. Record Mania in De Pijp and Rush Hour also stock electronic records.

### Where can I buy second-hand vinyl in Amsterdam?

Concerto, Platypus, Red Light Records, Distortion, Record Palace and Discostars all lead with second-hand stock in the sources we read. Concerto also buys collections.

### Is Rush Hour a record shop or a label?

Both. Its own site describes a retailer, distributor, record label and events organiser, and its about page dates the label's first record to just before the millennium.

### Is Red Light Records connected to Red Light Radio?

They shared a building, and a 2016 Dekmantel film covered both. The radio ended its daily shows in mid 2020, per Wikipedia, while I amsterdam still lists the shop. We found no source on shared ownership.

### Are Amsterdam record shops open on Sundays?

Some are. Concerto lists 12:00 to 18:00 and Rush Hour 13:00 to 18:00 on their own pages. Check before you go.

### When is the best time for a record shop crawl in Amsterdam?

Record Store Day in April and ADE week in October are the best-documented moments for in-store events. InDeep'n'Dance, for example, listed a free ADE session on 22 October 2026.

### Which Amsterdam shops sell soul, funk and hip-hop?

Waxwell Records and Black Gold are the clearest picks, per I amsterdam, with Zap Records for Caribbean and global records. Platypus also covers funk, soul and hip-hop second-hand.

### Is Clone in Rotterdam worth the trip from Amsterdam?

For electronic collectors, yes. The Vinyl Factory describes a label, distributor and shop in one, with new and second-hand independent electronic records. We could not confirm its opening hours.
