As of 24 November 2026, Yoyaku's YouTube channel holds 178 videos titled instore session, with 12.5 million views between them. The most watched is Tomoki Tamura's 87-minute set from June 2024 at 3.24 million views, more than four times the runner-up. This guide ranks the top 10 by views, with dates, lengths and sources.

## How did we rank the sessions?

We pulled every upload on the Yoyaku Record Store channel through the YouTube Data API on 24 November 2026 and kept the videos with "instore session" or "instore sessions" in the title. That gave 178 videos. We ranked them by public view count.

- **One video, one place.** The two Satoshi Tomiie and Tomoki Tamura sessions are separate videos from different dates, so they rank separately.
- **Views are a snapshot.** They change daily. The views per day column divides total views by the days since publication, as a rough guide to momentum, and it changes the picture: the July 2026 Tomiie and Tamura session averages about 2,700 views a day, far ahead of the 2017 sessions.
- **Not counted.** The channel's separate quarantine session series, track uploads and live sessions are outside the list, and so are sessions hosted only on SoundCloud.
- **Titles decide.** A session uploaded without "instore session" in its title would be missed.

## What is a Yoyaku instore session?

A Yoyaku instore session is a long DJ set, played on vinyl and recorded in the shop, then published on YouTube. Yoyaku Record Store describes itself on YouTube as France's leading record store dedicated to electronic music, in a 600 square metre space in Paris's 18th arrondissement. Electronic Groove reported on 6 April 2026 that Seth Troxler's session was recorded at 14 Boulevard La Chapelle in Paris. Our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) covers the shop, the label and the Barcelona branch.

The series is older than most people think. The earliest session in our data is Janeret on 11 May 2016, and the channel has published 137 of its 178 sessions since 2024: 38 in 2024, 35 in 2025 and 64 so far in 2026. No video with instore session in its title is dated 2020 or 2021. The median session runs about 65 minutes and the median view count is about 24,700, so every entry in the top 10 is far above normal.

Production is professional. The Nina Kraviz video credits direction to Jim Trebes, cinematography to Arnaud Deregnaucourt and editing to Haytam Moukrim. Not every guest is famous: Shotgun lists an instore session with a 100 percent Yoyaku staff line-up, and the shop's contact page links an RSVP form for in-store sessions.

## What do the top 10 have in common?

Three patterns stand out.

- **One name dominates.** Tomoki Tamura appears in three of the ten videos, which together hold 4.39 million views, or 67.0 percent of the top 10 and 35.1 percent of all session views. His solo set alone is 25.9 percent of every session view and 9.1 percent of all views on the channel, and 3.75 times the next most viewed video on the channel.
- **The format is recent.** Eight of the ten were published in 2024 or later. Only two sessions from the older 2016 to 2019 run, S.A.M. and Andrey Pushkarev, both from 2017, are in the top 10, nine years after upload.
- **Length varies widely.** The ten run from 59 minutes (Helena Hauff) to 119 minutes (Andrey Pushkarev), with a median of about 79 minutes. Likes run from 2.1 to 3.5 per 100 views. Helena Hauff's session has the highest rate at 3.53, followed by Tamura's solo set at 3.23.

Age matters too. A video from 2017 has had far longer to collect views than one from 2026, which is why the views per day column is worth a look next to the totals.

## What are the top 10 sessions, one by one?

### 1. Tomoki Tamura, June 2024: 3.24 million views

Published on 25 June 2024, 87 minutes 37 seconds, 3,242,829 views, 104,721 likes and 2,604 comments. It is the most viewed video on the whole channel: the next one is a 2019 release video for Giuliano Lomonte's Stay, at 864,734 views.

Tamura's Bandcamp page lists him as a Berlin-based DJ, producer and CEO of the Holic Trax label. The video description links only his Instagram and SoundCloud, and Exhibition magazine names him alongside Nina Kraviz when it describes the filmed Instore Sessions. We found no source that explains why this set travelled so far, so we do not guess.

### 2. Satoshi Tomiie b2b Tomoki Tamura, December 2024: 782,636 views

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Yoyaku instore session?

It is a long vinyl DJ set recorded in the Yoyaku shop and published on the shop's YouTube channel. Electronic Groove reported that Seth Troxler's 2026 session was recorded at 14 Boulevard La Chapelle in Paris.

### Which Yoyaku instore session has the most views?

Tomoki Tamura's session from 25 June 2024, with 3,242,829 views on 24 November 2026. It is also the most viewed video of any kind on the Yoyaku Record Store channel.

### How many Yoyaku instore sessions are on YouTube?

We counted 178 videos titled instore session or instore sessions on 24 November 2026, from Janeret on 11 May 2016 to the latest in November 2026.

### How long is a Yoyaku instore session?

The median is about 65 minutes. The top 10 run from 59 minutes 38 seconds (Helena Hauff) to 119 minutes 36 seconds (Andrey Pushkarev).

### Who has played the most Yoyaku instore sessions?

In our data Satoshi Tomiie has five videos with his name in the title, including the two joint sessions with Tomoki Tamura, with 1.33 million views combined. Cabanne has three.

### Can you attend a Yoyaku instore session?

Sometimes. The shop's contact page links an RSVP form for in-store sessions. For Barcelona's Off Week sessions in 2026, Yoyaku said they were reserved for ticket holders.

### Are Yoyaku instore sessions free to watch?

Yes. They are public videos on YouTube. Yoyaku's descriptions link to its record shop and social channels.

### Where is Yoyaku Record Store?

The Paris shop is at 14 Boulevard La Chapelle, 75018, and a Barcelona shop opened on 15 June 2026. See our [Yoyaku guide](/blog/yoyaku-paris-barcelona-record-shop-label-guide) for addresses and hours.
