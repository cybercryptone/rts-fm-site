## Which electronic and techno acts are on Coachella 2027?

The full day-by-day lists agree across [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/coachella-2027-electronic-dance-festival-1236379469/), Beatportal, We Rave You and [DJ LIFE](https://djlifemag.com/2026/11/coachella-announces-anticipated-2027-lineup/), which we cross-checked on 13 November 2026.

**Friday (9 and 16 April):** Madonna, billed "In The Dance Tent", Peggy Gou, Solid Grooves, Giorgio Moroder, Chris Stussy, SG Lewis, Franky Rizardo and Vintage Culture. Peggy Gou, the Berlin-based DJ and Gudu Records founder, and Solid Grooves share the second line of the poster, according to We Rave You. Chris Stussy, the Dutch house producer, sits among the house names beneath them.

**Saturday (10 and 17 April):** Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Robyn, Solomun, Underworld, Charlotte de Witte, Cloonee, Barry Can't Swim, KI/KI and Brutalismus 3000, plus Enigma, the act behind "Sadeness (Part I)", which We Rave You calls a genuine curveball.

**Sunday (11 and 18 April):** Dom Dolla, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, Four Tet, Amelie Lens x Sara Landry, Chase & Status, Boris Brejcha, Odd Mob, Shygirl, Adam Ten and Marten Lou x Alex Wann. Four Tet is the producer Kieran Hebden, and Boris Brejcha is the masked German producer behind High-Tech Minimal.

**Not tied to a day:** Justice, billed at the bottom of the poster for "The Bunker Debut" of a project titled "Blinded By The Lights". The Bunker is the audiovisual space that debuted this year with a Radiohead installation, per Billboard.

## When is Coachella 2027, and who headlines?

Coachella 2027 returns to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, across two weekends: 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April 2027. [Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1720533-coachella-2027-lineup-dua-lipa-tame-impala-sza-more), [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/daddy-yankee-announced-as-headliner-coachella-2027-news) and [Coachella's own site](https://www.coachella.com/lineup) agree on those dates. Billboard's list gives the second weekend as 16 to 19 April, which looks like a typo, since every other source and the festival itself say 16 to 18.

Both weekends carry the same bill, so Friday is 9 and 16 April, Saturday is 10 and 17 April, and Sunday is 11 and 18 April. The headliners are Dua Lipa on Friday, Tame Impala on Saturday and SZA on Sunday, with Daddy Yankee billed separately for a special production called "In The Desert", per [We Rave You](https://weraveyou.com/2026/11/coachella-2027-lineup) and Mixmag.

## Is Charlotte de Witte playing Coachella 2027, and on which day?

Yes. Charlotte de Witte plays Coachella 2027 on Saturday, 10 and 17 April, on the same bill as Solomun, Underworld, Tiësto and Martin Garrix, with Tame Impala as the day's headliner. Billboard, Beatportal, We Rave You and DJ LIFE all list her on Saturday, and no stage assignment has been published as of 13 November 2026.

She finished No. 6 in DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs 2026, up three places, per [Bandwagon](https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/david-guetta-tops-dj-mag-s-top-100-djs-2026-anyma-charlotte-de-witte-climb-rankings) and our [artist page](/artists/charlotte-de-witte), and was again named the world's top techno DJ in the same poll.

## Is Solomun playing Coachella 2027 on Saturday or Sunday?

Solomun plays Saturday, 10 and 17 April. A few secondhand summaries had floated Sunday, but we could not find that placement anywhere we could read. Billboard's day-by-day list, Beatportal, We Rave You and DJ LIFE all put Solomun on Saturday, and Billboard's Saturday heading gives the dates as 10 and 17 April, which matches the calendar. We could not open DJ Mag's own write-up, so we have not checked its placement directly.

## Who sits at the underground end, and who at the mainstage end?

The electronic bill splits into rough camps across the three days, though the poster itself does not say which stage anyone plays. Billboard and DJ LIFE both note that the Do LaB and Quasar lineups have not been announced yet, and set times come later.

**Mainstage and arena end:** Tiësto and Martin Garrix on Saturday, Kygo, Dom Dolla and Chase & Status on Sunday. Garrix arrives, per We Rave You, about a month after his album *Marty* is due on 5 March. These are acts built for wide fields.

**Club and underground end:** Underworld on Saturday. Four Tet, who sits on the same second line as Kygo and Dom Dolla on Sunday. Solid Grooves, Chris Stussy and Franky Rizardo on Friday. And the two acts most readers here will care about, Charlotte de Witte on Saturday and the Lens x Landry pairing on Sunday.

**In between:** Solomun, a melodic house headliner who, per our [artist page](/artists/solomun), runs Diynamic Music and the Solomun +1 nights at Pacha Ibiza. And Boris Brejcha, a festival draw and streaming phenomenon whose announced retirement at the end of 2028 is covered in our [career story](/blog/boris-brejcha-retirement-2028-joker-career-story).

## What does techno on a pop festival bill actually signal?

Billboard says dance and electronic acts make up roughly 40 percent of the 2027 lineup and that the genre appears on nearly every stage, with the Quasar Stage and the Yuma Tent dedicated to it. That 40 percent figure is Billboard's estimate and we have not seen it from a second source, so treat it as an approximation. Billboard also argues that even the headliners lean dance: Dua Lipa's recent work skirts dance-pop, and Tame Impala's 2025 album *Deadbeat* was, per We Rave You, built around Western Australian bush doof and rave culture.

Three things stand out for a techno and house audience.

First, harder techno is now a booking category in the American desert. Amelie Lens and Sara Landry are billed as a pair, which We Rave You says tore up Ultra Miami earlier this year. We covered the demand behind that in [hard techno's American boom](/blog/hard-techno-american-boom): SoundCloud reported US plays of hardtekk-tagged tracks up 75 percent over two years. A Coachella slot is the same story at festival scale.

Second, there is the volume of the underlying market. Luminate's midyear report found dance and electronic gaining the largest US streaming share of any genre in the first half of 2026, which [we broke down here](/blog/electronic-music-fastest-growing-genre-us).

Third, there is the DJ Mag gap. As we argued in our [piece on the Top 100 and its limits](/blog/nina-kraviz-dj-mag-top-100-2026-poll), that poll measures popularity and mobilised fanbases rather than club credibility. De Witte, No. 6 in the 2026 poll, is the clearest case of a techno artist whose poll position and festival billing now line up, with Underworld and Four Tet showing that the same poster also has room for acts whose standing does not come from a vote.

The caveat: a Coachella bill is not a club night. Saturday's techno names share a poster with pop and rock acts and a crowd that will mostly be there for Tame Impala. For a proper techno programme, our guide to the [best techno and house festivals in 2027](/blog/best-techno-house-festivals-2027) is a better map.

## When do Coachella 2027 tickets go on sale?

As of 13 November 2026, passes for both weekends go on sale on Friday 13 November at noon Pacific time through the official Coachella site, per DJ LIFE and We Rave You. The official passes page labels general admission, VIP and camping options as "On Sale 11/13", while some earlier tiers are marked sold out.

Official hotel packages, which bundle passes with lodging and transport, are already available. Prices are subject to change while supplies last, so check coachella.com rather than a third-party listing. The site points resale to AXS; for the other big US ticketing company, see our [Ticketmaster verdict explainer](/blog/ticketmaster-live-nation-doj-verdict). YouTube returns as exclusive livestream partner for both weekends, now in its 15th year.
