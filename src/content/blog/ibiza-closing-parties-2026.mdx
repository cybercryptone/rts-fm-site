Ibiza doesn't end its season so much as stage a two week argument about what the season meant. Between September 28 and October 12, 2026, every major club on the island runs its last parties of the year, and the heaviest stretch, October 2 through 5, packs more headline names into four nights than most cities get in a full year. This isn't one generic "end of summer" bash repeated six times across six venues. It's a layered calendar of anniversaries, farewells and reshuffles: Amnesia closes its 50th year with a sun-to-sun festival, UNVRS runs its first ever closing season after debuting at number one in [DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs poll](/blog/berghain-unvrs-dj-mag-top-100), and Glitterbox says goodbye to an eight year home at Hi Ibiza to start a new one at Amnesia the very same week.

What follows is the full confirmed schedule, club by club, plus the context that turns a list of dates into an actual story: what these nights mean for the artists closing them, and what they cost the island whose season just generated this much money and attention. All dates below were verified via [mneemo's 2026 Ibiza closing parties roundup](https://www.mneemo.com/blog/ibiza-closing-parties-2026/) as of September 23, 2026, and are subject to the usual late Ibiza changes, so treat anything not yet on sale as provisional.

## Ushuaia

Ushuaia's outdoor daytime format means its closings run earlier in the day than most of the island's other rooms, and this year's lineup leans on the promoters who built the venue's reputation for spectacle in the first place.

- **Wed Sep 30:** Tomorrowland presents Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike
- **Fri Oct 2:** Calvin Harris closing party (17:00) with MK and Tyson O'Brien
- **Sat Oct 3:** ANTS final party (15:00 start)
- **Mon Oct 5:** David Guetta's "F\*\*\* Me I'm Famous!" closing

## UNVRS

This is UNVRS's first full closing season since it opened in May 2025 and immediately vaulted to number one in DJ Mag's 2026 poll, the first debut club in the poll's 21 year history to do so. A club that spent its first year being judged on spectacle now gets judged on whether it can close a season the way an established room does, and the lineup reads like a venue trying to cover every base at once, from Armin van Buuren's trance institution to Carl Cox's all-night techno reputation.

## Amnesia

Amnesia carries more weight this closing season than any other room on the island, because 2026 is the club's 50th anniversary year, and its final weekend is built to match. RTS.FM has already covered Amnesia's Pyramid party in depth, [Nina Kraviz's Sunday residency there across the summer](/blog/nina-kraviz-amnesia-ibiza-pyramid-residency), and Pyramid's own closing lineup on October 4 is new information beyond that earlier piece: Ricardo Villalobos b2b Raresh headline alongside Franco Cinelli, Shanti Celeste, Seth Troxler and Mar-T, a bill that puts two of the party's own curating artists on the same closing night.

- **Mon Sep 28:** "NRG" closing party
- **Tue Sep 29:** Joseph Capriati's "Metamorfosi" closing
- **Thu Oct 1:** Josh Baker presents "You&Me" closing
- **Sun Oct 4:** Pyramid closing, Ricardo Villalobos b2b Raresh, Franco Cinelli, Shanti Celeste, Seth Troxler, Mar-T
- **Fri Oct 9:** Glitterbox's first ever closing at Amnesia, having relocated there after an eight year run at Hi Ibiza
- **Sat Oct 10:** Amnesia Closing Festival, a sun-to-sun event marking the club's 50th anniversary edition, lineup pending at time of writing

Glitterbox's move alone is worth sitting with. Eight years at one venue is a long residency by any club's standards, and ending it at Hi only to open the same brand's first closing night at Amnesia a week later means two of the island's biggest club identities are effectively swapping partners in the same closing window that Amnesia is using to mark its own half century.

## Pacha

Pacha spreads its closing run across nearly two weeks rather than compressing it into one weekend, ending with a two part "Grand Closing Weekend" that splits Music On's residency finale from a separate lineup a few days later.

- **Sun Oct 4:** Solomun +1 closing (all-night format)
- **Mon Oct 5:** Sonny Fodera closing party
- **Thu Oct 8 & Fri Oct 9:** Music On two part closing (Part 1 lineup includes Michael Bibi and Cloonee)
- **Sun Oct 11:** Grand Closing Weekend Part Two, with PAWSA and Miguelle & Tons

## DC-10

DC-10's Circoloco closing on Monday, October 12 at 18:00 traditionally functions as the island's last major party of the season, the one date every other club's calendar is built around avoiding. Tickets are running around 97.69 euros via DICE. Circoloco closing this late, ten days after most of the island's other headline nights have already wrapped, is part of why DC-10 keeps its reputation as the room serious dancers save for last.

## Eden

Eden's 2026 closing remains unannounced as of late July, with a first weekend of October date expected based on 2025 precedent, when the club ran a partnership called Secret Sessions around the same point in the calendar.

## What This Season's Closings Actually Add Up To

None of this happens in a vacuum. The same week that generates headline after headline of closing parties is also the week the island's tourism economy comes under the most scrutiny, and [young Ibiza residents staged their own protest on a beach in September 2026](/blog/ibiza-anti-tourism-protest-cala-saladeta), arguing that the nightlife economy filling rooms like Amnesia and UNVRS has priced out the people who actually live there. The closing season is Ibiza's most profitable fortnight and its most visible one, which is exactly why it keeps surfacing as the backdrop for that argument rather than existing apart from it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do Ibiza's clubs close for the 2026 season?

The core closing window runs from September 28 through October 12, 2026, with the busiest stretch between October 2 and October 5, when Hi Ibiza, Ushuaia, UNVRS, Amnesia and Pacha all hold multiple headline closing parties within the same four days.

### Which party is considered Ibiza's actual final party of the season?

Circoloco's closing at DC-10 on Monday, October 12, traditionally holds that role. It runs ten days after most other venues have finished their closing runs, and doors open at 18:00 with tickets priced around 97.69 euros via DICE.

### Why does Amnesia's 2026 closing season matter more than usual?

2026 is Amnesia's 50th anniversary year, and its closing run reflects that: Glitterbox's first ever closing night at the venue on October 9, followed by a sun-to-sun Amnesia Closing Festival on October 10 built specifically to mark the milestone, with the lineup still pending at time of writing.

### What is happening with Pyramid's closing party at Amnesia?

Pyramid, Amnesia's long running Sunday night party, closes its 2026 season on October 4 with Ricardo Villalobos b2b Raresh headlining alongside Franco Cinelli, Shanti Celeste, Seth Troxler and Mar-T. It's a lineup that puts two of the party's own curating artists together on the same closing bill.

### Is UNVRS's 2026 closing its first?

Yes. UNVRS opened in May 2025 and topped DJ Mag's 2026 Top 100 Clubs poll in its debut eligible year, so this is the first time the venue has run a complete closing season. Its schedule spans six nights between September 29 and October 7, including Armin van Buuren's 25th anniversary "A State of Trance" closing.
