## What is Record Store Day?

Record Store Day is a yearly celebration of independently owned, brick-and-mortar record shops. According to the [official About page](https://recordstoreday.com/CustomPage/614), it was conceived in 2007 and first held on 19 April 2008. Special vinyl and CD releases are made for the day, and shops often add performances, DJs and artist signings around them. The main event is held in April: Record Store Day 2026 took place on Saturday 18 April 2026. A second, smaller edition, Record Store Day Black Friday, falls on Friday 27 November 2026. In the UK and Ireland, its releases go on sale from 8am on 27 November, only at participating independent shops, according to Official Charts. As of 18 November 2026 no date for Record Store Day 2027 had been confirmed, and the organisers describe 2027 as the 20th anniversary. Event dates come from recordstoreday.com unless noted.

### Who founded Record Store Day?

The official site says it was conceived at a gathering of independent record store owners and employees, and calls Michael Kurtz a co-founder. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_Store_Day) names six founders: Eric Levin, Michael Kurtz, Carrie Colliton, Amy Dorfman, Brian Poehner and Don Van Cleave, and says the model was Free Comic Book Day. Wikipedia is inconsistent about 2007 versus 2008, so we use the official wording.

## When is Record Store Day 2026?

Record Store Day 2026 was held on Saturday 18 April.

The UK and Ireland organisers' [post-event release of 23 April 2026](https://www.recordstoreday.co.uk/biggest-ever-record-store-day-celebration-boosts-uk-high-streets) calls it the 19th edition and reports more than 300 shops, 500 limited-edition releases and sales up 25 percent on 2025. Those are the organisers' own figures.

Record Store Day 2025 was held on Saturday 12 April, [reported by The Music Universe](https://themusicuniverse.com/record-store-day-announces-2025-date-post-malone-as-ambassador/) as a week earlier than usual.

## When is Record Store Day Black Friday 2026?

RSD Black Friday 2026 is on Friday 27 November. The [official RSD Black Friday FAQ](https://recordstoreday.com/Page/1210) says it falls on the day after Thanksgiving and gives 27 November for this year, and the [official 2026 list announcement](https://recordstoreday.com/NewsItem/12610) says the same.

It is not a second Record Store Day. The FAQ says it is not a stand-alone holiday and has fewer titles by design, and shops may carry the releases through the holiday season while supplies last. The first one was in 2010, per the official About page.

In the UK and Ireland, [the Official Charts Company reported](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/record-store-day-2026-black-friday-vinyl-releases-sombr-shawn-mendes-queen/) on 6 October 2026 that releases go on sale from 8am on 27 November, only through participating independent shops. Headliners include Florence + The Machine, sombr, Shawn Mendes and a Bruce Springsteen live box set.

If you plan to shop online, the FAQ says US shops can sell RSD Black Friday titles online from the morning of Saturday 28 November at the earliest, and each shop decides whether to. We did not find the UK rule.

## When is Record Store Day 2027?

We could not find an official 2027 date. The [UK FAQ](https://www.recordstoreday.co.uk/faq) says it takes place annually in April and tells fans to watch its news page and social channels for the next date. The UK organisers say 2027 marks the 20th anniversary.

The pattern is a Saturday in April. Treat any 2027 date you see elsewhere as provisional until recordstoreday.com or recordstoreday.co.uk confirms it.

## How does Record Store Day work?

The organisers do not sell the records. The [official explainer](https://recordstoreday.com/NewsItem/12259) says every shop orders its Record Store Day titles directly from distributors, as it would any new release, and that shops make their own decisions about which titles to bring in. There is no minimum stock level or dollar amount required to order.

So no two shops have the same selection. Most will not bring in everything on the list, and some bring in none. Titles are limited, so a shop can also sell out before you arrive.

There are no pre-orders on the US list. After the day, shops may sell leftover stock online, and the organisers recommend shop websites over flippers.

### What do Exclusives, RSD First and Small Run mean?

The official list splits titles into three categories, per the [US list page](https://recordstoreday.com/NewsItem/11535):

- **Exclusives:** released physically only at independent record shops.
- **RSD First:** found first at independent shops, but may reach other retailers or webstores later.
- **Small Run or Regional:** sold at specific shops or pressed in runs under 1,000, so harder to find.

### How do you buy Record Store Day releases fairly?

Rules differ by country and by shop, so read the shop's own page first. What the official sources say:

- **UK and Ireland:** the [UK FAQ](https://www.recordstoreday.co.uk/faq) says releases are first come, first served, exclusives are in-store only on the day, and shops may not reserve or pre-sell them. It says participating shops may sell remaining stock online one week after the event.
- **One shop's rules:** [Rough Trade's 2026 guide](https://blog.roughtrade.com/record-store-day-2026-at-rough-trade) set one copy per person, warned that tickets do not guarantee entry at capacity, and offered a wish-list shopping service. It put leftovers online from 19 April (US and EU) and 20 April (UK), earlier than the UK FAQ's one-week wording, so shop rules vary.
- **US:** no pre-orders. Online sales from shops start the day after, which in 2026 was Sunday 19 April.

In practice: make a short wish list, treat titles as targets rather than guarantees, and check shop websites in the days after rather than paying a reseller.

## Which stores take part in Record Store Day?

Independent record shops. The [About page](https://recordstoreday.com/CustomPage/614) defines a participating store as a brick-and-mortar retailer with a major commitment to music retail, independently owned and not publicly traded. Online retailers and corporate chains are out.

In the UK and Ireland, around 300 shops take part each year, and the official site lists 314. In the US, shops that sign the Pledge carry a badge on their listing and are the ones able to bring in the list.

### Is Record Store Day only in the US and UK?

No. Wikipedia lists official organisers in the UK, Canada, Ireland, Mexico, Europe, Japan and Australia, and says Piccadilly Records in Manchester and Rough Trade in London joined in the first year. Rough Trade's 2026 guide shows its Berlin shop opening at 9am on the day. The US site says it is primarily a US site, so for the UK and Ireland use the UK site.

## How do electronic and dance labels take part?

Labels submit proposals, and shops decide what to order. The UK FAQ says labels can submit proposals for exclusive releases, and the US About page says that for several years 60 percent or more of its list came from independent labels and distributors.

For dance music, the typical shape is a 12-inch, a short-run EP or a club reissue. In the [UK 2026 list](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/record-store-day-2026-full-list-of-releases/), our own genre reading finds The Chemical Brothers' Leave Home 12-inch, Scuba's Petrology 12-inch, Purple Disco Machine's Soulmatic, Rival Consoles' MindsEye and Pete Tong's Ibiza Classics B-sides.

The [2025 UK list](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/record-store-day-2025-full-list-releases-oasis-taylor-swift-sam-fender-charli-xcx) had Fred again..'s Actual Life Piano EPs alongside Deep Dish, Dave Clarke, Dennis Ferrer, Royal House and Joe Smooth. The genre labels are ours, not the Official Charts'.

## Are Record Store Day exclusives worth it?

It depends. If you want a specific limited pressing at shop price and can reach a shop that stocks it, yes, that is what the list is for. If you want to resell it, the organisers advise against it.

Record Store Day has also drawn criticism, much of it from labels and distributors. Most of the sourced criticism we found dates from 2014 to 2018, and we found no recent data on how much has changed.

### Does Record Store Day strain vinyl pressing plants?

Several labels said so. In March 2014 open letter reported by [Exclaim!](https://exclaim.ca/music/article/uk_vinyl_distributor_kudos_blasts_record_store_day_in_open_letter), the UK distributor Kudos said Record Store Day had tied up pressing plants and halted more than 20 of its manufacturing jobs, leaving smaller labels "locked out of business". In 2015, [The Vinyl Factory](https://thevinylfactory.com/features/exclusive-record-store-day-responds-to-accusations-of-betraying-small-labels) reported that the labels Howling Owl and Sonic Cathedral said the day clogs plants with gimmicky records.

Record Store Day's response, signed by Kim Bayley of the Entertainment Retailers Association, was that its purpose is to promote independent shops, not independent labels, that three in four releases were on independent labels, and that it knew it was not perfect and more pressing plants were needed.

Our own reporting on the plant side is in [Vinyl Pressing Plants Can't Keep Up With Demand](/blog/vinyl-pressing-plant-capacity-crisis). The short version is that capacity has grown but independent labels still wait months, and Record Store Day is one demand source among many.

### Is flipping on resale a problem?

Flipping, buying titles to resell at a markup, is a long-running complaint. Numero Group, in an April 2018 newsletter [reported by Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/amp/record-label-calls-record-store-day-an-unwieldy-grip-and-bitch-fest), called the day an "unwieldy grip-and-bitch fest" and listed lines, fights, flippers and backed-up plants as problems. It also said it would release two records on the ordinary Friday instead.

The organisers' own response is rules. The UK bans reservations and pre-sales "to keep things fair", the US has no pre-orders, and the official explainer points fans to shop websites over resellers. Whether that works well enough is a matter of opinion, and we did not find recent measurements of resale prices or volumes.

### Do small labels get squeezed out?

That is the argument from Kudos and from Howling Owl and Sonic Cathedral. The organisers' answer is the Entertainment Retailers Association statement above. The official US About page describes the list as including artists and labels both large and small, and says 60 percent or more of it came from independent labels and distributors for several years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Record Store Day?

It is an annual celebration of independent record shops, with special vinyl and CD releases made for the day. It was conceived in 2007 and first held on 19 April 2008, according to the official site.

### When is Record Store Day 2026?

Record Store Day 2026 was held on Saturday 18 April 2026. The next official event is RSD Black Friday on Friday 27 November 2026.

### When is Record Store Day Black Friday 2026?

It is on Friday 27 November 2026, the day after US Thanksgiving. In the UK and Ireland, the releases go on sale from 8am at participating independent shops, as reported by the Official Charts Company.

### When is Record Store Day 2027?

We could not find an official date. The UK organisers say 2027 will be the 20th anniversary, and the event is normally held on a Saturday in April, so watch the official sites for the announcement.

### How does Record Store Day work?

Labels and artists make limited releases, shops order them from distributors and decide what to stock, and fans buy them in person on the day. The organisers do not sell the releases or give them to shops.

### What is the difference between Record Store Day and RSD Black Friday?

Black Friday is not a second Record Store Day. It has fewer titles by design, is tied to the post-Thanksgiving shopping weekend, and shops may keep selling the releases through the holiday season while stock lasts.

### Are Record Store Day exclusives worth it?

They are worth it if you want a specific limited pressing and can reach a shop that stocks it. Critics, including labels and distributors, have raised concerns about plant capacity, flipping and small labels, though most of the sourced criticism we found dates from 2014 to 2018.

### Do electronic and dance labels release Record Store Day exclusives?

Yes. The UK lists on the Official Charts site include 12-inch and album releases that fit dance and electronic music, from Fred again.. in 2025 to The Chemical Brothers and Scuba in 2026. Whether a title counts as electronic is our reading of the list.
