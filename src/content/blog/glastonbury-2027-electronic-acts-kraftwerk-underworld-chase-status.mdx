## Which electronic acts are on the Glastonbury 2027 lineup?

As of 23 November 2026, the electronic and dance acts on the first Glastonbury 2027 lineup include Kraftwerk, Underworld, Chase & Status, Bonobo, Basement Jaxx, Hot Chip, Nia Archives, Sammy Virji, underscores and The Avalanches, plus a three-way DJ set from Josh Baker, Kettama and Prospa. Kraftwerk, billed as West Holts Stage headliners, make what MusicRadar reports as their first Glastonbury appearance. None of the lineup reports we opened labels acts by genre, so this list follows Skiddle's grouping. Apart from Kraftwerk, no stage or day has been published for any of them, so treat any timetable guess as unconfirmed. Massive Attack, The xx and Robyn sit in the wider bill. Glastonbury runs 23 to 27 June 2027 at Worthy Farm, and Music Week says organiser Emily Eavis has promised more names in the new year, so this list will grow.

## When is Glastonbury 2027?

Glastonbury 2027 runs from Wednesday 23 to Sunday 27 June 2027 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, per Time Out, Stereogum and the Official Charts report. Time Out calls it the 40th Glastonbury Festival. MusicRadar gives the end date as 26 June, which does not fit a Wednesday start and a Sunday finish, so we have used 27 June.

The festival took a fallow year in 2026, which Official Charts and Louder both state. Louder describes the break as a year off, and Stereogum says Glastonbury periodically rests so the Worthy Farm site can recover. Music Week quotes Emily Eavis: "We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year."

## Who is headlining Glastonbury 2027?

Three Pyramid Stage headliners lead the bill, according to Official Charts: Sam Fender on Friday 25 June, Harry Styles on Saturday 26 June and Billie Eilish on Sunday 27 June, closing the festival. Time Out says it is Fender's second Glastonbury headline slot.

Cher takes the Sunday legends slot. Time Out and Music Week both note that at 81 she would be the oldest act to play it. None of the three headliners is an electronic act, which is normal for Glastonbury: the dance end of the bill lives on the other stages and the late-night areas.

## Is Kraftwerk playing Glastonbury 2027?

Yes. MusicRadar reports that Kraftwerk will headline the West Holts Stage and describes it as the first time they have played Glastonbury. Stereogum also says they are playing the festival for the first time. The sources we opened do not give a day or a set time.

Kraftwerk formed in Düsseldorf in 1970, founded by Ralf Hütter and Florian Schneider, per Wikipedia. Its albums include *Autobahn* (1974), *Trans-Europe Express* (1977) and *Computer World* (1981). Schneider left in 2008 and died in April 2020. Hütter leads the group, which still tours, and its live album *3-D The Catalogue* won the 2018 Grammy for Best Dance/Electronic Album, per the same page.

For readers new to that lineage, our page on [Jean-Michel Jarre](/artists/jean-michel-jarre), the French electronic pioneer behind *Oxygène* (1976), is a good companion to the German side of the story.

## What do Underworld, Chase & Status and Nia Archives bring?

The electronic names on the bill are not one scene. They run from 1970s synth pioneers to a 2020s jungle revival, and the sources let us say a little about each.

### Underworld

Underworld are Karl Hyde and Rick Smith, who have worked together since 1987 and formed the band in Cardiff, per Wikipedia. Their most commercially successful song, "Born Slippy Nuxx", dates from 1996, the year *Trainspotting* was released. The sources we opened do not say whether they have played Glastonbury before, so we make no claim either way. They also appear on the Coachella 2027 bill.

### Chase & Status

Chase & Status are the London drum and bass duo of Saul Milton and Will Kennard, formed in 2003, per Wikipedia. They won the 2024 Brit Award for Producer of the Year, and "Backbone" with Stormzy reached UK number one that year. Their album *Generic Everything* is dated 2026. They are also on the Coachella 2027 bill, on Sunday.

### Nia Archives and the UK club scene

Nia Archives, born in Bradford in 1999 and now London-based, is described by Wikipedia as a leading figure in the post-2020 drum and bass revival. Her 2024 album *Silence Is Loud* was Mercury Prize nominated, and her second album, *Emotional Junglist*, came out on 17 July 2026. Wikipedia cites a DJ Mag report that it was shortlisted for the 2026 Mercury Prize.

Jungle's festival history at Worthy Farm goes back further: [Roni Size](/artists/roni-size), whose Reprazent reformed in 2008, played Glastonbury among major European festivals, as our artist page records from Wikipedia. Nia Archives sits in the line that runs from that scene to today's UK dance bookings.

### Sammy Virji, Josh Baker, Kettama and Prospa

The sources list Sammy Virji and the three-way set from Josh Baker, Kettama and Prospa without commentary, and we have not opened anything that says more. A Josh Baker is also on the Time Warp Mannheim bill on 3 April 2027, per our lineup report listed under Related reading below, though we have not confirmed it is the same artist.

## How does the electronic lineup compare with Coachella 2027?

Two acts appear on both: Underworld and Chase & Status. Beyond that, the two festivals are booking different ends of the dance world, going by the lists we opened for each.

[Coachella 2027](/blog/coachella-2027-electronic-acts-charlotte-de-witte-solomun) leans into club names, with Charlotte de Witte, Solomun, Amelie Lens x Sara Landry and Boris Brejcha placed on named days. The Glastonbury first wave has no equivalent hard techno names in the lists we read. Its dance bookings sit closer to British bass music, drum and bass, indie dance and live electronic acts: Chase & Status, Nia Archives, Basement Jaxx, Hot Chip, Bonobo and Kraftwerk.

The other difference is detail. Coachella published day placements; Glastonbury has so far published only the Pyramid Stage headliners and Kraftwerk's West Holts billing. A proper timetable normally follows later, so treat any day or stage guess for the other acts as unconfirmed.

## Where else do these acts and festivals overlap?

Bonobo is on the Glastonbury bill and on the first wave for Sónar in Barcelona, which our [Sónar festival page](/festivals/sonar) puts at 17 to 19 June 2027, the week before Glastonbury. The same first wave lists [Carl Cox](/artists/carl-cox) and [Peggy Gou](/artists/peggy-gou), and our Peggy Gou page notes that Wikipedia lists Glastonbury among her festival appearances. For the story of why Sónar's 2027 edition looks the way it does, see our report on the [Sónar campaign and the festival's future](/blog/sonar-is-dead-campaign-barcelona-festival-future).

Glastonbury's dance side is not only the lineup poster. Our guide to [hi-fi sound systems at festivals](/blog/hifi-sound-systems-festival-lineups-despacio-sunflower) records that the Sunflower Sound System, built by [Floating Points](/artists/floating-points), appeared at Glastonbury's Silver Hayes, per Groove Atelier. And the team behind Glastonbury's Shangri-La area ran the London Pleasure Gardens site for the 2012 edition of Bloc, as told in our [Bloc reunion story](/blog/bloc-reunion-2027-the-story).

None of these ties is a 2027 booking. They show how far Glastonbury's dance programme reaches beyond the names on a first-wave poster.

## Can you still get tickets for Glastonbury 2027?

Not through general sale. Stereogum reports that tickets went on sale and sold out before the lineup was announced. Music Week and Skiddle both report a sell-out in 42 minutes, though the on-sale date is described only as "last month" in Music Week.

A resale is planned for spring 2027. Louder says the festival will hold a resale in the spring, with details on the Glastonbury website, and Skiddle says returned tickets go on sale in "Spring 2027", likely late April. Time Out adds that original buyers' remaining balances are due between 1 and 7 April 2027, and expects the resale in late April after that window, though no date has been confirmed.

The practical advice is to use the official Glastonbury site and its resale only. We have not opened a price list, so we give no ticket price here.
