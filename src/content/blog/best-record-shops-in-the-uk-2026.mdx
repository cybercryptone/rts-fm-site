As of 18 November 2026, the UK's best-known independent record shops include Piccadilly in Manchester, Honest Jon's and Rough Trade in London, Resident in Brighton, Tribe and Jumbo in Leeds, and Spillers in Cardiff, which says it is the world's oldest. This guide checks 29 shops city by city and flags what we could not confirm.

British shops have a tailwind. The Hoot Leeds, citing 2024 figures, reports 6.7 million vinyl albums sold in the UK, a 17th straight year of growth. Our [look at the 2025 vinyl numbers](/blog/vinyl-sales-record-2025) shows the trend continuing.

## What did the Financial Times list say about UK shops?

The FT's January 2025 list of the world's best record shops is paywalled, and we could not open it. Time Out UK reported that 18 British shops were on it, and Kingston's Nub News gave the same 18, which it said sat in a global list of 38. That is the full UK part, as reported by two outlets.

By Time Out's count, London had Sounds of the Universe, Third Man, Honest Jon's, Hawkeye Enterprises, Starlight Records, Rough Trade on Denmark Street, and Soul Brother Records. Banquet in Kingston upon Thames also made it. Leeds had Tribe, Vinyl Whistle and Jumbo.

The rest were Cob Records in Porthmadog, Carnival Records in Malvern, Truck Store in Oxford, Klang Tone in Stroud, Thorne Records in Edinburgh, Assai Records in Dundee and Uptown Vinyl in Weston, Lincolnshire.

Note what is missing. Piccadilly, Rubadub, Resident, Idle Hands and Spillers were not among the 18, though The Vinyl Factory has featured most of them. The two lists overlap only on Honest Jon's and the Rough Trade brand, and the FT picked the Denmark Street branch, per Time Out London.

## Which UK city has the best record shops?

By volume, London, with seven of the FT's 18 per Time Out's list. The Hoot Leeds notes that Leeds came just behind with three. For dance music, Glasgow, Bristol, Leeds and London each have a specialist with real history.

## Which record shops are in London?

London has its own deep guide, so here is the short version. Rough Trade West is at 130 Talbot Road, and Wikipedia says Geoff Travis opened the first shop in 1976 before it moved to Talbot Road in 1982. The Vinyl Factory dates the shop to 1978, so sources differ. Wikipedia also lists Rough Trade East on Brick Lane (2007) and Denmark Street (October 2024).

Honest Jon's says it was established in 1974, with shops at 278 Portobello Road and Coal Drops Yard. Phonica opened in 2003 at 51 Poland Street, per The Vinyl Factory, and Time Out listed its Record Store Day basement party for April 2025.

Time Out London's January 2025 piece also covers Sounds of the Universe on Broadwick Street, the base of Soul Jazz Records, and Third Man, the only branch of Jack White's chain outside the US. It adds Soul Brother Records in East Putney, founded in 1994 by Laurence and Malcolm Prangell, and the two Harlesden reggae shops Hawkeye and Starlight.

For the full run, including Peckham, Brixton and Brick Lane, read our [best record shops in London guide](/blog/best-record-shops-in-london-2026).

## Where are the best record shops in Manchester?

### Piccadilly Records

Piccadilly's own site says it opened in 1978, was taken over by its current management in 1990 and moved to 53 Oldham Street in the Northern Quarter in 1997. It cites a Music Week award for best independent record store and a Gilles Peterson Worldwide award. Its site lists Monday to Saturday hours and was stocking 2026 reissues when we checked on 18 November 2026.

## Where are the best record shops in Leeds?

### Tribe Records

Mixmag's 2019 history says Simon Scott has run Tribe since it opened in 1995 in the Corn Exchange, with techno, dub, drum and bass and ambient at its core. It has moved several times since. The FT, as quoted by The Hoot Leeds, calls it a mainstay of the dance scene in the north of England.

### Vinyl Whistle

The Hoot Leeds reports that the FT praised Vinyl Whistle in Headingley for new and second-hand vinyl, DJ nights and live performances that reflect Leeds' musical history. We could not open its own site, so we have no founding year.

### Jumbo Records

Jumbo's official site gives 1-3 Merrion Centre as its address and sells tickets alongside records, hi-fi and books. The FT called it an institution, especially for keeping up with live music, as quoted by The Hoot Leeds.

## Where are the best record shops in Sheffield?

### Record Collector

Skiddle describes Record Collector on Fulwood Road as a second-hand shop with over 50,000 records and 25,000 CDs, and tells a story of Public Enemy nearly missing an arena show after stopping by. A 2022 Forge Press student guide dates it to 1978 and calls it the oldest surviving record shop in Sheffield.

### Bear Tree Records

Forge Press says Bear Tree opened in October 2015 inside the Forum arcade on Division Street. It is known for reissues, experimental and electronic releases, and in-store gigs.

## Where are the best record shops in Liverpool and Newcastle?

### Probe Records

Wikipedia says Geoff and Annie Davies and Dave Keats founded Probe in 1971 on Clarence Street, and that it moved to Bluecoat Chambers on School Lane in 2010. It became a hub for Liverpool's post-punk scene and spawned the Probe Plus label in 1981. For something newer, Wikipedia says Rough Trade opened in Liverpool One in 2024, its first shop in northern England.

### Beatdown Records

A 2021 Courier Online student guide to Newcastle names Beatdown, near Central Station, as one of the larger shops in the North East, with a second room and a popular fill-a-bag sale. That is an older source, so check it still trades before you travel.

## Where are the best record shops in Bristol and Brighton?

### Idle Hands

The Vinyl Factory says Chris Farrell opened Idle Hands in February 2011 and named it for his label. Mixmag reported that it closed in August 2022, citing the pandemic and Brexit, and reopened on 20 November 2024 in a new central shop. Its site now lists 20-21 Lower Park Row, and Wikipedia also lists a Rough Trade on Nelson Street, opened December 2017.

### Resident

The Vinyl Factory says Derry Watkins and Natasha Youngs opened Resident at 28 Kensington Gardens in 2004, after meeting at Virgin Our Price. It doubled in size in 2015 by taking the unit next door. Its official site was promoting a 2026 annual when we checked.

## Where are the best record shops in Scotland?

### Rubadub

Rubadub, at 35 Howard Street, Glasgow, was established in August 1992, per The Vinyl Factory. It started as a dance music shop and grew into studio gear and a distribution business. The Skinny describes it as now a hub for synths, modular gear and interfaces as well as records. We found no 2025 to 2026 source confirming its trading status, so check first.

### Thorne Records

Mark Thorne, who is manager and owner, runs Thorne Records at 125 Bruntsfield Place in Edinburgh, per a July 2026 interview with The Skinny. He names the FT listing as a highlight of the past year. Record Store Day UK profiled the shop in 2024, and we could not verify when it opened.

### Assai Records

The FT, as quoted by Dundee Culture, says Assai's founder built an online vinyl business first, opened a physical shop in a Dundee suburb in 2015 and moved to the centre four years later. It is on Union Street and is known for in-stores and obi-strip exclusives.

## Where is the oldest record shop in the UK?

### Spillers Records

Spillers in Cardiff was founded in 1894 by Henry Spiller, per Wikipedia and Nation.Cymru. Wikipedia attributes the "oldest record shop" title to Guinness World Records, though we could not open Guinness's entry. In October 2026 Nation.Cymru quoted owner Ashli Todd, who took over from her father Nick Todd in 2010, saying a Maltese rival began as a furniture shop. The shop has been in Morgan Arcade since 2010.

### Cob Records

Visit Wales says Cob in Porthmadog has traded since the late 1960s and was shipping records worldwide by 1971, from a cramped and packed shop. Time Out UK describes it as "a Tardis of musical rarities".

## Which small-town UK record shops made the FT list?

### Truck Store

Truck Store manager Carl Smithson told Isis Magazine in November 2023 that parent company Rapture teamed up with the Truck Festival crew in 2011 on a pop-up in an empty video shop on Cowley Road, then made it permanent. The Oxford Times called it Oxford's last surviving independent music shop at its seventh birthday.

### Uptown Vinyl

Uptown's site says it fills four rooms at Baytree Garden Centre in Weston with 200,000 vinyl records and 25,000 CDs, and claims to be the UK's largest independent vinyl shop. That is its own claim. Lincolnshire Life reported a ten-year anniversary in April 2025, and LincsOnline reported owner Alan Barnsdale saying business was the busiest ever in January 2025.

### Banquet Records

Banquet in Kingston upon Thames hosts about 200 gigs a year, per Time Out London. The FT, as reported by Nub News, praised its edit of contemporary indie, rock and dance records.

### Carnival Records

Carnival's site gives 83 Church Street, Great Malvern, and describes new and used vinyl, vintage hi-fi and buy, sell and trade.

### Klang Tone Records

Sean Roe runs Klang Tone in Lansdown, Stroud, per a March 2024 Stroud Times piece that also names Sound Records and Lost Track Records as neighbours. Time Out UK lists it among the FT picks and calls its stock eclectic.

## Where are the best record shops in Northern Ireland?

### Sound Advice

The Vinyl Factory's January 2024 Irish guide says Marion Hawkes founded Sound Advice in 2021 at Banana Block, 310 Newtownards Road, Belfast. It describes a leftfield mix of new and used electronic, soul and jazz.

### Number One Records

Belfast Live reported in April 2024 that Number One Records in Larne opened in 1979, and that John O'Lynn has owned it since 2014. It took part in Record Store Day for a second time that year.

## Which famous UK record shops have closed or moved?

Transmission in Margate, on The Vinyl Factory's older list, was reported by Kent Online in June 2021 to be closing for good. Idle Hands moved after its 2022 closure. Probe left Button Street and Slater Street for the Bluecoat. Wikipedia's Rough Trade page says its Beak Street branch closed on 31 July 2026.

## What we could not verify

- **The FT article itself.** It is paywalled. Our UK list rests on Time Out UK and Nub News, which agree.
- **Rubadub's current status.** Its site was blocked to us and our sources are undated.
- **Probe's management.** The Bluecoat page credits Annie Davies, but Wikipedia says she and Geoff Davies both died in 2023.
- **Founding years** for Sounds of the Universe, Third Man, Vinyl Whistle, Jumbo, Banquet, Carnival, Klang Tone and Thorne.
- **Beatdown** beyond a 2021 student guide, and **Record Collector's** address, given as 233 Fulwood Road by Skiddle and 223 by Forge Press.
- **Guinness's entry** for Spillers, and Rough Trade's start year, 1976 per Wikipedia and 1978 per The Vinyl Factory.
- **Shops we left out** because we could not open a reliable source, including Monorail in Glasgow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best record shop in the UK?

There is no official ranking. Piccadilly in Manchester, Honest Jon's and Rough Trade in London, Resident in Brighton and Spillers in Cardiff are the most consistently cited. The FT's own 18 British picks did not include Piccadilly or Resident.

### Which UK record shops did the Financial Times name?

Time Out UK listed 18, including Sounds of the Universe, Third Man, Honest Jon's, Tribe, Vinyl Whistle, Jumbo, Thorne, Assai, Cob, Truck Store, Banquet, Carnival, Klang Tone, Uptown Vinyl and Soul Brother Records. Hawkeye Enterprises, Starlight Records and Rough Trade's Denmark Street branch complete the set.

### Where is the oldest record shop in the UK?

Spillers Records in Cardiff, founded in 1894, which Wikipedia says Guinness World Records recognises as the oldest shop selling records. Its owner says a shop in Malta is not a rival because it began as furniture retail.

### Which UK city has the most record shops on the FT list?

London, with seven of the 18 by Time Out's count, followed by Leeds with three. Both counts come from Time Out UK's list.

### Where should I buy techno and house records in the UK?

Phonica in London, Tribe in Leeds, Idle Hands in Bristol and Rubadub in Glasgow are the dance specialists in this guide. Check Rubadub's status first.

### What is the biggest independent record shop in the UK?

Uptown Vinyl in Weston, Lincolnshire, says it is, with over 200,000 records and 25,000 CDs. That is the shop's own claim, and we have not verified it against rivals.

### Are there good record shops in Northern Ireland?

Yes. Sound Advice in Belfast opened in 2021 and favours electronic, soul and jazz, per The Vinyl Factory. Number One Records in Larne has traded since 1979, per Belfast Live.

### Is Rough Trade a UK chain?

Wikipedia lists UK shops in London, Nottingham, Bristol and Liverpool. It dates the first shop to 1976, though The Vinyl Factory says 1978.

### Did Record Store Day help UK shops?

Many UK shops, including Phonica, Thorne and Number One Records, take part, according to Time Out, Record Store Day UK and Belfast Live.
