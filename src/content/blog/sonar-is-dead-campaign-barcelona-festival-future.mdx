On Tuesday 27 October 2026 Sónar, the Barcelona festival of advanced music, announced its own death. Its Instagram was wiped except for one video of a coffin engraved "Sónar 1994-2026", a death notice appeared in the print edition of La Vanguardia, and its website was replaced by a book of condolences. One week later it turned out to be a relaunch teaser: on Tuesday 3 November Sónar announced its 34th edition for 17 to 19 June 2027, the first 30 artists, a new format and early-bird passes.

**Update, 8 November 2026:** the festival is not ending and has not been renamed. This page began as a report written on 31 October, when nothing was known, and we have kept the account of the campaign and the ownership story behind it. Everything about 2027 below is from Sónar's own site and the outlets cited at the bottom.

**The short version:** the "death" was a marketing stunt for a rebuilt festival. Sónar by Day leaves the Fira Montjuïc and Fira Gran Via sites and returns to what the festival calls the seafront in central Barcelona, where it began in 1994, while Sónar by Night runs on Friday 18 and Saturday 19 June at Fira Gran Via. The stunt landed against a real backdrop: the founders left in October 2025, the 2026 edition merged day and night on one site, and two senior Sónar+D figures left in October 2026.

## What did Sónar announce on 3 November?

- **Dates and format.** Sónar 2027 runs 17 to 19 June, according to Sónar's site and [RA](https://ra.co/news/86333). Sónar by Day runs all three days. Sónar by Night runs Friday and Saturday across four stages at Fira Gran Via. Sónar+D is moving to the seafront site, runs on the mornings of 17 and 18 June, and is billed with a focus on AI, emerging tools and critical thinking.
- **Where Sónar by Day will be.** The festival says "the seafront in the centre of Barcelona". [Catalan News](https://www.catalannews.com/culture/item/sonar-announces-changes-to-festival-format-and-first-2027-headliners) says media suggest the Tinglados at Port Vell, and the exact venue has not been confirmed by the festival.
- **The first 30 artists.** [Carl Cox](/artists/carl-cox) returns to Sónar for the first time in more than two decades. The announcement also includes [Peggy Gou](/artists/peggy-gou), KI/KI, Romy, Oscar Mulero, [Honey Dijon](/artists/honey-dijon) presenting Sinister, Bonobo, Tokischa, Yung Lean, Frost Children, Jessie Ware, Cornelius, Goldie performing live, Collabs3000 (the pairing of [Chris Liebing](/artists/chris-liebing) and [Speedy J](/artists/speedy-j)), Job Jobse, [Donato Dozzy](/artists/donato-dozzy) and [Batu](/artists/batu), and Kaitlyn Aurelia Smith. [909originals](https://909originals.com/2026/11/03/not-dead-yet-sonar-barcelona-announces-first-acts-for-2027-edition/) headlined its report "Not dead yet", and the festival promises more than 80 performances and activities in total.
- **The framing.** The festival described the campaign as a rebirth. [Music Times](https://www.musictimes.com/articles/113173/20261104/week-after-its-own-obituary-sonar-returns-carl-cox-peggy-gou-jessie-ware-lead-2027-lineup.htm) reported that a teaser imitated the death and the wake. We found no named executive explaining the stunt, and critics have questioned it, so we do not offer a reason.

## What exactly happened on 27 October?

- **The video.** Sónar's Instagram account was cleared of its earlier posts, leaving one video of a coffin with "Sónar 1994-2026" on it and a caption in Catalan, Spanish and English saying that Sónar is dead. EFE reported that every earlier post had been removed. Reports differ on the details of the video, so we describe only the coffin and the dates.
- **The notice.** A black-bordered death notice appeared in La Vanguardia's print edition on 27 October, and ads in the same style ran around Barcelona, according to Primera Fila, DJ Mag Germany and EFE.
- **The website.** When we fetched sonar.es on 31 October it showed only "Sónar is dead", the dates 1994 and 2026, and a book of condolences with a form asking for a name and email address. It thanks visitors for being part of the festival's story. Crónica Global suggests the email field works as newsletter capture, which is its opinion.
- **The dates.** The 17 to 19 June 2027 dates, announced after the 2026 edition, disappeared from the homepage during the campaign, and the 2027 presale page returned an error on 31 October. They are back as part of the 3 November announcement.
- **The sister events.** Sónar Istanbul's site listed 9 and 10 April 2027 on 31 October, with no mention of the campaign. We did not recheck it on an official page after the relaunch.

## What did Sónar say between 27 October and 3 November?

Nothing that explained the campaign. According to EFE, the festival said it would share news in the coming days, and ModoFestival reported on 30 October that nothing further had been published. On 31 October we found no statement from the CEO, the founders, the parent company Superstruct or KKR. The coming-days news turned out to be the 3 November announcement.

## What was speculation, and how did it turn out?

- **A relaunch, rename or new format.** Most coverage read the stunt as a teaser, because Sónar usually announces artists in November. That was right: it is a relaunch with a new format and the same name.
- **A venue move.** La Vanguardia reported on 28 October, according to ModoFestival, that the festival might split day and night again and move the daytime programme and Sónar+D to Barcelona's Port Vell. The split and the move to the seafront are now confirmed, and the exact Port Vell venue is still not.
- **The end of the festival.** No source said so, and Sónar 2027 is going ahead. The English Wikipedia page claimed the festival would no longer exist, which the live site never supported.

## What do tickets cost, and are refunds still possible?

Sónar's homepage on 8 November listed SonarPass at 160 euros and SonarPass+D at 175 euros, with VIP at 275 euros and VIP+D at 300 euros, so prices have moved from the June presale page, which listed 155 and 295 euros for the SonarPass and top VIP pass. Flex tickets stay refundable until 28 February 2027 according to the homepage and Music Times, which differs from the 1 March 2027 date on the older presale page. Check sonar.es for the current terms, because prices and dates may change by phase.

## How did Sónar get here?

- **1994.** Ricard Robles, Enric Palau and Sergio Caballero founded it in Barcelona. For three decades it ran as a day-and-night festival with the Sónar+D creativity and technology congress, and it spread to Istanbul and Lisbon, among other cities.
- **2018.** Superstruct, then backed by Providence, bought a majority stake in Sónar's parent, with the founders keeping creative control. The terms were not disclosed.
- **June 2024.** The investment firm KKR bought Superstruct, a deal announced on 21 June 2024; the price, about 1.3 billion euros, is press-reported. Our report on [the Boiler Room boycott over its KKR buyout](/blog/boiler-room-kkr-boycott) covers the same ownership chain and its reputational fallout.
- **2025.** Dozens of artists and collectives withdrew from the festival in a boycott linked to KKR's investments and the BDS movement. The count depends on who tallies it: 28 per the festival, more than 30 in several English-language outlets, about 50 in El País and DJ Mag, and 59 in the organisers' own count. Sónar said it condemned the violence in Gaza and distanced itself from investment activities contrary to its values, and said it had no influence or control over KKR's investments. The festival still announced a record 161,000 attendance in June 2025, an organiser figure.
- **October 2025.** The three founders and their partner Ventura Barba left. Ara reported a joint statement, and François Jozic, the founder of Brunch Electronik, became CEO. Superstruct called the exit orderly and cordial. The founders did not link their departure to the boycott, according to DJ Mag Germany.
- **June 2026.** The first edition without the founders moved to a single site, Fira Gran Via in L'Hospitalet, with day and night merged. The festival claimed 150,000 visitors against 161,000 in 2025, but Ara warns the figures are not comparable because the structure changed. Ara reported in 2024 that the festival had to leave Fira Montjuïc because of the fair's reform works.
- **October 2026.** The Sónar+D director Andrea Faroppa and Antònia Folguera are leaving this month, according to Crónica Global and DJ Mag Germany. Reports that Folguera called the festival more corporate come second-hand, so we paraphrase and do not quote.

For the festival's own history and the 2026 programme, see our [Sónar festival page](/festivals/sonar). The 2026 edition was headlined by acts including [Amelie Lens](/artists/amelie-lens) and [Charlotte de Witte](/artists/charlotte-de-witte), according to Primera Fila and Test Press.

## Who has reacted?

Very few have. Exron Music reported that Boys Noize commented on Instagram, thanking the festival and recalling some of his best moments playing and dancing there, and that Insomniac's Pasquale Rotella also commented. Spanish media played with the headline: ABC, according to Crónica Global, ran a headline playing on the royal formula of the king is dead, long live the king. We found no statement from an artist, promoter or agency reacting to the removal of the 2027 dates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sónar festival cancelled?

No. On 27 October 2026 Sónar announced its own death in a campaign, and on 3 November it announced Sónar 2027 for 17 to 19 June with a new format and the first 30 artists. The "death" was a relaunch teaser.

### What are the Sónar 2027 dates?

17 to 19 June 2027, the festival's 34th edition. Sónar by Day runs on all three days, and Sónar by Night runs on Friday 18 and Saturday 19 June.

### Where is Sónar 2027 held?

Sónar by Night is at Fira Gran Via in L'Hospitalet. Sónar by Day returns to what the festival calls the seafront in central Barcelona, and Sónar+D moves there too. Catalan News says media suggest the Tinglados at Port Vell, which the festival has not confirmed.

### Who is playing Sónar 2027?

The first 30 artists include Carl Cox, Peggy Gou, Bonobo, Jessie Ware, Romy, Oscar Mulero, Honey Dijon presenting Sinister, Goldie live, Collabs3000 (Chris Liebing and Speedy J), Donato Dozzy and Batu, Cornelius, Yung Lean and Tokischa. More are due.

### How much are Sónar 2027 tickets and can I get a refund?

Sónar's homepage on 8 November listed SonarPass at 160 euros, SonarPass+D at 175 euros, VIP at 275 euros and VIP+D at 300 euros. It lists Flex refunds until 28 February 2027, though an older presale page said 1 March. Check sonar.es for current prices and terms.

### Why did Sónar say it was dead?

It was a campaign before the relaunch. Sónar framed the new edition as a rebirth, but we found no named executive explaining the choice. The stunt followed the founders' exit in October 2025, a move to a single site in 2026 and the departure of two Sónar+D leaders in October 2026.

### Who owns Sónar?

Sónar's parent is controlled by the festival group Superstruct, which took a majority stake in 2018 and which KKR bought in June 2024. The founders left in October 2025 and François Jozic is the CEO.

### What happened to Sónar in 2025?

Dozens of artists and collectives withdrew over KKR's investments in a boycott linked to the BDS movement, with counts from 28 to 59 depending on the source. The festival still announced a record 161,000 attendance, an organiser figure.
