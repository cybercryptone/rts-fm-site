[Nina Kraviz](/artists/nina-kraviz) does not really do "off seasons" anymore. Between a January warehouse rave in Thailand and a November festival slot in regional Victoria, the Siberian-born DJ, producer, and трип (тrip) Records founder has built a 2026 calendar that reads less like a tour and more like a map of where underground dance music is currently expanding. Some stops are the expected ones: Ibiza, Barcelona, a fabric all-nighter in London. Others point somewhere newer, like DGTL's India edition or a two-weekend techno marathon in a Serbian spa town.

This guide pulls together the confirmed 2026 dates, first compiled in early September and re-checked on November 5, organized by season and region, along with context on what each festival or venue actually represents in club culture. Tour routings for artists at Kraviz's level shift constantly (new legs get added, some dates get swapped for others), so treat this as a living reference rather than a locked itinerary, and check the primary listings linked throughout before booking travel.

## Where Is Nina Kraviz Playing in 2026?

As of this writing, Kraviz has confirmed appearances across Asia, Europe, North and South America, and Australia, plus a recurring summer residency in Ibiza. The season breaks down roughly like this: a January festival date in Thailand, a March alpine stop in France, a heavy spring-into-summer run through the Netherlands, Colombia, France, and Spain, a July stretch that hits London, Madrid, Barcelona, and Malta almost back to back, August dates in Portugal and Serbia, a September return to Belgium, and a November run that spans India and Australia, now including a Strawberry Fields weekend and a Sydney open-air show. Her Amnesia Ibiza residency ran underneath all of it on Sundays from mid-June to early October.

For the most current version of this list, the artist-tour trackers on [Songkick](https://www.songkick.com/artists/3037096-nina-kraviz/calendar), [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/dj/ninakraviz/tour-dates), and [Bandsintown](https://www.bandsintown.com/a/387875-nina-kraviz) pull directly from promoter announcements and update faster than almost any editorial piece can, including this one.

### January to March: Thailand and the Alps

Kraviz opened 2026 at **EDC Thailand**, held January 16 through 18 in Phuket. Insomniac's Southeast Asian outpost of Electric Daisy Carnival has grown into one of the region's largest dance music gatherings, and the 2026 edition expanded its Rhythm Park footprint considerably, positioning Kraviz alongside a lineup spanning trance, bass, and techno.

In late March (the 21st through the 28th), she played **Tomorrowland Winter** in Alpe d'Huez, France. The snow-covered sister event to Belgium's Tomorrowland turns a ski resort into a week-long festival site, with stages built into the mountain and club nights running in the village below. It is one of the few settings where you can watch a Kraviz set and then, in theory, go skiing the next morning.

### Late Spring: Los Angeles, the Netherlands, Bogota

May brought a genuine world tour rhythm. She played **Tomorrowland's CORE** event in Los Angeles on May 2 and 3, staged at Los Angeles State Historic Park, before flying to the Netherlands for **Awakenings Upclose** at Spaarnwoude on May 16 and 17. Upclose is Awakenings' more intimate, boutique-scaled outdoor event, distinct from the label's much larger summer festival later in July, and it leans into closer, more conversational stage sizes than the brand's usual scale.

From there she went to South America for **BAUM Festival** in Bogota, Colombia, on May 22 and 23, a festival that has become one of the clearest signals of how far Colombia's techno and house scene has come in the last decade, drawing international headliners to a city that barely registered on European booking agents' radar fifteen years ago.

### Summer: Paris, Spain, London, and the Netherlands Again

Summer is where the calendar gets genuinely dense. Kraviz played **Solidays** in Paris (June 26 to 28), a festival built around HIV/AIDS research fundraising that has long paired mainstream pop and hip hop headliners with a serious late-night electronic program. She then moved through Spain and the Netherlands in quick succession: **Mad Cool Festival** in Madrid (July 8 to 11), marking the festival's tenth anniversary; the **Awakenings Festival** proper in Hilvarenbeek (July 10 to 12), the brand's flagship large-scale event and one of the most technically ambitious techno productions in Europe; and **SOUNDIT Festival** in Barcelona (July 17 and 18), a newer club-and-experimental-sound gathering at Parc Nou.

London came next on July 25, with a joint **fabric x Junction 2** date. Junction 2 is the UK's most prominent standalone techno festival brand, and this edition paired an afternoon and evening at Boston Manor Park with a separate Junction 2 x fabric by Night event at the legendary Farringdon club (fabric's own listing for that night does not include Kraviz), itself a fixture on any serious list of the world's most influential dance venues.

July closed out in **Malta**, where Kraviz headlined opening night of **Spiral Festival** (July 31 to August 2) alongside Fatboy Slim, a new addition to the EXIT festival organization's growing international touring format.

### August Into September: Portugal, Serbia, Belgium

August took her to Portugal for **NeoPop**, held August 6 through 8 at the Forte de Santiago da Barra in Viana do Castelo. For its 20th anniversary the festival briefly reclaimed its original name, AntiPop, a nod to its roots as one of the Iberian Peninsula's foundational techno gatherings. Days later, on August 7, she played **Lovefest** in Vrnjacka Banja, Serbia, a long-running Balkan festival that expanded to a two-weekend format in 2026 and remains a key stop for artists building an audience in Southeastern Europe.

September brings a return to Belgium for **Voodoo Village** in Grimbergen (September 11 to 13), a festival built into castle grounds and forested parkland that has developed a reputation for immersive, film-set-style stage design across its five stages.

### Autumn: India and Australia

The calendar's most significant expansion move lands in November. **DGTL India** marks the sixth edition of a festival brand originally founded in Amsterdam and now operating outposts on several continents. As [Rolling Stone India reported](https://rollingstoneindia.com/dgtl-india-2026-lineup-nina-kraviz-biianco-stephan-bodzin/) when the line-up dropped, Kraviz headlines an international bill alongside [Stephan Bodzin](/artists/stephan-bodzin) and Biianco, part of a broader push by European festival operators into South Asia's rapidly growing club scene.

One thing has changed since that announcement. On October 14 the festival moved its Mumbai leg to Goa, citing licensing uncertainty for live events in Maharashtra, [according to Time Out India](https://www.timeout.com/india/news/dgtl-india-2026-moves-its-mumbai-festival-venue-to-goa-101526). The Bengaluru edition stays at The LaLiT Ashok on November 13 and 14, and the new [DGTL Goa](https://dgtl-festival.com/en/dgtl-goa) edition is listed at W Goa on November 14 and 15, with Kraviz on the line-up for both. Time Out says the original November 13 and 14 dates are unchanged while printing the Goa dates as November 14 and 15, and DGTL's own page gives the latter, so check the festival's ticketing before booking flights.

Down Under, the year closes with three dates. **Strawberry Fields** at Tocumwal, New South Wales, runs November 20 to 22, and [FestivalPro's line-up report](https://festivalpro.com/festival-management/5702/news/2026/7/7/Strawberry-Fields-Festival-Australia-Confirms-Line-up-for-November-2026.html) names Kraviz alongside [Honey Dijon](/artists/honey-dijon) and Gerd Janson B2B [Marcel Dettmann](/artists/marcel-dettmann). On Saturday, November 28 she plays an open-air show at Manning Bar in Sydney, presented by Sounds Like and Niche, per [the venue's listing](https://www.manningbar.com/event/nina-kraviz-2/). Her last billing is **A3 Festival** at Lardner Park, Victoria, which [Mixmag Australia lists](https://mixmag.net.au/read/a3-shares-its-2026-lineup-news) as running November 27 to 30 (earlier versions of this guide gave November 27 to 29). A3 is an Australian event built around the intersection of audio, art, and architecture, and [Beat](https://beat.com.au/a3-festival-has-announced-its-biggest-lineup-yet-and-stage-one-ballot-is-already-gone/) confirms Kraviz on the bill. We have not found which day she is billed for, and the Sydney show falls on the same Saturday as the festival, so confirm set times with each promoter.

## What Festivals Is Nina Kraviz Doing in 2026, and What Are They Known For?

Taken together, the 2026 route is a fairly good cross-section of how dance music festival culture is currently organized: mega-brands with global franchises (Tomorrowland, DGTL, EXIT), regional flagships with decades of history (Awakenings, NeoPop, Lovefest), city-specific newcomers testing the market (SOUNDIT, Spiral), and single-club institutions that still matter as much as any festival (fabric, Amnesia). Booking across all of those tiers in a single year is fairly unusual, and it reflects how techno and house have moved from niche subculture into something closer to a genuinely global touring circuit, a shift covered in more detail in [our look at electronic music's growth in the US](/blog/electronic-music-fastest-growing-genre-us).

## What Is the Amnesia Ibiza Residency, and When Does It Run?

Running underneath all of the above is Kraviz's Sunday residency at **Pyramid, Amnesia Ibiza**, active every Sunday from June 14 through October 4, 2026 (with the occasional week off for a touring conflict). Pyramid is one of Amnesia's two main rooms and has hosted some of techno's most storied residencies over the years. A Sunday there functions almost like a season-long check-in point between her international dates, since fans who cannot catch a specific festival stop can generally count on finding her back on the island most weeks through the summer. For a closer look at what that residency actually feels like on the ground, see [our piece on the Pyramid residency itself](/blog/nina-kraviz-amnesia-ibiza-pyramid-residency), and for the bigger picture of her year, including the label news around it, there's [our full 2026 recap](/blog/nina-kraviz-2026-recap).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is Nina Kraviz playing next in 2026?

As of November 5, the dates still ahead are in India and Australia: DGTL India in Bengaluru (November 13 to 14) and Goa (November 14 to 15, moved from Mumbai), Strawberry Fields in Tocumwal (November 20 to 22), an open-air show in Sydney on November 28, and A3 Festival in Lardner Park, Victoria (festival dates November 27 to 30). The route began with EDC Thailand in Phuket in January and included a Sunday residency at Pyramid, Amnesia Ibiza, scheduled from June 14 to October 4.

### Is Nina Kraviz still doing a residency at Amnesia Ibiza in 2026?

Her Sunday residency at Pyramid, Amnesia Ibiza, was scheduled to run from June 14 through October 4, 2026, alongside her wider international festival schedule. That season has now passed its published end date, so for anything on the island after that, check Amnesia's Pyramid page.

### What is DGTL India and why is Nina Kraviz playing it?

DGTL is a festival brand founded in Amsterdam that has expanded to multiple countries over the past decade. Its 2026 India edition, held in Bengaluru on November 13 and 14 and in Goa on November 14 and 15 (after the Mumbai leg was relocated), reflects a broader trend of established European festival operators bringing headline-level techno and house bookings, including Kraviz, into South Asia's fast-growing club scene.

### What is NeoPop and why did it briefly change its name?

NeoPop is a long-running Portuguese techno festival held at the Forte de Santiago da Barra in Viana do Castelo. For its 2026 edition, marking 20 years, the festival temporarily readopted its original name, AntiPop, as a nod to its earliest years, before Nina Kraviz and the rest of the 2026 lineup take the fortress stages August 6 through 8.
