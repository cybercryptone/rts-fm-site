Grand Theft Auto VI does not come out until November 19, 2026, and Rockstar Games still has not published a full soundtrack list. But the confirmed fragments released so far, now including a 34-track album and six named radio stations, tell a specific story: alongside the trap, reggaeton and 1980s Miami nostalgia you would expect from a game set in a fictional stand-in for South Florida, the studio has already put real house and electronic names on record, not as a novelty, but as some of the most prominent music in its own marketing.

That matters more than it might look like on the surface. GTA is not a niche property picking up cool underground cosigns for credibility. It is one of the best-selling entertainment franchises ever made, and its soundtrack has a documented history of turning licensed tracks into cultural reference points for people who never set foot in a club. When house and electronica acts get a real seat at that table, it is worth understanding exactly what has been confirmed, what is still speculation, and what it has actually meant for artists in the past.

## What has Rockstar actually confirmed about the GTA VI soundtrack so far?

Rockstar's first wave of confirmed music came from three public reveals: the original December 2023 announcement trailer, the second trailer from May 2025, and the 26-minute "GTA VI: An Extended Look" gameplay reveal that premiered in partnership with Netflix on August 27, 2026, which left roughly two dozen songs verified. [Official Charts has been tracking the confirmed list](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/gta-6-songs-grand-theft-auto-vi-soundtrack-when/) as it grows, and it spans a genuinely wide net: Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," Depeche Mode's "People Are People," Def Leppard's "Love Bites," Dominican dembow from Chimbala and Omega, Haitian kompa from Zenglen, and hip-hop cuts from Kodak Black and Sexyy Red sit alongside two house records that dance music writers immediately flagged as significant.

Since late August, three more things have landed. On September 17, Rockstar and Atlantic Records announced [Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1638329-fred-again-etienne-de-crecy-and-daft-punks-guy-manuel-feature-on-gta-vi-soundtrack-singles), 34 original tracks due on November 19, with six singles out at once. On October 8, Rockstar revealed the [first six in-game radio stations](https://www.thefader.com/2026/10/08/grand-theft-auto-iv-radio-station-hosts), and on October 29 it announced [an original score by Justice](https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/rockstar-games-and-justice-unveil-grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score/095363) for release on December 11. Those are separate products: the album is new songs made for the game, the stations carry the licensed radio music, and the score is instrumental.

What Rockstar has not done is publish the full album tracklist (only the six singles are named), a complete station-by-station song list, or a final count of what the game will ship with. Past GTA titles have historically padded out their launch rosters after release, so treating any current list as final would be premature. What is confirmed is confirmed; everything else circulating online right now is fan tracking, leaks, or educated guesswork, and it should be read that way.

## Which underground house and electronic artists are already confirmed?

Two tracks did the most work in the August 2026 Extended Look trailer, and both are unmistakably house music rather than festival-EDM filler. "Mine O' Mine," a 2022 collaboration between UK-Jamaican vocalist Aluna and Canadian DJ-producer Jayda G, and "Inner Light," a 2021 track pairing UK producer Elderbrook with the Canadian duo Bob Moses, both scored meaningful screen time in the reveal, as [EDM.com documented](https://edm.com/lifestyle/gta-6-trailer-extended-look-edm-music/) in its rundown of the trailer's music cues and [Mixmag Asia](https://mixmag.asia/read/gta-vi-extended-look-features-music-jayda-g-elderbrook-lil-wayne-kodak-black-news) listed alongside the rest of the reveal's tracks.

None of these are unknown names to anyone paying attention to house music over the last decade, but they are also not radio-pop crossovers. Jayda G built her reputation on the Discwoman and Ninja Tune circuit before her 2020 EP earned a nomination for the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording, and she has spoken about balancing her production career against the master's degree in environmental toxicology she completed studying [the effects of pollution on Salish Sea orcas](https://www.grammy.com/news/jayda-g-environmental-scientist-house-music-djproducer-planet-needs-right-now/), a biography about as far from major-label pop as it gets. Elderbrook, meanwhile, is the vocalist and producer behind "Cola," his 2017 collaboration with CamelPhat that became one of UK underground dance music's rare Grammy nominees, for Best Dance Recording at the 2018 ceremony. Bob Moses, the Vancouver-raised, Los Angeles-based duo of Tom Howie and Jimmy Vallance, picked up their own Best Dance Recording nomination for "Tearing Me Up," whose RAC remix went on to win the Grammy for Best Remixed Recording outright.

In other words, the electronic artists Rockstar has chosen to spotlight are credentialed inside dance music specifically, not just generically "electronic-adjacent" pop acts wearing the genre label for streaming metadata. That is a meaningfully different signal than putting a Calvin Harris or Marshmello track on a trailer.

The newer announcements widen the picture without settling the radio question. The album's first singles include "Sexy Magic," a collaboration between CA7RIEL, Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. and French Touch figure Étienne de Crécy, and Travis Scott's "RHYNO," which [Beatportal reports](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1638329-fred-again-etienne-de-crecy-and-daft-punks-guy-manuel-feature-on-gta-vi-soundtrack-singles) was produced by Daft Punk's Guy-Manuel. On the radio side, [The FADER lists](https://www.thefader.com/2026/10/08/grand-theft-auto-iv-radio-station-hosts) Palms Trax among the hosts of AfroBank FM, alongside Burna Boy, and Robyn among the hosts of the returning Flash FM, which it describes as a club beats station. None of the six announced stations is a dedicated house or electronic one. [Keengamer notes](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-6-reveals-its-first-six-radio-stations-hosted-by-bad-bunny-slayer-and-more/) that Rockstar's announcement lists Dance and Electronic among categories that have no named stations yet, so where "Mine O' Mine" and "Inner Light" will sit on the dial is still open.

## How does this compare to what GTA V did for real artists?

This would not be the first time Rockstar's radio stations moved the needle for musicians outside the mainstream. Grand Theft Auto V launched in 2013 with [more than 240 licensed tracks across 15 stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Grand_Theft_Auto_V), and by the time Grand Theft Auto Online finished expanding that library over the following decade, the total catalog reportedly exceeded 700 songs. The game also commissioned original material from artists including A$AP Rocky, Flying Lotus, Twin Shadow, Neon Indian and Yeasayer, work that reached an audience most underground and left-of-center acts never touch through conventional release cycles.

The clearest precedent for what GTA VI's house cuts could become, though, is the 2018 "After Hours" nightclub update to GTA Online. Rockstar built an entire in-game questline around opening and running a nightclub, and staffed its resident DJ booth with real techno and house figures: [Solomun](/artists/solomun), Tale Of Us, [Dixon](/artists/dixon) and The Black Madonna each became hireable, playable in-game DJs, complete with exclusive mixes recorded for the game's Los Santos Underground Radio station, as [DJ Mag reported](https://djmag.com/content/grand-theft-auto-reveals-new-nightclub-featuring-residencies-solomun-black-madonna-tale-us) and [Resident Advisor covered](https://ra.co/news/42191) at the time. For a generation of players who had never been near a proper club, that update was, functionally, an introduction to European house and techno culture, delivered through a game most of them already owned.

That history is exactly why dance music press treated "Mine O' Mine" and "Inner Light" landing in the Extended Look trailer as more than incidental. Rockstar has a track record of using its platform deliberately for this genre, not accidentally.

## Why does a GTA placement matter specifically for underground dance artists?

Sync licensing pays artists directly, but the real value in a placement this size is almost never the check. It is exposure at a scale independent labels and self-releasing producers structurally cannot buy. A single GTA VI radio station cue reaches an audience that, based on prior installments, will run into the tens of millions of unique players over the game's lifespan, filtered through years of gameplay streams, YouTube compilations, TikTok clips and highlight reels that keep resurfacing the same handful of tracks long after launch week ends.

For house and techno specifically, that kind of exposure lands at a moment when the genre is already gaining ground in the mainstream data. Luminate's own reporting found Dance/Electronic posted the largest US streaming-share gain of any genre in the first half of 2026, a trend [we broke down in detail here](/blog/electronic-music-fastest-growing-genre-us), and a GTA VI placement is the kind of cultural event that can accelerate exactly that curve for the specific artists involved rather than the genre in the abstract. It is one thing for a Luminate report to say dance music is growing. It is another for millions of players to spend a hundred hours inside a game with a Jayda G record wired into the soundtrack of their own playthrough.

None of that changes the calculus for the smaller, more purposefully underground end of the scene RTS.FM has operated in since 2006. A Rockstar placement is not a plan any label our size can build a strategy around, and it should not be. But it is proof that house and techno, at their most credible and least compromised, can sit inside the biggest entertainment properties in the world without being flattened into generic "EDM" for the occasion. If you want to hear where that lineage actually lives day to day, [RTS.FM's stream](https://rts.fm) keeps running the artist-run, non-commercial version of the same music, and the label's [Bandcamp catalog](https://rtsfm.bandcamp.com/) is worth digging through if a trailer cue is the first time house music has actually caught your attention.

## Is the full GTA VI soundtrack out yet?

No. As of 5 November 2026, Rockstar has confirmed music through its trailers, the August 27 Extended Look, six singles from the 34-track album and six announced radio stations. A full album tracklist and a station-by-station song list, the kind fans compiled retroactively for GTA V, have not been published. Rockstar has [said it will share more](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-radio-stations-hosts-on-demand-podcasts-revealed-by-rockstar), including more album singles and details of the dynamic score, but the coverage we reviewed gives no date for the rest of the stations, so expect the full picture closer to, or at, the November 19, 2026 release.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Grand Theft Auto VI come out?

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to release on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, after Rockstar delayed the game from its originally targeted late-2025 window.

### Which artists are confirmed for the GTA VI soundtrack?

Songs confirmed through the trailers and the August 2026 Extended Look span Tom Petty, Depeche Mode, Def Leppard, Kodak Black, Sexyy Red, Chimbala and Omega, and Zenglen, alongside two house tracks: "Mine O' Mine" by Aluna and Jayda G, and "Inner Light" by Elderbrook and Bob Moses. The separate album adds six named singles, among them Travis Scott, Yung Lean, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro, Keith Richards and the Fred again.., Étienne de Crécy and PinkPantheress collaboration "Sexy Magic".

### Has Rockstar released the full GTA VI soundtrack or radio station list?

No. As of 5 November 2026 Rockstar has announced six of its in-game radio stations and six of the 34 album tracks. A complete tracklist and station breakdown, similar to what eventually surfaced for GTA V, has not been published, and Rockstar has said it will reveal more, with the game arriving on November 19, 2026.

### Has GTA featured underground house and techno artists before?

Yes. GTA Online's 2018 "After Hours" nightclub update installed Solomun, Tale Of Us, Dixon and The Black Madonna as playable resident DJs with exclusive in-game mixes, a documented precedent for Rockstar deliberately platforming house and techno culture rather than treating it as filler.

### Why does a placement like this matter for underground dance artists?

Beyond the licensing fee, a GTA placement delivers exposure at a scale most independent dance artists cannot otherwise reach, filtered through years of gameplay streams, video compilations and social clips that keep resurfacing long after a game's launch window closes.
