As of 18 November 2026, London's best record shops cluster in Soho (Phonica, Sounds of the Universe, Reckless, Sister Ray, Third Man), Notting Hill (Rough Trade West, Honest Jon's) and East London (Rough Trade East, Flashback, World of Echo), with newer specialists in Peckham and Brixton. Most carry a 2025 or 2026 source, and we flag those that do not.

This is part of our best record shops in the world series. We built it from shop websites, Time Out London, Resident Advisor, Wikipedia and The Vinyl Factory, and describe only what a source we opened supports. UK vinyl revenue rose 19.9 percent in 2025, per BPI figures in [our vinyl sales report](/blog/vinyl-sales-record-2025), and Time Out's Record Store Day 2025 list named 37 London shops. Hours change, so confirm before you travel.

## Which London area has the most record shops?

In the shops we could verify, Soho has the densest cluster, with six, two of them on Berwick Street. East London has the widest variety, spanning Brick Lane, Shoreditch, London Fields and Dalston, while Peckham and Brixton hold the most active newer dance shops.

## Where are the best record shops in Soho?

The Vinyl Factory's 2017 London guide put Phonica, Sounds of the Universe and Sister Ray in its ten, and Time Out's October 2026 guide includes Reckless, Sister Ray, Phonica and Third Man.

### Phonica

Phonica opened in 2003 at 51 Poland Street, per Time Out, which describes club music from minimal techno to dubstep, drum and bass and French electro. The Vinyl Factory credits manager Simon Rigg with a broad approach and lists Four Tet, Floating Points, Caribou and Dixon among regulars.

### Sounds of the Universe

Sounds of the Universe, at 7 Broadwick Street, is the base of the Soul Jazz label, per The Vinyl Factory and Time Out. Founder Stuart Baker began with a Camden stall, and Soul Jazz began in 1992. The basement holds second-hand jazz and music books, and Time Out's report on the Financial Times list names it among London's greats.

### Reckless Records

Reckless has sold records on Berwick Street since 1984, says its site, which lists 30 Berwick Street and hours of 10am to 7pm daily. Time Out places new LPs at the front, with second-hand rock and roll, reggae and soul and bargain bins at the back.

### Sister Ray

Sister Ray's site says it was founded in 1989 and sells vinyl and CDs on Berwick Street. Time Out puts CDs on the ground floor and vinyl in the basement, and notes its shopfront appears behind an Oasis album cover. Its news page lists a U2 listening party for 12 November 2026.

### Third Man Records

Jack White's Third Man opened at 1 Marshall Street in 2021, per Time Out, which calls it the chain's only store outside the US. Its official page lists a retail floor, a basement venue called The Blue Basement and a booth for recording straight to vinyl. Its Sunday hours read like a typo, so confirm before going.

### Rough Trade Denmark Street

Rough Trade opened at 24 Denmark Street in October 2024, per Wikipedia and its own announcement. Time Out says this was the branch the Financial Times included, not the Brick Lane flagship. Rough Trade said it would have two floors and no live stage.

## Where are the best record shops in Notting Hill?

### Rough Trade West

Rough Trade West is at 130 Talbot Road. Wikipedia says Geoff Travis opened the first shop in 1976 on Kensington Park Road and moved to Talbot Road in 1982. The Vinyl Factory describes a stock from indie to dance 12 inch records and a punk-era wall of 7 inch singles.

### Honest Jon's

Honest Jon's, at 278 Portobello Road, says it was established in 1974. Hours are Monday to Saturday 10am to 6pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm, and the stock spans blues, dance, folk, jazz, reggae and soul. A smaller branch at Unit 115, Coal Drops Yard, King's Cross opens 11am to 6pm Monday to Saturday and 11am to 5pm on Sunday. The Vinyl Factory traces the shop to a jazz stall in a former butcher's on Golborne Road and links its label to Damon Albarn.

### Music & Video Exchange

Time Out says Music & Video Exchange buys and swaps used records, and traces it to a Hull market stall in the late 1960s. It names the Notting Hill shop and a Greenwich branch. We did not confirm a street address from a source we opened.

## Where are the best record shops in East London?

### Rough Trade East

Rough Trade East opened in July 2007 in the former Truman's Brewery off Brick Lane, per Wikipedia. The Vinyl Factory says David Adjaye designed it, and Time Out notes a live stage, books and merchandise. Resident Advisor named it in its 27 June 2026 East London crawl.

### Flashback Records in Shoreditch

Flashback's Shoreditch branch sits alongside its Islington original, which Time Out says opened in 1997. The Vinyl Factory's 2017 guide gave Bethnal Green Road as the address, and Resident Advisor named it in the June 2026 crawl.

### World of Echo

World of Echo, on Cheshire Street just off Brick Lane, stocks early electronic music, krautrock, noise and EBM, per Resident Advisor. In November 2026 it began selling the first part of the JD Twitch collection, covered in [our JD Twitch report](/blog/jd-twitch-optimo-record-collection-sale-world-of-echo).

### BBE Store

The BBE Store is the label BBE's shop and events space in a London Fields railway arch, opened in 2016, per Time Out. On 23 October 2026 Time Out reported that Hackney Council had received an application to review its licence after noise complaints from a new residential building. The council said it had not taken steps to close the store.

### Kristina Records and Recycle Vinyl

Kristina Records was a participant in Resident Advisor's 31 May 2026 report on The Dig crawl, so it was trading this year. The Vinyl Factory's 2017 guide gave a Stoke Newington Road address in Dalston, and we did not open a source for its current address. Recycle Vinyl's Dalston branch on Birkbeck Mews opens Friday to Sunday and has a bar licence, per Resident Advisor in December 2024.

## Where are the best record shops in north London?

### Flashback Records in Islington

Time Out says Flashback opened on Essex Road in 1997 with clothing and now sells second-hand and new vinyl, with branches in Crouch End and Shoreditch. The Vinyl Factory's 2017 guide lists number 50 Essex Road and calls out krautrock, library and soundtrack stock.

### Camden: All Ages Records and Raven Records

Time Out calls All Ages Records a punk and hardcore specialist that began as a Camden Market stall. Raven Records on Camden Road opened in 2024 for hard rock and metal, with an alcohol-free bar and an events space called Lucifer Lounge.

## Where are the best record shops in south London?

### Inverted Audio in Peckham

Inverted Audio opened on 15 September 2023 at Unit 11, Holdrons Arcade, 135A Rye Lane, per The Spaces. It stocks new and used house, techno and experimental records, with labels such as Ilian Tape and FXHE. A newsletter dated 14 April 2026 announced open decks for Record Store Day on 18 April.

### Maestro in Peckham

Time Out calls Maestro Peckham's oldest record store, founded over 20 years ago and formerly Reed's Records. It stocks roots reggae, pop, jazz, hip-hop and dancehall.

### Palace Vinyl in Brixton

Resident Advisor reported on 2 March 2025 that Palace Vinyl was leaving Crystal Palace for Brixton Village, opening on 8 March, after seven years of focusing on electronic music. We found no 2026 source we could open.

### Lion Vibes in Brixton

Lion Vibes is a vinyl-only reggae shop at 98 Granville Arcade, Brixton Village, opened by Matt Downs, per a Time Out listing dated January 2016. It is on Time Out's Record Store Day 2025 list, our latest evidence.

### Soul Brother Records in Putney

Soul Brother opened in 1994, founded by Laurence and Malcolm Prangell, per Time Out's report on the Financial Times list. It holds more than 10,000 vinyl records and more than 3,000 CDs of soul, jazz and funk.

## Where can you buy techno and house vinyl in London?

Start at Phonica in Soho, then try Inverted Audio and Palace Vinyl on the south side. Sounds of the Universe stocks dub-techno 12 inch records per The Vinyl Factory, and World of Echo suits early electronic music and EBM. For where the records get played, see our [London clubs guide](/blog/best-london-clubs-2026-guide).

## Where to buy second-hand records in London?

Try Flashback, Reckless, Music & Video Exchange and Soho's Sister Ray. Reckless says it buys collections weekly, and Sister Ray says it buys used vinyl. Rough Trade Vintage, a used-records basement on Beak Street, is reported by Wikipedia as closed on 31 July 2026, which we could not confirm.

## Where to find jazz, reggae and soul records in London?

Honest Jon's is the classic. Soul Brother, Maestro and Lion Vibes are specialist alternatives, and Time Out calls Supertone near Trafalgar Square one of the UK's oldest reggae shops, trading since 1983. Time Out's Financial Times report also names Hawkeye Enterprises and Starlight Records in Harlesden, both from the 1970s.

## Where to find indie and rock records in London?

Rough Trade West and East are the default, with Sister Ray and Reckless in Soho. Banquet Records in Kingston hosts about 200 gigs a year, per Time Out's Financial Times report, and Raven Records covers metal.

## What we could not verify

- **Rough Trade Vintage**: Wikipedia cites a social media post for a 31 July 2026 closure, which we did not see.
- **Rough Trade's start date**: Wikipedia says 1976, while The Vinyl Factory's older feature says 1978.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best record shop in London?

For dance music, Phonica in Soho is the strongest pick, per Time Out's 2026 guide and The Vinyl Factory. For history and range, Rough Trade West and Honest Jon's are the classics. "Best" depends on whether you want club releases, second-hand digging or reggae and jazz.

### Which London record shops did the Financial Times name?

Time Out reports that the Financial Times named Sounds of the Universe, Third Man Records, Honest Jon's, Hawkeye Enterprises and Starlight Records, Rough Trade's Denmark Street branch, Soul Brother Records and Banquet Records. We could not read the paywalled list, so this comes from Time Out's report of 20 January 2025.

### Where can I buy techno and house records in London?

Start at Phonica in Soho, then try Inverted Audio in Peckham and Palace Vinyl in Brixton. Sounds of the Universe also stocks dub-techno 12 inch records.

### Is Rough Trade in Notting Hill or Brick Lane?

Both. Rough Trade West is at 130 Talbot Road in Notting Hill, and Rough Trade East is in the former Truman's Brewery off Brick Lane. A third London shop opened on Denmark Street in October 2024.

### What is the oldest record shop in London?

We cannot name one with certainty. Honest Jon's says it was established in 1974, and Wikipedia dates the first Rough Trade shop to 1976. Time Out traces Music & Video Exchange to a Hull market stall in the late 1960s, which is not the same as a continuous London shop.

### Are London record shops open on Sundays?

Many are. Honest Jon's lists Sunday opening from 11am to 5pm, and Reckless lists daily hours from 10am to 7pm. Some smaller shops, such as Recycle Vinyl in Dalston, trade only Friday to Sunday.

### Where can I find reggae records in London?

Try Honest Jon's in Notting Hill, Lion Vibes in Brixton Village, Maestro in Peckham and Supertone near Trafalgar Square. Hawkeye Enterprises and Starlight Records in Harlesden are named in Time Out's Financial Times report.

### Which London record shops have gigs or a stage?

Rough Trade East has a live stage, per Time Out's 2026 guide, and Third Man has The Blue Basement, per its official page. The BBE Store also runs events. Rough Trade said Denmark Street would have no live stage.
