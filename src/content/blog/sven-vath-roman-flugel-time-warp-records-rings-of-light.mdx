## What is Time Warp Records?

Time Warp Records is a new record label launched by the Time Warp event brand with Insomniac Music Group as its partner, according to Resident Advisor and 909originals. As of 23 November 2026 it has one release: "Rings Of Light" by Sven Väth and Roman Flügel, catalogue number TWR001, out on 20 November 2026 as a digital release and a limited 12-inch vinyl edition. The B-side is a rougher "Knarz Mix". The label's Bandcamp page calls it the first release from Time Warp's new label, tied to the Time Warp festival, and the official Time Warp site presents the imprint as a way to carry the club night's sound further, all year round. Time Warp founder Steffen Charles said it mattered that the first release came from an artist who plays only vinyl. No second release has been announced.

The launch moves Time Warp from promoter to publisher. Until now the name meant a night in a fairground hall, plus a growing list of international editions. Now it also means a catalogue number.

### Who is behind the label?

Time Warp Records sits inside the same company structure as the events. 909originals reports that Insomniac Music Group helped establish the label with Time Warp, building on a partnership that began in 2024 with events in North America. Dubiks adds that Insomniac's house and techno arm is Factory 93, and Rave Jungle quotes Insomniac's Joe Wiseman saying the partnership let his team help Time Warp launch its own label.

Electronic Groove reports that the move marks a return to roots for Charles, who started Time Warp out of his own record shop. Our [Time Warp festival page](/festivals/time-warp) covers that origin: club nights organised from a Mannheim record shop in 1993, and the first event under the Time Warp name on 26 November 1994.

## What is "Rings Of Light"?

"Rings Of Light" is a hypnotic, stripped-down minimal techno track built on rolling rhythms, ominous organ chords and spoken word, with light and darkness as its theme, according to the label's Bandcamp page. We Rave You describes spiralling rhythms and gravelly spoken word, and Electronic Groove adds evolving textures. The original mix runs 8:24, and Bandcamp credits production to Väth and Flügel and mastering to Hans-Philipp Graf.

It was recorded in Flügel's basement studio in Berlin, according to We Rave You and 909originals. Both outlets describe it as the first time the two have made music together in more than two decades. Väth is quoted calling it a track born from a long musical friendship, and Flügel is quoted saying they did not need much time to synchronise their frequencies.

### What is the Knarz Mix?

The B-side, "Rings Of Light (Knarz Mix)", runs 5:58 and is described by Bandcamp as a rougher, industrial take with hissing hi-hats, rattling drums and acid basslines. We Rave You and Electronic Groove both mention 303-driven acid sequences. RA's tracklist writes it as "Knarz Remix", while the label's Bandcamp page and most trade outlets write "Knarz Mix".

### Who is Knarz?

None of the sources we opened says. Rave Jungle and Dubiks note that "Knarz" translates from German as "creak", which fits the sound described, but neither says whether it names a person, an alias or simply a mix title. Bandcamp lists production credits for Väth and Flügel only and gives no separate credit for the B-side. Until the label or a retailer credits it, treat Knarz as a mix name, not as a confirmed third artist.

## Who are Sven Väth and Roman Flügel?

Both are Frankfurt-rooted figures who have been central to German electronic music since the early 1990s, and their friendship predates the label by decades.

### Who is Sven Väth?

[Sven Väth](/artists/sven-vath) was born in Obertshausen near Frankfurt in 1964. He co-founded the Omen club in November 1988, ran Eye Q and Harthouse in the early 1990s, and registered Cocoon Recordings in 2000. Time Warp's own artist archive lists him from 1995 onward, with 66 listed appearances in total and four in 2026. Our [Cocoon label timeline](/blog/cocoon-recordings-history-timeline) traces how that label grew from a 1,000-copy box set to a catalogue of more than 300 releases, still under a single owner.

### Who is Roman Flügel?

Roman Flügel is a Frankfurt producer and DJ. Time Warp's artist page credits him with founding the Ongaku and Playhouse labels. In a 2016 Mixmag feature he described Playhouse, Klang Elektronik and Ongaku Musik as starting from the Delerium Records shop in Frankfurt from 1993, and said he used many aliases because labels disliked DJs working in different styles. That feature also mentions his solo album "Happiness Is Happening" from 2014 and "All The Right Noises" on Dial, announced for 28 October 2016.

He has also appeared on Cocoon's anniversary compilations. Our own coverage lists him on "20 Years Cocoon Recordings" in 2021 and on "25 Years Cocoon Recordings, Volume Two" on 5 June 2026. On the festival side, our [Waking Life festival page](/festivals/waking-life) notes his place on the 2019 bill.

### How do Väth and Flügel know each other?

According to We Rave You, the pair met at Frankfurt's Omen club after turning up in the same Fresh Jive T-shirt, and Flügel has songwriting credits on Väth's albums "Contact" and "Fire" on Cocoon around the turn of the millennium. Rave Jungle dates their creative relationship to the late 1990s. That backstory comes from the label's press material as relayed by those outlets, so treat the details as their account, not as something we checked against the album credits.

## Is Time Warp Records connected to the Time Warp festival?

Yes. The official Time Warp site promotes the release on its homepage with the catalogue number, a pre-save link and a vinyl pre-order link, and runs a "Time Warp Records" section describing the label as a new home for the sound of the Time Warp club event, which began in 1994. The Bandcamp page for the release sits on the Time Warp account and describes the label as tied to the festival.

What is less clear is the legal and commercial split. The sources describe the label as launched by Time Warp in partnership with Insomniac Music Group, but none discloses who owns the masters, how revenue is shared or how releases are distributed beyond the Bandcamp and streaming links.

The digital album is on the Time Warp Bandcamp page as a streaming and download release in MP3, FLAC and other formats, with a 24-bit/44.1kHz download, priced at €3 when we checked. The vinyl is a limited 12-inch on 140-gram vinyl, bundled with the digital album and capped at 500 copies worldwide. When we checked, Bandcamp listed the vinyl as sold out. ## When was "Rings Of Light" released?

The label's Bandcamp page, RA and 909originals give 20 November 2026. The official homepage says the release is out on that date, digitally and on vinyl. We Rave You published its report on 23 November and calls it out now. Techno Music World shows a publication date of 3 November 2026, which is consistent with an earlier teaser, and Rave Jungle and Dubiks give no exact date. One retailer listing in search results suggested a different date in late November, but we could not open it, so we are not relying on it.

The official Records page also prints the catalogue number as "TRW001", while the homepage and Bandcamp say "TWR 001" and "TWR001". We treat TWR001 as correct and TRW001 as a typo.

## Why does a festival label matter now?

Festival and event brands have long grown out of record shops and labels, and the reverse move is rarer. Time Warp started in a record shop; [Kompakt](/blog/kompakt-records-cologne-history) in Cologne also began as a record shop in 1993 and became a label that defined a sound. A label gives an event a year-round presence and a product that can be sold without a ticket.

The timing is also deliberate in format terms. According to our [vinyl sales analysis](/blog/vinyl-sales-record-2025), US vinyl revenue passed $1 billion in 2025 and UK sales grew 19.9%, and a 500-copy 12-inch that sells out at a label's own Bandcamp page fits that appetite. The sold-out status is the part we can verify; whether it reflects demand or a deliberately small run is not something the label has said.

For Väth, a vinyl-first DJ, the format is on brand. For Time Warp, the label gives the brand a second revenue stream next to tickets, though no source discusses economics, and we will not guess at them.

## Will Väth and Flügel play Time Warp Mannheim in 2027?

Väth is on the bill. Time Warp Germany 2027 takes place on Saturday 3 April at the Maimarkthalle, and our [Time Warp Mannheim 2027 lineup report](/blog/time-warp-mannheim-2027-lineup) lists him among the 49 announced artists. We Rave You also says he is booked for Mannheim 2027, and Rave Jungle says he is scheduled for the New York edition in the release week.

Flügel is a different case. Time Warp's artist page for him shows one appearance, in 2024, and lists the Los Angeles and Germany 2027 events among upcoming dates without confirming he is booked for either. His name is not among the artists named in our lineup report, so we do not claim he is on the 2027 bill. Check the official event page.

### What is Time Warp Records?

Time Warp Records is a new label launched by the Time Warp event brand in partnership with Insomniac Music Group. As of 23 November 2026 it has issued one record, "Rings Of Light" by Sven Väth and Roman Flügel. The label's Bandcamp page ties it directly to the Time Warp festival.

### What is the first release on Time Warp Records?

The first release is "Rings Of Light" by Sven Väth and Roman Flügel, catalogue number TWR001. It is a minimal techno track of 8:24, with a 5:58 "Knarz Mix" on the B-side. Press coverage calls it Time Warp Records 001.
