As of 26 November 2026, much of Berlin's New Year is already on sale or on paper. Tresor is running six events from 28 December to 2 January, Renate promises 60 hours, [Groove reports](https://groove.de/2026/11/26/berliner-club-marathons-zum-jahreswechsel-2026-27-die-line-ups-im-ueberblick/) an 82-hour weekender at RSO.BERLIN, and ://about blank has a programme running into 3 January. Berghain has posted dates and rooms but no artists.

The pattern this year, as Groove puts it, is toward multi-day marathons and events that start on 1 January. That changes how to plan: the question is less where to be at midnight than where you want to be on Saturday afternoon.

Each entry below says where it comes from. "Official" means we found it on the club's own site or ticket shop. "Groove" means we found it in Groove's 26 November overview and could not confirm it first-hand. Line-ups can change, so check the club before you travel.

## How should you plan a Berlin New Year?

Pick one or two anchors, buy where you can, and leave room for refusal at the door.

1. **Decide your start.** Midnight, Friday afternoon and Saturday night are different parties. Several of the biggest billings begin after midnight or on 1 January.
2. **Do not assume a ticket gets you in.** Tresor and Renate both say so in writing. Read each house policy before paying.
3. **Bring a card, and check.** Renate takes card only. We found no payment rules for most other venues.
4. **Confirm times on the day.** Several of the listings we read give no closing time.
5. **Have a second option.** With multi-day events, re-entry rules and queues matter. We did not find re-entry rules for these New Year events, so check before you leave a venue.

Not every familiar name is open. ELSE says on its [events page](https://www.else.tv/events-tickets) that it is closed until April 2027. We did not find official New Year listings for Sisyphos, KitKatClub or OXI in this search, which does not mean they have no plans.
