## When is Tomorrowland 2027?

Tomorrowland Belgium 2027 takes place over two weekends at De Schorre in Boom. Weekend 1 runs from Friday 16 to Sunday 18 July 2027 and weekend 2 from Friday 23 to Sunday 25 July 2027, according to the official Tomorrowland Belgium site, which also gives the location as Boom, Belgium. The festival announced the dates on 18 November 2026. Several outlets repeated the same dates, including We Rave You, EDMTunes, FestivalInfo and Paris Match Belgium, so there is no conflict on when it happens. Tickets are sold per weekend or per day, and you pick your weekend when you buy: the official ordering guide says upgrades cannot be made after purchase. The theme and the lineup have not been revealed, and We Rave You and FestivalInfo both say they will follow later. The official site describes the festival as having 16 stages, 400,000 People of Tomorrow and 191 nationalities.

## When do Tomorrowland 2027 tickets go on sale?

The official sales dates are 30 January for Global Journey packages, 6 February for the worldwide pre-sale and 13 February for the worldwide ticket sale, all at 17:00 CET. That comes from the Sales Dates and How to Order pages on belgium.tomorrowland.com, which we read on 23 November 2026. The earlier date of 29 January that some reports lead with is a different thing: a sale reserved for residents of the local region.

### Why do reports give different Tomorrowland ticket dates?

The reports look contradictory because they list different sales. We checked each against the official site.

| Report | What it says | Our reading |
|---|---|---|
| Brussels Times, 19 November | Sales "will open on Friday 29 January, with an exclusive window for local residents" | Correct for residents of Boom, Rumst and the Rupel region. Not the general sale. The same article also lists 6 February pre-sale and 13 February general sale |
| FestivalInfo | The article lists 29 January for residents, 30 January Global Journey, 6 February pre-sale and 13 February worldwide. Its ticket listings for both weekends show a pre-sale start of 13 February at 10:00 | The article matches the official dates. The 10:00 listing conflicts with the official 17:00 CET, and we could not explain it |
| EDM Identity | Reported by a search snippet as pre-sale 6 February and general sale 13 February | We could not open the page (blocked), so we do not rely on it |
| DJ Mag | Headline covers dates and ticket details | We could not open the page (blocked), so we do not rely on it |
| EDMTunes and We Rave You | 29 January residents, 30 January Global Journey, 6 February pre-sale, 13 February worldwide | Consistent with the official dates, plus the residents window |

So the conflict resolves like this: 6 February is the worldwide pre-sale, 13 February is the general sale, and 29 January is a local residents window. The one open question is the 29 January sale itself. It appears in Belga's wire copy and in several outlets, but the official Sales Dates page we read does not list it. If you live in the Rupel region, check your Tomorrowland Account for details.

### How does Tomorrowland pre-registration work?

You pre-register through your Tomorrowland Account between 2 December 2026 at 15:00 CET and 12 February 2027 at 15:00 CET. The official site says pre-registration is needed to take part in the ticket sales and does not guarantee a ticket.

A few official rules to know before the sale:

- Create your account at least two hours before a sale starts, and check your personal details, because they are filled in automatically at checkout.
- You can buy tickets for a maximum of four people per account across all sales, and Global Journey packages for a maximum of six.
- The first 20 people of each country of residence who pre-register can buy four tickets in a "First 20 Sale", at worldwide prices.
- You must pay straight away, or the order is cancelled and the tickets go back on sale.
- Check that your card allows international payments to Belgium and that its limit is high enough.
- Every ticket must be personalized with a full name and email address.

All purchases are final. Tickets cannot be changed, upgraded or cancelled, but you can list them on the official Exchange Desk, which opens on 13 February 2027. The listing deadline is 29 April 2027, though the Exchange Desk page and the How to Order page give different cut-off times. A 10 euro fee per ticket is deducted from any refund.

## How much do Tomorrowland tickets cost?

The official ticket pages list a weekend Full Madness Pass at 335 euros in the worldwide and Belgian pre-sale and 410 euros in the worldwide ticket sale. A day pass is 144 euros in pre-sale and 159 euros in the general sale. These pages sit on the 2027 site, and the Brussels Times article lists the same standard prices as 2027 prices, but the pages themselves do not print a year, so treat them as the current listing rather than a 2027 price announcement. We have not compared them with 2026 prices.

| Ticket (official listing) | Pre-sale | Worldwide sale |
|---|---|---|
| Full Madness Pass (one weekend) | 335 euros | 410 euros |
| Full Madness Comfort Pass | 581 euros | 700 euros |
| Day Pass | 144 euros | 159 euros |
| Day Pleasure Pass | 194 euros | 224 euros |
| Day Comfort Pass | 256 euros | 275 euros |

On top of that, each ticket carries a Treasure Case fee for the bracelet box: 15 euros for Belgium, 22 euros for the EU and 31 euros for the rest of the world, per person. Comfort passes add access to bar areas at the MainStage, the Great Library stage and the Freedom stage. The Day Pleasure Pass includes the last two but not the MainStage Comfort area.

Brussels Times also reports resident prices of 125 euros for a day and 310 euros for three days. We found that only in Brussels Times, not on the official pages, so we cannot confirm it. Global Journey travel packages, which bundle a ticket with hotel or DreamVille accommodation, with or without transport, are priced separately, and the official site offers a simulator.

## What is Tomorrowland Winter 2027?

Tomorrowland Winter 2027 is the festival's alpine edition in Alpe d'Huez, France, from 20 to 27 March 2027, according to the official Tomorrowland Winter site. Tickets always include a lift and ski pass. The seven-day ticket covers 20 to 26 March and the four-day ticket 23 to 26 March, and one-day tickets are sold for either 20 to 22 March, when the MainStage is closed, or 23 to 26 March, when it is open.

The sale ran earlier than the summer one. According to the official How to Order page, seven-day festival packages went on sale on 3 October 2026 at 15:00 CEST, four-day packages on 10 October at 15:00 CEST, and seven-, four- and one-day festival tickets on 10 October at 17:00 CEST. So 3 October applies to the seven-day packages only.

On 23 November 2026, the Tomorrowland Winter homepage showed festival tickets as on sale and the four- and seven-day packages as sold out. The site cites more than 150 artists, more than 30,000 People of Tomorrow and a "Great Library of Tomorrow" story as the 2027 theme. A line-up page exists, but the artist names load in a way we could not read, so we do not list any. We Rave You reports that the Winter lineup is already out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Tomorrowland 2027?

Tomorrowland Belgium 2027 runs over two weekends: Friday 16 to Sunday 18 July and Friday 23 to Sunday 25 July 2027. It is held at De Schorre in Boom, Belgium, according to the official site.

### When do Tomorrowland 2027 tickets go on sale?

Per the official site, the worldwide pre-sale is Saturday 6 February 2027 and the general worldwide sale is Saturday 13 February 2027, both at 17:00 CET. Global Journey packages go on sale on 30 January at the same time. Residents of Boom, Rumst and the Rupel region are reported to get a first window on 29 January.

### Why do some articles say 29 January and others 13 February?

They describe different sales. The Brussels Times and other outlets report 29 January as an exclusive window for local residents, while 13 February is the general worldwide sale. FestivalInfo's 13 February 10:00 listing differs from the official 17:00 CET, so go with the official time.

### How do you pre-register for Tomorrowland 2027?

Sign in to or create a Tomorrowland Account and pre-register on the Tomorrowland Belgium 2027 event page. It opens on 2 December 2026 at 15:00 CET and closes on 12 February 2027 at 15:00 CET. The official site warns that pre-registration does not guarantee a ticket.

### How much do Tomorrowland tickets cost?

The official ticket pages list a weekend Full Madness Pass at 335 euros in pre-sale and 410 euros in the general sale, and a day pass at 144 and 159 euros. A Treasure Case fee of 15, 22 or 31 euros per ticket depends on where it ships. The pages do not print a year, so check them at the time of the sale.

### Who is playing Tomorrowland 2027?

The lineup has not been announced. We Rave You and FestivalInfo both say the theme and lineup will be revealed later, without giving a date.

### How many tickets can you buy?

The official site allows tickets for a maximum of four people per Tomorrowland Account across all sales, and Global Journey packages for a maximum of six. The first 20 people of each country of residence to pre-register can buy four tickets in a separate First 20 Sale.

### Can you resell or cancel Tomorrowland tickets?

Purchases are final and cannot be changed, upgraded or cancelled. You can list tickets on the official Exchange Desk from 13 February 2027 until 29 April 2027. If a ticket sells, a 10 euro fee per ticket is deducted from the refund, which is processed within eight weeks.

### What is Tomorrowland Winter 2027?

Tomorrowland Winter 2027 is held in Alpe d'Huez, France, from 20 to 27 March 2027, with tickets that include a lift and ski pass. On 23 November 2026 the official site showed festival tickets as on sale and packages as sold out.

### Did Tomorrowland sell out last year?

VRT reported on 31 January 2026 that the worldwide sale for Tomorrowland 2026 had sold out. The article does not say how long that took, and we have not seen an official figure.
