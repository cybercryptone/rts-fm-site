As of 23 November 2026, Kraftwerk Berlin has apologised for hosting the 11 November launch of Helsing's Clarity military AI platform. Its head of sales called the booking "a serious error of judgement", Tresor condemned the event, and Berlin Atonal said it was shocked. Kraftwerk has pledged a booking-policy review and a donation of the proceeds.

## What happened at Kraftwerk Berlin on 11 November?

On 11 November 2026, Helsing, a German defence technology company, launched Clarity, which it describes as an AI-powered military intelligence platform, at Kraftwerk Berlin, the former power station on Köpenicker Straße that also houses Tresor, OHM and Berlin Atonal. According to Resident Advisor, the launch took place in the Turbine Hall. The booking drew criticism from Berlin's club community. Kraftwerk's head of sales, Tjabo Reuter, took responsibility and apologised, saying the commercial rental department had accepted the booking without consulting the cultural organisations in the building. Tresor condemned the event, and Berlin Atonal said it was shocked and asked for an ethical booking clause. Kraftwerk has pledged to review its booking policy and to donate the proceeds of the event. Reports between 18 and 22 November 2026 describe the apology, and we could not open every primary statement, so this account attributes each claim to the outlet that reported it.

## What was the Helsing event like?

Coverage describes an invitation-only product launch with a military theme. According to The Telegraph, syndicated by AOL on 22 November, the space was dressed as a command post, with drones hanging from the ceiling, a light show and projections of AI-generated landscapes. Military vehicles were parked outside, and a bomb-detection dog checked bags.

The Berliner reported projections of maps, target markers and impact trajectories, and said weapons were on display. Novara Media, citing Die Zeit, described combat vehicles outside and bomb-sniffing dogs checking bags. We did not open Die Zeit directly.

CDM, in a piece by Peter Kirn dated 17 November, reported speeches by Helsing co-CEO Gundbert Scherf and chief technology officer Rob Fink, and said the event was not on Kraftwerk's public programme. Happy Magazine also said it was not listed and that Helsing circulated a press pack of images from inside the venue.

## What is Clarity, according to Helsing?

Helsing describes Clarity as a platform that sifts military data, including sensor feeds, open sources and reports, to find adversary assets and flag possible targets. A report on Helsing's announcement, published by defence-industry.eu, says the company calls it a "sovereign AI-native agentic intelligence platform", built in Europe and run on customers' own infrastructure.

Helsing says a target is nominated only when independent sources agree and confidence is high, and that an operator must confirm it and approve each transfer of information to a weapon. Those are the company's own claims as relayed by that outlet. We could not load Helsing's own press release, so we have not checked the wording against the original.

Several outlets frame Clarity as a European alternative to Palantir's Maven. RA says Spotify co-founder Daniel Ek is an investor in Helsing, and no source we read says he attended the event. We take no position on Helsing's products or on defence policy; the rest of this article concerns the venue and the reaction.

## What is Kraftwerk Berlin?

Kraftwerk Berlin is a large event space in a former combined heat and power plant on Köpenicker Straße in Berlin Mitte. Our [Berlin Atonal festival page](/festivals/berlin-atonal) notes that the plant was built between 1960 and 1964, decommissioned in 1997 and revived in stages from the mid-2000s by Dimitri Hegemann. The official site lists spaces such as Level 0, Level 1, Level 2 and a Control room for hire, which is the rental business at the centre of this story.

Our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide) describes Kraftwerk as the building around Tresor rather than a club in its own right.

### Which clubs and festivals are in the building?

According to Resident Advisor, five operations work in the building: Tresor Club, Tresor Records, OHM, Berlin Atonal and Outer GmbH, and Kraftwerk Berlin. RA also names Globus among the cultural organisations in the building.

- **Tresor** is the techno club and label, in the complex since 2007 according to our guide.
- **OHM** is the smaller club in the same complex.
- **Berlin Atonal** is the experimental festival that, per Midnight Rebels, has held every edition at the site since 2013. Its 2027 edition is announced for 25 to 29 August 2027.
- **Hard Wax**, the record shop, moved into the complex on 30 October 2023, as covered in our [Berlin record shops guide](/blog/best-record-shops-in-berlin-2026). RA's list of the five operations does not include it, and we found no statement from the shop in the coverage we read.

### Who actually runs what?

The structure is part of the story. RA says Dimitri Hegemann is managing director of both Tresor and Kraftwerk. CDM adds that the Tresor, OHM and Kraftwerk companies sit under one umbrella with Hegemann as chief executive of each, but publishes a correction: Atonal is Outer GmbH, owned solely by founders Laurens von Oswald and Harry Glass, and Hegemann has no stake or business role in it.

## How did Kraftwerk Berlin respond?

Kraftwerk responded on two levels, and the wording differs between outlets.

- **Head of sales.** According to RA (18 November), Tjabo Reuter said the commercial rental department accepted the booking and that, as its head, he takes responsibility. RA reports him calling it a "serious error of judgement" and saying the event made Kraftwerk a platform for promoting weapons and military AI. He apologised to staff, artists, visitors, partners and the cultural organisations, and said he would review the department's booking policy and set clear criteria.
- **Venue statement.** The Berliner (20 November) reports a statement on Instagram that called accepting the booking "a serious mistake". We Rave You (22 November) adds that it was posted in English and German, apologised to staff, artists, visitors and cultural organisations, and said Tresor, OHM and Atonal were neither involved nor consulted.
- **Restructuring.** We Rave You says Kraftwerk promised to bring the cultural organisations into its management, introduce clear criteria for commercial rentals and make decisions more transparent.
- **Donation.** The Quietus (19 November) said the fee would go to a charity not yet chosen, and RA said the four organisations would choose recipients together, to be announced publicly. The later We Rave You and Telegraph reports (both 22 November) say Kraftwerk named Médecins Sans Frontières. We could not see the original post.

## What did Tresor say?

Tresor and Tresor Records condemned the event. According to The Quietus, they said "We condemn this decision" and that the booking was made without their involvement. RA reports that they said they operate separately from Kraftwerk's commercial rental business and receive no income from it, and that promoting weapons and military AI conflicts with values they have held since 1991.

The Berliner adds that artists, promoters, staff and visitors had contacted Tresor about the event. RA reports that Hegemann called agreeing to it unacceptable and said he was unaware of the booking. Tresor, Tresor Records, OHM and Berlin Atonal also committed to transparent booking criteria and formal participation in decisions affecting the building, per RA.

DJ Mag reported a Tresor statement on 20 November, but its site refused our request (HTTP 403), so we have not read it and rely on the other outlets above.

## What did Berlin Atonal and OHM say?

Berlin Atonal, per RA, said it was deeply shocked and upset that the building was rented to an arms company. It described itself as an independent venture that rents Kraftwerk for its own events, and said its association with the building cannot be taken as consent to the booking.

The Quietus reports that the festival asked Kraftwerk to add an ethical clause excluding arms and military companies from commercial bookings. CDM says Atonal welcomed the announced restructuring and the donation. Midnight Rebels reports that Atonal still plans its next edition at the venue and says it can push for change from inside.

We found no standalone statement from OHM. It is named in the Kraftwerk and Tresor statements as uninvolved, and RA reports it committed to the joint review of booking criteria.

## What happened with Palantir in 2022?

According to CDM, Kraftwerk's own FAQ says it declined a proposed Palantir-sponsored event in 2022 after consulting Berlin Atonal and other cultural teams. The same FAQ says the Helsing booking was not subject to "ethical review" and that cultural teams were not consulted, and that the staff member should have consulted them first.

The Telegraph notes that critics pointed to the 2022 refusal, and to anti-war messaging at a Ukraine benefit that year, as a contrast with the Helsing booking. Novara Media says the Tresor Foundation hung "NO WAR!" posters on the venue's outer wall in 2022. We have not seen the FAQ itself.

## How did Berlin's club community react?

We describe the reaction only as it was reported.

- A Berlin-based DJ wrote on social media that weapons companies should not launch products in Berlin techno clubs, per The Telegraph and Novara Media.
- The Telegraph also quotes DJ Michail Stangl, who argued that war in Europe should not mean giving up critical thinking.
- RA's own response came from Amy Van Baaren, its chief impact and culture officer, who called the booking a unilateral decision by Kraftwerk's commercial office. RA's 25th anniversary Berlin event at Tresor, Globus and OHM was due on 21 November, and RA said it respects any artist's decision to step away.
- We Rave You reports that commenters under Kraftwerk's post were divided. Some asked why the venue acted only once the event became public, while others argued that a company supplying Ukraine's defence should not be the subject of an apology.
- CDM's Peter Kirn wrote an opinion piece questioning whether such a booking could have happened without coordination. That is his view, not a reported fact.

## Why does the booking matter for Berlin club economics?

The sources do not say why the booking was accepted, and we do not speculate. What they do establish is the structure: Tresor says it receives no income from Kraftwerk's rental business, while the building is a large commercial venue. The Telegraph reports that it hosted a reception in October featuring Chancellor Friedrich Merz and Jensen Huang, so corporate events there are not new.

For wider context, our [Europe club closures tracker](/blog/europe-club-closures-tracker) summarises the Clubcommission Berlin study, presented on 7 August 2026, which found 61 percent of Berlin clubs and promoters covered their costs in 2025, down from 79 percent in 2017. Our [nightlife attendance analysis](/blog/why-people-go-out-less-nightlife-data-2026) covers the revenue mix behind that figure. Neither source links that pressure to this booking, and we do not either.

For how another Berlin institution is structured, see [who runs Berghain](/blog/who-runs-berghain-ownership-operators).

## What happens next?

The coverage names several pending steps, with no dates. Kraftwerk said it would review its rental booking policy and set clear criteria for future bookings. RA reports that Tresor, Tresor Records, OHM and Berlin Atonal will decide together which causes receive the proceeds, and that recipients will be announced publicly.

Berlin Atonal has asked for an ethical clause and shared decision-making. Its next edition is announced for 25 to 29 August 2027. We will update this article if Kraftwerk publishes the new criteria or the recipients.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What did Helsing launch at Kraftwerk Berlin?

Helsing launched Clarity on 11 November 2026. The company describes it as an AI-powered military intelligence platform that flags possible targets for human operators. Outlets describe the event as invitation-only.

### Why is the Helsing event at Kraftwerk Berlin controversial?

Kraftwerk Berlin houses Tresor, OHM and Berlin Atonal, which are part of Berlin's club and festival scene. Criticism came from the club community, and Tresor and Atonal said they were not consulted. Kraftwerk's head of sales said the booking should have been declined.

### Did Kraftwerk Berlin apologise?

Yes. According to RA, head of sales Tjabo Reuter apologised on 18 November and took responsibility. The Berliner and We Rave You also report a venue statement on Instagram calling the booking a serious mistake.

### Did Tresor know about the Helsing booking?

Tresor and Tresor Records said they were not involved or consulted. RA reports that Dimitri Hegemann, managing director of Tresor and Kraftwerk, said he was unaware of the booking. We could not read the original statements.

### What did Berlin Atonal ask Kraftwerk to change?

According to The Quietus, Atonal asked for an ethical clause excluding arms and military companies from commercial bookings. RA reports it also asked for transparent booking decisions with shared decision-making for cultural organisations.

### What did Kraftwerk Berlin do in 2022 with Palantir?

CDM reports that Kraftwerk's FAQ says it declined a proposed Palantir-sponsored event in 2022 after consulting Berlin Atonal and other cultural teams. The Helsing booking, it says, did not go through that process. We have not seen the FAQ ourselves.

### Where is the money from the event going?

Kraftwerk pledged to donate the proceeds. We Rave You and The Telegraph, both on 22 November, say Médecins Sans Frontières. RA and The Quietus, earlier, said recipients would be chosen jointly and announced publicly.

### Is Hard Wax affected?

Hard Wax moved into the Kraftwerk complex on 30 October 2023, per our Berlin record shops guide. RA's list of the building's five operations does not include it, and we found no statement from the shop in the coverage we read.

### Is Berlin Atonal 2027 still happening at Kraftwerk?

Berlin Atonal 2027 is announced for 25 to 29 August 2027 at Kraftwerk Berlin. Midnight Rebels reports the festival still plans its next edition there and wants to push for change from inside. Check the festival's website for updates.

### Will Kraftwerk Berlin change its booking rules?

Kraftwerk said it would review its rental booking policy and set clear criteria. We Rave You also reports a promise to bring cultural organisations into management. We have seen no published criteria or dates.
