As of 18 November 2026, the Berlin record shops we could verify as trading for techno and electronic music include Hard Wax (now at Köpenicker Straße 70, Mitte), SEVEN, Spacehall, Sound Metaphors and OYE Records. This guide covers 16 shops by district, with sources, published hours and what we could not confirm.

Berlin has more than 80 record shops, which The Berliner's music editor called the highest concentration of vinyl specialists in Europe in May 2026. ## Is Hard Wax worth visiting?

Yes, if you care about techno, dub or house. Top10 Berlin's 2026 ranking puts Hard Wax first of ten Berlin record shops, and The Berliner described it in 2024 as a store that has shaped the Berlin music scene. Its history is tied to Basic Channel and to the Berlin and Detroit exchange of the 1990s.

### How did Hard Wax start, and how does it connect to Basic Channel?

Hard Wax opened in 1989 in Kreuzberg, on Reichenberger Straße, according to [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/news/79685). Most sources name Mark Ernestus as the founder; the Vinyl Factory's 2015 list credits both Ernestus and Moritz von Oswald, so sources differ on that point.

The label link is documented. Wikipedia says Basic Channel, founded by von Oswald and Ernestus in 1993, grew out of the shop, and that Chain Reaction was an offshoot label by the same pair, though it gives no date for it. Resident Advisor reports that Ernestus and von Oswald founded the cutting house and mastering studio Dubplates and Mastering in 1995, in the same building as the shop.

The shop also took part in what the Vinyl Factory and Top10 call the Berlin and Detroit axis of the 1990s. Our [Detroit techno guide](/blog/detroit-techno-guide-2026-clubs-labels-history) covers the other end.

Former staff named by the Vinyl Factory (2015) and Top10 Berlin (2026) include [DJ Hell](/artists/dj-hell), Modeselektor's Gernot Bronsert and [Marcel Dettmann](/artists/marcel-dettmann).

### Why did Hard Wax leave Kreuzberg?

The building at Paul-Lincke-Ufer 44a was being redeveloped. Resident Advisor reported on 13 October 2023 that the shop would move on 30 October to the Kraftwerk complex on Köpenicker Straße, between the clubs Tresor and OHM, and quoted a source saying the old building was being converted into luxury flats. Hard Wax had been at Paul-Lincke-Ufer since 1996, per Wikipedia.

The Berliner's January 2024 piece contrasted the new single flight of stairs with the old metal staircase covered in label stickers. Kraftwerk also hosts [Berlin Atonal](/festivals/berlin-atonal), and Tresor, covered in our [Berlin clubs guide](/blog/best-berlin-clubs-2026-guide), shares the complex.

### When is Hard Wax open, and does it ship?

Hard Wax's own contact page lists Monday to Saturday, 15:00 to 20:00, at Köpenicker Str. 70, 10179 Berlin Mitte. It says the shop closes on 24, 25 and 26 December and 1 January. Its shipping page offers a calculator by country, post code and quantity, with Deutsche Post, PostNL, DHL and UPS options.

Top10 Berlin lists new 12-inch records from about 10 euros, LPs at 20 to 30 euros and used records from about 5 euros. We could not find a published policy on cash versus card or on in-store listening.

## Which record shops are in Mitte?

### SEVEN Community Record Store

SEVEN sits at Torstraße 220 and, per The Berliner's August 2026 guide, opened in May 2026 with a focus on modern house and techno from Berlin labels and a queer and FLINTA* centred approach. Top10 Berlin's October 2026 review counts four seated listening stations with Technics SL-1200 Mk7 turntables and Sennheiser headphones.

The shop carries crates from Dekmantel, [Kompakt](/blog/kompakt-records-cologne-history), Kalahari Oyster Cult, Berg Audio and Partisan, plus its own labels SEVEN, A.2AUM and PRNCPTL. Top10 lists hours as Monday and Tuesday 12:00 to 18:00, Thursday and Friday 14:00 to 20:00, Saturday 14:00 to 19:00, closed Wednesday and Sunday.

### Record Store Berlin

At Invalidenstraße 148, Record Store Berlin is not a techno shop. Top10 Berlin says it stocks sixties-focused original pressings, signed rarities and shellac records, with much of its stock sourced from the United States, and that the owners have run it for more than 15 years.

### Dussmann Kulturkaufhaus

Dussmann is a culture department store at Friedrichstraße 90, open since 1997 on five floors and 7,300 square metres, according to Berlin.de. Its music division stocks CDs and vinyl, including electronic music. The Berlin.de listing is undated, so we cannot confirm current stock or hours.

## Which record shops are in Kreuzberg?

### Spacehall

Spacehall's own site gives Zossener Straße 35 and hours of Monday and Wednesday to Friday 12:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 11:00 to 20:00. It is closed Tuesdays, Sundays and public holidays, warns that days may vary, and buys vinyl and CDs.

Top10 Berlin describes three rooms with a separate techno section at the back where labels such as [Ostgut Ton](/blog/ostgut-ton-berghain-label-history) are well represented, plus several DJ booths for listening. It puts used 12-inch records at 8 to 15 euros.

### Bikini Waxx

Bikini Waxx is at Manteuffelstraße 48, in a courtyard behind a buzzer. The Vinyl Factory says founder Gerd Tammist opened it in 2013 with four fellow diggers, and Electronic Beats reported in 2018 that it began as a living-room operation on Blücherstraße. Both pieces are old, but The Berliner's August 2026 guide still lists it at the same address.

## Which record shops are in Neukölln?

### Sound Metaphors

Sound Metaphors opened in 2015 in Kreuzberg, founded by DJs Nemo Ripoll and Castro Moore, per The Berliner. Resident Advisor reported on 9 May 2025 that it would close on 30 June 2025 because it could not extend its lease.

It came back. Top10 Berlin's July 2026 review places it at Weichselstraße 22, open Monday to Saturday 13:00 to 20:00, with second-hand records, a focus on 1990s electronic music and a reissue label. We could not find the date of the reopening.

### Soultrade

Soultrade at Sanderstraße 29 is the soul, funk, jazz and hip-hop specialist, and per Top10 Berlin grew out of the Jazzanova circle. Its July 2026 review counts at least 5,000 records, two listening turntables and card payment, with used LPs from about 8 euros.

### KIMCHI Records

KIMCHI, at Richardstraße 7 to 8, sells mostly second-hand records across electronic and other genres, and doubles as the base for its own label and distribution, according to The Berliner's August 2026 guide.

### Rough Trade Berlin

Rough Trade's Berlin shop opened in April 2024 on Karl-Marx-Straße 101, per Resident Advisor, as a broad new-release and independent-music shop with a bar and a live programme. Rough Trade's own blog post of 24 August 2026 announced a move to Ganghoferstraße 10, 12043, with a purpose-built stage. The post gives 1 and 2 September 2026 as the reopening date, and we did not find a post-opening confirmation.

## Which record shops are in Friedrichshain?

### Galactic Supermarket

Galactic Supermarket is at Petersburger Straße 89, just north of Frankfurter Tor. The Vinyl Factory described it in 2017 as holding roughly 35,000 second-hand items, especially jazz, krautrock and psychedelia. The Berliner's August 2026 guide lists it for hard-to-find records, so it still appears to be trading, though we found no hours.

### HHV Store

The HHV Store at Grünberger Straße 54 started in the late 1990s as a hip-hop import shop, as its name (Hip Hop Vinyl) says. Top10 Berlin's 2026 entry counts about 2,500 records, six listening stations, in-store pickup of online orders and Monday to Saturday hours of 12:00 to 20:00.

### Bis Aufs Messer

Bis Aufs Messer at Marchlewskistraße 107 has sold records since 2006, per Top10 Berlin. It stocks punk, drone, avant-garde, jazz and electronic music and runs the ADAGIO830 label, with weekday hours of 11:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 11:00 to 18:00.

## Which record shops are in Prenzlauer Berg?

### OYE Records

OYE Records began around 2002 in a basement near Kastanienallee, founded by DJ Lovis Willenberg and first catering to Latin, soul and funk collectors, according to Slow Travel Berlin. Tinko Rosht and Markus Lindner became partners in 2007. The Vinyl Factory's 2015 list calls it a hub of Berlin's analogue house scene.

Per its official site, the main shop is at Oderberger Straße 4, open Monday to Saturday 12:00 to 20:00, and a second shop at Friedelstraße 49 in Neukölln is open Monday to Friday 13:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 12:00 to 20:00. The webshop quotes delivery of 1 to 2 days within Germany and 5 to 30 days elsewhere.

## Which record shops are in Schöneberg?

### Dodo Beach

Dodo Beach at Vorbergstraße 8 describes itself as holding over 30,000 records, from 1960s beat to metal. Top10 Berlin highlights a metal section in the basement, and visitBerlin calls its East Berlin branch the oldest record shop in that half of the city.

### Awesome Tapes From Africa

The label's shop and listening library is at Crellestraße 26. Its own site lists Tuesday and Thursday 10:00 to 16:00, Wednesday 11:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 13:00 to 18:00, which makes it a shop to plan around.

## Which Berlin district has the most record shops?

By our count of The Berliner's August 2026 guide, which lists 29 shops, Neukölln has the most (eight, counting OYE's second shop), ahead of Kreuzberg (six), Friedrichshain (five), Schöneberg (three), Mitte (two) and Prenzlauer Berg (two). Four more are in Weissensee, Spandau, Wedding and Pankow.

That is an editor's selection, not a census, and The Berliner counts Hard Wax in Mitte, which matches the shop's own address. For techno specifically, Mitte and Kreuzberg hold Hard Wax, SEVEN, Spacehall and Bikini Waxx.

## Which Berlin record shops have closed?

- **Rotation Records.** Top10 Berlin says the techno and house shop, opened in 2003 near Rosenthaler Platz, became Rotation Boutique, a slow-fashion store.
- **Mr Dead and Mrs Free.** Tagesspiegel reported on 6 December 2017 that the Schöneberg shop was closing after 35 years. We found no later trading source.
- **The Record Loft, Bass Cadet and DENSE.** The Berliner's May 2026 column lists them among shops the city has lost over roughly a decade.

For the supply side, see our reports on [pressing plant capacity](/blog/vinyl-pressing-plant-capacity-crisis) and [vinyl's best year since 1983](/blog/vinyl-sales-record-2025).

## How do you shop for records in Berlin?

Check hours before you travel. Most shops we checked close on Sundays, and some have odd days: Spacehall is shut on Tuesdays, SEVEN on Wednesdays, and Awesome Tapes From Africa opens four days a week. Spacehall's own site and The Berliner both warn that days may vary.

For listening, SEVEN has four stations, HHV six, Soultrade two turntables and Spacehall several DJ booths. Soultrade says card payment is possible; for most other shops we found no published payment policy, so check before you go.

If you cannot visit, Hard Wax's shipping page covers dozens of countries with its own calculator, SEVEN ships to 49 countries and offers tax-free shopping for non-EU visitors over 50 euros with a passport, per Top10, and OYE's webshop publishes delivery times.

## What we could not verify

- **Dubplates and Mastering.** Mixmag reported in December 2022 that its lease ended in October 2023 and it had no new home. We could not find where it went.
- **Hard Wax cash policy and listening.** Nothing published that we could open.
- **Hard Wax's address on visitBerlin.** The tourist board's list gives Köpenicker Straße 170; the shop's own site says 70.
- **Who founded Hard Wax.** Sources split between Ernestus alone and Ernestus with von Oswald.
- **Rough Trade's new shop.** Opening confirmed only as announced, not as completed.
- **Dussmann's vinyl range.** Berlin.de's listing is undated; the store's own site blocked our request.
- **Hours for Galactic Supermarket, Bikini Waxx and KIMCHI.** Not found.
- **Melting Point, Latitude, Anagram Space.** Dropped: no 2025 to 2026 source we could open confirmed them as trading.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best record shop in Berlin for techno?

Hard Wax is the best known, ranked first in Top10 Berlin's 2026 list of record shops. It specialises in techno, dub and house and opened in 1989. SEVEN and Spacehall are the main alternatives.

### Where is Hard Wax in Berlin now?

Hard Wax is at Köpenicker Straße 70, 10179 Berlin Mitte, inside the Kraftwerk complex next to Tresor and OHM. It moved there on 30 October 2023 from Paul-Lincke-Ufer 44a in Kreuzberg, the address older guides still list.

### What are Hard Wax's opening hours?

The shop's own site lists Monday to Saturday, 15:00 to 20:00. It closes on 24, 25 and 26 December and 1 January. Check the site before visiting.

### Is Hard Wax connected to Basic Channel?

Yes. Mark Ernestus, who opened Hard Wax in 1989, co-founded Basic Channel with Moritz von Oswald in 1993, per Wikipedia. Hard Wax also distributes several Berlin labels associated with Basic Channel, per Top10 Berlin.

### Which Berlin district has the best record shops?

It depends on the genre. Mitte has Hard Wax and SEVEN for techno, Kreuzberg has Spacehall and Bikini Waxx, and Neukölln has the most shops overall by our count of The Berliner's guide.

### Is Sound Metaphors still open?

Yes, in a new location. It left Kreuzberg on 30 June 2025 and, per Top10 Berlin in July 2026, now trades at Weichselstraße 22 in Neukölln, Monday to Saturday 13:00 to 20:00.

### Are Berlin record shops open on Sundays?

Mostly not. Hard Wax, SEVEN, Spacehall, Sound Metaphors, HHV and Bis Aufs Messer all list Sunday as closed. Check each shop's hours before you go.

### Can you buy records from Berlin shops online?

Yes. Hard Wax offers a shipping calculator by country, SEVEN ships to 49 countries, and OYE quotes 5 to 30 days for delivery outside Germany.

### Which Berlin record shops have closed?

Rotation Records became a fashion boutique, Mr Dead and Mrs Free was reported closing in 2017, and The Berliner's May 2026 column lists The Record Loft, Bass Cadet and DENSE as lost.
